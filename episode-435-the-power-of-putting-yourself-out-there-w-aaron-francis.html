about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with aaron francis: how putting your work out there publicly creates opportunities through visibility, credibility, and connection.

share work publicly as you create it, not after you've perfected it or become an expert. lower the bar for what's shareable by focusing on insights novel to you, not the universe. position yourself as sharing lived experience, not absolute truth, to invite conversation not criticism. keep a capture system for ideas while working so sharing becomes a regular habit, not a chore. show up and give your best effort regardless of audience size or circumstances—people notice. ask for the opportunity you want since the worst case is the same no but the best case opens a new door. do your best work even when the audience is tiny because you never know who is watching or who they will tell later.

hanging out with the amazing Aaron Francis to really dig into his talk about putting yourself out there!
