about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: practical job search tactics including using blind carbon copy emails with recruiters and tracking your applications systematically.

keep job searching separate from personal email to avoid mixing up professional and personal communications. build genuine relationships with recruiters so they will share feedback and leads even when not actively working on a role. use calendly to make it easy for people to schedule quick calls with you during networking instead of endless back and forth messages. always know which recruiter submitted you where so you are not duplicated or left in the dark. use a scheduling link and ask for a short call like five minutes to make it easy for busy people to say yes.

Recruiters like to say general ways to find a job. This episode I give you practical tips you can implement in your day to day immediately to elevate your job search.
