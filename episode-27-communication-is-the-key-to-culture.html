communication is the key to culture

3 takeaways

  • > set expectations with your manager early about time off so there are no surprises later.
  • > ask new employers what their expectations are for after hours communication before you accept.
  • > over communicate during the workday so your team always knows where you are.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: over communicate in the workplace about your schedule, blockers, and availability to build trust and culture with your team.

post your status in slack or teams when you step away, work out, or take lunch so teammates know where you are. use video calls instead of messages when there is frustration or conflict to build rapport and prevent misunderstandings. leaders should keep video on during calls to show up for your team and build culture even when you do not want to. pick up the phone or hop on video instead of letting tension build over slack or dm.
