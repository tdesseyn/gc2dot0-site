about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with jacob herrington: how to navigate hiring obstacles as a senior engineer and why companies hurt themselves with extreme interview processes.

show your actual work like github instead of doing unnecessary take-home projects if you have proof of skills. have confidence in your market value and walk away from jobs with bad energy or unrealistic demands. build genuine relationships through networking rather than only reaching out when you need something. take rejection and being ignored as part of the process when cold outreach and applying broadly. early career grind matters but be strategic about which levers you pull instead of just doing dumb things. before doing a lengthy take home test, offer your public code history as proof of skill instead.
