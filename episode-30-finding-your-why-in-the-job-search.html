about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: identify your core reasons for job searching to make better career decisions and avoid regret after taking a role.

write down your top three to five reasons for job searching, ranked by importance. money is a valid why; be honest about what actually drives your decision. know your full compensation package including salary, benefits, and costs before accepting an offer. ask about benefits early in the process, not at the offer stage when it may be too late. dig deeper into job dissatisfaction to see if fixing one thing would solve the real problem. figure out your actual why before starting a job search instead of applying just to see what happens. its fine if your why is simply chasing more money, just be honest about it and own that reason. write down and rank your top priorities like money, title, work life balance, and commute before you search.
