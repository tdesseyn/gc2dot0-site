about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with reid evans: how critical thinking and personality separate you in job search and interviews, not just technical skills.

position yourself as a problem solver, not just someone with specific tech skills. communicate with business stakeholders, ask why questions, think beyond just executing tickets. in interviews, show you can think critically and admit what you don't know rather than claiming expertise. customize your outreach, include thoughtful cover letters, and use video calls with recruiters to build relationships. on resumes list past technologies you've used even if years ago, add personality and context about what you want next. a resume should tell a hiring manager what you do in 15 seconds, skip fancy fonts, columns, and headshots. your most recent job or two should have real detail since that is what hiring managers focus on most.

In this episode my dear friend Reid Evans (@ReidNEvans) and I discuss the importance of critical thinking in today's workplace and how to bring your personality to the forefront from a hiring perspective.
