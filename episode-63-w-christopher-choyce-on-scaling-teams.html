about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with christopher choyce: how to hire and scale teams fast while prioritizing soft skills, onboarding, and contract-to-hire models.

screen for one or two key attributes that predict success, then teach the rest during structured onboarding. contract-to-hire works as a working interview to assess cultural fit and job performance without commitment risk. analyze your top performers to identify what makes them succeed, then hire for those specific traits. give people visibility into promotion paths and what success looks like in their role from day one. contract to hire works well as a mutual trial period and avoids the awkward process of letting someone go. if you ask candidates to do unpaid take-home tests, consider paying them for their time and effort.
