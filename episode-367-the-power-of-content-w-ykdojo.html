about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with yk: build your career through content by starting small, focusing on linkedin, and sharing what you do instead of just your thoughts.

start with direct messages and one-on-one communication before creating content at scale. linkedin favors active profiles; a solid profile picture and filled-out profile matter for how people perceive you. even one post can reach thousands of people; content creation is like giving a talk to hundreds at once. practice communication skills because they take work and directly affect your online reputation and career. tag people you meet at events in your posts to get engagement even with zero followers.
