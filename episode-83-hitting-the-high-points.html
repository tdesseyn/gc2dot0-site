hitting the high points

sep 27, 2021 · 14 min · hosted by taylor desseyn

3 takeaways

  • > start your resume summary broad then get specific, naming your top years of experience and main tech skills.
  • > write your resume in project format under each job, naming the project and listing responsibilities as bullet points.
  • > keep summaries to about five or six bullet points covering your most relevant tech.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to write a resume that gets you interviews, including structure, summary section, and project-based experience.

structure experience section by project, describing what the application does and your key responsibilities. tailor your resume for the job you want, not generic; highlight the skills and projects that match the role. write your resume for hiring managers and recruiters who may not know your industry; be specific and quantitative. consider having multiple versions of your resume focused on different specializations rather than one generic version. if changing industries, rewrite past jobs in plain terms with numbers so someone outside your field understands your impact. build one to three targeted resumes for the specific roles you want instead of one generic version.
