about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with stephen white: how hiring managers can build culture, retain talent, and advice for aspiring leaders in tech.

create shared value systems and live them out daily to attract mission-driven engineers over mercenaries. meet remote teams where they are by understanding individual communication styles and needs. celebrate small failures and experiments to build psychological safety and encourage innovation. as a leader, prioritize your team's success over your own career growth and advancement. job seekers should ask candidates behavioral questions about empowerment, failure, and outcomes vs outputs. as a new manager put your organization and team ahead of your own career ambitions and be patient about moving up. as a candidate turn interview questions back on the employer and ask how they live out their values day to day.

In today's episode, I'm joined by Stephen White, AVP, Software Engineering at Healthtrust in Nashville. We dive into how to build culture and brand, advice for up-and-coming hiring managers, and questions you should be asking companies when you interview!
