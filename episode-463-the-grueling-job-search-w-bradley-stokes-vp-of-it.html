about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with bradley stokes: a vp of it navigated a grueling seven-month job search by ditching mass applications and instead targeting companies directly with tailored documents matching requirements to experience.

applying to hundreds of jobs on linkedin produces nothing; go directly to company career pages instead. network with intent by reaching out to relevant people at target companies with personalized, specific messages. take coffee meetings and lunches with people who know people at companies you want to join. always send thoughtful thank you notes after interviews even if rejected to maintain relationships in small communities. stay active and transparent about your job search on linkedin so your network can support and advocate for you. always send a genuine thank you or close out note even after rejection since the industry community is small.
