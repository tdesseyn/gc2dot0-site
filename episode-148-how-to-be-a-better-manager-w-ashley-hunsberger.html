about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with ashley hunsberger: learn how to recruit, care for yourself, and conduct reviews as a manager through practical strategies that prioritize people first.

sell your team and mission to candidates by being clear about goals, values, and what makes your team unique. remove interview anxiety by sharing questions and themes in advance so candidates can prepare authentically. weekly one-on-ones with clear goal-setting may be enough without quarterly reviews if your company doesn't tie them to pay. as a job seeker, ask tough questions to understand culture and try to talk yourself out of offers to be intentional. if you give regular one on one feedback your quarterly or annual review should just summarize what you already told them. ask so many questions in interviews that you could talk yourself out of the offer to really understand the opportunity.
