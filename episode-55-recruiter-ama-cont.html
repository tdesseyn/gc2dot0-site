3 takeaways

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: why recruiters keep outreach vague, demand resumes immediately, and ask where you're interviewing.

use the 10 questions form to screen recruiters and ask for manager intake notes instead of generic job descriptions. resumes get requested immediately because they need your data in their system, not because they read them carefully. recruiters ask where you're interviewing to gather market intel and alert their sales team about potential business opportunities. have natural conversations about fit instead of rigid questionnaires; it takes seven minutes to assess if someone is a good match. if a recruiter is just reading prefixed questions off a script instead of having a conversation, that is a red flag. check in with your usual recruiter about local openings even if they have not called you, they may already be working on it.
