about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with chris nowicki: how chris transitioned from 18 years in defense to landing an engineering lead role by going to bootcamp, building in public, attending conferences, and networking intentionally.

bootcamp teaches technical skills but landing a job requires networking, community involvement, and visible projects beyond the course. focus deeply on one tech stack rather than learning many stacks superficially when starting out. attend conferences and meetups, volunteer in communities, and ask creators if you can help on real projects without expecting pay. build in public by writing about what you learn, not to become an influencer but to retain knowledge and show your learning process. reach out directly to people you admire on social media or at events; most creators are accessible and willing to talk if you ask. offer to help someone on a real project for free to build experience that goes beyond side projects.

I am joined by my dear friend Chris Nowicki to talk about how he landed his first job in tech!
