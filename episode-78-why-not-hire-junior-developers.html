why not hire junior developers?

sep 15, 2021 · 23 min · hosted by taylor desseyn

3 takeaways

  • > companies default to hiring senior devs because clients ask for senior talent, not because juniors cant do the job.
  • > if you dont have time to train new hires you are by definition understaffed.
  • > juniors who get trained and given a chance tend to stay longer than senior devs who get poached constantly.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: why companies avoid hiring junior developers and how investing in their growth creates loyalty.

junior devs stay longer at companies that train them compared to senior developers who often leave within 18 months. hire for talent and how someone fits your organization long-term, not just for a specific role's requirements. senior developers need good onboarding too; poor first-week experience causes them to leave despite high salaries. companies stuck in a cycle: they won't invest in juniors because seniors leave, but seniors leave because companies don't invest. always hire one person ahead of your current need instead of waiting until youre in a crunch.
