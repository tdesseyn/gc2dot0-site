about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with taylor desseyn: build your career through community involvement and consistent online content sharing to become discoverable to hiring managers.

start small with community by inviting two friends to do something you enjoy together, not just tech topics. follow up with people you meet at conferences within a week as the real value happens after the event ends. engage regularly on linkedin through likes and comments as it builds relationships similar to in-person networking. challenge yourself to join or start a monthly community activity within the next 30 days to stay connected to others. after a conference block time to follow up with everyone you met since the follow up matters more than the event itself. reach out to old contacts on linkedin even after many years since one message can lead directly to your next job.

switching things up a little this week with a recap of a recent talk. putting yourself out on the internet is more important now than ever when it comes to growing your career. this talk sets the framework on how to start building community and content to become discoverable in the sea of job seekers.
