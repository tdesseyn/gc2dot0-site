about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: live panel discussion on why attending tech conferences and community events matters for career growth, networking, and building authentic relationships.

in-person connections create authenticity and vulnerability that's harder to replicate online or through video calls. multiple touchpoints across channels (conferences, content, social media) build brand loyalty and trust over time. companies that support employee conference attendance see better retention and can discover talent through their employees' networks. recruiters should prioritize genuine conversations and long-term relationships over immediate transactions with developers. being present at conferences gives you a support group and makes future events less intimidating. building trust with developers takes multiple touchpoints like twitter, conferences, and content before they remember or trust you. recruiters should introduce themselves as people first and skip generic pitches that ignore someone's actual background or skills.
