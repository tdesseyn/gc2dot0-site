about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with stephon parker: engineering manager stephon parker shares how to build balanced teams with juniors and seniors, and why most it leadership struggles to stay connected to their people and the work.

hire juniors only if you have mentorship structure and senior folks ready to support their growth. ship work often and iterate rather than waiting for perfection, building trust through consistent delivery. performance feedback should never make firing or promotions a surprise; communicate constantly and clearly. companies prioritize short-term money over long-term investment in people, making junior hiring harder industry-wide. a good performance review or termination should never surprise an employee if the manager has been consistently honest about expectations.
