about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with Tom Redman: how to stand out in job search by researching companies deeply, building projects, and making direct contact with decision makers.

research company founders, their background, and thesis work to find genuine connection points before applying. build a small project or demo related to the company's work to make direct contact with technical leaders. quality over quantity: tailor your application to show specific excitement about the company rather than blasting generic resumes. identify the right person at the company and craft a message that will resonate with their values and goals. reach out to people you admire in your target role and ask for help; many successful people are willing to mentor. most people are self limiting in their job search, pushing past what you think is possible often reveals you can go much further.
