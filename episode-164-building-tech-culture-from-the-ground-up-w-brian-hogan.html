about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with brian hogan: how to build inclusive tech teams, develop junior talent, and create psychological safety as a leader.

every engineering team should hire at least one junior developer per year to create industry-wide opportunities. culture exists at team level within large companies; use your network to learn the real culture before accepting a job. research company culture through back channel conversations with current or former employees rather than relying only on interview answers. focus on people skills alongside technical skills since much of software work involves communication, feedback, and pitching ideas. push for sponsorship, not just mentorship, by actively opening doors and advocating for junior developers in your network. on resumes, show accomplishments with quantifiable results instead of listing responsibilities to stand out from hundreds of applicants. good leaders build psychological safety and trust over time, modeling behaviors like taking time off so their team feels safe doing the same.

On today's episode, I'm joined by Brian Hogan, Director Of Education at Temporal Technologies. We are going to dive into how to build teams from the ground up, making tech more inclusive for newbies, and touching on freelance/consulting!
