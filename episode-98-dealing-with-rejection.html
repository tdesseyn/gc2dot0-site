dealing with rejection

jan 5, 2022 · 17 min · hosted by taylor desseyn

3 takeaways

  • > if you haven't heard back from a recruiter, reach out and simply ask for a yes or no either way.
  • > recruiters often sit on bad news because they struggle to deliver it, so following up helps both sides.
  • > rejection usually reflects fit or timing, not your worth, so try not to take it personally.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to handle job search rejection, stay in contact with recruiters, and keep interviewing despite setbacks.

rejection is going to happen in your job search and is not personal or directed at you. reach out to recruiters asking for feedback either way so they actually deliver the bad news they are holding. keep taking interviews even when discouraged because timing and fit matter more than you think. rejection might mean the company had an internal candidate or it is not your fault at all. do more interviews so rejection hurts less and becomes a normal part of the process. keep taking interviews even when you're not actively looking so rejection stops feeling so heavy. building real relationships instead of mass applying can cut down how often you face rejection.
