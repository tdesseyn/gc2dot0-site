about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with conner bush: how to land big tech jobs through strategic dming, networking, and preparation instead of formal applications.

create a weighted scoring system for companies based on prestige, pay, purpose and culture to focus your outreach. respond quickly to dms, prepare 10 questions beforehand, and treat calls like interviews by being curious and listening well. once hired, schedule one-on-ones with your team and cross-functional partners in week one to build context and connections. learn new tech stacks on the job even without prior experience, ask questions without shame, and be intentional but authentic. keep outreach short specific and time boxed like asking for eight minutes and tie it to something you both have in common. when starting a new remote role spend the first few weeks scheduling one on ones across teams before diving into the work itself.
