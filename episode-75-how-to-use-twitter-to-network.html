how to use twitter to network

sep 1, 2021 · 14 min · hosted by taylor desseyn

3 takeaways

  • > set a clear banner photo and profile picture on twitter so people instantly know who you are.
  • > write a short bio that tells visitors exactly what you post about and why they should follow.
  • > add an about me style intro video to your profile so people connect with you before they follow.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: build a strong twitter profile and engage authentically to network in tech communities during your job search.

write a specific bio that tells people what you talk about and include a link to your website. follow curated lists and hashtags like dev community and 100 days of code to find people to engage with. network by liking and commenting on posts rather than only creating content yourself. audit your time and prioritize linkedin first, but use twitter as a secondary networking tool. use twitter lists and the search feature to quickly find job posts and people worth following. engage by liking and commenting on posts since that kind of networking has led to real dm conversations.
