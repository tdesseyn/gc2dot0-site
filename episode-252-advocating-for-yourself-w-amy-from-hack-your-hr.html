about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with amy: learn how to advocate for yourself at work by having candid conversations about pay and responsibilities without letting emotions derail the discussion.

use reasonable versus realistic framing to acknowledge what's fair even if the outcome isn't possible right now. separate emotions from facts by focusing on impact and what you'd cost the company if you left instead of market data. lead with candor by talking to your boss like a friend, being respectful but cutting through professional language and bs. understand that pay differences often reflect experience with organizational headwinds, not personal judgment of your abilities. validate your own feelings first since managers can't always advocate for you even if they care about your growth. when recruiting or interviewing someone, ask how they are doing in their job search before jumping into the role details.

I am joined by Amy @HackYourHR on advocating for your career and making sure your feelings are validated.
