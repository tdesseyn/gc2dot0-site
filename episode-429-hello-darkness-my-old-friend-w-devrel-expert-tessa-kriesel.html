on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with tessa kriesel: how isolation affected your mental health and why getting back into community, setting boundaries, and taking back control of your day matters.

isolation during solo business building led to depression; reconnecting with developers through streaming and events changed everything. remove phones, set meeting limits, time block ruthlessly, and protect time for movement or exercise or you are too busy. developers aren't antisocial; they want authentic peer connection, not pitches; in-person events build real community that virtual cannot replicate. companies should design work around employee needs like childcare and schedule flexibility, not force office return just to regain power. a scheduling tool can fill your calendar with meetings fast, so set limits on when people can book you. in person events build trust faster than virtual ones because people put themselves out there more face to face.
