about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with byron sommardahl: how pair programming with coding katas and test-driven development reveals developer skill better than algorithmic tests.

emotional intelligence matters as much as technical skills; developers need soft skills to collaborate and solve right problems. algorithmic tests work for recent grads but fail for experienced developers most jobs don't need advanced algorithms. scaling interviews requires team effort; use scheduling tools and rotate interviewers to handle volume without cutting corners. job seekers should focus on emotional intelligence skills alongside technical abilities before meeting hiring managers. developers need emotional intelligence since poor communication can lead to solving the wrong problem for a client. if being yourself hurts you in interviews, work on your emotional intelligence instead of just your technical skills.
