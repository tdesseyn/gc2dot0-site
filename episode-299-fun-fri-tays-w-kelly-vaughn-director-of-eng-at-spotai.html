about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with kelly vaughn: kelly vaughn shares hiring insights, time management strategies, and the importance of saying no to prioritize what matters most.

saying yes to something means saying no to something else, so get comfortable declining requests strategically. cap your daily meetings to protect deep work time, ideally scheduling them when you're least productive. identify your one must-do task daily, a few nice-to-haves, and what won't get done this week. ask for meeting context upfront so you can prepare mentally and assess if attendance is necessary. find a mentor early in your career; your mentor will change as you progress through different roles. before taking a meeting ask what it is about so you can mentally prepare instead of walking in blind. decide at the start of the week what you are simply not going to get to, since not everything can fit.
