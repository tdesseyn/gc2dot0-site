about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with kasey wahl: english teacher turned junior developer shares his covid-era journey through bootcamp, networking, and landing his first tech job through intentional content creation.

research bootcamps thoroughly by interviewing graduates on linkedin and asking about contracts and obligations. record lectures and spend extra hours reviewing material during bootcamp to build confidence in difficult concepts. network intentionally during bootcamp, not after, by connecting with successful graduates and asking specific questions. create content about what you're learning and send it to hiring managers to demonstrate depth of understanding. ask clarifying questions about contract to hire roles to ensure company has budgetary approval and history of converting. use mornings for job search and networking when your mind is freshest, save coding and content creation for afternoons. send recruiters and hiring managers content you created on topics discussed in interviews to show you followed up and learned.
