job search timing w/ me

may 16, 2022 · 19 min · hosted by taylor desseyn

3 takeaways

  • > avoid walking around with an inflated title like vp of engineering or cto if you only have a few years of experience, it can spook hiring managers.
  • > if your experience justifies a senior title, explain and sell that context clearly in your resume and to your recruiter.
  • > ask recruiters or hiring managers directly if a position is budgetarily approved before investing time in the interview process.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: job search rejection often comes down to timing and organizational factors, not your skills.

inflated job titles on junior resumes hurt your chances; hiring managers get spooked by titles that don't match experience level. always ask recruiters if the position is budgetarily approved and when; old approvals signal potential delays or cancellations. ask how many candidates are in the interview process to understand competition and get feedback on common rejection themes. hiring managers should give specific, quantifiable feedback on skill gaps rather than vague culture fit reasons. many job search rejections stem from company politics, budget freezes, and hiring process delays, not candidate ability. if you get rejected, ask for specific quantifiable feedback instead of vague comments like culture fit so you know what to improve.

Just me! Chatting about all the other factors that no one talks about which is probably why you're not landing a job.
