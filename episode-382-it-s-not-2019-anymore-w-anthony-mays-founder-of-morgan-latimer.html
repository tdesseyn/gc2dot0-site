about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with anthony mays: tech job market has changed since 2019 and professionals need stronger resumes, networks, and communication skills to stand out.

develop genuine relationships through conferences, meetups, and audio spaces rather than just social media messaging. learn to communicate what you're working on publicly through posts and articles to get noticed by opportunities. focus on problem-solving process and thinking out loud during interviews, not just knowing syntax. if a hiring manager views your profile after you apply, ask specific questions rather than generic ones, and go through your recruiter if you already have one. treat live streams, audio rooms and small communities as the new meetups and pick the format that fits how you like to communicate.
