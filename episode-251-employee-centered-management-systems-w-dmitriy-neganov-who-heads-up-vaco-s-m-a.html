about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with dmitriy neganov: how employee-centered management systems drive business results through prioritizing people, purpose, direction and engagement.

stop calling people resources and start viewing them as the foundation of organizational success. ask yourself what value you add to talented people around you, not just what they add to you. quality over quantity matters more than doing everything at once; alternate focus strategically. sincere recognition and understanding employee workload prevents burnout and builds retention. align individual employee goals with company goals rather than making profit the primary stated goal. be sincere with recognition instead of treating it as a quick bonus or band aid fix.
