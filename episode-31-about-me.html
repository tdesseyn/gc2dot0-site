about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: taylor desseyn shares his career journey from musician to tech recruiter and why he started guidance counselor 2.0 to help people through job transitions.

taylor moved into tech recruiting after a failed music career tour and found he enjoyed learning about software development. he built a recruiting team from two to three people to nine recruiters over four to five years without losing staff. his motivation to create content and help at scale came from his dad calling after leaving his restaurant job of 20 years. he shifted from one-on-one recruiting to creating content during covid to help job seekers beyond just his immediate network. taylor focuses on helping people in career transitions, both tech and non-tech, by sharing real stories and practical job search advice. speaking at local user groups and code camps is a low pressure way to start building a public reputation. remember that behind every job search is a real person who is scared, not just a number to move fast past.
