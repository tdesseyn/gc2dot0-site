about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with mark thompson: how to position yourself as an ai-enabled engineer and stay valuable as generative ai transforms tech work.

knowing what's right and wrong in code matters more than typing speed as ai handles routine tasks. frame yourself as ai-empowered not replaced by learning tools like gemini cli to enhance your work. build portfolio projects with real ai features that solve actual problems not just copy templates. the bar for junior developers has raised so learn system design and architecture alongside coding basics. find a mentor and come prepared with specific questions, a focused fifteen minute ask gets better results than searching online alone. show up to conferences and meetups and work in public since a strong network often leads to unexpected job opportunities.
