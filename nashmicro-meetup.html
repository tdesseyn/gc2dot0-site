about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: taylor desseyn shares recruiting insights at a tech meetup, covering how to work effectively with recruiters, evaluate job opportunities, and navigate the hiring process.

request direct information from hiring managers rather than generic job postings to identify recruiters with real relationships. be transparent about your salary expectations and give recruiters a range so they can match you fairly to opportunities. stay in regular contact with recruiters you trust and ask hard questions about company culture from actual employees. meet with recruiters in person when possible to build relationships that help them place you successfully. be upfront and honest with recruiters about your interest level since lying makes it harder for them to place you well.

I had the pleasure of speaking at a local tech meetup in town. We shared some great stories about working with recruiters and many other great tips!
