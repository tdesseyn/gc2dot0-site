about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with leon noel: how to stand out in your job search through community, networking, and sweating the small details that recruiters notice.

join a community of people actively job searching to stay positive and learn what actually works right now. fix the small stuff on your resume and linkedin first: title clarity, company context, and making it scannable in six seconds. network in person and online consistently by showing up to meetups weekly and engaging with people's content regularly. approach people in dms by finding common interests or hobbies instead of leading with job talk. set daily controllable goals like engaging with ten people on twitter rather than obsessing over landing the job. join a community so you can see peers getting interviews and jobs instead of sitting in a negativity bubble. build real relationships through small consistent actions like commenting online or showing up to the same meetup repeatedly.
