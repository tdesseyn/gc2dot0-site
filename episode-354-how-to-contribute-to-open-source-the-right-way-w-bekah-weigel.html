  • > ask questions in the open issue or pr instead of dming maintainers directly, since transparency is the norm in open source.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with bekah weigel: learn how to contribute to open source effectively and use it as a job search tool without burning out project maintainers.

communicate transparently in issues and pull requests, never via direct messages to maintainers. open source contributions build your network and showcase skills publicly while helping you grow technically. identify skill gaps and find open source issues that let you practice those specific areas. leadership skills like good communication and mentoring matter more than coding ability when job searching. pick open source issues that target skills missing from your resume, like documentation or a specific framework, to grow and get noticed. leadership, clear communication, and mentoring others make people stand out and get recommended faster when job searching.
