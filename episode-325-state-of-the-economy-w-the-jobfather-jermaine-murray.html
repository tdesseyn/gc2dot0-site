about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with jermaine murray: career coach and recruiter jermaine murray shares his journey from radio to tech, discussing flexibility, pivoting careers, and finding opportunities in the current job market.

take lateral moves or different roles to build skills needed for your ultimate career goal. disruption and discomfort often signal growth; layoffs can be opportunities to pursue what you actually want. look for tech jobs everywhere: established companies, startups, transit systems, airlines, grocery stores all need engineers. network, stay flexible, and think outside traditional tech companies when searching for roles. look beyond typical tech companies and consider startups and older established companies that still rely heavily on tech. stay flexible on relocation and contracting, and keep networking and building community to create opportunities.
