about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with seth alexander: how remote work is changing candidate recruitment and what engineers should know about their market value.

engineers are getting contacted five to ten times a week from recruiters offering significant salary increases. remote work lets companies pay based on value, not location, even if cost of living differs. know your minimum acceptable raise before talking to recruiters to avoid wasting time on small offers. hiring managers should be transparent about budgets and business realities to keep talent engaged. compensation is more than salary—healthcare, benefits, and culture matter when evaluating offers. location based pay is shifting as companies pay for the value an engineer provides, not where they live. share market outreach data with your leadership so the company stays aware of retention risk and can respond with transparency.
