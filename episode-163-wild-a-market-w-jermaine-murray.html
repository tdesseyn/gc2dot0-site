about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with jermaine murray: how to break into tech recruiting, increase black representation in tech, and why job seekers should own their accomplishments.

resumes should sell value like a pitch, not list 18 months of experience on 6 pages. ats rejection myth: most rejections are from people, not automated systems filtering you out. hiring for diversity means recruiting at junior and intermediate levels with intention, not just senior roles. recruiters need empathy and should eliminate transactional messaging to stop being seen negatively. job seekers must brag about wins with the same energy they own their mistakes. use boolean string searches with your core skills on job sites to find relevant postings even without knowing the exact job title. when rejecting candidates after multiple interviews, offer a short feedback call instead of just a rejection email.

I am joined by Jermaine Murray, DEI Champion and The Jobfather, who is on a mission to help 500 Black people get new Jobs in Tech. We dive into how to increase black representation in tech, talk about this crazy market, and the success Jermaine has had in his career!
