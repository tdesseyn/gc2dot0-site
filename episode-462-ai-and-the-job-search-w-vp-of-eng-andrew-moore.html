about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with Andrew Moore: vp of engineering andrew moore discusses ai's actual impact on junior developers, debunking hype while offering practical advice for standing out in tech careers.

ai isn't forcing junior devs out of jobs; economic conditions and layoffs get wrongly blamed on ai. learn enterprise fundamentals like core java or .net to stand out against 900 applicants for one role. use ai as a sounding board and tool, not a crutch; understand why you're writing code, not just how. make your resume, cover letter, and online presence cohesive and human; use ai only as a starting point. different industries adopt ai differently; enterprise and regulated sectors move slower than startups. in fast moving startups speed and revenue matter more so being hands on with ai tools can help you move quicker. make your resume and cover letter sound like you since generic ai written content is obvious and frustrating to read.
