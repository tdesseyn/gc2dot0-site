about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to search for jobs on linkedin, message hiring managers effectively, and use networking to find opportunities.

put relevant job titles and keywords in your linkedin headline so recruiters can find you quickly in search results. send personalized connection requests with notes to hiring managers and decision makers, keeping messages short and specific. run job searches daily filtering by past 24 hours to see newest openings before other applicants apply. connect with people at companies you want to work for and engage with their posts to build visibility and relationships. pay for linkedin premium to send more personalized messages and have better access to decision makers. personalize every connection request with a short human note naming the role and your top matching skills instead of a generic pitch. like and comment on posts consistently so your name becomes recognizable to people who might later hire or refer you.
