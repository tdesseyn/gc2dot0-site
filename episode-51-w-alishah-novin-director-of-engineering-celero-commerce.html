about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with alishah novin: how to know when it's time to leave your job and what keeps people engaged at companies they choose to stay at.

empowerment to make decisions and room to fail builds trust and loyalty with teams. ask yourself regularly what you don't know to avoid getting comfortable and missing industry changes. have weekly or biweekly one-on-ones with your manager to discuss growth opportunities before looking elsewhere. fear of change is healthy and signals growth opportunity, but approach job moves with confidence not desperation. join local tech communities to understand where you stand, get challenged, and find mentorship. tie your growth ask to a realistic timeline like a year or two instead of expecting a big jump in six months.
