about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with braydon coyer: how to break into web dev through 800+ job applications, networking, and persistence over a year-long search.

submit applications but lean harder into networking; every opportunity after the first came through relationships. attend conferences and meetups ready to engage; keep conversations short and genuine, focus on commonality. senior devs solve problems anywhere and know who to ask; become polyglot but go deep in one area. junior devs stand out by networking and showing initiative, not just doing personal projects. mentorship works best one-on-one; mentor others too regardless of experience level. seniors should be adaptable across frameworks and able to find solutions anywhere, not tied to one stack. build networking as a skill by practicing in low stakes settings like coffee shops before conferences.
