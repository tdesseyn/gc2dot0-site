about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to build a twitter profile and use it for networking in tech, including profile setup, finding communities, and engaging effectively.

use your banner photo and profile picture to show who you are, not just text. join lists and communities to find your people rather than tweeting into the void. engage by liking and commenting on others' posts, not just tweeting yourself. twitter requires frequent activity to succeed, unlike linkedin which rewards less frequent posting. engage by liking and commenting even if you never tweet, since twitter rewards consistent interaction. pin a tweet that shows your mission or who you are to invite follows right away.
