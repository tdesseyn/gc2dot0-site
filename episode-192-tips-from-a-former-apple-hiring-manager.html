about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with thiago ghisi: how to ace your interview as a job seeker and what hiring managers look for when building their teams.

write your own recommendation letter and send it with your referral so the person has specific language to use. prepare behavior interview stories with data and context using the star method so you know them by heart. do mock interviews in a high-pressure environment with peers or mentors to find your gaps before the real thing. as a hiring manager, make interviews reflect actual day-to-day work and be honest about team challenges and vision. prepare a bank of star method stories ahead of time so you are not caught off guard in behavior interviews. practice system design interviews with time boxing so you do not get stuck on one section like data design and run out of time.

I am joined by Thiago Ghisi, Director Of Engineering at Nubank. Thiago was recently on the job search after his time at Apple so we are going to talk about his story as a job seeker and then thoughts as a hiring manager!
