about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with ad slaton: long-term growth at one company beats constant job hopping; stay for people, meaningful work, and work-life balance.

having frank conversations with your manager about career frustrations can lead to internal moves instead of leaving. document your work so others in different timezones can learn from you without needing live meetings. focus on what you can control; let go of things outside your power to avoid burnout. show your work on github and build side projects to prove your skills, not just talk about them. prepare hard conversations by writing down key points and leading with empathy for the other person. treat documentation as a core skill, writing things down helps new hires and global remote teams ramp up faster. keep building on github and showing real work, not just talking about skills, since hiring managers actually look at it.
