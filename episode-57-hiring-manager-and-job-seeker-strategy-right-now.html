hiring manager and job seeker strategy right now

3 takeaways

  • > job seekers should keep one or two trusted local recruiters plus three or four recruiters in other hot tech cities.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how job seekers and hiring managers should adapt their recruiting strategies in a remote-first market.

finding good recruiters takes time and effort through many short calls to identify who you trust and work well with. recruiting firms can now serve clients nationwide while providing the same candidate screening and vetting as local teams. hiring managers can now work with recruiting firms anywhere in the country instead of only locally based options. recruiting timeline for senior talent is two to four weeks so hiring managers should engage recruitment help early. hiring managers no longer need to use a recruiting firm in their own city since remote recruiting now works nationally. expect candidate lead time of two to four weeks right now, especially for senior technical talent, so start the search early.
