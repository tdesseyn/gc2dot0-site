about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with amanda hughes: how to succeed in tech recruiting: build deep relationships, understand your market, and move fast in a candidate-driven job market.

most recruiters quit in first 18 months because they hear no more than yes and underestimate the grind required. go deep with relationships instead of wide with volume; trust and genuine problem-solving beat transactional recruiting. today's job market is candidate-driven and national; companies must move quickly, improve interview quality, and compete on more than salary. candidates now interview employers as much as employers interview them; poor process or disrespect can lose offers. negotiate equity vesting cycles and salary bands; stock value depends on how long you plan to stay at the company. hiring managers should move fast and make quick decisions since the market is highly candidate driven right now. pay attention to interview process and speed since candidates are now turning down offers over slow or impersonal interviews.
