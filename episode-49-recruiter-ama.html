about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: get answers to common recruiter questions about job search strategy, dm tactics, resume tips, and contract work.

ask recruiters specific questions upfront using a structured guide before committing to calls. craft dms with flattery, ask for specific time, and pose a real question to get responses. most recruiter outreach goes nowhere so keep talking to multiple recruiters and check in regularly. junior devs stand out with clean resumes using consistent formatting and documented side projects. ask contract to hire recruiters about conversion salary, pto, benefits, and whether it's a true conversion role. when a recruiter dms you, ask them to answer at least half of a short list of screening questions before engaging further. only invest time in recruiters who have been at their company and in the industry for at least two years. if you dont want more job pitches from a recruiter just stop responding instead of saying no thanks.
