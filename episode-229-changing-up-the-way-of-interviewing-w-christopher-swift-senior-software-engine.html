about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with christopher swift: how to interview better by asking about real experience, being authentic, and what junior developers need to grow in remote work.

look for energy, honesty, and willingness to admit what you don't know during interviews. intentionally create space where admitting mistakes and asking for help feels safe on your team. junior developers must be intentional about learning and reaching out for mentorship in remote settings. your network and peer connections matter more than recruiters for your next job opportunity. value honesty and willingness to admit you dont know something over trying to fake an answer. build real connections with peers since those relationships often lead to future job opportunities more than recruiters do.

I am joined by Christopher Swift, Senior Software Engineer at Live Oak Bank in Raleigh. We are diving into how he interviews and evaluates talent and getting started as a junior developer. Recruiter Against Recruiters - Click the Link Below For Job Postings, Newsletters & more!
