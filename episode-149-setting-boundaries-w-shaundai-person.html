about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with shaundai person: learn how to set boundaries at work, prioritize ruthlessly, and build your network to advance your tech career without burning out.

identify your non-negotiables and cut everything else to focus on what matters most to you. use a framework to evaluate requests: does it spark joy, align with your goals, and benefit your community?. build your network on twitter and linkedin through authentic connections, not formal mentorship asks. protect your time by setting clear work hours and actually stopping work when the day ends. outline your top goals every six months and only commit to things that move you toward them. set a hard stop time for work each day and actually shut the computer down to protect your personal time. networking through speaking, twitter and genuine conversations can lead directly to job opportunities without ever cold pitching for a role.
