about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: navigate conferences strategically by picking speakers and sessions to attend, using social media to connect, and pacing yourself to avoid burnout.

connect with people on social media after meeting them, not just business cards, to stay in touch. use the hallway track by finding one or two people you know, then get introduced to others through them. it's okay to take breaks, go to your hotel room, and rest during conferences to avoid burnout. small conferences with three to five hundred people create better networking opportunities than very large ones. its fine to skip sessions and rest in your hotel room if a conference starts feeling overwhelming.
