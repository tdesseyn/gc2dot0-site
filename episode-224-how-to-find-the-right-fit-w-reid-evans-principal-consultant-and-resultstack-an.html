about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with reid evans: hiring managers should focus on finding people who want to grow, ask direct questions about what makes them feel valued and where they want to develop, and involve existing team members in the hiring process.

include your existing team in hiring decisions to ensure good fit and that new hires won't stump others' growth. hire for the person they could become in 1-2 years, not just the immediate job need, as long as you give them safe opportunities to learn. be authentic in interviews and conversations; people can tell when you're faking it, and retention matters more than constantly hiring replacements. as teams scale past a small size, regular one-on-ones become essential for keeping people around.
