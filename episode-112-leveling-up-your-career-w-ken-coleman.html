about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with ken coleman: discover your professional purpose by combining your talent, passion, and mission to find work you love while earning well.

don't chase jobs for money alone; the salary boost feels good for six to eight weeks then wears off. soft skills like being likeable, coachable, reliable, adaptable, and honorable separate you from competition in tech. when negotiating salary, come grateful and honest about your research, then stop talking and let them respond. take entry level jobs to get on the industry ladder; experience and trajectory matter more than starting salary. to retain people, check in on them personally and ask how you can help them win professionally.
