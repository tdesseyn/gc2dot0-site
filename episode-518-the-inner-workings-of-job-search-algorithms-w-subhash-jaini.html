about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with subhash jaini: how job search algorithms actually work and what candidates need to know to optimize resumes for automated screening systems.

three separate automations control job search: finding jobs, applying, and scoring resumes via matching algorithms. resume keywords must match job description exactly and appear in work experience sections, not just skills, with proper timeframe context. different ats systems like workday, greenhouse, and lever score resumes differently so research which system a company uses. you can view source code of job postings to sometimes see scoring mechanisms and understand what specific keywords get weighted. test how an ats parses your resume by applying to a random job on that same system before applying to ones you actually want. track every application you submit so you can spot patterns like instant rejections tied to specific ats systems.
