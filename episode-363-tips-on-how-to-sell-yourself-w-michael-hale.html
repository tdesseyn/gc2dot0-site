about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with michael hale: how to authentically sell yourself in interviews and networking by being genuine, finding common ground, and reading the room.

figure out who you actually are and what you're good at, then show up as that person consistently. develop a personal style that makes you memorable and gives people something to connect with. network and build relationships before you need them, especially when facing a layoff. get into tech through adjacent roles like sales or customer success if direct coding roles aren't opening. be interested in helping others, not just selling yourself, and adapt your approach based on who you're talking to. find common ground with people by asking about something small they have, like an item or interest, to disarm them. after a layoff, take a moment to ground yourself, then reach out to your existing network before searching for new contacts.
