about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with josh comeau: how to build an audience and transition to independent work through blogging, teaching, and creating courses without needing to follow conventional networking advice.

start documenting problems you solve on a blog to build reputation and help others, not just to become famous. test your course content early with a small group, then expand gradually through beta launches and collect feedback systematically. focus on secondary skills like css that can be improved quickly rather than chasing what employers say they want most. play to your strengths when building an audience. if you're introverted, build in public online instead of forcing yourself to networking events. when reviewing feedback look for patterns across many people rather than reacting to one comment.
