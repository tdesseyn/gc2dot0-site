about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to identify and work with recruiters when job hunting, starting with what qualities to look for in a recruiter you can trust.

find a recruiter who has stayed at the same company for over two years, not job hopped frequently. a recruiter with three to five years at one company and one to two years in your city is worth considering. getting posted on job boards is just the first step; working with the right recruiter is the next move. this is the start of a multi-part series on how to effectively partner with recruiters during your job search. favor recruiters who have stayed at the same company for an extended time, ideally two or more years.

You get let go, write your resume, get your resume uploaded to the job boards, now what? In this episode i start breaking down how to work with a recruiter and what to look for when working with one.
