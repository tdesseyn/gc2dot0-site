on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with nate walker, joseph jewell: recruiting leaders discuss what motivates them, how they train teams, and advice for job seekers and hiring managers navigating the tech market.

shorten hiring processes to avoid losing candidates; contract-to-hire often works faster than direct hire. recruiters should act as advisors and partners, not just vendors, using their market knowledge to guide clients. building genuine relationships and caring about people sets recruiters apart in a crowded industry. job seekers changing careers should network, practice interviews, showcase relevant skills through projects or certs, and stay persistent. time blocking your calendar creates focused work time and signals availability to your team. when changing careers, network constantly, practice interviewing, and be honest about your real capabilities instead of overselling.
