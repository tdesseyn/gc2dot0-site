about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with jacob evans: how jacob landed a staff engineering job at woop by networking on x instead of using job boards that gave him almost zero responses.

job boards are far less effective than direct networking; jacob got 1 response from 150 applications versus many calls from one x post. build genuine connections by engaging authentically with people you admire, not by asking to be mentored formally. find people through hashtags, followed accounts, and conversation threads, then interact on their existing content consistently. you can have meaningful professional relationships without formal arrangements; passive mentorship through engagement works. say yes to opportunities like in-person meetings; jacob's willingness to visit the team in new york led directly to the job offer. when a post gets traction, be ready to say yes to opportunities like flying out to meet a team. job boards and easy apply can have a very low response rate, so build real connections elsewhere.

Jacob did the cold applying and typical job search tasks but he was getting no where. we're talking about how he landed his most recent job!
