about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to build trust and move faster in tech hiring through better candidate treatment and showing empathy on both sides.

hiring managers can assess company culture by how they treat you during recruitment conversations. good candidates are moving off the market in about a week so start hiring now even if you think it's early. creative welcome gifts and personalized touches matter when budget for higher pay isn't available. job seekers and hiring managers both struggle with the process so approaching with empathy makes everything better. a shorter, simpler interview process respects candidates already working long hours and juggling family life.
