back to the basics (solo show)

feb 17, 2023 · 12 min · hosted by taylor desseyn

3 takeaways

  • > treat your resume and linkedin as the building blocks of your job search and be obsessed about getting them right.
  • > keep linkedin as a short summary with just three or four bullet points per role, not your full work history.
  • > keep your resume simple, especially for creative roles, so reviewers focus on content instead of design choices.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: learn the four foundational steps to job searching: optimize your resume and linkedin, get recruiter feedback, and start networking directly with companies.

linkedin should show three to four bullet points per role with industry buzzwords; google job descriptions to find terms to include. message recruiters asking for one piece of resume or linkedin feedback; do this 10-15 times to improve for free. dm people at companies you want to work for with admiration, specific question, and clear intent; skip job board applications. include key terms and buzzwords from your industry in both resume and linkedin or you'll miss being discovered. google your target job title and pull common buzzwords from job descriptions to add to your resume and linkedin.
