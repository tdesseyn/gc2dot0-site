about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with Daniel Norton: how recruiting firms and junior developers can help growing startups move faster when leadership lacks hiring bandwidth.

strong senior engineers mentor juniors effectively, creating sustainable growth instead of burnout. bootcamp graduates have immediately applicable skills, attractive salaries, and hunger to prove themselves. contract-to-hire removes onboarding burden so you focus on getting them coding day one. junior developers backfill roles vacated by promoted mid-level engineers creating natural team scaling. consider contract to hire so the staffing partner handles onboarding paperwork and logistics for you. bring in junior developers from coding bootcamps since they arrive with practical skills, strong motivation, and let senior engineers move into bigger roles.

I am joined by my friend Daniel Norton, CTO of Neural Payments. We are going to dive into how to leverage a recruiting firm as well as junior developers!
