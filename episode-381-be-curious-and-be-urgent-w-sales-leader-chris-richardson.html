about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with chris richardson: learn how to build an authentic professional brand through service industry lessons, strategic networking, and owning your career narrative.

practice your elevator pitch on video repeatedly to get comfortable speaking about yourself authentically and naturally. be urgently curious by researching people before reaching out, finding common ground, and asking thoughtful questions. take intentional career risks in roles that challenge you and push growth rather than staying comfortable in one lane. read people and anticipate needs like you would in service work to build genuine connections in any professional setting. own your narrative and don't let others box you in; create the story of what you can do, not what you've always done. before messaging someone on linkedin, research their background and reference something specific instead of a generic ask. don't over focus on job description requirements, talk to people who have done the hiring to learn what really sets candidates apart.
