about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with dan digangi: engineering leader navigates layoff, discusses job search mental health, network strategy, content building, and interview techniques.

layoffs impact confidence and mental health. support networks, mentoring others, and self-compassion are essential during the search. job application sites have frustrating fragmented login systems. focus energy on networking, warm outreach, and content instead of mass applications. structure your search loosely. mix focused work with breaks, coding projects, mentoring to avoid burnout and maintain flexibility. craft genuine messages with specific questions when reaching out. ask about culture and roles without expecting favors to build real conversations. in interviews, identify the core problem youre hired to solve and tailor answers around it. ask strong questions like what success looks like in 30-60-90 days. when emailing people to schedule time, give two to three specific days with clear time blocks so they can pick easily.
