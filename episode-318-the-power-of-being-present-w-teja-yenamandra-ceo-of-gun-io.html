about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with teja yenamandra: how being present, building relationships, and prioritizing team energy creates better hiring and company culture at gun.io.

make mistakes in lower-stakes environments to grow; strong relationships with managers create psychological safety. in-person connection builds trust faster than virtual meetings; one drink plus ten minutes in person equals multiple hours of zoom calls. focus on team vibe and energy fit when hiring, not just skills; the right personality mix matters as much as competence. generalist engineers do well on gun.io because clients need polyglot developers who can touch multiple languages and stacks. throw events and build community to strengthen professional relationships; this drives better business outcomes than metrics alone. when hiring, skills and resume are baseline, the real differentiator is the energy a person brings to the team. in your career take risks big enough that a failure would really sting, smaller risk usually means you are underplaying it.
