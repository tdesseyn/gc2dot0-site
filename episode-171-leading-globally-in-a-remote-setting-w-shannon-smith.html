about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with shannon smith: how to lead a global remote team, optimize interview processes, and build genuine workplace community across time zones.

set quarterly in-person meetups with intentional activities and budget, mixing work, community building, and inclusive events like charity. be light on email and heavy on slack with strategic channel organization by squad, use threads and kudos channels thoughtfully. interview differently for different people: some want upfront context about the full process, others need to evaluate the company first. get to know your whole person as a leader, not just their technical output, to build belonging and understand how to support them. own your career path, take thoughtful risks early, find community around your goals, and view failures as learning opportunities not endpoints. set up a buddy program for new hires so they have a safe person to ask basic questions without feeling judged. on your resume or linkedin, dont just list technical skills, explain what makes you uniquely valuable compared to other candidates.
