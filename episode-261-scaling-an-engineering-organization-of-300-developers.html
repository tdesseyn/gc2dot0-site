about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with ryan nel and joel segerlind: how volvo cars hired 300+ engineers in a year by streamlining interviews, empowering recruiters, and building flexible hiring processes.

implement rotating rosters with assigned assessment reviewers and 48-hour turnaround times to prevent hiring bottlenecks and burnout. separate technical and culture interviews, always use two people per interview, and give detailed feedback to hiring managers without numeric scores. match candidates to roles after interviews rather than before, then let hiring managers make final placements based on full context. maintain junior hiring as a cultural priority even during rapid scaling, knowing teams must adapt their mentorship and code review practices. have recruiters lead candidate-to-team matching once they know the tech space well enough to spot the right fit. set expectations early that candidates may not know their exact team until later in a fast-growing org.
