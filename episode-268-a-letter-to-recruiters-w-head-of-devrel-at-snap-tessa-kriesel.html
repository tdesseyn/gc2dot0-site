about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: recruiters need to attend tech conferences in their hometowns and build long-term relationships instead of only focusing on filling immediate openings.

attend conferences in your city to meet engineers and hiring managers face to face. get contacts into your social ecosystem immediately using linkedin qr codes or email. building relationships online takes time but pays off when you meet people in person. networking conversations can be simple: ask what they do, exchange contact info, stay in touch. volunteer at local tech conferences if your company won't pay for attendance. keep networking simple, ask what someone does, get connected, and move on without overthinking it. building relationships online before meeting in person makes the in person meeting feel instant and natural.

I am joined by Tessa Kriesel who is Head of Platform DevRel at Snapchat and we are diving into how recruiters need to handle the engineering ecosystem of tech.
