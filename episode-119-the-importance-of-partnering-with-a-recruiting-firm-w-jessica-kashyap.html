about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with jessica kashyap: how exclusive recruiting relationships and consistent messaging help companies hire faster and better in a tight talent market.

working with one recruiter exclusively creates accountability and ensures consistent messaging to candidates. hiring managers should prioritize speed in decision-making once a qualified candidate is presented or risk losing them. recruiters who understand your industry can serve as advocates for both job seekers and employers navigating career moves. large hiring initiatives need end-to-end support from job descriptions through onboarding to manage the full scope. finding a recruiter willing to walk away from bad fits shows awareness and commitment to long-term success stories. speed matters, some hiring cycles moved from friday interview to offer within about five business days. a good recruiter will tell you honestly when they are not the right fit instead of forcing a square peg into a round hole.
