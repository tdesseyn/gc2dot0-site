about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with joe previte: how to navigate getting laid off twice in one year, from mindset to job search strategy to public documentation.

reach out to your closest friends and former colleagues to see if their teams are hiring. create a sustainable daily cadence for job searching; eight hours straight burns you out fast. document your journey publicly to open doors, but be strategic about what you share. stream technical interviews if permitted; it shows confidence and can make you stand out. think about non-negotiable parameters like benefits, stability, and family leave before picking your next company size. be careful about posting too much detail on failures or exact interview stages since companies can use that against you.
