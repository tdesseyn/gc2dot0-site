  • > try to interview directly with the person you would report to, not just their team members.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to prepare for interviews by researching companies, asking questions, writing down accomplishments, and being yourself.

ask questions about why the position is open and meet your direct manager during interviews. write down your accomplishments and projects before interviews so you can speak to them clearly. interview with people you'll actually work with to avoid taking a job that's a bad fit. be yourself in interviews instead of trying to be someone else the employer might not value. write down your accomplishments and relevant project examples ahead of time so you do not blank under pressure. bring a notepad to jot down thoughts during the interview instead of interrupting the interviewer.
