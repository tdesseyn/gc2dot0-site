about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with rizel scarlett: learn what devrel is, how to break into the field, and why building your personal brand matters for your career.

devrel includes developer advocates, community managers, and technical writers all focused on bridging companies and developer communities. you don't need a big portfolio to start in devrel; junior roles exist and learning publicly can open doors. document your learning journey even on basics like html or react to help others and show hiring managers you understand your work. being yourself and sharing what you learn in real time builds community investment in your journey more than appearing as an expert. your online reputation is your brand; consistent posting about what you're learning helps people know and trust you. building in public, even posting basic learning notes like how you made a button in html, helps hiring managers see you understand the process. think of brand as your online reputation, built by consistently sharing what you are learning rather than waiting until you feel like an expert.
