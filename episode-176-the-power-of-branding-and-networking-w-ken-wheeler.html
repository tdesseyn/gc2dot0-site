about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with ken wheeler: how open source contributions, authentic personal branding, and networking shaped one engineer's career in tech.

open source work creates visibility and networking opportunities that lead to job offers with endorsements. being genuinely yourself online stands out in tech culture and builds trust with potential connections. networking at conferences matters more than talks; leave circles open, engage speakers, ask real questions. separate consumption from creation; focus on making things rather than passively consuming content. reading the room and strategic restraint early in your career can open more doors over time. at conferences leave your group circle open so others can join, and approach speakers after their talks since they want to connect too. watch your ratio of consumption versus creation and try to be a net creator since that has the bigger professional payoff.
