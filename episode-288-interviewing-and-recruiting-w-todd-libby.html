about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with todd libby: learn how recruiters can improve outreach and how to handle technical interviews and job search anxiety from an accessibility expert with 25 years of experience.

recruiters should spell candidate names correctly and personalize messages based on actual profile review. read the full job description and understand role requirements before reaching out to candidates. tell candidates who will be on interview calls and what to expect to reduce surprises and anxiety. saying i don't know in interviews is acceptable if you follow up with how you would find the answer. keep networking and applying while waiting to hear back from companies rather than getting discouraged. build accessibility into a project from the start rather than bolting it on at the end to save time and stress.
