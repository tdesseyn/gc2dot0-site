linkedin live episode 5 - tips and tricks on how to write an effective resume

nov 13, 2020 · 16 min · hosted by taylor desseyn

3 takeaways

  • > early career resumes should include more detail, not less, so hiring managers see everything you have done.
  • > tailor your resume to the specific job you want to get called for instead of leaving in unrelated old experience.
  • > replace vague bullet points with specific project details and quantitative results like team size or timeline.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to write a resume that gets you called for the right jobs, with tips on length, detail, formatting and tailoring.

resumes can be longer than one page. junior careers need more detail, senior careers up to two to three pages max. tailor your resume to the jobs you want. remove outdated skills and technologies that lead to wrong calls. include project details with numbers and context instead of generic bullet points. show your role and impact clearly. use readable formatting with good spacing and font size. stop bolding everything. let your resume breathe. create multiple resumes for different skill sets to target specific job types and increase your chances. give comparative stats when switching industries so hiring managers can see drive and performance in a new context.

Writing a resume is hard and weird. I break down on what I like to see in a resume!
