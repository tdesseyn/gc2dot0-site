about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with jordan rogers: how to get a cool job by building skills, showing up, and being distinct, plus lessons from leaving your dream role.

show up in person and ask how you can help instead of asking for favors or mentorship time. side projects and passion work often matter more to hiring managers than resume bullet points. who you work for matters more than your job title once you get your foot in the door. when trying to land a role, ask what the team needs and how you can help instead of only talking about yourself. build a passion project outside your day job, since initiative and effort often matter more than a polished resume.
