about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with lawrence lockhart: how one senior developer advocate landed a job in 89 days after a layoff by building a public brand and testing a strategic job search approach.

take time to process emotions after a layoff before applying anywhere to avoid desperation in interviews. build public impact through social proof like conference talks and blog posts, not just random code posts. create a specific measurable goal for your job search with timeline and ideal company profile. use linkedin open to work with a strong profile optimized for recruiter search so you get found without cold applying. know your exact strengths and communicate them confidently because employers need to see you believe in yourself. share your impact publicly, not just daily coding reps, so companies can verify your skills before they even talk to you. turn on open to work and build a strong linkedin profile so recruiters and hiring managers find you first.
