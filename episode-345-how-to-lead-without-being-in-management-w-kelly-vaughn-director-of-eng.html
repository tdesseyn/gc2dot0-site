about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with kelly vaughn: how to build influence and lead without a management title by developing key leadership skills like big-picture thinking, amplifying others, and emotional intelligence.

leaders understand how their work impacts the entire organization, not just their assigned project. amplify others' voices and work without expecting credit or making support transactional. seek feedback constantly, receive it gracefully without defensiveness, and work on genuine weaknesses. high emotional intelligence means managing your own emotions and responding empathetically to others. show leadership tendencies and initiative before expecting a leadership title. work on genuine weaknesses like being defensive or abrasive instead of writing them off as just your personality.
