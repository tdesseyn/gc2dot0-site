about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with taz singh: how to build tech communities that actually work, why guild is free, and what makes people stay engaged long-term.

start communities for members, not to hire devs or gain followers. communities solve job search better than postings because people trust peers. take projects to 100% completion, not 90%, to learn and help others use them. surround yourself with strong performers in a community since who you spend time with shapes how much you improve. push any project past the easy first 90 percent, since the final 10 percent is where the real learning and growth happens. treat community building like the creator economy, where the ecosystem pays through sponsors, not the organizers or members. founders misalign incentives when they start a community just to hire developers, then let it die after one event. mix event formats like tech talks, workshops, socials, and hack nights, each on its own natural schedule.
