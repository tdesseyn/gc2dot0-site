about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with harry wolff: engineering leadership at mongodb: hiring with intentionality, building team culture through flow states, and managing the emotional toll of job searching.

job searching remains difficult at all experience levels; prepare emotionally for six months of active searching. hiring managers should define role clarity and team fit beyond technical skills to reduce candidate burnout. culture thrives when teams reach flow state, passing information naturally without explicit coordination. management requires learning interpersonal skills not taught as individual contributors; prepare for unfamiliar work. stay persistent during job search and keep applying until offer is signed; external factors outside your control matter significantly. before moving into management expect to learn interpersonal conflict and non-technical skills you were never taught as an engineer. expect the job search to take around six months and be ready for the emotional toll since even senior engineers find it draining.
