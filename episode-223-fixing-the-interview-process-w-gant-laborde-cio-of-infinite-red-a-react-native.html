about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with gant laborde: how to fix tech hiring by prioritizing values and trust over metrics, and why companies that invest in people attract better talent naturally.

job seekers should research each company and customize applications instead of sending generic resumes everywhere. hiring managers focused on metrics over values end up gaming systems and losing good talent to competitors. companies that invest in culture, conferences, and employee visibility build reputation that attracts candidates without heavy recruiting. leaders in the interview process signal company values immediately and build trust faster than automated screening. mission statements mean nothing unless they actually drive hiring and promotion decisions at every level. when companies over rely on metrics instead of values, people learn to game the system instead of doing good work. ask about a company's real values in the interview, since a mission statement that is just a wall plaque means little.
