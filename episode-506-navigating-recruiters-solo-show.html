about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: learn how to work with recruiters effectively, including how to identify good recruiters and ask the right questions to cut through job search noise.

ask agency recruiters where your skills rank, what to improve on your resume and linkedin, salary guidance, and preferred follow-up frequency. when a recruiter messages about a job, ask how long they've been recruiting the role, if they have a contract, the hiring manager name, intake call notes, and placement history. only work with recruiters who have at least five years of experience and stay at companies long term; skip those who job hop frequently. use one dedicated email with folders for companies and recruiters to track all outreach and stay organized throughout your search. if multiple recruiters pitch you the same role, getting double submitted can get you rejected, so pick the one who can answer your questions fastest and ask about a right to represent.
