what to give when remote is not enough

jan 7, 2022 · 17 min · hosted by taylor desseyn

3 takeaways

  • > set a clear pto policy instead of vague unlimited pto so employees actually know what they get.
  • > build a defined mentorship program for junior devs instead of leaving them to figure it out alone.
  • > let employees use part of their time for side hustles or volunteering since it can boost motivation at work.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: what companies should offer employees beyond remote work and pto to attract and retain talent.

set pto policies beat unlimited pto because people actually take defined time off. mentorship programs for junior developers are critical but rare at most companies. employees with side hustles and lives outside work perform better and need support. spot raises and regular pay increases matter more than waiting for people to leave. parental leave, childcare support, and company-wide time off weeks help retention. give spot raises so people don't have to quit just to get paid fairly. consider offering things like a work from home budget, childcare support, or paid parental leave to stand out.
