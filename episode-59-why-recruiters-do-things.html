why recruiters do things

jul 26, 2021 · 10 min · hosted by taylor desseyn

3 takeaways

  • > ask a recruiter how many people they placed last year to gauge experience and track record.
  • > ask if the recruiter has a specialty since focusing on one skill set leads to better matches than covering everything.
  • > ask how a recruiter tracks candidates and whether they will stay in touch even if the current role does not work out.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: learn three questions to ask recruiters and why they often lack deep company and candidate knowledge.

find recruiters with specialties in specific skill sets rather than those covering all tech roles. ask how recruiters stay organized with contacts and whether they follow up after failed placements. recruiters prioritize speed over quality, which is why many send resumes without proper vetting. quality recruiting takes time for testing, calls, and reference checks before sending candidates. if a company is getting a flood of resumes for one role the recruiting firm is not vetting candidates properly. recruiters who take time to test and reference check candidates often lose placements to faster firms but it leads to better quality hires.
