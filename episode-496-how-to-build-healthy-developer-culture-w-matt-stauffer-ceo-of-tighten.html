about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with matt stauffer: build healthy developer culture by leading with empathy, treating people as whole humans, and being transparent about company finances and decisions.

make decisions considering who most needs support, not just who benefits most. share financial info and company direction openly so employees can make informed choices. hire for soft skills and interpersonal ability; remote work means communication matters more. keep company small enough to maintain flat structure and genuine relationships with everyone. when job hunting, research the hiring manager and company deeply since a generic resume sent to hundreds of openings gets deleted immediately. if you want a promotion, ask your employer to clearly define the next role and the specific steps needed to get there.
