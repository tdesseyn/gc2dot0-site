about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with jeff blankenburg: a principal engineer shares how he landed a new role after a six-month job search by reaching out directly to hiring managers on linkedin instead of applying online.

skip the online application system and message hiring managers directly with a personalized note about the role. set your salary anchor based on previous roles, but know your lowest acceptable number before negotiations begin. ask hiring managers what success looks like in a year to understand if the role aligns with your strengths. get interviewers talking about themselves and their experience to build genuine connection and stand out. building a real network over years matters more than cold applications; prioritize relationships with colleagues and peers. do not take rejection personally, it is usually business and fit, not a judgment on your worth or skill.
