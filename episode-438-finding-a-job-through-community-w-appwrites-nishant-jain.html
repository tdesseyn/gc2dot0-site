about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with nishant jain: how building in public, joining communities, and attending hackathons led to multiple job opportunities in tech.

start sharing your work publicly early, even if it feels rough or incomplete. join communities and attend hackathons to meet people and discover career paths you didn't know existed. one-on-one conversations matter: reach out to people you want to learn from without being shy. hackathons don't have to be complex or competitive to be valuable, beginner tracks exist for everyone. being active as a community contributor can lead directly to job offers from people who notice your work. hackathons do not have to be complex or competitive, a simple idea with passion behind it can win and make an impact. dont be afraid to dm people for one on one conversations since most people in tech communities genuinely want to help.
