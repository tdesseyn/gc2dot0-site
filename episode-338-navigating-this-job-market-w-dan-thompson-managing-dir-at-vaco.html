about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with dan thompson: navigating the 2024 job market with realistic expectations on hiring, salaries, remote work, and how to stand out as a candidate.

companies are hiring again after 2023 slowdown but salaries will flatline, not grow like the pandemic years did. know why you're worth your asking salary and how you solve problems for that specific company, not just what you earned before. remote work is shrinking; expect more return to office and hub locations where teams gather in one area. if you need work, take a job that pays bills without guilt; use it as a bridge to find what you want. junior devs should build real projects, network with peers not just managers, and show what you solved not just learned. reach out to other juniors, mids, and seniors for advice since they get asked less often than managers and directors. be purposeful in your job search by researching a company's actual products and services before interviewing, not just their website.
