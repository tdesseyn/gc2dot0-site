about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with rhia dixon: how to gain visibility, build skills for advancement, and make success repeatable in tech careers.

keep a bragg book with screenshots of client feedback and accolades for reference. moving from mid to senior requires people skills and applying experience, not just technical depth. document processes, use checklists and templates to make success repeatable across your team. get visible through internal blogs, employee resource groups, and skip level meetings with leadership. moving from mid level to senior is less about technical skill and more about aligning people to the why behind decisions. use checklists and templates like pr templates so your successful process can be repeated and taught to others.
