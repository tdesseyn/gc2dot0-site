about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with aaron harmon: how to land a job in 4 weeks through strategic networking, personalized outreach, and repositioning your experience when a role doesn't officially exist yet.

panic apply first, then pivot to targeted outreach to actual people in roles at companies you want. research who someone is before messaging them, match your tone to their communication style, keep dms short and direct. most unadvertised jobs come from referrals and manager networks, not a secret job market that's as big as people think. listen to what problems a company actually has, then pitch how you solve those problems rather than fitting into posted job descriptions. accept help when offered and take conversations from unexpected places, even at companies you've never heard of. personalize your outreach messages based on the person's role and how they communicate on linkedin, matching their tone. accept help when people offer it during a job search, even small gestures like an introduction can open doors.
