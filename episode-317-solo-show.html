about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: taylor walks through job search strategy, networking tips from a recent conference, and why following up matters more than applying to hundreds of jobs.

bring a friend to networking events to feel more comfortable and make meaningful connections. stop mass applying to jobs; use networking, cold outreach, and recruiting contacts instead. document what you do daily on social media instead of worrying about creating perfect content. ask specific interview questions and research the company and interviewers before your meeting. if networking feels uncomfortable, bring a friend to events and follow them around until you warm up. for interviews, research the company and interviewer, review your resume against the job description, and prepare your own questions.
