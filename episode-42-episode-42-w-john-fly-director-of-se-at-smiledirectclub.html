about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with john fly: how to build strong company culture early by hiring for cultural fit, defining your mission, and avoiding silos as you grow.

hire for cultural fit and shared values, not just technical skills or experience. create standardized interview processes so candidates are evaluated fairly and consistently. executives must regularly talk to people below their direct reports to understand real company culture. communicate your why and mission from leadership regularly so it stays central as the team grows. build a standardized interview process so every candidate is evaluated through the same steps and skills criteria. skip the canned job description and think through why the role exists so you can speak to it honestly in the interview.
