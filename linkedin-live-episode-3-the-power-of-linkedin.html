about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to use linkedin to build your professional brand and jumpstart your career through consistent, authentic posting.

post on linkedin regularly, even twice a week, with authentic content about your work and accomplishments. linkedin's organic reach means your post gets shown to followers of people who like it, multiplying visibility. use hashtags like google searches to help people in your industry find your content. track who views your posts using linkedin's data tools to refine what content resonates with your audience. be consistent and patient; nothing may happen for months or even nine months, then suddenly a post gains traction. share authentic, specific stories about your work instead of generic self help or get rich quick content. stay patient since it may take months of consistent posting before a post takes off and builds your following.
