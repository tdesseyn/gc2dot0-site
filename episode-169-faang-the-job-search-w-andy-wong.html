about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with andy wong: how to break into tech from non-traditional backgrounds and succeed in faang interviews and negotiations.

bootcamp success depends on effort and research; don't fall for marketing without doing your homework first. failing interviews is normal; take notes and try again because there's luck and circumstances involved. saying i don't know is fine if you follow it with problem-solving ideas and ask for help or clarification. if you dont know an answer in an interview say so and work through it together instead of freezing up. in salary talks assume you have negotiating power, know your options, and be willing to walk away.
