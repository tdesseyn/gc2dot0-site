about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with david green: how a 20-year .net developer landed a python role at netapp by fixing his resume, getting inbound recruiting interest, and preparing seriously for interviews.

job boards and indeed are largely ineffective; you need a different approach to get noticed. a professional resume service ($200-400) improved keyword optimization and triggered inbound recruiter outreach on linkedin. research your interviewer, the company, and the job description before interviews to show genuine interest. have salary conversations early with recruiters to avoid wasting time when offers are far off target. prepare for technical interviews like whiteboarding and take-home assignments by studying for months beforehand. invest in a professional resume and linkedin rewrite so recruiters start reaching out to you instead of you chasing every lead.
