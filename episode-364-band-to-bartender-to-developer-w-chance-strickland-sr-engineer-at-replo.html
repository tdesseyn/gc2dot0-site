about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with Chance Strickland: how a band musician turned bartender became a senior engineer by building websites for his band, learning to network and ship products, then strategically moving to startups.

learn a skill because you need it for something you love, not because it's trendy or required. networking starts with genuine conversation and showing up, whether it's playing a gig or applying to a role. know why you're changing jobs before you jump, especially early career when you need to build optionality. look for jobs outside fang and mega-startups where sustainable companies still actively hire great talent. persistence pays off in recruiting, both as a candidate and as a company trying to hire someone. if youre early career expect to take opportunities that pop up instead of being picky since experience is what lets you get systematic later. know your specific reason for leaving a role before you jump so you dont make a move without a clear why.
