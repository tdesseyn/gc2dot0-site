about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with colin lord: engineering manager colin lord shares how to stand out in interviews by demonstrating communication skills, humility, and the ability to stay calm under pressure when production goes down.

show you can communicate clearly and be vulnerable about mistakes, not just that you can code. when describing past projects in interviews, talk about collaboration and process, not just technical specs. hiring managers can teach you react faster than they can teach you humility and good communication. take a break from coding to work on something completely different to keep your brain fresh. in take-home assessments, write comments explaining your thought process to show soft skills and reasoning.
