marketing & the job search

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to market yourself during a job search by staying top of mind through posting, engaging, and building community.

post one thing daily about your job search or career to stay top of mind with recruiters. comment and engage on others' posts every day instead of mindlessly scrolling social media. keep your resume off linkedin so people message you and have a real conversation. turn casual social media interactions into meaningful offline conversations through intentional networking. document what you're passionate about like blogging or posting about your interests to build an audience. write or blog about a topic you care about, even a niche one, since small posts can reach a surprisingly large audience over time.
