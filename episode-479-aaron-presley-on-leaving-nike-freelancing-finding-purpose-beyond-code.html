about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with aaron presley: how leaving a big tech company to freelance requires budgeting, niche focus, networking skills, and redefining success beyond money.

before going independent, ensure you can budget and live with lower income for an extended period. find a specific niche you're passionate about rather than chasing unicorn startups or generic opportunities. contract-to-hire lets you evaluate real fit better than traditional interviews ever could. redefine success as happiness and bills paid instead of startup valuations or titles. coding skill alone wont be your edge as ai makes writing code easier, so find what else sets you apart. try contract to hire arrangements since short interview processes rarely reveal how someone will really work out.
