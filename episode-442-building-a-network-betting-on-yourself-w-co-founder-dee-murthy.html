about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with dee murthy: learn how to build a valuable network over time by showing up in person, handling rejection, and focusing on long-term relationships rather than immediate returns.

attend conferences to connect with peers, understand industry trends, and build genuine relationships through shared experiences. start networking young by going to events alone to build confidence and communication skills without relying on friends. make a good impression quickly at networking events then move on; don't try to have long conversations with people you just met. hire people who have startup experience and understand your specific business model; cultural fit and communication matter more than big company names. when rejected from an opportunity, respond professionally and leave the door open for future conversations to stay top of mind. do your homework before interviews and be easy to work with during scheduling, since friction signals how you will be to manage. treat failure as a learning tool by taking accountability instead of blaming others for what went wrong.
