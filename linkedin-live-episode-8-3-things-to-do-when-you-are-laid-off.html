about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: three essential steps to take immediately after a layoff: update your resume, optimize your linkedin, and work with recruiters.

optimize your linkedin profile with good photo, banner, title, and recommendations from others. screen recruiters using 10 questions and the two-year rule: at least two years recruiting and two years in same city. upload your resume to job boards, especially dice.com and indeed for tech roles. work with recruiters first to do the heavy lifting before applying directly to job postings. update your resume first and tell a story with project details instead of a long list of bullet points. make sure your linkedin has an updated title, banner, and photo since it exists for recruiters and hiring managers to find you. collect recommendations on linkedin to help you stand out from other applicants.

The job search is exhausting and most people don't even know how to start! In this episode I give you 3 basic building blocks to get rolling.
