about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with Neil Chaudhuri: developer productivity isn't just about individual skills—it's about leadership creating flow state through privacy, collaboration, and support.

flow state comes from leadership creating the right environment, not from pushing developers harder. only 25% of a developer's day is spent coding; eliminate unnecessary meetings and ceremonies. hiring matters beyond tech skills—build culture around trust, documentation, and mutual support. going remote globally works for niche skill sets like elixir, but adds complexity around timezones and culture. build relationships and network—they matter as much as technical skills for your career. before going independent, make sure you have the skills, relationships, and safety net to cover healthcare and income if things dont work out. when hiring globally, think through time zone overlap, language, culture, and security practices before assuming it will save money.
