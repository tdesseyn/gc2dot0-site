contracting vs. direct hire

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with daniel oliver: explore contracting versus direct hire roles, how to position yourself in the market, and manage career perception as a contractor.

contract to hire lets companies speed up hiring by using recruiters to handle background checks and drug tests upfront. w-2 contract work through a recruiting company provides more support and simpler taxes than independent 1099 contracting. career contractors face employer skepticism about job hopping, but good references and finishing projects on good terms help your story. contracting offers exposure to new technologies and higher hourly rates, but projects can end early and budgets get cut without warning. there are no job guarantees in contracting or full time work, so focus on building strong relationships and doing quality work. being a career contractor is not a red flag by itself, but talking about your next move during an interview is. no job is fully guaranteed so dont avoid a contract role if its the kind of work you actually want to do.

In this episode I catch up with Daniel Oliver @a_software_dev over the pros and cons of contracting vs. direct hire. We also go over many other areas of contracting and how to position yourself in the market.
