about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with bram gruneir: enterprise architect at cockroach labs shares insights on company culture, interviewing practices, and career growth through side projects and mentorship.

take time off regularly to recharge; companies benefit when engineers can think clearly without constant meetings. blind interviews reduce bias; don't screen resumes before interviews to avoid favoring big company names or schools. build projects you're passionate about, including side projects and open source work, to show genuine interest. internships and co-ops during school create real work experience that's different from classroom learning and look good on resumes. good tech leadership requires coaching and mentors; promoting top developers into management without support makes bad bosses. avoid filtering resumes by big name companies or schools since that bias can cause you to miss good candidates. build side projects you are passionate about since that experience and initiative stands out to employers.
