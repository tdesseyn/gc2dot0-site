about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with jacob herrington: self-taught dev shares how he got organized during intensive job search, landed role at desired company by being selective about opportunities.

get organized early: jacob averaged 4-5 calls daily for a month, then created an a/b/c ranking system to filter companies. ask about pay equity and management structure in interviews to find companies aligned with your values. be discoverable on social media and through your network; jacob found his role via a twitter post and referrals. decline interviews if you're not excited; don't waste time on companies with long processes that don't respect your energy. onboarding quality matters: two-month structured learning period with mentors and documentation sets new hires up for success. put yourself out there on social media and content creation to become discoverable instead of relying only on applications. don't be intimidated by job postings since most requirements are a wish list and no candidate checks every box.
