why do recruiters ghost

mar 1, 2021 · 12 min · hosted by taylor desseyn

3 takeaways

  • > recruiters often ghost because they are juggling hundreds of candidates and heavy admin work, not because they do not care.
  • > follow up with your recruiter yourself, do not assume they will always reach out first.
  • > check in once or twice a week for the first few weeks after meeting a recruiter, then ease off.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: why recruiters go silent on job seekers and what you can do about it.

recruiters meet hundreds of people yearly and manage multiple roles simultaneously, causing them to get overwhelmed. the relationship between recruiter and job seeker is two-way; you should check in with recruiters weekly. companies are the paying client, not job seekers, so recruiters prioritize hiring manager needs. if a recruiter ghosts after multiple check-ins from you, move on and find recruiters who communicate better. text or email recruiters instead of calling; they are busy in meetings and won't answer phones. remember the hiring company pays the recruiter, not the job seeker, so communication is not always guaranteed.
