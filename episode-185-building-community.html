about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to network at conferences and build community around your passions without burning out.

use tools like dot card to reduce friction when exchanging contact info at networking events. start a community around something you care about, even with just a handful of people. networking is a muscle that requires regular practice, aim for at least monthly events. start networking before you need to, especially if layoffs are happening in your industry. build community around something you are already passionate about and keep the first group small. carry something like a tap card or qr code to cut networking friction at conferences and meetups. pick about five talks and five people to meet at a conference instead of trying to do everything. at local meetups narrow down to around ten people to connect with so you do not burn out.
