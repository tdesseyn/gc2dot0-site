dealing with recruiters

jun 30, 2021 · 22 min · hosted by taylor desseyn

3 takeaways

  • > if a recruiter dms you a role that doesn't fit, send screening questions back or just don't respond and move on.
  • > recruiters get paid by companies through commission, not by candidates, so never pay a recruiter for help finding a job.
  • > if there's no job posting for an interview, ask the recruiter for the actual intake notes from the hiring manager.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: learn how to work effectively with recruiters, handle job search questions, and improve your interview and communication skills.

use a screening questionnaire when recruiters message you irrelevant roles to filter out bad fits. recruiters make money from company fees, not job seekers, so never pay a recruiter for their services. ask recruiters for specific intake notes or job descriptions instead of relying on vague postings. develop a 15 second elevator pitch about yourself to stand out in interviews and networking. practice speaking by recording yourself, doing mock interviews, and posting video content to improve communication. practice your elevator pitch out loud and record yourself to catch habits and improve how you sound in interviews. to find fair pay, research ranges and talk openly with trusted peers in your field instead of relying on vague titles like junior or senior.
