about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with anthony mays: former google engineer anthony mays explains why he left big tech to do coaching and consulting work helping people navigate tech careers and underrepresented talent.

slow down your approach to fang jobs. success requires both correct information and consistent work ethic over time. networking means actually being social. personalize outreach with genuine interest rather than just sending resumes to people. write and document your accomplishments regularly so you can articulate your value in interviews and on resumes with specific details. always negotiate offers. everything is negotiable and you need multiple offers as reference points to understand fair market value. hiring managers should simplify job requirements and be transparent about growth path. middle managers have power to create change for underrepresented talent. always negotiate every offer, do your homework by comparing with others, and never let anyone rush your decision.
