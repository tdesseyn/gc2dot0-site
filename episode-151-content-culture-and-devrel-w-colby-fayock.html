about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with colby fayock: why content creation and developer relations matter for job seekers and companies building remote culture.

create any content to show employers you actively learn, from tweets to blog posts. build your own developer advocate presence through consistent content to stand out from other candidates. network directly with people on twitter or linkedin instead of relying only on job postings. set work hour boundaries in slack and turn off notifications to prevent burnout and anxiety. build remote culture through video calls, respecting schedules, and starting with trust in your team. networking through a direct message can lead to a job faster than submitting applications through postings. turn your camera on during remote calls because seeing faces builds more trust with teammates.

I am joined this morning by Colby Fayock who is a Senior Dev Experience Engineer @ Cloudinary. We dive into why content and DevRel needs to be apart of your organizational approach, how to build good culture, and maybe a job tip or two for job seekers!
