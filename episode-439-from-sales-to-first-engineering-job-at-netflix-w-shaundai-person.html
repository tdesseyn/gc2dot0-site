about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with shaundai person: how a former salesperson went from self-teaching code to landing a senior ui engineering role at netflix through strategic networking and genuine community building.

network authentically inside your company by scheduling coffee chats and building genuine relationships with engineers before you need anything. when messaging someone senior, keep it short and specific with a concrete ask, skip formal pleasantries, and come prepared for any call you request. in interviews, research the team members and position yourself as the human who fits their needs while assuming others have similar technical skills. use video in your application and thank you emails to stand out as a person, showing your personality and making genuine human connection. come to a networking call fully prepared with your own questions since you are asking for someone else's time. use short personalized video in applications and thank you notes to stand out as a real person in a remote hiring process.
