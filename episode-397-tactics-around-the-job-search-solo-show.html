about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: learn tactics for job searching including flexibility across roles, building business knowledge, nailing your pitch, and staying responsive with follow-ups.

prepare a concise 90-second answer to tell me about yourself that shows what you do and why you matter. speed matters now: respond to recruiters within 24 hours and follow up consistently to stay top of mind. interview every interaction from emails to body language signals your professionalism and enthusiasm. use slack and discord communities to crowdsource job searches with peers in your skill range. stay positive and energetic in interviews, since hiring managers can sense negativity and it hurts your chances.
