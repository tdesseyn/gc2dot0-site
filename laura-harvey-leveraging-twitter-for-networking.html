about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with laura harvey: how to use twitter as a junior developer to network, find jobs, and build genuine connections in tech.

post less frequently with useful content rather than constant updates, use fewer hashtags for better reach. document your struggles and wins publicly to show you're human and help other juniors feel less alone. interact by congratulating people on jobs and birthdays before sharing your own content or asking for help. prioritize authenticity and kindness over polished content, attitude matters more than resume polish to employers. don't compare your early posts to others since most people only share their highlight reel. bring genuine enthusiasm to interviews, since attitude can matter as much as resume or portfolio.

In this episode I had the amazing opportunity to interview someone who I have been admiring from afar. Laura Harvey (@lauracharvey) has grown her Twitter following from 0 to 5,000+ in a little over 4 months. We discuss how amazing of a networking tool Twitter can be! I hope you enjoy!
