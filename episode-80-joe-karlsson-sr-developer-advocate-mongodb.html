about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with joe karlsson: why senior developers stop coding as much and how companies can structure teams to maximize their impact.

senior devs stop coding to avoid becoming blockers and to empower junior team members instead. companies should communicate with senior devs about what roles they want, not assume everyone wants the same path. technical managers should study people management as a skill, not just code a little. hire junior developers more often; most companies think their problems are unique when they're not. most people leave jobs because of their manager, not the work itself. when building teams, favor mostly internal promotions with some fresh outside hires to balance stability and new ideas. junior engineers get overlooked because companies fear training costs, but a strong onboarding structure lets juniors scale senior output too.
