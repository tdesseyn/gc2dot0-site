about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with frank bach: navigating career change, managing imposter syndrome, staying visible at work, and processing job transitions with intention and mindfulness.

be visible in remote work by having camera on and showing up in meetings so leadership knows who you are. talk to your manager about moving toward meaningful projects and frame it as supporting your growth. pause and process after a layoff instead of rushing into job search mode immediately. chip away at job search daily with networking and portfolio updates but take weekends off to avoid burnout. after a layoff take a beat before jumping into action mode then reach out to your network once you feel clearer headed. being public about a layoff on social media can open up interviews, coffee chats and part time opportunities you would not expect.
