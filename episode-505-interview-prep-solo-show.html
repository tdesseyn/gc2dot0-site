about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: learn basic interview prep tactics like researching companies and interviewers, asking strategic questions, and staying thoughtful during conversations.

research the company and the specific people interviewing you before any call or meeting. ask recruiters or hiring managers three key questions: why is the position open, what will you discuss, and how quickly are you hiring. bring written notes to interviews and tell the hiring manager you'll be taking notes to show you're engaged and thinking. pause and think before answering questions instead of filling silence with nervous rambling to show you process carefully. send personalized thank you messages on linkedin after interviews and ask about next steps or follow-up timing before ending the call. bring a notebook to interviews, tell the interviewer you are taking notes, and do not feel pressure to answer instantly.
