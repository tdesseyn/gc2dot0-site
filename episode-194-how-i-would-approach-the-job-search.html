how i would approach the job search

dec 16, 2022 · 21 min · hosted by taylor desseyn

3 takeaways

  • > connect with two to three local recruiters even in a remote job market to get a real pulse on hiring trends.
  • > ask recruiters direct questions like what are the hot jobs and what companies should i research instead of just applying on job boards.
  • > take interviews outside your target role as a junior to figure out what you actually enjoy and are good at.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to start a job search by networking with recruiters, attending events, and exploring different roles to find what fits.

show up consistently to meetups and events where your target industry gathers, even if you just listen. interview for different types of roles, not just your ideal job, to figure out what you actually enjoy doing. network is about making friends and getting people in your circle, not submitting resumes to job boards. there are many ways into tech beyond developer roles like qa, business analyst, project manager, or recruiting. show up consistently to meetups and conferences even if you stay quiet at first, presence matters more than talking. connect with every guest and commenter you interact with online, networking is really just making friends.
