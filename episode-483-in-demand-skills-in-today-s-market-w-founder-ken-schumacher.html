about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with ken schumacher: tech skills are shifting fast driven by ai adoption; companies now seek developers who can use ai tools effectively while maintaining fundamentals.

ai tool proficiency is becoming table stakes for developers; resistance to adoption mirrors past resistance to react. assessments are evolving to test how candidates use ai tools rather than blocking them; speed and approach matter more than perfect code. junior developers should lean into ai as accelerant for learning fundamentals, not replacement; everyone is still early with new tools. companies want different things from developers; customized assessments beat generic ones for matching candidates to real needs. show your work through projects and assessments, not just resumes; hiring managers care more about what you can do than what you claim. list ai tools like claude or github copilot as a bullet point if central to a project, otherwise include them in a technologies used line. standing out in todays market means doing visible work like finishing side projects, trying assessments, and sharing feedback instead of just polishing a resume.
