3 takeaways

  • > use twitter threads instead of single tweets so people can engage at any point in the story instead of waiting until the end.
  • > build a content habit by writing short essays or threads consistently to develop both a writing skill and a visible portfolio of ideas.
  • > when starting out on twitter, define your core principles, share examples of people doing it well, then tell stories around those ideas.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with austin schlessinger: how to use twitter for networking and testing ideas through threads and community discovery.

threads let people engage with specific ideas and create multiple entry points for conversation. use twitter search bar and tweetdeck to find communities around specific topics. twitter works best for startups and creator learning; linkedin still better for traditional job search. you don't need many followers to build real engagement in a niche community. use twitter mainly to test ideas and network, since small but engaged niche communities matter more than follower count. use linkedin for job searching but turn to twitter if you want to learn content creation, join startups, or build an online audience.
