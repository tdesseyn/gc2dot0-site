about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with Mary Haynes: mary haynes discusses why working with a recruiter matters and shares strategies for networking, especially for introverts in tech.

recruiters connect you directly to hiring managers instead of letting your resume disappear in automated systems. find networking events on meetup.com or ask recruiters for local tech community recommendations. prepare your resume and professional headshot before the new year rather than job hunting during holidays. share your interests and experiences on linkedin to build your network organically over time. recruiters can help you avoid getting lost in automated application systems that companies use to screen resumes. posting about your work or passions on linkedin can lead to unexpected opportunities since you never know who is paying attention.

Mary Haynes is apart of Vaco Technology's recruiting team where she specifically focuses on everything development. In this episode we have a very candid conversation of why you should work with a recruiter and her two cents on why you should use Vaco in your job search!
