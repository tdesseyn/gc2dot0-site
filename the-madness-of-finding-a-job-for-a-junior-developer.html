about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with thomas fitzgerald: a junior developer's path from graphic design to code school to landing their first dev job through networking and the right recruiter.

code schools teach fundamentals and teamwork skills like github and agile, not just building projects. networking matters more if you stay in the same city where you went to boot camp. give your resume to one or two reputable recruiters, not ten, to avoid getting ghosted. warm introductions from mutual connections work better than cold outreach from recruiters. coming from a non-developer background like design can actually help you stand out as a junior. freelance web development experience alone can be a tough sell when employers see it as less credible than full-time work. taking a non-developer role that still uses your design or coding skills can be a smart way into the industry. code school fundamentals like github, command line, and agile matter more once you are on the job than they seem to in class.

I catch up with Thomas Fitzgerald, a local junior developer, on his story of becoming a developer and landing his first job out of code school.
