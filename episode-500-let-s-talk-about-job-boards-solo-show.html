about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: learn which job boards work best for your tech career and how to use them effectively in your job search strategy.

create detailed profiles on job boards like dice and wellfound so recruiters can find you, not just to apply to jobs. set up a dedicated email for job board activity to manage incoming traffic and organize opportunities. apply directly to company career pages rather than through job board portals to avoid spam filtering. combine job boards with networking, direct outreach, and personal contacts for the most efficient search. create a job-search specific email so you can track applications and filter out spam. use job boards like dice to get found by recruiters, but apply to open roles directly on the company's career page.
