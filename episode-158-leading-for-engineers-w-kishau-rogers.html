about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with kishau rogers: how to lead engineers well by defining success metrics, building rest into planning, treating engineers as creators, and creating safe spaces to learn and experiment.

prep candidates before interviews by sharing what you'll discuss so they can prepare thoughtfully. have monthly one-on-ones with direct reports to give feedback regularly, not just at annual reviews. create sandbox environments where engineers can break things safely and learn through experimentation. use transparent hiring steps and prep candidates on what to expect so interviews feel conversational, not an interrogation. in job interviews explain the impact of your work, not just the skills, including customer feedback and team context.
