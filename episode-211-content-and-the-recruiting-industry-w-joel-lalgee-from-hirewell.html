about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with joel lalgee: why recruiting companies need people creating content to attract talent and build personal brands in a modern job market.

content helps you reach candidates mid-20s to mid-30s who connect through linkedin, tiktok, and podcasts instead of cold calls. companies should pay people to make content, encourage it from leadership, and provide support like editors and infrastructure. you don't need perfect production quality or millions of followers; focus on message and consistency over the first year before expecting results. personal branding and employer branding are different; let creators use their own voice instead of forcing company guidelines. internal recruiters building personal brands become more valuable because their posts reach more qualified candidates than standard job postings. internal recruiters who build a personal brand can bring their following with them to any future company they join. if youre just starting out, post about your day to day experience and lessons learned instead of trying to act like a thought leader.
