about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: taylor discusses wes bos's tweet about the current job search, breaking down interview trends, ai adoption, and why referrals matter more than ever.

referrals are now critical to cut through massive application volume and get real interviews. ai knowledge is becoming table stakes; ignoring it puts you at a disadvantage in the job market. in-person networking and building a niche personal brand open doors that cold applications cannot. take-home assessments are unreliable; companies are shifting to pair programming and trial periods instead. auto-apply tools are flooding companies with applications, which is a big reason you may not hear back. building a niche personal brand online can let you skip early interview rounds and go straight to senior decision makers.
