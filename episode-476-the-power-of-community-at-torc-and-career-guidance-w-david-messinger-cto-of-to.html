community reduces hiring risk by helping you understand people's goals before matching them to roles. network actively through meetups, podcasts, and honest conversations with mentors and peers. ask humble questions to show genuine interest in learning and expanding your knowledge. your profile should showcase projects, skills, and growth outside of traditional work experience. coming features include career coaching, job matching based on your profile data, and community rewards. find mentors and schedule recurring check-ins, even if meetings get rescheduled or spaced out. ask the so-called dumb questions and put yourself out there, since most people are more willing to help than you expect.
