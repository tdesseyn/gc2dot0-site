about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with tommy norman: how to build a hiring pipeline that scales while keeping company culture and values intact during growth.

communicate consistently with candidates even when you have no updates to share. streamline hiring touch points from seven to three: initial call, technical screen, final decision. hire for values alignment by asking for examples, not just yes-or-no questions. hiring managers lose candidates mainly from lack of communication, so overcommunicate even when there is no new update. pay close enough to market rate that money stops being the deciding factor, then win on autonomy, purpose and mastery. when scaling a company, know the people who thrived early on may not be the right fit for the next growth stage. define core values with specific examples, not buzzwords like integrity, so new hires know how to actually live them. let teams try to self correct conflict first through working agreements before escalating to formal coaching or a pip.
