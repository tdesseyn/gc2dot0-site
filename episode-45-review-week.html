about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: conducting regular performance reviews builds team culture, trust, and direct communication between managers and employees.

reviews create safe spaces for intentional conversations and help managers understand where their teams stand. stop being indirect in reviews; clear feedback on performance gains more respect than vague comments. staying consistent with review meetings and check-ins eventually improves team performance over time. employees should bring self-awareness to reviews: share wins, struggles, and your career direction. bring energy and passion to your work and review conversations to show you care about growth. stay consistent with check ins and reviews over time, results take years to show but they do show.
