about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with harry wolff: how to hire senior and staff engineers, from job descriptions and interview structure to evaluating collaboration and culture fit.

get clear on what you want before searching—both technical requirements and how candidates add to culture, then refine as you learn. avoid overly prescriptive job descriptions that limit your candidate pool; focus on role responsibilities and key skills needed. structure phone screens to gather signal efficiently; always leave time for candidate questions as part of evaluating their curiosity. prioritize team collaboration assessment in final rounds by asking about cross-team challenges, relationship building, and working style fit. cold emailing a hiring manager rarely works, a referral or just applying through the recruiting team is the better path.
