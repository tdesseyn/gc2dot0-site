about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to build a networking funnel by meeting people at events, connecting on social media, and engaging consistently over time before asking for anything.

meet people at conferences or meetups, get them connected on your preferred social media channel. post content regularly so people have a reason to engage with you over time. comment on others' posts and respond to comments to make people feel seen and heard. accept connection requests from people you don't know to expand opportunities for future engagement. expect it to take months or a year of consistent engagement before you ask for something in your network. if you avoid social media, you need daily coffees or lunches instead to keep meeting people. keep posting even when no one engages at first, it can take months before people start responding.
