about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: taylor answers listener questions about tech recruiting trends, networking strategies, and job search tips in a solo q&a episode.

back end developers are in high demand right now; front end roles are oversaturated, so consider learning back end skills. follow up multiple times with contacts; one message gets lost, but three messages signals genuine interest and gets responses. networking is about showing up in person and being present, not necessarily having perfect conversations or meeting everyone at an event. pick three specific people to connect with at conferences or meetups instead of trying to meet everybody. keep elevator pitches short and concise, around 30 to 45 seconds, and post video introductions to your linkedin featured section. networking does not require meeting everyone at an event, picking one or two people to intentionally connect with is enough. record a short selfie style video introducing yourself and add it to the featured section on linkedin to stand out to recruiters.
