about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with tyler clark: navigating early career growth through intentional networking, building portfolios aligned with target companies, and staying interview-ready even when employed.

get your first tech job in any role at a tech company, then network internally to move into development positions. ask your employer to sponsor conference attendance and training, and use those opportunities to network with recruiters. interview regularly even when happy at your job to stay sharp, build confidence, and avoid desperation if layoffs happen. check company policies on side hustles before starting them, and use side projects to add domain expertise beyond your current job. build a portfolio by picking companies you want to work for and matching your projects to the tech stack in their job descriptions. if you lack experience, get close to a dev team at your current job and ask them to vouch for you when a junior role opens.
