about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with michael chan: how creating honest content about your job search and building trust with your community can lead to job opportunities.

document your job search publicly through video to learn about industry trends and connect authentically with people. combine traditional job applications with content creation, not instead of them, to increase your chances. know what you are and aren't good at so you can turn down opportunities that aren't a real fit. if you are technical but struggle to find dev roles, consider pivoting to content creation or developer marketing roles. storytelling and communication skills matter as much as coding skills for advancing your career. it is okay to tell an interviewer a role is not the right fit for you, even at a company like netflix. if you have any interest in video editing, pursue it, since developer content and storytelling skills are increasingly valuable in tech careers.
