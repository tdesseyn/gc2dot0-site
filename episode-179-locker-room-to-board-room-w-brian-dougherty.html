about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with brian dougherty: learn how football principles like staying in your lane, playing loose, and focusing on small details apply to building effective engineering teams and managing through crunch.

respect roles and expertise in your organization; don't micromanage by stepping outside your lane. leaders take responsibility for failures so teams can play loose without fear and make better decisions. when crunch hits, motivate by looking to teammates not authority; teams win fourth and one situations together. break big projects into specific small targets rather than trying to hit everything at once. cross-train across positions and departments early to understand how other roles work and become more valuable. in crunch time look to the teammates next to you for motivation instead of just working for the company or the boss. when starting out or leading later, volunteer to try other roles outside your usual lane to build context and value.
