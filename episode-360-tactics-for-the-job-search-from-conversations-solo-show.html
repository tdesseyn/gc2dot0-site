tactics for the job search from conversations - solo show

apr 29, 2024 · 20 min · hosted by taylor desseyn

3 takeaways

  • > stop mass applying and instead dm hiring managers directly with why you are a good fit for the specific role.
  • > build a short mission statement that explains what you bring to the table so you can answer what do you do clearly.
  • > give yourself time to breathe after a layoff since its a traumatic event and deciding things on pure emotion rarely goes well.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: move from mass applications to direct outreach with hiring managers, manage emotions during job search, and build a strong mission statement.

keep dms short, direct, and ask actual questions to get responses from busy recruiters and hiring managers. give yourself time to breathe after job loss before starting interviews, as emotional decisions rarely work out. ask recruiters five things: resume tip, linkedin tip, how you compare to other candidates, salary feedback, and recruiter referrals. use a monthly bcc email to your favorite recruiters reminding them who you are and what you are looking for. when a recruiter cant place you still ask for a resume tip, how you stack up on skills and salary, and other recruiter contacts.
