about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with jesse gray.

junior developers should build practical projects with real users to show impact, not just theoretical coursework. communicate specific wins in interviews: what you personally moved the needle on and measurable business impact. be a team player people want to work with; strong cultural fit beats solo brilliance in a tight market. say yes to opportunities and take action rather than waiting for the perfect role to appear. build a small real project, even something silly, to show practical skills beyond bootcamp coursework. be easy to work with and stay engaged with your team, since that often matters more than raw output.
