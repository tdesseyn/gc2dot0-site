about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with michael richardson: how public speaking and networking transformed a developer's career by building community and opening doors.

start with small opportunities like meetups and conferences to build confidence and connections. submit talk proposals even if you expect rejection; the first submission gets you started on the path. choose topics you're passionate about that also fit your audience and conference focus. keep speaking over multiple years and submissions; rejection is normal and leads to improvement. speaking creates a network effect that opens doors with organizers, speakers, and opportunities you didn't expect. talks generally fall into technical deep dives, high level overviews for managers and beginners, or people skills topics.
