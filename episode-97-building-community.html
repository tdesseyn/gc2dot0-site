building community

jan 3, 2022 · 13 min · hosted by taylor desseyn

3 takeaways

  • > pick one real passion and build a small group around it instead of trying to network in general.
  • > start simple with meetup.com, facebook, linkedin, slack, or discord groups to give people a place to gather.
  • > set a consistent schedule like once a month so your community stays active without burning you out.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to build community around your passions to network naturally and advance your career.

start a meetup group around something you care about using meetup.com, facebook, linkedin, or discord. building community works better than traditional networking because people connect naturally through shared interests. consistency matters: commit to regular meetings like once a month so people know when to show up. community becomes a funnel for job opportunities and relationships without it feeling awkward or forced. you have to do the work yourself; there's no shortcut to building a group people will join. use a shared interest to turn a cold dm into a real relationship instead of a generic networking ask. if you are struggling to find a job or make friends, building a community around something you love can open doors.
