about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with ray morrison: junior developer shares first 90 days tactics and how oc tanner builds a fun, supportive environment for engineers.

buddy programs and pair programming help junior devs ramp up without overwhelming them in first weeks. give new developers autonomy to learn at their own pace with few hard deadlines in first 90 days. recognize and celebrate peer achievements through internal recognition programs and awards. create regular team meetings like retros and roundtables where everyone can share ideas safely. unexpected company gestures like surprise gift cards and salary band updates show genuine care for employees. be upfront about your story point comfort level so managers do not overload you early on. ask if your company will cover a course or certification tied to your bonus or growth goals.
