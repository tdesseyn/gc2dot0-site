about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with julian dasilva: engineering manager shares job search strategy, interview prep, and first 90 days onboarding after switching to misfits market in october.

network actively and cold apply to multiple pools; the job you land might come from unexpected sources. research hiring managers and team leads on linkedin to understand what skills they actually need beyond the job posting. prepare specific stories from your past work that match the role; write them down beforehand for easy recall during interviews. soft skills like mentorship, collaboration, and learning ability matter more than technical depth at manager and ic levels. pace yourself with interviews to bring full energy; spend first 60 days learning how things actually work, not copying your last job. research the actual people interviewing you, not just the job post, to tailor your examples to what they value.
