about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to build your online presence through posting, engaging, and networking on social media to advance your job search and career.

post about what you learn from live streams, meetups, or conversations and tag the people involved. liking and commenting on others' posts is the modern equivalent of networking at in-person events. at meetups, aim for brief 90 to 120 second conversations, connect on linkedin immediately, then follow up. follow up consistently via email and dm; persistence with multiple touchpoints matters more than a single attempt. when networking in person, ask simple questions like what do you do and what do you love about it, then move on. connect on linkedin right after meeting someone and follow up afterward to make the relationship stick.
