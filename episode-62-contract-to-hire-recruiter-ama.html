about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: guidance on contract to hire roles, recruiter practices, and how job seekers and hiring managers can improve their approach to hiring.

job seekers should consider contract to hire and pure contract roles as viable paths to permanent positions. hiring managers limit candidate pool by only seeking direct hire; expand options to include contract and contract to hire. candidates need to ask detailed questions during offer stage and stand up for themselves in negotiations. companies should avoid low-balling offers and getting in their own way with overly strict hiring requirements. cut fluff phrases like great communicator from your resume and let your project history speak for itself. job seekers should stay open to contract, contract to hire, and direct hire roles since the right pay and benefits can make any of them worth it.
