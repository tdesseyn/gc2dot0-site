about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with eric yager: how to retain engineering talent and implement effective management practices like tech leads and structured one-on-ones.

treat retention as urgent; engineers receive multiple recruiter outreach daily and have many options. empower teams through tech lead roles and self-organization rather than top-down decision making. implement team tenants as decision guardrails and run structured one-on-one meetings with planned weekly topics. consider adjacent roles like qa or program manager if entry level engineering positions aren't working. give managers clear goals and freedom to execute; nobody wants micromanagement regardless of market conditions. run one on ones every week with direct reports and plan specific topics ahead of time instead of winging it. if you lack the exact skills for an entry level tech role, take an adjacent role to get in the door and then work toward the role you want.

Joined by Eric Yager, Site lead @ Amazon for Air Science and Tech org in Nashville, we dive into some tactical things he is implementing on his team when it comes to running his software teams more effectively.
