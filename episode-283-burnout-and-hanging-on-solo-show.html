about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: recognizing burnout in tech and practical ways to protect your mental health and take back control.

set boundaries on work hours and meeting volume to prevent mental exhaustion. find people in your industry who understand your work struggles, not just outside friends. many people feel relieved when laid off, which signals it's time to evaluate if your job is worth the toll. structure your day with blocks for different activities to avoid burnout and stay present with family. ask yourself honestly if you'd feel relieved or sad if you got laid off today, it tells you something. set a hard stop time for your workday instead of letting work bleed into evenings. get outside and move your body daily, even short walks or workouts help with burnout. limit how many meetings you take in a day so you have mental energy left for family.
