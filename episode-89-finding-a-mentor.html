finding a mentor

oct 11, 2021 · 13 min · hosted by taylor desseyn

3 takeaways

  • > go after mentorship yourself instead of waiting for a mentor to reach out to you.
  • > connect with people you admire in your field on twitter and linkedin, then follow, comment, and like their posts before asking for anything.
  • > when you reach out be specific about what you want to learn instead of just asking someone to mentor you in general.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to actively find and build a mentorship relationship by engaging online, asking specific questions, and offering value first.

mentees need to drive the conversation and bring good questions, not expect mentors to do all the work. build rapport with potential mentors online first if cold dms feel uncomfortable, then ask intentionally. offer something to potential mentors first like collaboration or your attention to create real relationships. offer something of value or help first so you end up in that person's circle before asking for mentorship. come prepared with good questions and drive the conversation yourself instead of expecting the mentor to lead it.
