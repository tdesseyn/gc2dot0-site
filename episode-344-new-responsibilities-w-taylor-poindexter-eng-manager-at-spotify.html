about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with taylor poindexter: engineering manager at spotify shares job search tactics, networking strategies, and thoughts on career progression beyond climbing titles.

use your network to get to the front of the line, but also explore non-tech companies and products you love. take breaks during job hunting to recover mentally; rest helps you interview better than pushing through exhaustion. engage consistently on social media before applying; show genuine interest in roles and explain why you fit. you don't have to chase the next title; staying in a role you excel at is valid and worth reconsidering. take your pto and sabbaticals when possible; recovering from burnout helps you reassess your actual career goals. when reaching out to a hiring manager point to the specific role and explain why you fit instead of a generic cold message.
