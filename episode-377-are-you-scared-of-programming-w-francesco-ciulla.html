about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with Francesco Ciulla: career pivot to tech at 32, why ai won't replace programmers, and rust as an emerging language for developers.

switching careers to tech is possible at any age if you reset your mindset and stop being influenced by past decisions. ai tools like github copilot assist developers but require human expertise to make critical decisions and handle unexpected problems. rust is gaining adoption as a systems language but shouldn't be your first programming language; learn it as a second or third. content creation and public visibility matter more now; putting work out early beats waiting to feel ready. daily.dev helps developers stay current with free curated content across frameworks, languages, and tech topics. rust is gaining attention for being as powerful as a low level language while simplifying developer experience, but its not recommended as a first programming language since tutorials often reference other languages. stop worrying about what people think and get out of your comfort zone earlier, publishing work publicly matters more than waiting until you feel ready.
