about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with john rouda: learn what hiring managers prioritize in candidates, how to stand out as a college grad, and why soft skills matter more than technical expertise.

fit and personality matter more than skills because skills can be taught, but cultural fit determines team success. attend meetups and tech talks, then talk to speakers afterward to build relationships and gain knowledge from experienced professionals. develop communication skills by speaking verbally with people since many new grads lack phone conversation practice from growing up digital. build a portfolio and personal website with your own domain to stand out, and use a distinctive font on your resume instead of times new roman. senior roles require mentorship ability, asking for help when stuck shows strength not weakness, and simplifying complex ideas matters more than technical jargon. make your resume font and design slightly different from generic templates so it catches a hiring manager's eye.

In this episode I catch up with John Rouda @johnrouda who is a hiring manager and part time professor in South Carolina. We talk about what he looks for when hiring for his staff and some advice for recent college grads!
