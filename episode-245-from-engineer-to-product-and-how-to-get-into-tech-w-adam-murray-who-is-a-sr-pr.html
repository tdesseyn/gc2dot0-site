about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with adam murray: engineer turned senior product manager at cloudflare shares how to transition into product, why tech roles extend beyond coding, and how to build a sustainable career.

as a pm, focus on explaining the why to your team rather than dictating how; your role is connecting business, engineering, and customers. don't chase titles alone; they can hurt your career if given early, and there are only so many at the top of any ladder. during interviews, admit what you don't know, show your work publicly via github or portfolio, and let character and willingness to learn speak louder than perfect answers. be careful accepting inflated titles early since you may later have to walk them back. in interviews it is fine to admit you do not know something rather than giving a long answer to dodge it.
