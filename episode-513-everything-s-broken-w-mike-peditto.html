about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with Mike Peditto: how the job market actually works now: fewer hiring resources, thousands of applicants per role, and why traditional job search advice no longer applies.

companies now have massive applicant volume with minimal hiring teams, creating a traffic jam affecting both sides. focus energy on roles where you're a ninety-five percent match, not spray applications across hundreds of jobs. your resume's top third must immediately show relevant skills and accomplishments; recruiters spend seconds deciding to keep reading. the ats is not a robot rejecting you; it's a filing system recruiters use to organize and search resumes. building a professional network takes months or years and helps your next job search more than your current one. when reaching out to a recruiter or hiring manager, be specific about why you match that exact role instead of sending a generic message.
