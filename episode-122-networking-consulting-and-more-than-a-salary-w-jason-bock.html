about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with Jason Bock: career consultant turned developer advocate discusses networking as an introvert, consulting vs direct hire paths, and why company culture matters more than salary alone.

consulting firms offer benefits and diverse experience but mean less ownership; freelancing requires financial buffer and hustle for next gigs. as an introvert, aim for quality over quantity—one or two meaningful conference connections beats trying to meet dozens of people. relationships built slowly over years can lead to unexpected opportunities when you reconnect at the right moment. interview the company as much as they interview you; ask about culture and get equal talk time, not just 15 minutes at the end. as an introvert, aim to make one or two real connections at an event instead of trying to meet everyone.
