hiring strategy

sep 22, 2021 · 16 min · hosted by taylor desseyn

3 takeaways

  • > if your hiring process takes longer than two weeks, something in it is broken.
  • > let recruiters handle scheduling and logistics so interviews move fast, not just sourcing candidates.
  • > give candidates specific feedback like three bullet points on strengths and gaps instead of vague rejections.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to hire fast and efficiently without letting egos slow down your interview process.

set clear interview expectations upfront so candidates know exactly how many rounds to expect. hire within two weeks regardless of how many interview steps you have by being incredibly efficient. stop rejecting candidates because they didn't answer questions the way you wanted them to. recruiters should understand why a position is open and what problem it solves before submitting candidates. if a great candidate is not right for the role you interviewed them for, push to find them another fit in the company. as a candidate, ask directly at the end of an interview how to follow up and what the next steps are.
