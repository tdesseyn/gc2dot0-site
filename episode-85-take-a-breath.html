take a breath

3 takeaways

  • > if you have a current job, narrow your search to a few real-fit opportunities instead of chasing everything at once.
  • > be upfront with employers if you need more time to decide, asking for a week is reasonable.
  • > always ask questions in interviews so you know what the job is really like before accepting.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: managing job search stress by staying focused, asking good questions, and building genuine relationships with recruiters and hiring managers.

narrow your focus to one to three jobs instead of chasing every opportunity that comes your way. ask the employer for more time if you need it and be transparent about where you are in your search. send thank you messages via linkedin after interviews to stay top of mind with hiring managers. track where you are interviewing and journal after each interview to note what you learned and improve. view interviews as practice and relationship building, not just pass or fail moments. after interviews send a quick thank you message so you land back at the top of the hiring manager's inbox.
