about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with Lawrence Lockhart: how lawrence transitioned from restaurant management to coding at 46, built community connections, and grew a tech audience through content creation.

find a human mentor or community from day one, not after months of solo learning struggle. attend local meetups as a beginner to learn, listen, and build relationships with experienced developers. focus on communication and empathy skills as much as technical skills throughout your career. create genuine content about what you know to build your professional brand and open unexpected doors. keep going deeper into tech fundamentals and real-world problem solving rather than surface-level learning. show up to events with curiosity and humility, ask open-ended questions, and let people talk about themselves. post content consistently and mean what you say, since putting yourself out there can lead to unexpected speaking invites and career jumps.
