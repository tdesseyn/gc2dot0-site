about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: taylor discusses how chat gpt and ai will change recruiting, why networking matters more than ever in job search, and how to handle rejection.

your next job likely comes from your existing network, not job boards or career fairs. follow up after career fairs with linkedin connections and zoom calls to stand out. most rejections come from company issues, not your skills; take emotion out of the process. keep a short cover letter that highlights your career milestones even though many hiring managers skip reading them. expect chat gpt to assist recruiting work rather than replace the industry since relationships still drive hiring. if you are stuck job searching, aim to have one new networking conversation every day. treat the job search like a full time job and fill your schedule with networking when unemployed.
