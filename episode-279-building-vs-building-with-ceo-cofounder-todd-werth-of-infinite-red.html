about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with todd werth: how to transition from building software as an engineer to building a company as a founder, and why long-term thinking beats short-term returns.

being a good developer doesn't mean you'll lead well; treat leadership like learning a new skill with zero assumptions. sales is a learnable skill like any other; practice the same pitch repeatedly and get feedback after every call. invest in long-term brand building through open source, community, and conferences even if they lose money upfront. just show up and do more visible work than others; people notice and interact with what they can see and touch. after every sales call do a retrospective with your team to improve what you say and do.

I am joined today by Todd Werth who has spent his entire life building software and decided to make the jump to build a company! We are going to dive into this and the decisions he had to make!
