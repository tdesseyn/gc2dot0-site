about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with Kelly Vaughn: how to structure pto to actually encourage employees to take time off and why transparency in hiring and leadership matters.

leaders must model taking time off to show teams it is actually expected and valued. avoid sending messages late at night because it creates anxiety for team members even if unintended. hire for both technical skills and team fit through honest conversations about what you know and don't know. mentor others and find mentors yourself regardless of where you are in your career. in interviews, be upfront about what you know and don't know instead of pretending to have experience you lack. job seekers should ask hard questions back and talk to current employees to research a company before accepting an offer.
