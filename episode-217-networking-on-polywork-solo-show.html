about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn.

use badges to find and connect with people who work in your industry or share your interests. the collaborate tab shows specific opportunities like podcast speaking gigs and collaboration requests. message people on polywork about things they already want to do instead of cold pitching them. spend 10 to 15 minutes daily on polywork browsing collaborate opportunities and reaching out. polywork is better for networking and dming than for posting content and getting reach. when messaging someone on polywork, reference the exact collaboration type they listed, like podcast guesting, to skip the guesswork. use polywork badges to show what you do so people can find you without a full conversation. search a badge like software engineering to see everyone tagged with it and start connecting. use the collaborate tab to find people posting podcast, speaking, and live streaming opportunities.
