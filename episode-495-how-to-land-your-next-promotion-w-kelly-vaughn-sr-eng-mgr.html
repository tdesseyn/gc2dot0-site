about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with kelly vaughn: how to position yourself for a promotion by communicating upward, understanding business constraints, and proving readiness through actions beyond your job description.

upward communicate monthly with your manager about accomplishments; managers can't advocate for what they don't know. promotions need two things: you being ready and a business case for the role existing; you control only one. practice talking about your achievements without shame; cost of living raises are baseline, promotions require exceeding expectations. write a promotion document now even if applying later; identify gaps and build a documentation trail of accomplishments. do the job before you get the job by taking ownership, suggesting solutions with reasoning, and asking for feedback regularly. when giving suggestions to leadership, present multiple options and clearly state which one you recommend and why to show ownership. if you get rejected for a role or promotion, ask why, work on the gaps, and reapply later since a no now is not a no forever.
