about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with chase tinkham: how to recruit effectively during covid by building genuine relationships, staying top of mind, and providing value beyond just filling job openings.

position yourself as a market expert who educates candidates about realistic expectations, not just someone looking for a quick placement. use video calls, regular emails, and multiple touchpoints to stay connected with candidates even when you don't have immediate opportunities. post thoughtful content regularly on linkedin to build your brand and stay top of mind with thousands of people in your network. deploy empathy and be upfront about what you can and cannot do; offer resume help, introductions, and guidance even without a current job match. go deep with fewer people rather than wide with many; quality relationships and genuine follow-up matter more than big numbers. be upfront when you have nothing to offer but still help with resume or job search advice so the relationship outlasts any single role.

I had a fantastic conversation with Chase Tinkham (@chasetinkham) out of our Virginia Beach/Richmond office on how to recruit effectively in the current market and what does recruiting/sales look like moving forward post COVID-19.
