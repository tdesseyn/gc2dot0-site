about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: quick recap of resume writing, job boards, linkedin, and how to evaluate recruiters before your next steps.

use font 10 and garamond on resumes, keep them one to five pages depending on experience level. upload your resume to dice, career builder, and monster; linkedin shows you're a real person. look for recruiters who've stayed at the same company for at least two years, not job hoppers. make your resume tell your story with tech jargon and background details recruiters can use to sell you. interview recruiters like they're interviewing you before meeting in person. keep your linkedin updated with a professional photo and two or three sentences per position summarizing your work.

If you're lazy like me sometimes and want to get the "cliff notes" of my last few episodes...then listen to this!
