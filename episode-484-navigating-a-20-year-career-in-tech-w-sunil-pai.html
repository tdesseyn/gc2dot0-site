about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with sunil pai: how to navigate 20 years in tech without getting caught in hype cycles, and why ai is neither a fad nor a reason to panic.

social media creates false urgency about tech trends; stay grounded in fundamentals rather than daily news. ai has sixty years of research behind it; current progress is real but we're still very early in implementation. your job is to become familiar with new tools through hands-on work, not to predict winners or make absolutist bets. develop a routine of learning something new daily or weekly; write about what you learn to deepen understanding. build small projects with new tech to understand capabilities; there's fifteen years of work ahead even without major breakthroughs. write about what you're learning in public, even badly, since showing curiosity builds your reputation over time. before interviewing somewhere, try the product and send feedback or a quick demo directly to the hiring manager.
