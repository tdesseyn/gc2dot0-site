about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with colin lord: how to approach a layoff during a market downturn: handle practicalities first, grieve intentionally, then network and stay active without desperation.

handle unemployment and health insurance immediately, then take time to grieve before job searching. keep emotional investment low until you have an offer in writing to protect your mental health. activity matters more than perfection: more conversations and connections increase chances of opportunity. rejection reflects the job market, not your worth as an engineer or person. don't get emotionally attached to a role until you have a signed offer, since interviews can fall through for reasons outside your control.
