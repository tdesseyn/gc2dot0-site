about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with ingrid epure: learn negotiation tactics, build a non-linear career path, and advocate for fairness and transparency in tech hiring and promotions.

your career doesn't need to follow a set path; lateral moves and role changes are valuable ways to discover what interests you. representation of minorities at leadership levels matters more than diversity events; prioritize actual results and promotion equity. hiring managers should allocate challenging work to underrepresented employees, not just support roles, to create real growth opportunities. set yearly learning goals tied to skills and vision rather than just titles; balance technical and leadership development over time. set longer term learning goals tied to industry trends instead of comparing your progress to other people's timelines. push for real representation and equal opportunities for minorities instead of just diversity talk or campaigns.

Joined by Ingrid Epure, Staff Software Engineer at Netlify, we dive into a bunch of applicable topics to everyone in today's market! Topics like how to negotiate, how to evaluate your career progression, and ensuring fairness for minorities in tech.
