about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with james q quick: how changing companies can accelerate your career through new skills, higher pay, and growth opportunities you might not get staying in one place.

build your network before you need it by showing up in communities, meetups, and conversations over time. research job requirements, identify skill gaps, then work backwards to fill them strategically. staying checked out at a job signals it's time to explore, even if leaving feels risky or uncertain. new companies teach you new skill sets and often pay more for the same work and happiness level. staying at one company can cap your salary and growth, so compare offers and be open to moving. after a layoff give yourself grace and a day to breathe before starting to research and apply again.
