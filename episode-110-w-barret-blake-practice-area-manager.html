about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with barret blake: career changer and developer manager shares hiring insights: skip long interviews, prioritize communication and culture fit over technical skills alone, keep resumes to one page.

it's never too late to change careers; network in school because you don't know who might help. focus on communication skills and willingness to learn rather than perfect technical match. don't micromanage; trust your team to do their jobs with minimal oversight. remote work is future; companies requiring office time will lose talent to those offering flexibility. keep resumes to a single page with a short bullet list of top skills and brief paragraphs per job. practice talking about your accomplishments with family so you can confidently brag about yourself in interviews.
