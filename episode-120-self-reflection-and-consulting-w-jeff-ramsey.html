about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with jeff ramsey: how consulting helps you explore new industries, evaluate company cultures, and advance your career while keeping control over your opportunities.

use self-reflection to identify what makes you happy at work before exploring new roles or industries. work with recruiting firms to understand the market and find culture fits rather than applying blindly to job boards. most consulting roles offer w-2 employment, benefits, and 401k options, not just freelance gigs. contract-to-hire and consulting can be entry points into industries you want to join but find hard to break into. use a contract role at a company on the fringes of an industry as a stepping stone into that industry. lean on recruiters to learn the market instead of only relying on job boards and easy apply.
