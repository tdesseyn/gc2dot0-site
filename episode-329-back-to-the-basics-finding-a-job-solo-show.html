about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: practical steps to organize your job search starting with resume and linkedin, then job boards, networking daily, and tracking follow-ups.

have one new conversation daily with someone in or outside your network via thoughtful dms. use a tracking system like trello to follow up with contacts, most job searches happen in the follow-up. take breaks and unplug during your job search to maintain mental and physical health. use the three pillars of a good dm: admiration, a specific time ask, and a clear question. follow up consistently on applications since staying top of mind can change a hiring outcome.
