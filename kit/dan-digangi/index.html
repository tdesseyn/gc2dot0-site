Had a great time joining Taylor Desseyn on Guidance Counselor 2.0 to talk about navigating a layoff in real time.

A few things I shared:

> After a layoff, lean on your network harder than ever and come out of the gate with momentum
> Before you cold apply, always find somebody at the company
> Don't DM strangers asking for a referral. Build the relationship first
> Write down your wins every week so you're not scrambling when the search starts
> Take a second to breathe. It's not the end of the world
