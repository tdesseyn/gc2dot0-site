Had a blast joining Taylor Desseyn on Guidance Counselor 2.0 to talk about surviving the job search rollercoaster.

A few things I shared:

> The job search sucks. You can stay positive without faking it, and a small community makes the hard weeks easier
> I measured success with one question: did I do at least one thing today to move the needle in my search?
> Make your LinkedIn headline stop the scroll. "Software developer" alone doesn't
> Coffee chats take time. Don't just ask for one, find something you can actually talk to them about
> Become known for something. My marketing background is what landed my new role
