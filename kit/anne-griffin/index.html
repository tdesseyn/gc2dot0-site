Had so much fun joining Taylor Desseyn on Guidance Counselor 2.0 to talk about what companies won't tell you about your AI experience.

A few things I shared:

> If you're not experimenting with AI on your own time, even if it's not a red flag, it's definitely something that's gonna come to bite you
> Come to interviews with an opinion. What have you built, what tools have you tried, what did you like and what didn't you?
> AI makes it obvious when you're checking boxes instead of working toward an outcome
> Customers care about outcomes, not the tech behind them, so lead with the value you delivered
> It's okay to be frustrated with the market. Channel it into the next thing you need to do
