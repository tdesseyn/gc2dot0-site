about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with victoria stahr: learn how gun.io matches senior developers with contract work through community, onboarding, and personalized profile reviews.

gun.io accepts about 10% of applicants; rejection isn't personal but based on experience, skills, and client fit. spend time entering detailed work history and specific impact in your profile rather than listing generic skills. after approval, you join slack community with weekly office hours, job postings, and learning sessions. the developer relations team advocates for you during hiring by presenting top applicants to clients personally. contract roles typically run three to six months or longer, with some engagements lasting well over a year. start contributing to a 401k early, especially if your employer matches, even if it feels unnecessary when you're young.
