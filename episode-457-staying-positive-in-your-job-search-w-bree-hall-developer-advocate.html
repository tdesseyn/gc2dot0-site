about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with bree hall: how to stay positive through a three-year job search and rejection cycle, then break into tech by shifting mindset, feeling your feelings, and building community.

document interview questions you miss to identify weak areas and update your resume and prep strategy. shift from any job mindset to intentional skill building by researching what technologies are actually in demand. stay positive by doing one small thing daily to move forward and actively feel your feelings before moving on. don't navigate career challenges alone, community and in-person connections combat comparison and imposter syndrome. don't be afraid to ask interviewers questions about the process or to negotiate things beyond salary like remote days or tools. don't search alone, surround yourself with a positive community since comparing yourself to others online can hurt your confidence.

diving into Bree's career and how she stayed positive after not getting her first job for 3 years after graduating.
