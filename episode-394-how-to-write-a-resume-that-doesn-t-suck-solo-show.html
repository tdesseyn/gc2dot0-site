about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to structure and write a resume that gets hiring managers to call you back.

start your professional summary with years of experience matching the job posting's required skills. describe what the company does and your main role, then use action verbs for each bullet point. keep bullet points to one sentence each, be quantitative with numbers and results, avoid walls of text. for contractors with multiple projects, list the client once with each project as a sub-entry with dates. start every bullet point with an action verb and make it quantitative instead of just listing tasks. keep your name and contact info in the header, not buried further down the page. use a 14 to 16 point font for your name, not an oversized font that wastes space. list only email instead of phone number so you can track and manage responses in one place.
