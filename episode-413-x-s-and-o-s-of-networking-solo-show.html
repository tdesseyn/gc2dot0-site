about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to network effectively on linkedin and discord by building genuine relationships around topics instead of asking for jobs directly.

never message someone about a job opening; instead message about a specific topic or skill you're learning. join discord and slack communities relevant to your field and comment consistently to build rapport over time. search for jobs by keyword topic, find people who went to your school or share interests, then reach out about that topic. show up to meetups, live streams, and online spaces regularly; each appearance plants a seed for future connections. if job searching, consider starting a linkedin live or twitter space interviewing people about topics you want to learn.
