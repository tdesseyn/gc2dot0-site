recruiting ama

jun 14, 2021 · 13 min · hosted by taylor desseyn

3 takeaways

  • > ask a recruiter for contract vs direct hire, remote status, and salary range upfront instead of waiting for them to volunteer it.
  • > ask a recruiter if the client is an ats or vms and whether they have direct contact with the hiring manager before investing time.
  • > keep your resume to two or three pages and load it with the specific tech stack for the job you want.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to work effectively with recruiters during your job search and what questions to ask them.

recruiters withhold salary info upfront because their metrics track phone calls, not emails sent. avoid submitting resumes to job postings; instead dm individual recruiters directly at companies. message actual people at target companies asking for a few minutes of time, not cold emails to general inboxes. use the correct technical terminology on your resume even if the job description has it wrong, since you are the professional. to reach someone at a company you want to join, dm an employee directly and ask for a few minutes of their time instead of cold emailing.
