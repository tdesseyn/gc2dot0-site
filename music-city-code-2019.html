about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to work with recruiters effectively, screen them well, and navigate your job search with confidence and market knowledge.

ask recruiters these ten questions about contract roles: placement history, budget approval, position duration, conversion salary, tier status. fill out your dice profile well and keep your resume to two to three pages with quantifiable achievements. your linkedin summary needs three sentences: what you do for non-technical people, primary technologies, entire tech stack. screen recruiters with the two and two rule, two years in the market and two years at their current company. work with only two or three recruiters at a time so they stay locked in step with you and don't price gouge you. ask a recruiter how many people they have placed with a specific team before trusting their contract to hire pitch.
