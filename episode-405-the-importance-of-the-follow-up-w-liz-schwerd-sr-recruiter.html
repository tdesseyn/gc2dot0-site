about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with liz schwerd: follow up with recruiters via linkedin dm after applying, then escalate to hiring managers if no response within a week or two.

aim to move yourself out of purgatory into a yes or no rather than turning rejections into offers. attend industry learning events instead of generic networking events to build genuine connections with people in your field. be prepared with answers to small talk questions and respond in ways that invite further conversation. once a recruiter has actually contacted you, never jump over their head to the hiring manager, that move backfires. when networking feels awkward, pick learning or industry events over pure networking mixers so conversation happens naturally around shared interest.
