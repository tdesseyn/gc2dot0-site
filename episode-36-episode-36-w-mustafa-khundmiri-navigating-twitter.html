about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with mustafa khundmiri: how to use cold email and twitter to build relationships and find jobs by focusing on people over numbers.

stop treating cold email as a numbers game and focus on reaching the right people through research. twitter is better than job boards for job seekers because you can show skills publicly and connect with hiring managers directly. content on twitter matters less than making sure the right people see it, especially company ceos and decision makers. start building relationships with hiring managers months before you need a job so they already know your work. use twitter to showcase your skills publicly since people can start reading your work from day one. follow and engage key hiring people on twitter for months before dming them about a job.
