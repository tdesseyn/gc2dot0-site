about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: practical tips for looking and sounding professional on video interviews, from lighting and camera equipment to background setup.

always turn your camera on for interviews and video calls. it shows engagement and builds rapport with the interviewer. avoid fake virtual backgrounds. they look unnatural and distract from your content during the conversation. keep your camera on by default during interviews and work calls to show you are engaged. set up your interview space with a clean well lit background instead of a messy room. get a 4k or 1080p camera a ring light and a basic usb mic to look and sound better on calls. position your ring light above you instead of directly in your face for better lighting.
