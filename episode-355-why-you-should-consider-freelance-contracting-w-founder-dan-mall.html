about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with Dan Mall: how to build security through freelancing and contracting when facing layoffs and economic uncertainty in tech.

start with personal relationships and referrals rather than cold outreach to find your first clients. get testimonials from early work, even free work, to break objections with future clients. frame your services as essential to clients' business strategy, not as optional extras. get your first freelance clients through friends of friends and overflow work, then trade a great testimonial for future higher paying projects. frame your freelance or contract work as crucial to a client's business, not a nice to have, so it survives budget cuts.
