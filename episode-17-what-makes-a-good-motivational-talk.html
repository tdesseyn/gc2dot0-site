about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: what makes a good new year talk: bring energy, tell personal stories, stay positive, and be direct and concise.

leaders must bring genuine energy to motivational talks or teams won't engage. share personal struggles and mistakes to help employees feel comfortable improving. focus on 2021 positives without dwelling on 2020 difficulties. be direct and specific with messaging instead of vague or long-winded. keep meetings efficient; longer doesn't mean better if you can say it faster. keep new year talks clear and concise since long rambling meetings lose people's attention. bring real energy to a new year pep talk or your team will feel flat and uninspired. share personal stories about your own struggles so employees relate to you as a leader. balance honesty about hard times with genuine positivity instead of dwelling on the past year.
