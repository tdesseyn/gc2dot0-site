about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with stacey smith: why staying at one company for 10+ years can be the right choice, and how to know if your workplace is worth it.

ask about employee tenure during interviews to gauge company stability and culture. distinguish between industry issues you'll face anywhere versus organizational issues worth leaving for. focus on learning early in your career rather than maximizing pay right away. build relationships in person when possible, especially early on, through pair programming and collaboration. don't expect work to fulfill your entire life; invest in family, friends, and activities outside your job. practice answering tell me about yourself since confidence there carries into the rest of the interview.
