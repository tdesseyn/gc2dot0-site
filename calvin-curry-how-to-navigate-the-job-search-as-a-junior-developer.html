about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with calvin curry: how junior developers can stand out in a competitive job market by building portfolio projects, networking strategically, and approaching the job search like recruiting.

connect with alumni from your boot camp on linkedin to get warm introductions to hiring managers and senior developers. stop applying to jobs blindly and instead dm people at companies directly to have conversations before submitting your resume. ask for introductions, mentoring, or connections after every networking call rather than waiting for someone to offer help. spend mornings messaging people and afternoons on calls for about 90 days to build a substantial network. stop mass applying and instead message alumni or employees at target companies to get warm introductions. after any networking call always ask for a next step, like an intro to a manager or another developer.
