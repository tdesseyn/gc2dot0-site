about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: taylor discusses job search strategy, hiring approaches, and remote work culture without a guest.

ask thorough questions during interviews and interview the company as much as they interview you. companies should consider contract to hire and consulting options alongside traditional direct hire roles. move quickly in hiring; companies that move fastest win talent in competitive markets. remote work builds culture differently but effectively; intentional quarterly in-person gatherings help. expect some pay dip when converting from hourly to salary since hourly often covers benefits and pto. building strong culture remotely is possible but it takes real intentionality, not just copying onsite habits.
