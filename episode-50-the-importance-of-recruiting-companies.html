about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: learn why recruiting firms matter beyond just finding jobs, how recruiters access market intel, and what to look for when choosing a recruiter.

recruiters do three things: find talent, provide market intelligence, and manage the hiring process to get candidates over the finish line. good recruiters know what's happening inside companies before the companies themselves do, and use confidentiality to build trust. vet recruiters by checking their pedigree, tenure, online presence, content, and whether you have good chemistry with them. the direct hire market is at historic levels right now as companies recover from last year and hire with new budgets. build relationships with recruiters before you need a job; they can offer resume tips, linkedin guidance, and career navigation beyond just placements. keep a relationship with one or two local recruiters plus a few more outside your area since remote work is here to stay.
