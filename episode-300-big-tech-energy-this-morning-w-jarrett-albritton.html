about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with jarrett albritton: networking and community building matter more than follower count when building a career in tech, and showing up consistently to events and adding value to others creates real opportunities.

attend tech conferences and use referrals over online applications to skip screening and get preferred consideration. find work you enjoy doing first then figure out how to apply it in tech rather than chasing high pay in roles you hate. build community by highlighting others and collaborating across different areas of tech not just talking about yourself. offer something concrete when asking for people's time like coffee or lunch to stand out and show genuine interest. going after series a or b or c startups is an easier way to break into tech sales than competing for roles at the biggest companies. referrals and tech conferences skip steps in the hiring process because a referred resume gets flagged and seen directly by the hiring manager.
