about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: why recruiters need to attend tech conferences in their hometowns to build long-term relationships with engineers and hiring managers.

build relationships online first, then meet in person to deepen connections that actually stick. networking conversations can be simple: introduce yourself, exchange linkedin, stay in touch. use linkedin qr code feature on your phone at conferences to quickly connect with people. play the long game in recruiting by showing up and being present in your community. after meeting someone, connect on their most active social channel right away instead of relying on a business card. keep networking simple, introduce yourself, ask what they do, trade contact info, and move on.
