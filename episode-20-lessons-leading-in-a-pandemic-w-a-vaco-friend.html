about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: leaders should listen more, control less, and prioritize people's wellbeing over task completion in remote work.

notice when team members seem off and pause work to listen to what's really going on. align business goals with where people actually want to go, not just where you direct them. notice when team members start proactively checking in on each other and build that into a habit. pay attention to what people say with their hands and their hearts, not just their words. start meetings by checking in on the person before jumping into tasks. let go of controlling every detail and trust your team at a deeper level.
