about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with chris holland: learn how to build high-performing engineering teams by fixing root causes of software quality through patterns, clean code, and test-driven development.

technical debt from fast coding compounds quickly; fix it through patterns, clean code, and tdd. test-driven development is faster than it seems because it eliminates manual testing and makes refactoring painless. hire for learning potential and eagerness over exact level match; invest in growth through wikis, pair programming, and katas. as a leader, your job is putting others in position to succeed; passion for people matters more than your own engineering skills. test driven development can speed up delivery by cutting out manual steps used to verify code works. moving into leadership means your success depends on your team's success, not just your own technical skill.
