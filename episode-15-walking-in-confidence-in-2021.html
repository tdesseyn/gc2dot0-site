about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to start 2021 with confidence instead of carrying forward 2020's challenges and difficulties.

don't use 2020 as an excuse or play the victim card moving into the new year. have confidence in who you are and what you bring to your work and relationships. let others see your work and abilities instead of holding back due to self-doubt. leave behind the weight of 2020's challenges to avoid torpedoing your success in 2021. be confident in your identity across all roles you play at work and at home. treat challenges as part of the process instead of using them as a reason to stay stuck.
