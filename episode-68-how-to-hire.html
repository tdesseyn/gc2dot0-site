how to hire

3 takeaways

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to structure hiring processes and when to use contract-to-hire versus direct placement.

recruiters find talent; companies are responsible for keeping them long-term. contract-to-hire works better for culture fit and reducing bad hires than direct hire. complete direct hire in two interviews within two weeks or risk losing candidates. spend first 15-20 minutes of interviews selling your opportunity, then 40-45 minutes on candidate. sell the opportunity in the first 15 to 20 minutes of an interview before diving into technical questions. if a company wants a six month guarantee on a hire, push for contract to hire instead of direct hire. contract to hire works as a working interview since six months reveals more than a two hour interview ever could. use contracting to fill specific skill gaps or projects, use consulting when you need someone to manage an entire project for you.
