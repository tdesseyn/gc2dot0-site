about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with jason torres: building real skills and community before landing your first tech job, using soft skills from other careers to stand out.

do the work before you get the job. earn your position through initiative, learning, and proving your value consistently. adapt your communication style to read people quickly. disarm interviewers by making conversations comfortable and leading with questions. when rejected, change your approach instead of repeating the same thing. level up skills that are blocking you from moving forward. find your why and keep going. rejection will pile up, but refocusing on family or personal reasons helps you pick yourself back up.
