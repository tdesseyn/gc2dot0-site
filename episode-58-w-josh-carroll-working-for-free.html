about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with josh carroll: understand how to stand out in hiring by building real projects and putting in consistent extra effort.

free work means extra effort like one hour daily or side projects, not necessarily 40-hour commitments. fancy colored resumes slow hiring managers down; simple, clear resumes with genuine projects work better. for junior devs, focus on communication skills and ownership over complexity; demonstrate you can explain your work. putting in a little extra time consistently compounds over months into a much bigger skill gap than you expect. build one project from start to finish instead of following a tutorial, so you can explain every decision you made. hiring managers sift through hundreds of resumes fast, so a generic bootcamp github project blends in and gets skipped. record a short video walking through your side project and post it on linkedin or twitter to stand out.
