about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with joel lalgee: build your professional brand through daily linkedin content and meaningful networking to stand out to employers and recruiters.

commenting on others' posts is underrated content that helps job seekers build visibility without creating original posts. quality networking beats quantity: focus on deep relationships with a few people rather than applying everywhere. post about problems you solve or personal stories tied to business wins to show value to potential employers. keep networking even after you get a job to build insurance for future job searches and stay top-of-mind. recruiters need digital strategy urgently: content and inbound attraction matter more than cold calling and email outreach now. tie personal stories into business lessons on linkedin, overcoming a struggle resonates more than a resume. treat clubhouse as a way to start conversations, but always move the relationship to a real one on one call.
