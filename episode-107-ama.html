about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: job search basics, polywork features, hiring tips for the holidays, and advice on networking and mock interviews.

invest in a good headshot and consider professional help with resume and linkedin. use polywork badges to find and message people in your target roles or industries. hiring managers should keep recruiting through the holidays to access better talent. request mock interviews and mentorship using specific asks: flattery, admiration, timeframe, and one clear question. plan on roughly five to six weeks from first interview to start date when budgeting your hiring timeline. focus your job search on three basics first a strong resume, a solid linkedin profile, and a good headshot. treat your resume as top of funnel and linkedin as bottom of funnel when marketing yourself. on polywork use badges and dms to directly message people open to specific topics like hiring.
