contracting and contract to hire

jul 30, 2021 · 17 min · hosted by taylor desseyn

3 takeaways

  • > know your full compensation package including base, bonus, pto, and 401k match before comparing it to a contract hourly rate.
  • > ask recruiters to build pto and two weeks of bench pay into long term contracts since they wont offer it unless you ask.
  • > build relationships and create content around the team or department you want to move into for internal transfers.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: understand contract to hire jobs, know your total compensation value, and ask recruiters the right questions to negotiate better terms.

contract jobs are full time work with the same 40 hour weeks, not lower commitment positions. be kind, communicative, and productive on contract to hire to increase chances of conversion to permanent. hiring managers use contract to hire to move faster and bypass hr approval delays on direct hires. bring up your performance reviews and market research on pay when negotiating a contract to hire conversion. being kind, communicative, and reliable on a contract job greatly increases your odds of getting converted to full time.
