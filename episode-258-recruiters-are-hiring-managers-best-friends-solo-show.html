recruiters are hiring managers best friends (solo show)

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: why hiring managers and recruiters should work closely together to hire better talent faster and protect company culture.

when recruiters join team outings and know company culture, they place people who fit long-term and stay. recruiters provide market intel about how your company is perceived and help you adjust retention strategy. working with recruiters under contract protects your people from being poached by that same recruiter. companies that treat recruiters as partners and part of the team hire 10+ people who stick around. when you sign a contract with a recruiter they agree not to poach your employees but that protection goes away if you stop working with them.
