about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with andrew wyborski: how to hire junior developers creatively by getting to know candidates beyond resumes and coding tests, focusing on problem-solving mindset.

look for problem solvers who got tired of doing work the inefficient way and automated it themselves. use coding exams and formal processes to build candidate profiles, not as pass-fail gatekeepers. hiring managers should learn how to learn and teach their teams, or reconsider their approach to hiring. build your network before you need it through local user groups, coffee chats, and meaningful dm conversations. know who you are and be confident in it, then push junior developers into uncomfortable growth situations. build your network before you need it, join local dev meetups and keep a master resume with every skill and project listed.
