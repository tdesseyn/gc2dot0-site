about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: use ai to write personalized dms, cold emails, and cover letters for your job search by finding real people at target companies.

build a company hit list first, then find specific people on linkedin to reach out to directly. download linkedin profiles as pdfs and ask claude to find commonalities between your background and theirs. keep dms and connection request notes short, under 300 characters when possible. use ai to draft messages but edit them to sound like you before sending, not generic templates. find hiring managers and peers at target companies, not just recruiters, for more genuine outreach. paste a job description into ai and ask honestly if it's a good fit for you, including salary, location and scope red flags.
