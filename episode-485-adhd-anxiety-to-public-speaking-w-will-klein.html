about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with will klein: how to overcome anxiety and public speaking fears through meetups, conferences, and deliberate practice to advance your tech career.

start by talking to local meetup organizers about speaking on something you're currently learning. practice your first few slides extensively and do light physical activity like pushups before speaking to manage adrenaline. show your nervousness and vulnerability on stage; audiences root for speakers and find rawness endearing. being able to communicate and articulate ideas in meetings is as important as writing code for career advancement. use physical movement like pushups or a short walk to work through pre-talk adrenaline and nervous energy. apply for roles that feel out of reach since getting rejected still teaches you what you need to learn next.
