about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how recruiters work behind the scenes, why they ghost candidates, managing expectations, and negotiating rates fairly.

recruiters meet 10-15 people weekly and handle hundreds annually, so follow up if you don't hear back. companies pay recruiters, not job seekers, so understand you're one of many candidates they manage. research salary and hourly rates beforehand, be firm on your worth but stay flexible during negotiation. approach job search with empathy and less anxiety; desperation and ego hurt your chances. research your current pay, benefits and market rate before negotiating so you can talk real numbers with a recruiter. be firm on your worth but stay flexible, since being too rigid on rate can cost you an otherwise good opportunity.
