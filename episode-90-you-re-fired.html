about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: focus on building relationships with recruiters and creating strong resume and linkedin before applying to jobs.

invest time in resume and linkedin first, these are the most important job search tools. send job postings to recruiters you know rather than applying directly to increase your chances. treat job search as full-time work and prioritize relationship building over sending out many applications. when you find a good job posting, bcc a few recruiters you trust and ask if they know the company. treat a full time job search as an actual full time job focused on relationships, not just applying.
