about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with david neal: david neal shares his struggles with layoffs, job searching, and finding meaning in work after a difficult year.

take time off during job search to reflect on what actually excites you, not just chase any opportunity. identify companies you'd love to work for even without open roles and reach out directly to them. public speaking and community involvement are career superpowers that create unexpected opportunities. consider seeking counseling or coaching when feeling stuck, especially in high-pressure careers. skills like coding and illustration can be learned through practice and patience. getting involved in public speaking or community work builds skills and connections that can directly lead to future jobs.
