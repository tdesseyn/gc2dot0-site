about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with gabe greenberg: founder shares how prioritizing health and work-life balance actually improves business outcomes and helps attract top talent.

reducing work hours doesn't decrease productivity when you eliminate inefficiency and give people real rest like athletes receive. job descriptions should include video and transparency about company vision to stand out when competing for talent. workaholism is an adrenaline addiction that creates health debt similar to technical debt and needs direct conversation. employees can advocate for balance by framing it as improving their own productivity rather than just personal need. best people at mid-career will leave high pay for companies offering genuine work-life balance and care for their wellbeing. when raising work life balance with a manager, frame it as how you feel rather than what you need. watch for workaholism as a reliance on adrenaline, not just long hours, and address the underlying habit.
