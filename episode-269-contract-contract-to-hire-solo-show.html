contract/contract to hire (solo show)

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: learn how to navigate contract and contract to hire roles, including questions to ask recruiters before accepting positions.

contracting through a recruiter differs from freelancing because you get w2 status and benefits instead of managing taxes solo. ask recruiters how many people they've placed with the company, their relationship with managers, and the conversion salary upfront. contract to hire relationships require high touch communication and ongoing feedback loops from your recruiter throughout the process. your hourly contract rate will typically be higher than the eventual full time salary due to recruiter overhead and burden. companies are using more contract and part time roles now to keep staffing flexible without committing to large salaries. look into hourly contract roles right now since more companies are avoiding big salary commitments and keeping staffing flexible.

Diving into contract/contract to hire topic that so many people don't know how to navigate.
