about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with vitor oliveira: learn phone screen interview preparation strategies including resume alignment, storytelling techniques, and how to answer common interview questions effectively.

job search takes three to six months on average, not weeks. apply in batches and treat failures as learning opportunities. prepare a one-minute introduction highlighting relevant background and career expectations for the specific role. use the star method (situation, task, action, result) to answer behavioral questions with specific metrics. research companies and interviewers thoroughly. mention what you learned about their products or values to create genuine connection. answer tell me about yourself in under two minutes, tie it directly to the role, and find a personal connection with the interviewer.
