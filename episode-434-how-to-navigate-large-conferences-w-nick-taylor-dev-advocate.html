about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with nick taylor: strategies for navigating large conferences like kubecon, from setting specific goals and booking podcast interviews to hosting smaller dinners for deeper connections.

set one to three specific goals before attending, like meeting particular people, rather than trying to talk to everyone at a massive conference. have meaningful two to five minute conversations instead of surface-level handshakes; these create lasting professional relationships. use existing conference infrastructure like podcast booths and speaker dinners to create structured settings for deeper networking. create public content during talks by live tweeting or posting notes; speakers appreciate it and it helps you stand out. invest in high quality conference swag that people actually want to wear, since good merch becomes free promotion. create content from talks you attend and tag the speaker, most speakers will repost or appreciate the shoutout.
