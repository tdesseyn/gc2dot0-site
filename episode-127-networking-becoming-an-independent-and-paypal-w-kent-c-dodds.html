about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with kent c. dodds: how networking, content creation, and demonstrating expertise landed kent c. dodds multiple jobs without traditional applications.

speaking at meetups and conferences led to course opportunities that funded early career decisions. twitter following and public reputation for expertise became better job security than any single role. only job kent applied for was his first; everything after came through people who knew his work. interviewing should show what candidates are best at, not filter for specific buzzwords. open source contributions give free code reviews, demonstrate expertise, and become resume numbers. if an interview process relies on leetcode style questions, ask the recruiter what to expect and prepare with study resources ahead of time. being easy to work with matters more than being the smartest or hardest working person in the room.
