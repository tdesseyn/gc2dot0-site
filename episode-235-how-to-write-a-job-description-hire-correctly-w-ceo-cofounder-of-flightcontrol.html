about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to write job descriptions and design interview processes that attract great candidates and reflect company values.

job descriptions should tell a story about who you are as a company, not just list duties and requirements. include pictures, team bios, and a typical week section so candidates can actually picture themselves in the role. keep hard requirements to the bare minimum to avoid screening out great candidates and underrepresented groups. design application questions that reveal values alignment and signal what your company actually cares about. a strong job description and application process means you can skip screening calls and move straight to substantive interviews. run your job description through a bias checker to catch language that skews too masculine or feminine. design your application questions around values and growth mindset so you get a high signal to noise ratio of good fits.
