about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with andrew coleman: how to network strategically, build your skills publicly, and use referrals to land tech jobs instead of endlessly applying online.

the three c's: code (or your craft), content (document what you learn), connection (network from your audience). focus deeply on one skill rather than chasing shiny objects like learning multiple programming languages at once. connect with people on social before applying; referrals typically guarantee a first round interview. personalize outreach by finding genuine common ground beyond just wanting the job. avoid chasing new languages or frameworks right after a bootcamp, go deep in one skill instead of staying a generalist.

I am joined today by Andrew Coleman who is a React Recruiting Lead at Bitovi! We are going to dive into Andrews job search framework, networking vs applications, and the power of referrals!
