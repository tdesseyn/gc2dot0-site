about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with andrew wyborski: focus on doing simple things well: bring solutions not problems, take initiative, and build your career through consistent effort and real connections.

most of your career opportunities come from genuine relationships and showing up, not formal recruiting channels. bring solutions to problems instead of just complaining about what's wrong. take on work and responsibilities before the title arrives, but only if it aligns with your goals. communicate constantly with your team and manager about progress and needed changes. reuse proven solutions instead of over-engineering; focus on adding real value, not proving how smart you are. keep a background thread running on side projects or goals so you make steady progress without burning out. if you cannot affect change where you are, change your participation there because this is your problem to solve.
