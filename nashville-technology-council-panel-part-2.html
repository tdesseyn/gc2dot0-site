about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: part two of nashville technology council roundtable discussing job search strategies, handling rejection, and navigating today's hiring market.

identify one to three non-negotiables for your job search and be willing to compromise on lower priorities. attend two networking meetings every week and personalize your outreach to show genuine interest, not templates. update your resume for each job posting but stay authentic to your experience to avoid being questioned in interviews. ask recruiters directly about timeline and process to understand when you need to make decisions between multiple offers. junior developers should build personal projects beyond bootcamp demos and network actively to stand out from other candidates. tailor your resume to each posting but stay focused on one to two target roles instead of scattering across many. for junior developers a few personal or free projects can separate you from others who only have bootcamp demo work.
