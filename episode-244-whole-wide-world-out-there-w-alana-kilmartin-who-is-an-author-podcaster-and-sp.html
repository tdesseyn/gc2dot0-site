about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with alana kilmartin: design your career around your life instead of sacrificing life for career progression. explore different work environments, travel, and pursue what actually fulfills you.

try new environments and industries to discover what you enjoy instead of following a prescribed career path. take time away from work for activities that energize you and break you out of career-focused thinking. figure out what you don't want in a career first, then work backward toward what you do want. make intentional choices about what you value and build your life around those priorities, not status symbols. expose yourself to different people and places early in your career to realize more possibilities exist. build in activities outside of work that totally absorb you so you can reset instead of only grinding. figure out what you dont want in your career, since that clarity moves you closer to what you do want.
