about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with pariss athena: learn how a viral twitter post became black tech pipeline, a job board and talent directory for black technologists, plus advice on bootcamps and job searching.

start with passion projects, not business plans. communities and revenue can grow from what you genuinely care about. bootcamps offer equitable education. explore free resources first before spending money on degrees or formal training. rebuild technical skills regularly. staying current with fundamentals like vanilla javascript protects your career long-term. ai tools like chatgpt help with outlines, but always review and modify output yourself before using it. expand your job search beyond big companies. check small startups, local companies, and brands you like for unexpected tech roles. look for tech roles at small companies and consumer brands you already use by checking their careers pages directly. once you land your first tech job focus on getting your foot in the door rather than chasing the biggest salary right away.
