about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with benjamin pyle: how to lead from any level, create psychological safety for your team, and hire for attitude over experience.

fill gaps on your team without waiting for permission or a senior title to lead. celebrate when people bring you bad news—it means they trust you and you can actually help. model the behavior you want to see, give specific praise, and know your people as humans not just workers. hire for aptitude and attitude over technical skills; move fast in hiring and give feedback promptly. when hiring, prioritize aptitude and attitude over exact tech stack experience since good people ramp up fast with solid onboarding. move fast in the hiring process and give candidates feedback quickly instead of making them wait weeks to hear back.

I am joined by CTO, Benjamin Pyle out of Texas. He is CTO of Curantis Solutions out of Texas.
