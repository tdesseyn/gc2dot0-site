about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: a veteran recruiter explains why companies struggle with hiring, how to navigate the application process, and why tailoring your cv for each job still matters in the ai era.

tailor your cv for every job application because hiring managers receive thousands of applicants and may only review the first few hundred. most companies still review cvs manually despite claiming to use ai, due to lack of training, tool fatigue, and risk concerns. bad hires cost companies $40,000 on average and senior hires up to $179,000, which is why fast-growing companies prioritize experience over potential. ai screening tools evaluate whether your cv matches job requirements; put relevant skills in context so the tool recommends you for interview. take a contract role if it helps you grow, since permanent jobs are not more secure anyway. put must-have skills like specific tools on your cv in context so ai screening tools match you correctly.
