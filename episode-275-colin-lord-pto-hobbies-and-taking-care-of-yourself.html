about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with colin lord: how to use pto, hobbies and breaks to stay productive and avoid burnout as an engineer or manager.

recognize two types of walls: quick 15-minute breaks versus week-long disconnects, then act accordingly. communicate stress or life events to your manager so they can help and adjust workload. cultivate honest relationships with your team and manager by sharing struggles, not just successes. pursue hobbies and activities away from screens to solve work problems with fresh perspective. during a job search treat it like a full time job and aim for one solid intentional conversation a day instead of nonstop applying. get comfortable asking for help early since most people do not take advantage of help that is already offered to them.
