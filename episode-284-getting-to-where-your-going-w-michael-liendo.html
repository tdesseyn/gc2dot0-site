about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with Michael Liendo: how life decisions and community shape your tech career, from humble beginnings to senior developer advocate at aws.

your circle of influence matters more than talent alone; find mentors and community early. you don't need a college degree for tech roles like front-end development, but you do need deliberate learning. when pursuing big goals, communicate expectations with your partner about seasons where you'll need to focus hard. you can't force everyone to grow with you; meet people where they are and protect your own mental health. make yourself memorable and known; showing up and being visible opens more doors than staying quiet. job searching right now takes grit, since hiring is tougher than it was when tech was booming. show up and let people know you're in the room, being memorable opens doors that staying quiet never will.

I am joined by Michael Liendo, Sr. Developer Advocate at AWS. We are going to dive into what decisions Michael had to make to get where he is at and what others need to consider!
