why finding your why is important

aug 25, 2021 · 13 min · hosted by taylor desseyn

3 takeaways

  • > write down your why on pen and paper before accepting any job offer.
  • > if an offer comes in fast, tell the company you are flattered but still interviewing and ask for a deadline.
  • > recruiters respect candidates who communicate honestly about continuing their search.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: understand your reasons for job searching before accepting offers, so you don't end up in the same situation again.

journal with pen and paper about what you like, dislike, and want before interviewing anywhere. when an offer comes quickly, ask the company or recruiter how long you have to decide. recruiters respect candidates who are honest about still interviewing and setting timelines. don't stay somewhere just because you're scared of leaving; you'll likely be there only two to five years anyway. know what you want to fix in your next job, like pay or promotion track, before you jump. few people stay at one company for ten years anymore, so dont put too much pressure on a single decision.
