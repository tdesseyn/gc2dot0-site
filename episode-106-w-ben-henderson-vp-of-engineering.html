about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with ben henderson: how to structure interviews around problem-solving, hire flexibly with contractors and part-time roles, and move fast to make offers.

distinguish between management (deliverables/kpis) and leadership (developing people) when moving into supervisory roles. use contract-to-hire and part-time roles to fill gaps quickly while you search for full-time candidates. ask candidates to walk through code they wrote and to analyze your codebase instead of timed coding tests. make hiring decisions in real time during interviews and extend offers within 15 minutes if you have a yes. use warm network connections rather than cold outreach to get noticed by hiring managers.
