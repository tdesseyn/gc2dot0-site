about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to land interviews by networking authentically, using linkedin strategically, and standing out beyond just submitting resumes.

send a note when connecting with people on linkedin to get around dm limits and increase visibility. mass applying resumes doesn't work; focus on finding and researching specific companies and decision makers. for junior developers, build real projects for real people, not just portfolio projects from bootcamp. join discord and slack communities to network and show up to streams where hiring managers and engineers gather. if you're a new developer with no experience, offer to build something for a small business or creator to prove your skills.
