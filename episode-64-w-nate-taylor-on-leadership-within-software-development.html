about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with nate taylor: learn why companies promote great engineers into bad management roles, and how to build technical career tracks that don't require leadership titles.

leadership doesn't require a title; you can lead an epic or project as an individual contributor. create parallel technical tracks with titles like staff engineer or principal so experts stay engaged without managing people. managers should focus on team success, not write code; coding pulls attention away from developing people. start learning software by solving a real problem you care about, build an mvp, then iterate from there. leadership does not require a title, leading an epic or owning a deliverable counts as real leadership experience. before promoting someone into management, sit down and ask if they actually want to manage people instead of assuming. companies often promote their best coders into leadership without checking if they have the aptitude or desire for it.
