about this episode

download claude code on a paid plan and start using it this week to build real intuition about ai capabilities and limits. ai is a multiplier for existing skills; deep technical knowledge helps you catch mistakes and guide ai toward better solutions. build something real for actual users or companies, even small local ones, to stand out and prove you can ship value. expect enterprise adoption to be messy and slow; focus on being a thoughtful engineer who uses ai as a tool, not a replacement. in-person events matter more than ever because hallway conversations and relationship-building are what's hard to automate. build something real and put it online, a live project beats a resume for showing self starter skills and ability to learn new tools. at in person conferences focus on the conversations between talks, that is where real opportunities and connections happen.
