about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with mark techson: how to stop chasing more and find contentment in your career and life by focusing on what actually matters to you.

contentment is hard when you grow up with scarcity, but therapy and reminding yourself of finite time helps shift priorities. don't chase titles or money just because others have them; make sure you want it for your own reasons, not comparison. rest is a skill you have to practice, not lazy; it makes your brain work better and lets you show up for people you care about. devrel is mostly invisible work: debugging, helping developers, communicating. the public facing stuff is only 5-10 percent. small moments with family matter way more than you think; ten minutes of attention from you won't crash your work. to break into devrel, write blogs, contribute on github, help with docs and issues, and focus on serving the developer community. you do not need a big twitter following to get into devrel, since other channels and ways of connecting matter just as much.
