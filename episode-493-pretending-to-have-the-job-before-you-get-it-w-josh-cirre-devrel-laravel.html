about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with Josh Cirre: build your next role by doing the work before you're hired—create content, help your community, and position yourself as if you already have the job you want.

become known for something specific by posting consistently about it, whether technical or not. entry-level roles like support or sales engineering are valid paths to your dream job—don't wait for the perfect title. put things out into the world early and often, even if they feel imperfect or you're new to the topic. balance doing your current job well with building skills and content for your next role outside work hours. find out where your target companies and future coworkers actually hang out online instead of assuming everyone is on linkedin. treat creating content as first round interviewing at scale rather than only applying to job listings.
