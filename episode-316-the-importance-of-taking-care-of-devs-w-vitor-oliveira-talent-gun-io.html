about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with vitor oliveira: how gun.io supports developers through high-touch mentorship, coaching, and soft skills development in freelance work.

soft skills like communication, active listening, and managing up matter as much as technical ability in engineering. gun.io uses coaching and mentorship to identify root causes of project problems and help developers improve. regular one-on-one check-ins with both developers and clients catch issues early and prevent engagement failures. self-awareness and ability to tell your career story clearly matter more than code tests in hiring decisions. building rapport and connections with clients often leads to full-time opportunities for freelancers. when giving feedback or bad news as a freelancer, show up honest and transparent instead of avoiding the conversation. managing up means proactively updating your manager with your progress instead of waiting to be checked on. in coaching sessions, let the other person talk most of the time so you can find the real root cause of a problem.
