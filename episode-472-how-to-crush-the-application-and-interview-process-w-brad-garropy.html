about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with brad garropy: learn how to ace tech interviews and navigate job searches by practicing coding interviews, asking targeted culture questions, and building a strong application strategy.

practice talking while coding to get comfortable thinking out loud during interviews. apply directly on company career pages instead of linkedin to avoid the noise and increase callback rates. front-load applications early in your search, then shift into focused study mode for tech screens and interviews. connect with current employees at target companies to get honest information about workplace culture you won't find in interviews. keep a tracker of every interview contact including shared background details to build rapport fast.
