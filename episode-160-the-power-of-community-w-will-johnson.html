about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with Will Johnson: how building community and helping others got a factory worker into tech without a traditional developer path.

document what you're learning on social media to prove progress and build trust with people in your industry. attend local meetups and volunteer to build relationships with people who can mentor and refer you. networking and building relationships reduces job applications from hundreds to ten with better success rates. speak at meetups and conferences to become a magnet for opportunities and learn deeply about your topic. approach every interaction by asking how you can help others first. speaking at meetups or conferences makes people come to you instead of you chasing connections.

On today's episode I'm joined by Will Johnson, Developer Advocate at Auth0, Instructor at Egghead.io and Speaker/Technical Writer. Will and I got connected through Twitter, and he has been such a connector of people. You won't want to miss this episode as we dive into how he got his first job in tech!
