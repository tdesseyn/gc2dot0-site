about this episode

network with one person a day through genuine conversations, linkedin posts, and coffee chats to compound opportunities over time. research companies and interviewers beforehand, prepare accomplishments on paper, and find commonalities to make interviews feel like conversations. follow up persistently every few days with kind messages and document where you are interviewing to stay organized. use linkedin daily by documenting what you learn, liking and commenting on posts, and tagging people to build visibility. follow up every few days during an interview process since staying top of mind matters more than worrying about annoying someone.
