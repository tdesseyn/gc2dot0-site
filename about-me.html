about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: taylor's background as a tech recruiter and how she shifted from generalist recruiting to focusing solely on software developers.

started at vaco doing national recruiting across multiple industries before narrowing focus to tech. moved to raleigh to work in person with developers after cold calling wasn't working in bay area. got invested in the dot net community in raleigh and decided to focus only on developer recruiting. moved back to nashville to do software developer recruiting exclusively for the last three years. podcast will break down recruiting insights and best practices from her presentations. a recruiter who shifts from cold calling to in person relationships tends to understand candidates better. don't just dismiss recruiters, learn how to use them well since they can actually help your search.
