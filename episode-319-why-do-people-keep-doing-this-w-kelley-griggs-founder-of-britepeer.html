about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with Kelley Griggs: how starting a community can solve your job search problem and build meaningful connections in tech.

create communities around real goals and clear purpose, not just curiosity or hype alone. write about your industry to build connections and opportunities even if you can't network in person. choose in-person meetup venues carefully: good parking, nice atmosphere, not just office spaces with pizza. match people by skills and asks within communities to create one-on-one connections that stick. be a facilitator or connector in your field if you're not naturally outgoing to build your network. pick meetup locations that feel different from an office and make sure parking is easy or people will not show up.
