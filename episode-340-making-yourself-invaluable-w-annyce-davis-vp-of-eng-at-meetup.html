about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with annyce davis: how to become invaluable at work by connecting your technical work to business impact and metrics.

become the subject matter expert people know by name. be the person others go to for specific answers. communicate simply and directly. answer questions concisely first, offer details only if needed. moving from management to individual contributor is not a step back if it fits your life right now. stay current through newsletters, books, and conversations. learn in areas where you're weak. give busy stakeholders a direct yes or no answer first, then offer more detail if they need it. ask your manager how your work moved the needle so you have concrete impact to put on your resume.
