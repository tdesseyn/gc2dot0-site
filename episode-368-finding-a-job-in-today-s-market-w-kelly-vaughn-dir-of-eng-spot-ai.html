about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with Kelly Vaughn: hiring manager insights on job search strategy, interview process efficiency, and what candidates should actually do to stand out.

ask specific questions in dms before or after applying, not requesting meeting time upfront. get company and hiring manager names right, take notes during interviews, and show you care about details. resumes should be 2-3 pages max for senior roles, focused on impact and results not just duties. cut interview processes down, avoid multiple rounds driven by ego, and trust your recruiting process. prioritize mental health during job search, take breaks, and be your best self so stress doesn't show in interviews. always update your resume every quarter or during performance review season so you are ready if you lose your job unexpectedly.
