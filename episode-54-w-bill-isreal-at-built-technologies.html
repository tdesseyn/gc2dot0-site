about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to hire and scale engineering teams by balancing mentorship, personality fit, and attitude over pure technical skills.

juniors need to struggle for an hour before asking for help; don't hand them every answer. in interviews, care way less about junior code quality and way more about enthusiasm and questions. screen for personality fit and team gaps first; you can teach python in weeks but attitude matters most. for junior devs let them struggle for a bit on their own before stepping in to help, but dont let them stall for days. in interviews honesty beats bluffing, say you dont know something and show enthusiasm to learn instead of guessing.
