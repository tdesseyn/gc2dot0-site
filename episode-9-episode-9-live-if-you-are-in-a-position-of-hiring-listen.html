about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: hiring managers should trust recruiters, hire faster, use contract-to-hire, and allow remote work to compete for tech talent.

recruiters do extensive vetting and testing before sending candidates; hiring managers should trust that work. contract-to-hire for six months lets both sides evaluate fit without long-term risk. good developers are employed and getting recruited away; remote work is now expected in tech. companies using multiple recruiting firms defeats the purpose; focus on partnering with one recruiter you trust. requiring on-site work signals weak leadership and cultural problems; remote-first is now competitive. companies requiring full time on site work in tech right now will lose people to fully remote offers elsewhere. if you insist on in office work for your team ask whether that reflects a lack of trust in your leadership.

Hiring is broken. Recruiting is broken. Listen to this to hear my thoughts on both.
