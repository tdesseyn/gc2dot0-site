about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to navigate the job market downturn with practical strategies like intentional networking and staying visible.

combine mass applications with one or two intentional calls daily to connect with real people. post content publicly about what you're learning to build social proof and get discovered. your next job usually comes from your existing network, not cold outreach. ask for specific feedback when rejected to identify patterns and improve your interview approach. post content and engage publicly even as a junior developer so recruiters and hiring managers can find you. if you keep getting rejected, ask interviewers directly for feedback and how many candidates are in the process.
