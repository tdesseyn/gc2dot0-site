how gilad shoham cracked 52 interviews in 30 days

aug 25, 2025 · 23 min · with gilad shoham · hosted by taylor desseyn

3 takeaways

  • > when running many interview processes at once, keep a daily log of questions asked, how it went, and how the company felt to you.
  • > build a weighted scoring spreadsheet of what matters to you (tech stack, company size, product, fundraising) before you start job hunting, not during.
  • > space out interviews with each company even if you have time, so you dont get a contract deadline forcing you to rush everything else.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with gilad shoham: how to run 52 interviews in 30 days and build a world-class distributed team by finding top talent through open source.

track every interview in writing right after and process findings each evening to remember details across many companies. define what matters to you before applying anywhere so emotions don't cloud your choices once offers come. research the product before interviews, find bugs or gaps, and mention them to show you did real homework. find top talent by studying specific open source commits and reach out with personalized messages about their exact work. hire for independence and communication skills in distributed teams and carefully screen for ego to prevent team conflict. when hiring top talent, screen for ego during interviews since even one ego-driven hire can hurt team productivity.
