about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to navigate a slow job market by building savings, networking strategically, and documenting your search on social media.

network with one to two people daily and get referrals before applying, as submitted resumes rarely get seen. document your job search on social media because visibility leads to opportunities and top-of-mind awareness. ask genuine questions in networking conversations and be specific with skip-level managers about workplace problems. aim to have one to two real networking conversations a day during your job search. check for common connections on linkedin before applying so someone can refer or vouch for you. build a savings runway since some folks are taking six to nine months to land their next job. big tech layoffs are happening while startups and high growth companies are still hiring. get plugged into your local startup ecosystem through entrepreneur centers and founder communities.
