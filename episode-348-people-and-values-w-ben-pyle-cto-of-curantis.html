about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with ben pyle: focus on personal values and intrinsic motivation to find meaningful work and build strong teams as a leader.

define your core values and measure decisions against them to avoid incongruence and resentment. learn your people deeply to discover what makes their eyes light up and put them in positions to succeed. invest in yourself consistently through coaching, content creation, and networking regardless of financial circumstances. understand your why before chasing titles or money because you spend more time getting there than being there. find out who you are first because it shapes relationships and your career trajectory more than skills alone.
