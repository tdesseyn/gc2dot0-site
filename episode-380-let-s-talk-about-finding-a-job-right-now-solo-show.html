about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: focus on activity volume and networking quality to speed up your job search in a competitive market.

aim for two meaningful conversations per day, not just applications, to build your network. apply to 10-60 jobs daily, engage on linkedin and twitter, be a discord moderator, and hit your network. your attitude and tone matter more than ever; be pleasant and kind, avoid sounding desperate. build portfolio work by volunteering for local businesses or freelance projects instead of just taking courses. treat job searching like marathon training by mixing networking, applications, social engagement, and outreach every day. offer to build something free for a local business to gain portfolio work and real experience.
