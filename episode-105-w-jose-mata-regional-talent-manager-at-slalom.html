about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with jose mata: learn how agency and internal recruiters differ, what job seekers should know about contract-to-hire roles, and how to evaluate recruiters before trusting them with your career.

internal recruiters are gatekeepers; impress them or you won't reach the hiring manager, so treat every call seriously. put everything on your resume only if you can explain it in detail with business context during interviews. find recruiters who ask what you want and if roles fit your goals, not ones rushing to close one position. for contract-to-hire roles, calculate hourly rate by dividing salary by two, adding $10-15/hour for benefits and pto. if you worked a contract role, put that context on your resume with a short story explaining why it ended.
