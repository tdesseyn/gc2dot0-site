about this episode

remote work needs in-person time too. balance deep work at home with seeing your team regularly. conferences solidify online connections into real relationships. all major leads come through face-to-face networking. online reaches scale but in-person creates depth. use both to build genuine professional relationships. invest in your remote setup if you work from home. good camera, mic, and internet show respect to colleagues. don't stop learning or changing careers. you have 40 years to work, so try new things and build skills over time. when networking or interviewing, give context beyond short answers so people have something to ask follow up questions about. do not be afraid to change career paths, keep learning new skills since most careers last decades not just a few years.
