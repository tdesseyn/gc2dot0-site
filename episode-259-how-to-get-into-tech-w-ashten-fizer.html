about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with ashten fizer: explore non-linear career paths into tech by reframing your existing skills, trying different jobs, and finding work that fulfills you.

bootcamps can work but research them carefully, prioritizing ones with job placement success and company connections. give yourself permission to try different industries and roles before committing to a specific career path. if you hate your job, it's affecting your health and mental performance; making a change is necessary, not optional. find fulfillment by identifying what energizes you outside work, then seek roles or companies where you can do that thing. if you dont know what you want to study dont feel pressured to enroll right away, explore trade school or free online courses first. if you wake up hating your job treat that discomfort as a signal its time to make a change.
