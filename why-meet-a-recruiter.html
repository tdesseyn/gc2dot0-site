about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: why meeting recruiters in person matters more than phone calls when you're job searching in tech.

pick one or two recruiters to work with and meet them face to face in their office. meeting your recruiter helps you know who is representing you and how they work. dress business professional when meeting a recruiter, not gym clothes or casual wear. recruiters have salespeople who get job orders, so meeting the team matters for your opportunities. meet in person with a recruiter instead of relying only on phone calls when job searching. partner with one or two recruiters you trust rather than spreading yourself across many. visit the recruiter's office so you can also meet the team who may handle your opportunities.
