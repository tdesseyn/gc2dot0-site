about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with brian walter: director of engineering shares his 18-month job search journey and how networking and persistence led to his role at republic.

mass applying to jobs online doesn't work; cold outreach has roughly 5% response rate. join a community like never search alone to multiply network and get warm introductions. former connections and colleagues are most likely to surface real opportunities, sometimes years later. send tailored cover letters and thank you notes after every interview, even if you don't hear back. build your network early in your career by having conversations about how people built their paths. cold outreach rarely gets a reply, but a warm introduction from someone who knows and trusts you changes the odds completely. always send a specific thank you note after interviews and keep following up with recruiters and old contacts over many months.
