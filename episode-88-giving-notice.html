giving notice

3 takeaways

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to resign from your job with empathy and professionalism while avoiding common pitfalls.

avoid counteroffers because companies often say they can't afford raises until you leave. don't burn bridges since former managers can help your career and affect your network. document your work and offer to help with transition, possibly through contract work. know and be ready to explain your real reasons for leaving, even if you just got a raise or promotion. approach giving notice with empathy, thank your employer, then stop talking instead of over explaining. be very wary of a counteroffer since it rarely fixes the real reason you want to leave. give more notice the higher up or more technical your role is, with four weeks as a common cap.
