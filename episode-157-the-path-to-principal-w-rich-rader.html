about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with rich rader: what a principal engineer actually is and why companies should create individual contributor tracks to retain experienced developers.

companies retain talent by offering both management and individual contributor paths with equal growth opportunities. structured mentorship programs paired with monthly workshops and clear promotion criteria help people advance intentionally. culture built on rewarding teaching and knowledge sharing over overwork keeps remote teams engaged and connected. healthy retention comes from rewarding teaching and growth, not rewarding people for working unpaid overtime during big launches. building strong remote culture takes intentional effort like regular culture discussions and team activities, not just being in an office.

Joined by my friend, Rich Rader, a Principal Engineer at eviCore Healthcare here in Nashville. We talk about what a principal engineer actually IS and the importance of retention when it comes to implementing leadership tracks.
