about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with ben henderson: how to work effectively with recruiting firms as a hiring manager, from interviewing partners to structuring hybrid hiring approaches.

interview recruiting partners like you'd interview candidates to find someone who listens, digs into details, and understands your actual needs beyond the job description. rewrite job descriptions with personality and transparency about team size, pace, technology stack, and what you're actually building to attract the right fit. mix hiring approaches: direct hire for core team continuity, contractors for project work, and contract-to-hire to let both sides evaluate fit over time. break projects into domains that don't need full domain expertise so you can assign them to contractors and keep your core team focused. rotate team members across projects to build skills, solve new problems, and prevent burnout while keeping people engaged.
