about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: ask recruiters specific questions about contract roles to understand conversion potential, company stability, and your actual earning power.

find out conversion salary ranges for your seniority level since contract rates differ from permanent offers. ask if the recruiter is tier one vendor and how many people they've placed at that company. inquire how long the recruiter has worked with the client to gauge relationship strength. if you lack experience, offer free or volunteer hours to build a portfolio and get references. ask a recruiter how long a contract role has been open since months of vacancy is a red flag. always ask why the position is open, since turnover could be unrelated to the project or performance. ask if the hiring manager has budget to convert contractors to full time and if they have done it before.
