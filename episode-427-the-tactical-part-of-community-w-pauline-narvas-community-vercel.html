about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with pauline narvas: how to build engaged communities through consistent activity, authentic human connection, and strategic in-person meetups instead of large conferences.

create a central hub for community conversations using existing tools like forums, reducing fragmentation across discord, slack, and social media. maintain constant activity with daily live streams, sessions, and content across multiple channels; consistent posting matters more than volume. prioritize meetups over large conferences for better roi and deeper relationships with customers and community members. highlight people behind your brand through live sessions and events featuring customers, team members, and community stories. lead with genuine human connection and empathy; listen to community feedback instead of following rigid playbooks. when picking conferences, go where your actual users already gather, and ask customers directly where they spend their development budget. meetups often beat large conferences for roi because they create direct relationships at a much smaller, cheaper scale.
