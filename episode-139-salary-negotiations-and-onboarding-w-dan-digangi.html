about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with dan digangi: navigate salary negotiations with confidence and grounding, then build strong onboarding by focusing on product understanding and team relationships.

come to negotiations with clear reasoning about your value, not just market data or competing offers. counter offers are normal and expected within reason; companies that pull offers over negotiation signal culture problems. total compensation includes benefits, pto, and mental health; weigh salary against work-life balance and personal goals. new hires should take notes on onboarding pain points and share feedback to improve the process for next person. spend first weeks building one-on-ones with team members and learning where your role fits in company mission. look at the whole compensation picture including benefits, 401k match, and pto, not just base salary. schedule one on ones with teammates early in a new role to build connections and speed up onboarding.
