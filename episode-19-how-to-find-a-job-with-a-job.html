about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: three ways to find a job while working full-time: partner with recruiters, maintain active profiles on job boards, and network 30 minutes daily.

find seasoned recruiters with at least two years experience, then ask them for referrals to other recruiters in your network. log into job boards like indeed and dice every day to stay at the top of recruiter searches from recent activity. spend 30 minutes daily networking on linkedin or twitter by commenting, liking, connecting, and sharing your career journey. stop mass-submitting resumes blindly; instead work with recruiters who know the labor market and can match you properly. create calendar invites for networking and job search tasks to build habits and stay consistent with your search.
