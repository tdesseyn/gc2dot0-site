about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: use claude to clarify what job you actually want by working through prompts about your values, skills, and priorities before applying.

start with a skills audit to understand your background and strongest transferable abilities. write a one-paragraph target role profile to have a specific filter for evaluating job postings. score job descriptions against your profile one to ten and flag yellow or red flags before applying. use claude to find relevant job boards and communities that match your background and role type. ask claude what job boards or niche communities fit your background since most people do not know where to start looking.
