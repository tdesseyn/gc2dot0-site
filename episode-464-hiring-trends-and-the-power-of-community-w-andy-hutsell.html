about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with andy hutsell: hiring trends show core tech skills remain constant while companies face constraints; build genuine relationships with recruiters and invest in community for career longevity.

be curious when contacting recruiters; ask how to stand out rather than just sending resumes. define your career anchors and higher purpose to stay grounded through difficult job market seasons. know your worth independent of job market metrics; if companies don't see your value, that's their loss. community and human connection will never be replaced by technology in recruiting or career growth. when reaching out to a recruiter for the first time, ask how they prefer to interact with talent instead of just asking for a job.
