about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with David McCarter: preparation is critical for tech interviews, and community involvement drives long-term career growth in software engineering.

prepare heavily for interviews by researching the company, using ai to predict questions, and asking targeted questions back. interview the company as much as they interview you to avoid joining the wrong team. good recruiters have been in their role minimum two years and know the local market well. build projects and release them to separate yourself from ai and show real development experience. community involvement through user groups, speaking, and networking directly leads to jobs and career advancement. use ai to generate likely interview questions from a job description, but don't let it write your resume or code from scratch for you. get into the habit of writing and releasing your own small projects regularly, since that builds the practical skills that separate you from ai.
