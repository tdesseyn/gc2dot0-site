about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with jason lengstorf: why companies should invest in storytelling and episodic content instead of traditional conferences and feature-focused marketing.

tell stories about how tools improve lives, not technical specs or features. companies over-index on engineering talent and under-invest in people who can communicate and tell stories. replace expensive conferences with episodic tv-style content dropped weekly to sustain audience engagement. focus on metrics that matter: top of funnel awareness, signups, activation, and paid conversion, not vanity metrics. be a generalist early in your career and try different things before specializing. focus content on the story of who someone becomes by using a tool, not a list of features or specs. pick one clear narrative instead of burying people in technical specs they do not care about. watch out for building brand identity so tightly that changing your rules later feels like betraying your audience.
