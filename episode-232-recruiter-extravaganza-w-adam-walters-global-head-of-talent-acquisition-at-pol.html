about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with adam walters: learn how corporate recruiters think differently than agency recruiters and how to improve your hiring and onboarding process.

corporate recruiting requires thinking about team strategy a year ahead, not just filling seats today. onboarding matters—you cannot rush it, set clear expectations, and manage them throughout the process. your interview process is your first brand impression; provide feedback even to rejected candidates to build goodwill. network before you need something by having genuine conversations with recruiters about their company and work. hiring at smaller companies is surgical and selective; many open roles go unposted so direct outreach to recruiters matters. give direct specific feedback like naming the exact skill gap instead of a vague rejection, it helps candidates and builds goodwill. network with recruiters before you need a job, have a real conversation, stay in touch, and let timing work in your favor later.
