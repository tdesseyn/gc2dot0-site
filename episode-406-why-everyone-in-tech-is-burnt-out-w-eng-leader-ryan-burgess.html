about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with ryan burgess: why burnout is widespread in tech right now and what you can do about it.

companies cutting staff without slowing work means remaining employees take on more load incrementally. remote work blurs boundaries; notifications and messages keep you working past 5pm without realizing it. time blocking your ideal day helps protect space for breaks, walks, and mental recovery. burnout isn't just long hours; it's lack of impact, joy, and connection to your work and team. consider reducing discretionary spending to create financial space for a break if you're exhausted. if you are burnt out in your current job, try setting real boundaries for 30 days before deciding to quit. journaling can help you realize you may not be as stressed as you think once you write down the reasons why.
