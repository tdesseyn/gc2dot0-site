how to negotiate an offer

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: learn how to negotiate a job offer by keeping quiet, asking questions, and always providing reasons for your requests.

stay quiet after making your ask and let the employer speak first during negotiations. provide research-backed reasons when counter-offering, not just a desired number. negotiate during promotions too, and have trusted people review your offer before deciding. if you counter an offer, always give a clear why backed by research like glassdoor data. confirm your salary range is dependent on seeing the full benefits package before locking in a number. when you get an offer, keep your mouth shut and let the hiring manager keep talking. ask detailed questions about pay schedule, bonus structure and benefits before responding to an offer. always ask for time to review an offer instead of deciding on the spot.
