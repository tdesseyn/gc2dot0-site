about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with hank washington: how to land freelance opportunities by building genuine relationships, showing your work consistently, and solving problems rather than selling services.

diagnose before prescribing: ask high value questions to understand client problems instead of pushing solutions immediately. meet people where they are: use linkedin for b2b creative work, not instagram, and respond quickly to opportunities when they appear. build a clear accessible body of work: make it easy for potential clients to see your portfolio and contact you with one click. be a connector and listener: opportunities often come from genuine relationships and connecting others, not direct sales pitches. sell time and communication, not just skills: your ability to talk through work and provide context matters as much as the creative itself. be easy to reach with fast dm response since opportunities can disappear as quickly as they appear.
