about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with Dan Digangi: hiring manager shares insights on job searching, resume writing, and navigating a competitive tech market with practical advice.

job market is saturated with layoff talent, requiring more grinding and strategic career moves. moving between ic and manager roles laterally is normal, not failure if it fits your goals. use ai tools for research, resume writing, and productivity without letting them replace actual work. go deep on skills instead of staying surface-level to avoid becoming a generalist early career. use ai tools to research companies and competitors before interviews, not just to write for you. read your resume or important writing out loud before sending it, it helps you catch what doesn't flow.
