about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with harjis deksnis: how curiosity and genuine interest matter more than experience when starting a tech career, and why human connection defines engineering leadership.

genuine curiosity about how things work beats having perfect experience before your first tech job. communication skills and ability to learn matter more to hiring managers than checking every box on a job description. building real relationships with your team through vulnerability and regular one-on-ones is what makes you an effective leader. attending conferences gives you inspiration and fresh perspective even if you don't learn specific technical details. prepare for interviews by researching the company and asking clarifying questions about what level of detail the interviewer wants. when interviewing, turn short answers into a real conversation and ask the interviewer what kind of depth they want. go to industry conferences even if you don't learn much on the spot, since the inspiration and connections pay off later.
