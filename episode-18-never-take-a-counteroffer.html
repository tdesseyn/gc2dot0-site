never take a counteroffer

feb 12, 2021 · 12 min · hosted by taylor desseyn

3 takeaways

  • > write down why you are job searching before you talk to your manager so counteroffers don't shake your resolve.
  • > once your employer knows you are looking, they often plan to replace you at a lower cost later.
  • > treat a new job offer like it matches your current salary unless money is truly the reason you are leaving.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: understand why you're leaving your job before accepting a counteroffer, and don't let money alone change your decision.

write down your real reasons for leaving before you give notice or accept a counteroffer. evaluate job offers as if salaries were equal to see if you actually want the role. only accept one counteroffer negotiation round; multiple asks will likely kill the offer. research your salary band and use your community to know fair market rates before negotiating. one round of asking for more after an offer is fine, but asking repeatedly risks losing the offer. write down why you are job searching before you talk to your manager so counteroffers don't shake your resolve. once your employer knows you are looking, they often plan to replace you at a lower cost later. treat a new job offer like it matches your current salary unless money is truly the reason you are leaving.
