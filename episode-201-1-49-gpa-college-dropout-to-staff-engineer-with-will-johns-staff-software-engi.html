about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with will johns: college dropout turned staff engineer shares how to break into tech without traditional credentials through networking, open source, and consistent skill-building.

networking and staying open to opportunities matters more than where you find them. update your resume every three months and bold keywords relevant to each job application. imposter syndrome affects everyone in tech; struggle doesn't mean you're doing it wrong. keep a master resume in a doc with a skills section rated by proficiency, then bold the keywords relevant to each job before exporting it as a pdf. jot down a quick note each day on what you learned or worked on, then roll it up weekly and monthly to track growth and prep for reviews or interviews.
