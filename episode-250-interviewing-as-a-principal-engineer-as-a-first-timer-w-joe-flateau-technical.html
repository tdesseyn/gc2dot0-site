about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with Joe Flateau: after 12 years at one company, a staff engineer navigates interviewing as a principal engineer for the first time and learns he has to actively sell himself.

shift from saying team did this to i did this when describing accomplishments in interviews. customize your resume for each job by highlighting relevant skills matching the job description. ask for referrals before applying to jobs so the referrer can advocate for you directly. keep cover letters to four to five sentences focused on specific job requirements not generic text. view yourself as a business selling your skills rather than just doing good work and waiting for offers.
