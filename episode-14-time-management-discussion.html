about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: practical time management and productivity strategies for staying organized with calendar, to-do lists, and email workflows.

block out dedicated calendar time for your to-do list to ensure tasks actually get done instead of sitting ignored. use voice memos to respond to emails faster, especially for longer messages like resume reviews. use multiple tools strategically: outlook to-dos for workflows, apple watch for quick reminders, and one note-taking method that works for you. flag and organize emails instead of trying to achieve inbox zero when volume is high, to keep important messages visible. use color coded flags on emails to mark what needs a response, a follow up, or can wait.
