about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with chris demars: exploring whether leaving tech is worth it after layoffs, failed roles, and the brutal job search that nearly led to homelessness.

apply to over 100 jobs if needed and schedule interviews aggressively, even early morning or multiple per day. build genuine community trust and reputation over time rather than expecting immediate hiring based on credentials alone. timing matters for both when you apply and what the company is experiencing financially and strategically. stay consistent with your personal brand and be direct and upfront about what you do and the value you bring. build a clear personal brand so people know what to expect from you at conferences and in interviews. timing matters as much as skill, since hiring speed depends on company budgets, fiscal calendars and holidays.
