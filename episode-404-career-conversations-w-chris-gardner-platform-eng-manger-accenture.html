about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with chris gardner: how an engineering leader with 30 years in tech moved from junior programmer to manager through relationships, reputation, and willingness to do the work before getting the title.

your network matters more than blind applications, but blind applying can work if you send hundreds out. show your worth by being reliable, asking good questions, and treating teammates well—not just coding skills. get promoted by doing the job you want before you get the title, not by waiting around. management means less hands-on coding but more teaching and building high-performing teams. take career setbacks in stride, side projects keep you sharp, and keep moving forward even after layoffs. treating teammates well and asking good questions builds a reputation that gets you referred into new roles. moving into management means stepping back from hands-on coding to focus on unblocking and developing your team.
