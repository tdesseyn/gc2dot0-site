about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with jake shepherd and daniel thompson: prepare for job search by updating your resume, learning new skills, working with recruiters who have deep market knowledge, and positioning yourself to move quickly when opportunities arise.

update resume with quantifiable achievements and technical specifics so both recruiters and hiring managers understand your impact. build side projects and contribute to github to demonstrate adaptability and learning in emerging technologies. work with recruiters who have been at their company for at least two years and maintain long-term client relationships. know your job priorities before searching so you can evaluate offers beyond salary, like company culture and team fit. show how you adapted and problem-solved during crises, not just hard skills, since companies want resilient employees. work with more than one recruiter and favor ones who have stayed at their company for several years.
