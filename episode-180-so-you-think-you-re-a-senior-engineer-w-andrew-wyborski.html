about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with andrew wyborski: what makes a senior engineer and how to demonstrate engineering skills versus just writing code in a competitive job market.

senior engineers impact groups and teams, not just deliver individual tasks; focus on leadership and growing others. document your work weekly showing business results, not story points; translate technical work into business value. developers are overvalued right now; true engineers who solve problems and adapt are undervalued. hire for tomorrow by investing in your best people and understanding retention strategy, not just acquisition. ask why five times on job requirements like degrees; validate assumptions about what candidates actually need to succeed. keep a running log of your wins in cause, action, result format so review time and resume updates are easy. ask hiring managers why a requirement like a degree exists so you can judge if it is a real need or just a checkbox.

In today's episode, I'm joined by Andrew Wyborski, Advanced Software Engineering Manager, Mobile and Web Technology at Kroger. We dive into what makes a senior engineer and some other job market conversations!
