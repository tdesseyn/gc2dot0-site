about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to work with recruiters, find them, and understand agency versus corporate recruiting roles.

search linkedin for technical recruiters in your city and dm them, checking their profiles for personality fit. text or email recruiters never call since they are always on calls or zoom meetings. make friends with corporate in-house recruiters who have deeper company knowledge and can move slower than commission-based agency recruiters. text or email recruiters instead of calling since most recruiters are on back to back calls all day and rarely pick up. know the two types of recruiters agency recruiters work with many companies while corporate recruiters work in house for just one. build a list of around ten agency recruiters you like and check in with them monthly instead of waiting on just one. use the two and two rule when picking a recruiter to trust look for at least two years in the industry and two years at their current company.
