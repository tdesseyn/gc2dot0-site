about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with jossie haines: how to build inclusive cultures in tech through hiring practices, interview feedback, and retention strategies.

structure interviews around competencies with scorecards instead of subjective ratings. ask about inclusive culture between offer and acceptance when you have negotiating power. tailor your resume language to match job descriptions using same terminology hiring managers use. build structured interview scorecards based on specific competencies instead of vague one to four ratings. focus on inclusion, not just diversity, or you'll keep losing the talent you hire. review job descriptions for gendered words like aggressive or rock star that can turn off women applicants. write impact based job descriptions that list 30 60 and 90 day goals instead of a long list of requirements.

I am joined today by Jossie Haines who recently left Tile as their VP of Engineering & Head of DEI and now has started her own company!
