recruiter processes and testing

jun 11, 2021 · 15 min · hosted by taylor desseyn

3 takeaways

  • > expect recruiters to test you and ask for references before submitting you to a client.
  • > always ask your recruiter to share the actual test results with you, not just a pass or fail.
  • > if you bomb a skills test, ask the recruiter to let you retake it rather than giving up.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how recruiters use testing and references to improve your chances with hiring managers and why candidates should participate.

if you bomb a test, ask the recruiter to let you take it again to get familiar with the format. free work like open source contributions or volunteer projects build portfolio experience and look good on resumes. be intentional about your job search by treating every conversation and application as strategic. stick with a small handful of recruiters you trust instead of testing with every recruiter you meet. while in school or bootcamp, do free work for your network to gain real experience for your resume.
