about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with genesis bonds: learn strategies for breaking into faang companies through internships, bootcamp selection, interview preparation, and persistent networking efforts.

choose bootcamps where the program shares risk in your success, not ones requiring large upfront loans. practice coding on paper and whiteboard, not in an ide, to prepare for technical interviews. study the specific interview style of each company since google and facebook have different approaches. treat rejection as circumstantial rather than personal and keep applying across many companies. dont let one rejection discourage you, a no from one company just means it wasnt the right fit, not a reflection of your worth.

In this episode I chat with Genesis Bond! She is a Software Engineer at Facebook/Meta, and we talk about how to improve your job search especially around breaking into FAANG.
