about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with ted neward: how hiring managers get constrained by systems, why networking beats applications, and how to build junior developer teams with mentorship.

job descriptions often ask for specific tech stacks like a menu order, but good hiring looks at depth and potential to learn. your degree doesn't determine your tech career; international relations prepared ted better than a cs degree would have. networking and building your tribe matters more than applying online; ted's best jobs came through people he knew. devrel blends tech and non-tech skills like marketing and writing to connect developers inside and outside your company. hiring juniors works if you add mentorship, psychological safety, book clubs, and senior team leads to support their growth. don't take job rejection personally since most of the time it comes down to fit or circumstances outside your control. when hiring juniors, pair them with a strong mentor, build in psychological safety, and invest time before expecting full productivity.
