about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with justin hoover: how to build hiring processes that attract good candidates and protect your company's reputation through streamlined, intentional interview steps.

whiteboarding and arbitrary coding challenges don't reflect real work and create unnecessary stress for candidates. screen for three pillars: will they relieve stress, can they actually do the job, and do they persist through challenges. keep interview processes to five rounds or fewer and move quickly; candidates leave if timelines drag. treat every candidate with respect because rejected candidates talk about your process; reputation affects future hiring. have a small cross section of the team interview together so they can build on each others questions and judge culture fit. be transparent with candidates at every step since a cheap or dishonest interview process damages your reputation and scares off good candidates.
