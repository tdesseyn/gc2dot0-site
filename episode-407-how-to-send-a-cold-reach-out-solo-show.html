about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to write cold dms that get responses by being specific, short, and direct about what you want.

go for the ask immediately instead of back and forth messaging. research their profile for common interests or connections before reaching out. follow up multiple times if you don't hear back right away. follow up multiple times since people are busy and rarely respond on the first try. check your current network first before cold messaging strangers since most jobs come through people you know. always submit your resume to the job before dming the hiring manager so you can reference it. keep cold dms short and specific, state who you are, your fit, and the clear ask.
