about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: where to post your resume and whether linkedin matters when job searching in tech and other fields.

use dice if you work in it because it has less noise than general job boards like career builder. post your resume on multiple job boards to increase visibility but be aware you may get many recruiter calls. linkedin is worth having with a professional photo and a two sentence summary describing your job simply plus your tech skills. write your linkedin summary as if explaining your job to your grandmother to stay concise and searchable. if you work in it, put your resume on dice since only it professionals use it, which cuts down on noise. know that recruiters search by last activity, so logging into dice to edit your resume can trigger calls even if you are not job hunting.

After you write your resume many people wonder what's next? In this episode I talk about what job boards to use and if Linkedin is even worth it.
