navigating contract to hire pt. 1

3 takeaways

  • > ask if the contract position has a history of converting to full time or has that opportunity.
  • > work with a recruiting firm that has been around for at least two years since turnover creates bad handoffs.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: ask recruiters about their experience with managers and companies before accepting contract to hire roles.

contract positions are typically full time 40 hours per week as a w2 employee of the recruiting company. confirm the recruiting firm offers benefits or provides other options for finding them. contract to hire is similar to permanent employment, not freelance 1099 work. confirm you are a w2 employee of the recruiting firm, not a 1099 freelancer, when contracting. ask recruiters how many people they have placed with this specific manager before taking a contract role. ask if the contract position has a history of converting to full time or has that opportunity. work with a recruiting firm that has been around for at least two years since turnover creates bad handoffs.
