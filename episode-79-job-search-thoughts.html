job search thoughts

3 takeaways

  • > before an interview write down specific accomplishments tied to the exact role so you do not blank when asked.
  • > treat interviews as a dialogue and ask your questions during the process instead of saving them for after an offer.
  • > when asked about salary expectations give a straight answer with a no brainer number and a real minimum instead of dodging.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to approach job search, interviewing, salary negotiation, and networking with hiring managers.

research the company and people before interviews, write down relevant accomplishments, and ask questions during the conversation. avoid quick apply buttons; instead connect with engineering managers, directors and vps on linkedin to start conversations. when discussing salary, do market research, know your minimum acceptable number, and mention you can accept today if the offer meets your needs. ask hiring managers about culture, why the position is open, and issues they've faced to understand what you're walking into. most hiring managers lack frameworks for interviews, so come prepared with your own questions to assess company fit. when messaging a hiring manager ask for a few minutes to learn about the company instead of asking about a specific open role.
