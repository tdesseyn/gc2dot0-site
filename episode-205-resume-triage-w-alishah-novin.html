about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with alishah novin: how hiring managers and recruiters triage resumes in seconds, and how to structure yours to get called immediately.

put your most important info first: summary, technical skills, then experience. save education and certs for lower down. keep your first page strong and captivating. hiring managers make snap decisions so structure matters as much as content. describe projects by stating the problem you solved, the tech used, and the result. include all languages and tools you touched. formatting and clarity affect how fast a hiring manager can understand you. messy layouts make them skip to the next candidate. keep formatting simple and consistent since cluttered layouts, dual columns, and mixed fonts make hiring managers pass even on strong candidates. if you lack paid experience, list self-study and side projects with the tech stack and the problem you were solving to show how you think.
