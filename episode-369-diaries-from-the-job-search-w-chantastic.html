about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to navigate a tough job market by building savings, networking strategically, and documenting your search on social media.

network actively with recruiters and people in your field, most jobs come through referrals. document your job search on twitter or linkedin, visibility leads to opportunities faster. attend in-person meetups and get involved in startup communities, big tech hiring slowed but startups still hiring. ask genuine questions in networking conversations instead of asking for favors to avoid seeming salesy. aim to have one to two real conversations a day during your search instead of just applying online. before you apply check linkedin for mutual connections at the company and ask them for insight, not a reference, unless they truly know you.
