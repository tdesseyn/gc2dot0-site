about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with chris spintzyk: how to navigate job search effectively by building recruiter relationships and avoiding online applications.

identify 10 companies you actually want to work for, then reverse engineer how to get in the door. avoid applying online to job postings as your main strategy since applications often disappear into black holes. treat recruiters like agents who advocate for your career growth, not just job placement. follow up with recruiters about your situation changes and interest in specific companies. work with two to three recruiters at most to get a well rounded view of the market without creating chaos. stay in touch with your recruiter and update them on changes since the relationship works best as a two way street.

Chris Spintzyk is an industry veteran and brings a ton of insight in how you should approach the job search. I hope you enjoy this episode! If you want to reach 'Spin' email him at spin@vaco.com
