about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with peter bell: how ai agents are reshaping dev roles, from headcount changes to new career paths like forward deployed engineers.

forward deployed engineers blend coding with business analysis, sitting with users to redesign processes. prove your capabilities through shipping code and building skills, not just resume credentials. deep industry knowledge becomes your competitive edge as ai commoditizes generic coding skills. to stand out in a job search, build a small project for a specific hiring manager and reach out directly instead of only applying online. pick a narrow niche at the intersection of two topics you care about so you become known for something specific rather than a generic developer.
