about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: use ai to write your resume by asking targeted questions, pressure testing against job descriptions, and auditing for ats compatibility.

start resume-building by asking ai what information matters most for your target role, not by writing from scratch. pressure test your resume against job descriptions and ats systems to find gaps before applying. include concrete numbers, metrics, and business impact rather than vague accomplishments. upload a resume template and job description to ai to get a diagnosis of what's working and what's missing. use dictation tools to speed up answering detailed questions about your background and achievements. use a project or dedicated workspace when job searching so ai keeps context and memory across your resume work. run an ats pressure check prompt on your resume to catch empty skills sections and missing keywords before you apply.
