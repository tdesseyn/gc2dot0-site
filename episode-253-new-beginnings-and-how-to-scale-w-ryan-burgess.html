about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with Ryan Burgess: how to build and scale new teams from scratch, create buy-in with inherited engineers, and navigate career growth through ambiguity and imposter syndrome.

document your proposal clearly before pitching; use google docs or loom videos to get async feedback and build alignment with leadership. spend months investigating and learning from existing teams before prescribing solutions; show up with questions, not all the answers. imposter syndrome signals you're growing and pushing limits; use it as a check-in tool rather than letting it paralyze you. as an individual contributor during reorgs, ask why decisions were made, show up with positivity, and offer concrete help to your manager. when a company reorganizes, ask your manager and even skip level leaders why it happened and how you can help, since context matters more than control. to judge if a company is scaling responsibly, ask whether an open role is a backfill and why the previous person left.
