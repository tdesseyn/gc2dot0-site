about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: why taking time off matters for your work performance and mental health, and how to push back against hustle culture.

if things feel off at home, work will suffer too; take time off to recharge and protect your long-term health. notice when you're drained or stressed as a signal you need a break; burnout in the short term hurts you long term. a workplace that frowns on taking time off is a sign of poor culture worth moving on from. notice when you feel drained or unsettled at work since that is a signal you need time off. track your pto usage and push yourself to actually take the time off you have earned. unlimited pto policies can backfire since people often take less time off without clear guidelines. if you are a 1099 or w2 contractor build time off into your hourly rate since you have no paid pto.
