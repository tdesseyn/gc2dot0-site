about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with deividi silva: how gun.io connects senior engineers with companies through community and support, not just matching talent.

gun.io focuses on senior engineers because clients need proven expertise from people with measurable impact in past roles. the onboarding process emphasizes communication skills and career discussion over coding tests to find contractors who can manage client relationships. talent success management keeps you supported throughout freelance contracts with regular check-ins and technical guidance from experienced mentors. the dev rel team spans time zones with former managers and ctos who can help with specific tech stacks and career coaching. being curious and asking questions without fear is key to navigating your career and learning from leaders in the field. going freelance does not mean going alone, ongoing support from a team can help you navigate tough calls and trade offs on client projects. stay curious and keep asking questions throughout your career, looking at the data yourself instead of being afraid to look uninformed.
