about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with chris demars: accessibility engineer chris demars discusses his nine-month job search, the importance of developer relations, mental health through hobbies, and career advice for pursuing tech early.

developer relations needs multiple specialists per language or sdk, not just one generalist covering everything. fishing, paintball, dirt bikes and community involvement help manage health anxiety and prevent tech burnout. start coding and pursuing tech careers early instead of chasing fast cash in other jobs first. interviews are two-way streets; ask interviewers questions to learn about the company and role. developer advocates reach individual developers at community events, not decision makers at product events. use erp (exposure and response therapy) style exposure and hobbies like fishing or paintball to manage work related anxiety and burnout. when prepping for a tech interview find role specific question lists on github and bring your own questions since interviews go both ways.
