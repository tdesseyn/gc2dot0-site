about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with nate taylor: how to hire the right engineering talent by starting with your team's actual needs, writing focused job descriptions, and using consistent interview questions tied to your values.

define team gaps using spider charts before writing job descriptions to hire for missing skills, not duplicates. narrow job descriptions to three core needs instead of thirty nice-to-haves so qualified candidates apply and screening gets easier. write interview questions before seeing resumes, with examples of bad, good, and great answers to compare candidates fairly. each interview question should tie back to your team values and principles to determine cultural and operational fit. partner with talent teams on respectful rejections because candidate experience becomes part of your employer brand. write interview questions and example good and bad answers ahead of time so every candidate is judged consistently. reject candidates quickly instead of dragging out rounds since candidates value a fast respectful no.
