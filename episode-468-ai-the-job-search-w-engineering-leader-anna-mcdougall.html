about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with anna mcdougall: how engineering leaders can fairly assess candidates using ai tools while maintaining psychological safety and understanding their critical thinking.

treat ai as a normal part of developer workflow in interviews, not something to fight against or police. pair programming style interviews let you see how candidates think, not just if they can code. spend first 15 minutes building psychological safety so candidates can perform at their best. assess whether candidates understand why they accepted or rejected ai suggestions, not just if they finished. start learning one ai tool now rather than trying everything; theres still time to catch up without panic. in technical interviews let candidates use whatever tools they normally would, including ai, to mimic a real first week on the job. the real signal in interviews is whether a candidate can explain and justify what the ai helped them build, not just whether they finished the task.
