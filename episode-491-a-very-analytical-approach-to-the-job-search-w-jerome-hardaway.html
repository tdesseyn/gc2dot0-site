about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with jerome hardaway: how to run a data-driven job search: break your resume into focused versions, use dice for recruiter reach, and treat the search like a strategic problem.

keep your resume to ten years maximum; five to ten years is the sweet spot to avoid ageism flags. create separate specialized resumes for different career tracks instead of one master resume. use dice to be discoverable by recruiters; log in every twenty four hours to stay visible in searches. build your linkedin presence by sharing knowledge on your target topics to signal expertise. approach job search like coding: use systems, plan ahead, and test what works before scaling. keep your resume active on niche job boards and log in daily since staying visible can get you found by recruiters for roles you never applied to. build real working projects you can fully explain in detail since being able to talk through decisions matters more than having a finished template.
