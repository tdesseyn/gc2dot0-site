about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: learn how to network effectively on linkedin through posting daily content, engaging with others' posts, and sending thoughtful direct messages to build your professional network.

post once daily documenting your work, struggles, wins, and insights to become known for something specific. craft personalized dms with admiration, specific asks, and three sentences max when reaching out to strangers. start networking with alumni from your college or bootcamp using shared connections as warm leads. research someone's profile and activity before messaging to reference something specific about them. search your school or bootcamp alumni on linkedin and filter by city to find warm contacts who are more likely to respond and help with your job search.
