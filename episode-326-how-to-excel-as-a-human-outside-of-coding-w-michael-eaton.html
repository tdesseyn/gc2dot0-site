about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with michael eaton: how to excel as a developer by mastering five skills that center on being a better human, not just writing better code.

write everything down—decisions, ideas, learnings—to externalize your memory and help teammates when you leave. learn how to learn by understanding how your brain actually works, not just cramming or memorizing. prioritize what matters by protecting time for sleep, reading, and growth even if it's ten minutes between meetings. share knowledge through blogging, team talks, or internal posts to improve communication and reinforce what you learn. lose your ego early in your career by staying curious about what you don't know and leading others ahead of yourself. use ai tools like github copilot as a helper similar to an intern, but double check outputs since they can be wrong.
