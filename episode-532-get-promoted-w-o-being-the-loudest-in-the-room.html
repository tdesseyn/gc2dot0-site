about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how introverts can advance their careers by building visibility and influence without changing who they are.

do one thing out of your comfort zone to build confidence through action, not a massive leap. ask thoughtful questions and listen in meetings instead of trying to speak more to contribute meaningfully. network by having one-on-one conversations with people you know first, then branch out with prepared questions. prepare your accomplishments and use strong verbs to frame impact, then share them without waiting for others to notice. at networking events set a small goal like talking to three people and prepare a few genuine questions ahead of time instead of winging it. keep a running list of your wins, sometimes called a dig list, so you have real examples ready for reviews and promotion conversations.
