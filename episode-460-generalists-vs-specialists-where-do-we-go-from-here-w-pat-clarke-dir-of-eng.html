about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with pat clark: companies now want specialists over generalists, but learn languages and fundamentals over frameworks to stay adaptable and valuable.

learn a language or core skill, not just a framework like react, so you can adapt when tech changes. curious people who context switch between languages are most hireable right now, especially for legacy tech. networking gets you hired more than perfect credentials; genuine relationships and comfortability matter. break things, make mistakes, and tinker on your own time to learn faster without fearing imperfection. past jobs in food service or retail teach context switching and communication skills that matter in tech. build a secondary learning community of people whose content matches how you learn, not just courses.

we are hanging out with director of engineering pat clarke to talk about the shift from the generalists trend that has occured the last few years so what seems like now companies wanting specialists.
