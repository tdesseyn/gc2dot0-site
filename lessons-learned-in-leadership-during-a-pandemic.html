about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with kyle allen: leaders navigating covid learned that vulnerability, listening, and empathy matter more than perfect systems or constant communication.

too many meetings and calls exhaust teams; listen to feedback and simplify communication structures. vulnerability from leaders builds trust slowly; owning mistakes and admitting wrong calls shows people you're human. your words carry weight as a leader whether you realize it or not; be intentional about what you say. redefine what success looks like during crisis; ask questions about priorities instead of demanding old metrics. give feedback by sharing both sides of a situation so the other person does not go on the defense.
