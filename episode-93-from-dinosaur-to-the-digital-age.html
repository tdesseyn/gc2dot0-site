about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how the recruiting industry changed over 25 years and what job seekers and hiring managers should know today.

good recruiters should push back on you and be honest about what they can do, not just say yes to everything. job market is moving fast but that doesn't mean you have to be frantic; think through your career moves carefully. don't chase money alone or take high-risk opportunities without knowing the company; align with key advisors. less formality in business now is good for authenticity but some professionalism and respect still matters. be honest about expectations and capabilities on both the candidate and client side. the job market is moving fast so slow down, think through your plan, and avoid chasing money blindly.
