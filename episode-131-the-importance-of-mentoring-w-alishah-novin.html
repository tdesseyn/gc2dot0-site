about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with alishah novin: how to build effective mentor-mentee relationships, what makes them work, and why companies should support them.

mentees must drive the relationship by being specific about what they need using situation, ask, and background format. keep initial outreach to three sentences max and be clear about your ask so busy people can quickly decide if they can help. mentors guide conversation but mentees must be transparent and open for the relationship to get to real issues. companies benefit from internal mentoring programs outside the chain of command, giving employees growth opportunities and mental health support. use the what else question repeatedly in mentoring conversations to get past surface answers and uncover real issues. companies should avoid pairing people with their direct manager as a mentor since that creates bias and limits honest feedback.
