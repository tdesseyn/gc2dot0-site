about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with christiaan heijmen: how to network intentionally, ace interviews with basics, and find mentors who match your career goals.

show up five minutes early with working tech, clean background, and genuine thanks to remove barriers. use recruiters as practice partners; ask them if your explanations make sense and adjust in real time. connect people to each other instead of staying in the middle; bigger slice of bigger pie beats whole small pie. engage on linkedin with genuine comments on wins and updates to stay relevant with your network. find mentors who walked the path you want, then learn from their whole life not just their job. build networking habits by connecting people to each other instead of staying the only link between them. bring your authentic energy to interviews since most of the impression comes from nonverbal communication.
