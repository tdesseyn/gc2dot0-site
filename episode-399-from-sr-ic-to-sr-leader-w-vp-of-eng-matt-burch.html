about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with matt burch: a vp of engineering shares five key practices for transitioning from senior individual contributor to senior leader at startups.

find a mentor outside your job to give unfiltered advice before tough situations arise. master brief communication and early feedback to build trust with your team and leadership. limit hands-on coding to around 50% of your time so you can focus on enabling your team. clarify expectations with your boss upfront instead of assuming how things will work. be calm and present like an owl but act with precision when problems need solving. as a new leader avoid spending more than half your time on ic work, your job is empowering the team not just building. do not assume your new boss needs the same things past managers did, ask questions and get expectations explicit early.
