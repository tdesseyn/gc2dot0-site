about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with stephen shary: moving from senior engineer to technical leadership roles requires solving bigger problems, writing reference implementations, and building good culture through encouragement.

technical leadership tracks are emerging as alternatives to management, with varying titles across companies. take on increasingly complex problems over time and write reference implementations others can learn from. career progression takes time in seat; rushing to senior level without depth limits future growth. good leaders get behind decisions made by leadership even if they initially disagreed with them. encourage your team openly and stay positive; people listen more to leaders who uplift them. refresh your resume every january regardless of whether youre job searching so youre always ready to move. as a leader get behind decisions once theyre made even if you disagreed, then fully learn why the choice was made.
