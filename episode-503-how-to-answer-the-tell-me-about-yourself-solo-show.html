about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to craft and deliver a strong tell me about yourself answer that breaks down interview barriers and sets a confident tone.

research the interviewer and company to tailor your answer and find shared experiences like kids, sports, or school. record yourself practicing to catch filler words and awkward speech patterns that undermine confidence. frame your answer specifically for the job and role, not your entire life story or early career. add one personal detail like a hobby or fun fact to break the ice and shift the interview toward natural conversation. research the company and interviewer so you can tie in relevant details even if you don't know the exact role. practice on video by recording yourself answering the question to catch filler words and nervous habits.
