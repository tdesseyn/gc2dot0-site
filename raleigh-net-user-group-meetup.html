about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to navigate job search in tech: resume strategy, interview prep, finding good recruiters, and what hiring managers actually look for.

make your resume quantitative and specific to projects you can explain, keep it three to five pages if experienced. build github projects and code samples to show you can learn new tech, especially for career changers. find recruiters who stay at one company for years and know the local market, not job hoppers. interview the company too by asking questions about the team, culture, and role to make sure it fits. show you keep learning through meetups, twitter, side projects, and contributing rather than collecting certifications. only work with two or three recruiters at a time and pick ones who have stayed at the same company for several years. start your job search before you get laid off so you have time to prep your resume and skills.
