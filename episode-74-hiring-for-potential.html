about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to interview for potential instead of perfect skill matches and why companies should hire more flexibly.

hiring managers should consider candidates who have most skills and can learn the rest. offer candidates flexibility in interview format like live coding or take-home tests. contract to hire lets you evaluate someone's actual work over months not just interviews. if you are a hiring manager rejecting people for missing a few skills that gap often points to weak training and mentorship in your organization. be brutally honest with recruiters about what you can and cannot do before entering an interview process. practice explaining your thought process out loud since communicating how you solve problems matters more than reaching the right answer. get comfortable saying i don't know during interviews instead of pretending to know everything.
