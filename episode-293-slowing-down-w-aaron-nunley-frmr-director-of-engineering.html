about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with aaron nunley: taking intentional career breaks and staying networked helps senior engineers reset priorities and find better opportunities without traditional job searches.

after a decade of overwork, a break forced perspective on work-life balance and revealed unhealthy habits within six weeks. networking during employment pays off more than job board applications; most of aaron's opportunities came unsolicited from past connections. avoid falling in love with job opportunities to stay objective during interviews and negotiations; emotional attachment clouds decision-making. build relationships with cross functional people like sales reps, not just engineers, since they often hear about openings first. do not fall in love with a job opportunity before you finish interviewing or it can hurt your judgment and your negotiation.
