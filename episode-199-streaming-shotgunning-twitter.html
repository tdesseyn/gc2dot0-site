about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with drew clements: how streaming, twitter, and community helped a developer rebuild after losing both jobs during covid.

post on twitter about your struggles. tech community is unusually willing to help compared to other industries. stream your coding work publicly. it helps build your portfolio and proves you can communicate technical concepts clearly. consistency matters more than intensity. show up regularly to your craft, even if just one day a week. layer your job search. combine building in public with late night job board searching and applications. join communities like virtual coffee where you feel like a member, not just present. genuine community accelerates growth. mix tactics, build and stream during the day, then block out late hours to mass apply and cross post your project. find a real community like virtual coffee instead of just lurking, consistent small shows of effort compound over time.
