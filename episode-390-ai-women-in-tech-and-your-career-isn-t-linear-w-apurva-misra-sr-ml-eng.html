ai, women in tech, and your career isn't linear w/ apurva misra, sr. ml eng

  • > before investing in ai, get your data organized, cleaned up, and well maintained first.
  • > remember generative ai is probabilistic not deterministic, so design interfaces that set expectations and collect user feedback like thumbs up or down.
  • > dont force ai into every problem just because its popular, use it where it actually improves your day to day workflow.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with apurva misra: ml engineer apurva misra discusses ai adoption strategies, non-linear careers, women in tech representation, and public speaking tips.

companies should focus on data organization and maintenance before pursuing ai projects. not every business problem needs ai; sometimes simpler heuristics work better. career paths shift unexpectedly; stay open-minded and explore different areas early on. getting more women in tech requires early intervention, representation, and company pipelines like internships. a step back early in your career, like an unpaid internship exploring new fields, can set up the path that leads to your real passion. dont take job rejections personally, most of the time it has nothing to do with you and expecting rejection can make it hurt less.
