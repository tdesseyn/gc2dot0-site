about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with kelly vaughn: how to stand out in job applications, interview preparation, and what hiring managers actually want to see from candidates.

be yourself and break from overly formal language to stand out from thousands of similar applications. practice interview answers with keywords and bullet points so you remember key points without sounding scripted. take time to think before answering interview questions; it's okay to pause instead of rushing to respond. to get noticed reach out to the hiring manager or someone in your second level network instead of just applying and waiting. stop using chatgpt to write your whole resume because recruiters can spot copy and pasted text word for word. when following up after applying keep it short and genuine instead of sending a generic auto filled linkedin message. write down bullet points before an interview so you dont blank, you only need to hit the key points not a perfect script.
