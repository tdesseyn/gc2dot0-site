building community

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • pick one real passion and build a small group around it instead of trying to network in general
  • start simple with meetup.com, facebook, linkedin, slack, or discord groups to give people a place to gather
  • set a consistent schedule like once a month so your community stays active without burning you out
  • use a shared interest to turn a cold dm into a real relationship instead of a generic networking ask
  • if you are struggling to find a job or make friends, building a community around something you love can open doors

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about how to start building a professional network?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to pick one of your genuine passions and build a small community around it rather than trying to network generically. he suggests creating a simple meetup using meetup.com, facebook, linkedin, slack, or discord where people with the same interest can gather. the key is consistency, meeting once a month so the community stays active without burning you out, and using shared interests to turn cold outreach into real relationships.

how does taylor desseyn suggest using a community group to reach hiring managers?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn suggests that after reaching out to a hiring manager for an informational chat, you can invite them to join a meetup you organize around a shared interest like tea or predators games. this approach lets you develop a genuine relationship with someone at a company you want to work for, using something you both care about rather than making a purely transactional networking request. he emphasizes that people are looking for leaders to organize these groups and will show up if you create the space.
