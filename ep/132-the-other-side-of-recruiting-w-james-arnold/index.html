questions this episode answers

what does james arnold say about reaching out to recruiters before applying?

on guidance counselor 2.0, james arnold says reaching out to a recruiter before you apply can put you ahead of candidates waiting in the application queue. he recommends stating exactly what you want from the interaction: mention the specific job you found, explain what you think you're good at, attach your resume, and offer your availability. this allows the recruiter to move you forward or give you honest feedback before you waste time applying.

does the ats automatically reject resumes according to james arnold?

on guidance counselor 2.0, james arnold says the ats does not automatically reject candidates with hidden keyword magic. almost every candidate that comes into his corporation gets looked at by a human being, not rejected by software. if you get rejected, it's because a person found someone better or the job closed, not because an algorithm filtered you out.

what does james arnold recommend about tailoring your resume?

on guidance counselor 2.0, james arnold recommends tailoring your resume to match the job description so the recruiter can easily see your bullet points align with what they need. even if you only have one and a half years of experience when the job asks for three, matching the specific requirements in the description can make a recruiter send you forward anyway because you're demonstrating the right skills.
