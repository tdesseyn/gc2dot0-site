questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about leaving a new job after only a few months?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says it is okay to leave a job after three to six months if the role is chaotic or does not match what was promised, especially when overhiring has created instability. he emphasizes that the job search is about storytelling, so candidates should explain to recruiters that the company overpromised and the situation is not working out. however, he clarifies that you should not leave after one instance of conflict but should try to work through problems first.

what does taylor desseyn say about asking companies about remote work policies?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says job seekers should directly ask companies about their remote policy and return to office plans before accepting an offer. he notes that companies are starting to change their stance on remote work, with some like microsoft and google returning to on site work. if you want fully remote work, he emphasizes that you need to ask these questions upfront because companies are shifting their approaches.
