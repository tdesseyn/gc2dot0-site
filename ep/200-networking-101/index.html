networking 101

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • use polywork badges to find people who share your specific interests instead of filtering generic linkedin searches
  • check the polywork opportunities section for job postings, freelance gigs, and podcast guest requests
  • on linkedin, message someone about a specific interest listed on their profile to get a reply almost every time
  • check a linkedin profile's activity section first, if they have not posted in 30 days consider moving on to someone more active
  • start your job search by networking with people from your own school or community before reaching out further

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about using polywork badges for networking?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says polywork badges let you network based on specific shared interests instead of filtering generic searches. you click on a badge like corgi lover or software engineering to find people with that interest, then message them about it. he says this is more valuable than linkedin searches because it connects you with people over interests, not just job titles or industries.

how does taylor desseyn recommend using the linkedin activity section when networking?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn says to check someone's activity section before messaging them on linkedin. if they have not posted in 30 days, consider moving on because they are probably not active. if they do post regularly, reference one of their recent posts in your message to start a conversation, like i saw your video about resumes, i would love to chat about it more.

what does taylor say about using linkedin interests to get replies?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to look at the interests section on someone's linkedin profile and message them about a specific interest listed there. he says if someone messages him about gary vaynerchuk or another interest on his profile, he will respond 10 out of 10 times because it shows they took the time to look at what he cares about.
