how to work with a recruiter

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • look for recruiters who hit the two and two rule, two years at their company and two years in your local market
  • insist on a zoom call instead of just a phone call, since you would not trust a financial advisor you never saw face to face
  • find one good recruiter, then ask them who else in the city does good work and get introduced
  • bcc five recruiters you trust on a job posting you find and ask if they are working that role or have leads on it
  • keep checking in if a recruiter goes quiet, since recruiters are busy and ghosting is usually not personal

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about choosing a recruiter to work with?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends looking for recruiters who meet the two and two rule: they have worked at the same company for two years and have been in your local market for two years. he explains that recruiters who bounce around frequently are often not good at their job, since the role is heavily commission-based and mediocre recruiters struggle to stay in one place. the ideal recruiter has been at the same company for five years, since that level of tenure indicates they are skilled and knowledgeable about the local market.

why does taylor desseyn insist on zoom calls instead of phone calls with recruiters?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says job seekers should demand a zoom call with recruiters instead of accepting just a phone call. he argues that you would not trust your finances to a financial advisor if all you did was talk on the phone, and recruiters serve the same trusted advisor role for your career. he adds that if a recruiter only offers a phone call, it shows they lack confidence and you should probably move on, since the effort difference between a phone call and a zoom call is minimal.

what is taylor desseyn's bcc tactic for working with recruiters?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends finding five recruiters you like and trust, then using bcc to send all five the same job posting you found on indeed or another job board. in the email, ask if they have any leads to that company or if they are working on that job opportunity. he says this tactic lets recruiters work for you and can lead to introductions and results within 30 to 90 days, and he believes almost no one uses this approach yet.
