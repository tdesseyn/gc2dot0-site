nashmicro meetup

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • partner with no more than two to three recruiters at a time instead of relying on just one
  • check how long a recruiter has stayed at their current company, longer tenure usually means better market knowledge
  • ask recruiters direct questions like who they know at the company and how long the role has been open
  • give recruiters a clear rate or salary range instead of a single number so they can negotiate on your behalf
  • be upfront and honest with recruiters about your interest level since lying makes it harder for them to place you well

questions this episode answers

what questions does taylor desseyn say to ask a recruiter to know if they have a real relationship with a company?

on this guidance counselor 2.0 nashmicro meetup episode, taylor desseyn says ask the recruiter who they know at the company, how long the role has been open, and how many people have interviewed. he explains that if a recruiter sends you a stock job description you can find online, they don't have a direct relationship with the hiring manager. nine out of ten recruiters cannot answer these basic questions about their job openings.

how does taylor desseyn say to check if a recruiter knows their market?

on this guidance counselor 2.0 nashmicro meetup episode, taylor desseyn says check how long the recruiter has been at their current company and in the local market. he recommends working with recruiters who have been at the same company for more than two years and in the local market for at least a year. recruiters who have only been in the industry six months or just graduated college will not know anything about the market.
