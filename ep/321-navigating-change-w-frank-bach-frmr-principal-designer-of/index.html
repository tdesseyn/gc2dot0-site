navigating change w/ frank bach, frmr principal designer of headspace

guest: frank bach, former principal designer of headspace

  • keep a folder of screenshots showing praise or wins so you can fight imposter syndrome when you feel a dip
  • if you stay camera off and hidden in remote work no one knows your impact when promotion time comes
  • ask your manager for a trade off take on a less exciting project now in exchange for a more meaningful one later

questions this episode answers

what does frank bach recommend doing to fight imposter syndrome as a designer?

on guidance counselor 2.0, frank bach, former principal designer at headspace, says to keep a folder on your desktop with screenshots of praise, shout outs and wins from work. when you feel the dip of imposter syndrome, open that folder to remind yourself you are worthy of your job, position and salary. he saves things like slack kudos or positive moments from conversations.

why does frank bach say remote workers need to be visible at work?

on guidance counselor 2.0, frank bach explains that in remote or hybrid environments it is very easy to hide out with your camera off most of the time. if no one sees you or knows you, when promotion time or review time comes, nobody knows your impact. he says you have to be seen so you can influence in the company.

what does frank bach suggest doing right after a layoff instead of immediately applying?

on guidance counselor 2.0, frank bach advises people not to jump into action mode right away after a layoff. take a beat and process what happened first, because in that moment you are probably not clear headed and may be emotional or frustrated. once you feel clearer, then reach out to your network and you will be surprised how quickly people show up for you.
