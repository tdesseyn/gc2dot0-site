starting over w/ clark sell

guest: clark sell, software engineer and entrepreneur, founder of unspecified

  • after running a conference for 12 years, clark sell had to shut it down when ticket sales and sponsorships dropped post pandemic
  • don't let your personal brand disappear while you pour everything into building something for everyone else
  • your career will ebb and flow, not just go up and to the right, so know what work you actually enjoy
  • stop waiting for your company to invest in you and start doing the work on yourself instead
  • action creates momentum, so take a small step forward instead of waiting until you feel ready

questions this episode answers

what does clark sell say about maintaining your personal brand while running a business?

on guidance counselor 2.0, clark sell says he let his entire personal brand disappear while focusing on building his conference and community for 12 years. he had no website, no personal presence, and when the conference shut down he was left with nothing to fall back on. he warns against pouring everything into building something for everyone else while neglecting the work on yourself and your own brand.

what does clark sell say about expecting your company to invest in your career development?

on guidance counselor 2.0, clark sell says too many developers expect their managers and companies to pay for their growth instead of investing in themselves. he says if you expect your company to propel you forward, you are playing their game instead of yours. while companies should participate, waiting for them to develop you means you are not taking ownership of your own career path.

what does clark sell recommend when you feel stuck in your career?

on guidance counselor 2.0, clark sell says action creates momentum, so you have to take action and do something to move forward rather than waiting. he advises consuming less content and doing more actual work. he says if you feel stuck, you probably are stuck, and the only way out is to take a step forward even when you do not feel ready.
