ep 275 · sep 20, 2023

colin lord - pto, hobbies, and taking care of yourself

guest: colin lord, engineering manager of front end teams at smile direct club

  • learn to tell the difference between needing a 15 minute walk and needing a full week off to recharge
  • uninstall work apps like outlook and teams during vacation so you are not tempted to check in
  • build a real relationship with your manager so it feels safe to say you are struggling before it affects your work
  • during a job search treat it like a full time job and aim for one solid intentional conversation a day instead of nonstop applying
  • get comfortable asking for help early since most people do not take advantage of help that is already offered to them

questions this episode answers

what does colin lord say about taking time off during the workday?

on guidance counselor 2.0, colin lord, engineering manager at smile direct club, says you need to learn the difference between needing a quick 15 minute walk around the building and needing a full week off because you are totally burned out. he says it is a skill like anything else to recognize which one you need at any given moment. he always tells his team to let him know if they are hitting either type of wall so he can help.

how does colin lord recommend disconnecting from work during vacation?

on guidance counselor 2.0, colin lord, engineering manager at smile direct club, says whenever anyone on his team takes a vacation he encourages them to literally uninstall outlook and teams from their phone. he says you cannot half do unplugging and recharging, because if you get a notification about standup or a bug you will instinctively click on it. he does not want to hear from his team members until he sees them the next week.
