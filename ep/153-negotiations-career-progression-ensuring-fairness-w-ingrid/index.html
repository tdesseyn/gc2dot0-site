ep 153 · jul 11, 2022

negotiations, career progression, & ensuring fairness w/ ingrid epure

guest: ingrid epure, senior staff engineer at netlify

  • always negotiate, whether it is initial job offers, day to day role decisions, or career progression discussions
  • build personal rapport in negotiations by asking questions and listening instead of jumping straight to solutions
  • it is okay for your career path to look different over time, lateral moves and team switches are normal and valuable
  • set longer term learning goals tied to industry trends instead of comparing your progress to other people's timelines
  • push for real representation and equal opportunities for minorities instead of just diversity talk or campaigns
