ep 51 · jul 7, 2021

w/alishah novin, director of engineering @ celero commerce

guest: alishah novin, director of engineering at celero commerce

  • use regular one-on-ones to talk about growth, not just performance, and push for that conversation every other week at worst
  • treat fear of changing jobs as useful information rather than something to ignore, it usually means something needs attention
  • read widely outside your day to day work so you notice what skills and tech are moving on without you
  • tie your growth ask to a realistic timeline like a year or two instead of expecting a big jump in six months
  • get involved in local tech communities and slack groups to see where you stand and get honest feedback on gaps
