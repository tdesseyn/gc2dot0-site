questions this episode answers

what does alishah novin say about when to leave a job?

on guidance counselor 2.0, alishah novin, director of engineering at celero commerce, says the key is to ask yourself what your growth potential is and honestly assess what you don't know. he says you should be having conversations about growth with your manager in one-on-ones at least every other week, laying out a realistic timeline of one to two years rather than expecting growth in six months. if those opportunities don't exist at your current company after those conversations, that's when you know it's time to look elsewhere.

how does alishah novin suggest dealing with fear about changing jobs?

on guidance counselor 2.0, alishah novin says fear about changing jobs is actually healthy information you should listen to, not ignore. fear tells you something is off and motivates you to grow rather than rest in the comfort of what you know. he warns that the worst thing you can do is ignore that sense of fear and become complacent, because relationships and jobs eventually end one way or another, and you need to use that fear to keep building your skills.

what does alishah novin recommend for staying current in tech?

on guidance counselor 2.0, alishah novin says you need to read widely outside your day-to-day work, including blog posts from people at similar levels and books, to avoid getting comfortable and missing technologies advancing around you. he also strongly recommends getting involved in local tech communities like nashdev, a slack channel for developers in nashville, where you can ask questions and get challenged by others to understand where you stand and what you don't know. he says you should use these communities not just to network but to get honest feedback about your skills and gaps.
