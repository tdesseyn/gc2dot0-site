the power of just getting started w/ torrance evans

guest: torrance evans, software engineer at jp morgan and chapter leader of black boys code in atlanta

  • start small and let it grow, one networking newsletter for 10 people can turn into something much bigger
  • when going to events it is fine to just listen and take notes instead of forcing an elevator pitch
  • connect with people on linkedin right after meeting them so your activity keeps showing up in their feed without extra follow up messages
  • narrow down what direction you want in tech early so networking conversations are more focused and useful
  • look for ways to attend expensive conferences for free by volunteering or working the event instead of skipping it

questions this episode answers

what does torrance evans say about following up after meeting people at networking events?

on guidance counselor 2.0, torrance evans says he does not send follow-up messages after meeting people at events. instead, he connects with them on linkedin immediately and stays active by posting pictures and videos from events he attends. because linkedin shows recent connections your activity first, his new contacts see his posts in their feed without him needing to send direct messages.

what does torrance evans recommend for people attending their first tech networking event?

on guidance counselor 2.0, torrance evans says it is fine to just show up and not talk to anybody at first. he recommends showing up to listen and take notes instead of forcing yourself to have an elevator pitch ready. he also says you should know what direction you want in tech before going so you know who to talk to and can have more focused conversations.
