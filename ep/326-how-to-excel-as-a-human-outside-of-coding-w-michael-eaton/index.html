questions this episode answers

what does michael eaton recommend about writing things down as a developer?

on guidance counselor 2.0, michael eaton says developers should write things down daily, whether it's quick notes, journaling, or documenting decisions in a developer notebook. he frames this as externalizing your memory so you can track where you left off between meetings and interruptions. when you join a project without onboarding documents, eaton advises creating them yourself so you leave the team better than you found it.

what does michael eaton say about sharing knowledge as a developer?

on guidance counselor 2.0, michael eaton says every developer should share what they learn, but it does not have to be a conference talk. he recommends writing short blog posts about small things you figure out or simply presenting something cool you learned to your own team for half an hour. eaton notes that the more you teach something the better you learn it, and writing creates a searchable record you can refer back to later.
