how to excel as a human outside of coding w/ michael eaton

guest: michael eaton, principal consultant at improving

  • write things down daily, from quick notes to journaling, to externalize your memory and track decisions
  • when you join a project without onboarding docs, create them so you leave the team better than you found it
  • treat continuous learning as a daily habit, even in small 10 minute chunks between meetings
  • share what you learn through blogging or quick team talks, since teaching something helps you learn it better
  • use ai tools like github copilot as a helper similar to an intern, but double check outputs since they can be wrong
