ep 134 · apr 25, 2022

philosophies on hiring and culture w/ ashley hunsberger

guest: ashley hunsberger, director of developer advocacy and velocity lab at anthology

  • lead with people first because nobody remembers the project at the end of the day, they remember how you made them feel
  • motivate your team by involving them in setting their own goals instead of leading through fear or pressure
  • try flexible schedules like a four day work week and evaluate weekly if goals are still being met
  • focus one on ones on relationship building and coaching rather than status updates you can already get on slack
  • defrag your calendar around your natural energy so strategic thinking, creative work, and listening happen at the right times of day

questions this episode answers

what does ashley hunsberger say about leading with a people-first approach in tech?

on guidance counselor 2.0, ashley hunsberger says nobody remembers the deliverables or projects at the end of the day, but they do remember how you made them feel. she emphasizes motivating teams by involving them in setting their own goals rather than leading from fear or pressure. hunsberger learned this after burning out in her early twenties and realizing relationships matter more than just shipping products.

how does ashley hunsberger structure her four-day work week?

on guidance counselor 2.0, ashley hunsberger explains she works ten-hour days monday through thursday and takes fridays off for grad school and mental space. she and her manager evaluate weekly whether she is missing anything critical or failing to provide value. hunsberger notes she does not feel like she is missing work and that she was often putting in similar hours anyway before the schedule change.
