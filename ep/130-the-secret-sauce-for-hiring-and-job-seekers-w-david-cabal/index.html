ep 130 · apr 4, 2022

the secret sauce for hiring and job seekers w/ david cabal

guest: david cabal, engineering manager at exodus

  • pay candidates upfront for take home code challenges to show you value their time
  • weigh soft skills and humility as heavily as technical skill since attitude affects how someone takes feedback on a team
  • keep hiring moving fast by reviewing code challenges within 24 to 48 hours and letting candidates pick the next interview slot
  • stay laser focused on the delivery date and push non critical bugs and extra features to the backlog instead of chasing perfection
  • as a candidate come with prepared questions for the interviewer since most people skip this and it instantly sets you apart
