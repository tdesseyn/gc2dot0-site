questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about replying to rejection emails?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says you should always reply to rejection emails with gratitude and kindness because it shows your character and heart to the hiring manager. he explains that when a company's first or second choice candidate falls through, they will call the person who responded kindly and gracefully to their rejection. responding with appreciation and saying the organization remains top of your list makes you first in line if the position reopens.

what does taylor desseyn say about being negative on social media during a job search?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says job seekers should avoid venting or being negative on social media because hiring managers can see all your public comments and activity. he explains that when you comment on linkedin posts, those comments appear on your profile for recruiters to review, and negativity can hurt your chances. he recommends finding another outlet for frustration instead of social media, noting that people who stay kind and uplifting during the search are the ones who find opportunities.

what does taylor desseyn say about sending messages after an interview?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says you should send a linkedin message or thank you note the same night after your interview to stay top of mind. he explains that if you send a message that evening, the hiring manager will wake up in the morning, accept your connection request, and your message will be at the top of their inbox. he also emphasizes that the job search is about building depth in relationships with a few people rather than surface-level conversations with many.
