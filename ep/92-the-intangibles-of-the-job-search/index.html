ep 92 · oct 18, 2021

the intangibles of the job search

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • avoid venting or being negative on social media since hiring managers can see your public comments and activity
  • reply to rejection emails with gratitude since it shows your character to the hiring manager
  • being kind after a rejection can put you first in line if the top candidate falls through
  • send a thank you message or linkedin note right after interviewing to stay top of mind
  • focus on building deeper relationships with fewer people instead of spreading yourself thin across many contacts
