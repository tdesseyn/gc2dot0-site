ep 108 · feb 2, 2022

w/ bram gruneir, software engineer at cockroach labs

guest: bram gruneir, software engineer at cockroach labs

  • if you get a job offer and feel pressured, take time to decide if you have the freedom to do so
  • prep for interviews by practicing algorithms since many companies still expect live coding performance
  • live coding interviews show debugging skills and real problem solving, not just memorized algorithms
  • avoid filtering resumes by big name companies or schools since that bias can cause you to miss good candidates
  • build side projects you are passionate about since that experience and initiative stands out to employers
