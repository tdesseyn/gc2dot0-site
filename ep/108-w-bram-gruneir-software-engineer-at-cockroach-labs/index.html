ep 108 · feb 2, 2022

w/ bram gruneir, software engineer at cockroach labs

guest: bram gruneir, software engineer at cockroach labs

  • if you get a job offer and feel pressured, take time to decide if you have the freedom to do so
  • prep for interviews by practicing algorithms since many companies still expect live coding performance
  • live coding interviews show debugging skills and real problem solving, not just memorized algorithms
  • avoid filtering resumes by big name companies or schools since that bias can cause you to miss good candidates
  • build side projects you are passionate about since that experience and initiative stands out to employers

questions this episode answers

what does bram gruneir say about taking time to decide on a job offer?

on guidance counselor 2.0, bram gruneir, a software engineer at cockroach labs, says it depends on your situation. if you don't have work and need it, take a good offer. if you already have a job, you can afford to be pickier. he notes some people take a month or two off between jobs to prep for interviews if they have the freedom to do so.

how does bram gruneir recommend preparing for coding interviews?

on guidance counselor 2.0, bram gruneir, a software engineer at cockroach labs, says you have to practice your algorithms because companies expect you to perform. he mentions cockroach does live coding rather than whiteboard interviews, which lets you get feedback from execution and showcases debugging skills. he notes live coding showcases more skills than just being able to recite an algorithm.

what does bram gruneir say about filtering resumes by company or school names?

on guidance counselor 2.0, bram gruneir, a software engineer at cockroach labs, says cockroach realized they were discounting resumes without big name tech companies like microsoft, google, facebook or amazon, and also filtering out candidates from lesser known schools. he says this was bad practice and they corrected it. now they do blind interviews where they don't check resumes beforehand, only hr confirms basic qualifications.
