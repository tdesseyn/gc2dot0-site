guidance counselor 2.0 w/milecia mcgregor - career paths outside of software

guest: milecia mcgregor, software developer and consultant

  • if you hit the three to five year mark and still like coding but want change, shadow other roles like devops or product before jumping ship
  • product managers talk to stakeholders, find pain points, and translate them into user stories for engineers to build
  • if you are burnt out on being told your code is broken, qa or sdet roles let you test and automate instead of just writing new features
  • when starting freelance consulting, take on small clients for a few hours a week and slowly grow your workload before going full time

questions this episode answers

what does milecia mcgregor say about shadowing other roles before switching careers in tech?

on guidance counselor 2.0, milecia mcgregor says if you're at the three to five year experience mark and still like coding but want change, shadow people in other roles at your company before jumping ship. she shadowed devops engineers at her own company to see what they did in aws all day, and some of her later roles started because she shadowed someone first. you win either way because shadowing makes you a better developer even if you stay put.

how does milecia mcgregor describe what a product manager does?

on guidance counselor 2.0, milecia mcgregor says product managers talk to different stakeholders like support or subsets of customers to figure out how people are using the app. they find pain points and bring them to engineers, translating them into user stories for sprints. they also try to make sure they don't promise a deadline the engineers can't meet.

what does milecia mcgregor recommend for starting freelance consulting without quitting your job?

on guidance counselor 2.0, milecia mcgregor says start super small by finding a client who wants just a few hours a week, like writing three or four articles a month. slowly ramp up from five hours a week to about fifteen hours before going full time. she did not go full time on consulting until maybe a year and a half into building her client base. for pricing, take the yearly salary you want to make and divide it by 2080 to get your hourly rate.
