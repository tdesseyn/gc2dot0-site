ep 271 · sep 11, 2023

entrepreneurship and startup life w/sheena allen

guest: sheena allen, founder, capway

  • treat non-technical communication like leaving no holes explain every button and screen so nothing is left to guess
  • if you work with technical people learn enough about the process so you know when a timeline estimate does not make sense
  • creativity and an outsider background can be an advantage as a founder instead of something to hide
  • figure out a schedule that protects sleep meals time with family and short walks instead of sitting for hours straight
  • focus on proving yourself right instead of trying to prove someone else wrong in your work
