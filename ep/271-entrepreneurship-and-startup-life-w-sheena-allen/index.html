entrepreneurship and startup life w/sheena allen

guest: sheena allen, founder, capway

  • treat non-technical communication like leaving no holes explain every button and screen so nothing is left to guess
  • if you work with technical people learn enough about the process so you know when a timeline estimate does not make sense
  • creativity and an outsider background can be an advantage as a founder instead of something to hide
  • figure out a schedule that protects sleep meals time with family and short walks instead of sitting for hours straight
  • focus on proving yourself right instead of trying to prove someone else wrong in your work

questions this episode answers

what does sheena allen say about non-technical founders communicating with developers?

on guidance counselor 2.0, sheena allen says she leaves no holes in her specifications, explaining every button and screen so developers never have to guess what she wants. she also recommends non-technical founders learn enough about development processes to recognize when a timeline estimate doesn't make sense. this prevents getting burned by unrealistic quotes or slow delivery.

how does sheena allen say being creative helps her as a founder?

on guidance counselor 2.0, sheena allen says being creative and coming from a non-traditional background in the south are her two biggest advantages as a founder. she explains that creativity means not wanting to be one-dimensional and being willing to throw different ideas at the wall. she argues this outsider perspective helps her see problems differently than founders from coastal areas or ivy league schools.

what does sheena allen recommend for managing time as an entrepreneur?

on guidance counselor 2.0, sheena allen says she schedules time to eat because she gets so deep into work that she forgets meals until the afternoon. she also blocks out time to go home to mississippi every eight weeks and schedules short walks because she tends to sit for hours without moving. she emphasizes that sleep, family time, and brief breaks are important time blocks to protect.
