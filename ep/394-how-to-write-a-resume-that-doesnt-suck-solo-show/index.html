how to write a resume that doesn't suck (solo show)

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • keep your name and contact info in the header, not buried further down the page
  • use a 14 to 16 point font for your name, not an oversized font that wastes space
  • list only email instead of phone number so you can track and manage responses in one place
  • tailor your professional summary to match the required skills and years of experience in the job post
  • start every bullet point with an action verb and make it quantitative instead of just listing tasks
