questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about where to put your name and contact info on a resume?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to use the header section at the top of the page for your name and contact information rather than starting further down. he recommends using a 14 to 16 point font for your name instead of an oversized 30 point font that wastes valuable space. he also suggests listing only your email rather than a phone number so you can track all responses in one place instead of getting flustered by calls.

how does taylor desseyn recommend writing the professional summary section of a resume?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends changing your professional summary for each job application to match the required skills and years of experience listed in the job post. he explains that as a recruiter, hiring managers primarily want to know how many years of experience you have with the required skills. if your summary aligns with what the job needs, he says he would call you immediately without reading the rest of the resume.

what does taylor desseyn say about how to write bullet points on a resume?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to start every bullet point with an action verb like managed, led, created or ran. he recommends making each bullet point quantitative rather than just listing tasks, and keeping each bullet to a single line or one sentence to avoid walls of text. he also emphasizes talking about what you specifically did rather than what your team did, and giving concrete numbers like revenue driven or users served.
