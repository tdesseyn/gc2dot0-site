why do people suck w/my dear friend andrew wyborski

guest: andrew wyborski

  • most of a career can trace back to just a handful of personal connections so treat every conversation as a potential job lead
  • you can learn a lot about a company by how they source their talent during the hiring process
  • bring solutions instead of problems to your manager and take initiative by proactively giving updates
  • keep a background thread running on side projects or goals so you make steady progress without burning out
  • if you cannot affect change where you are, change your participation there because this is your problem to solve

questions this episode answers

how does andrew wyborski recommend bringing problems to your manager?

on guidance counselor 2.0, andrew wyborski says to come to your manager with solutions, not just problems. he stresses that people often do not even put in the effort to think of what a solution could be, instead just complaining about why something cannot be done. he believes this is a key differentiator between good and great employees.

what is andrew wyborski's background thread advice for career progress?

on guidance counselor 2.0, andrew wyborski recommends keeping a background thread running on side projects or career goals. he explains this means letting ideas and initiatives sit in the back of your mind constantly, like an operating system background process, so you make steady progress without structured work sessions. this could be shower thoughts or an hour before everyone logs on in the morning, but the key is continually moving forward without burning out by forcing concentrated effort all at once.
