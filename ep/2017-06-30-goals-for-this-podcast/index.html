questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about how he structures his conference talks on job searching?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says his conference talks at events like code conferences are entirely live question and answer sessions rather than slide presentations. he explains that after every talk, people ask for slides but he doesn't use them because everything is conducted as interactive q&a. this approach is what inspired him to start the podcast to share similar content in audio format.

what topics does taylor desseyn plan to cover on guidance counselor 2.0?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says he plans to cover how to write a good resume and how to work with recruiters. he mentions he is a recruiter himself, which will inform his content on that topic. his overall goal is to help people in job transitions have an easier process by bringing practical content to the podcast.
