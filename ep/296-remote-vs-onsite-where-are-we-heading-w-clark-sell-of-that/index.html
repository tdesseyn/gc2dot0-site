ep 296 · nov 20, 2023

remote vs. onsite? where are we heading? w/clark sell of that conference

guest: clark sell, that conference

  • treat remote work like a skill, not a given, since covid exposed how many teams never built real remote processes
  • invest in a decent webcam and mic for video calls, it is a cheap way to look professional without buying a suit
  • go to conferences and meetups because in person connections often turn into real job leads faster than social media alone
  • when networking or interviewing, give context beyond short answers so people have something to ask follow up questions about
  • do not be afraid to change career paths, keep learning new skills since most careers last decades not just a few years
