raleigh .net user group meetup

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • avoid saying you crushed an interview, recruiters see that as a red flag that it actually went poorly
  • make your resume specific and quantify results instead of vague lines like works well with others
  • plan on a resume of three to five pages if you have real experience, not just one page
  • only work with two or three recruiters at a time and pick ones who have stayed at the same company for several years
  • start your job search before you get laid off so you have time to prep your resume and skills
