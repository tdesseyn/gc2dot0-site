ep 268 · sep 4, 2023

a letter to recruiters w/head of devrel at snap, tessa kriesel

guest: tessa kriesel

  • recruiters should show up at conferences in their own city, not just work job boards all day
  • get people into your network right at the event, connect on linkedin or get an email instead of collecting business cards you'll never follow up on
  • use the qr code icon in the linkedin search bar to connect with people on the spot at events
  • keep networking simple, ask what someone does, get connected, and move on without overthinking it
  • building relationships online before meeting in person makes the in person meeting feel instant and natural
