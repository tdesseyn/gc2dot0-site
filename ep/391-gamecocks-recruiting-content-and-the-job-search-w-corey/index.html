questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about pivoting from developer roles when the job search is tough?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says if you're struggling to find a developer role, consider adjacent paths like qa, analyst work, or customer support. companies want people who understand the business deeply, and starting in support or account management gives you that business knowledge before moving to technical roles. he notes that organizations prefer keeping people who get the business context over pure coders without domain experience.

what does taylor desseyn recommend for following up with recruiters during a job search?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says speed and follow-up are critical because timing often matters more than being the most qualified. if you don't hear back within 24 hours, follow up because companies might move to their second or third choice. he got his own job at gun.io only because he followed up a week later when the first vp candidate fell through.

what is the bcc hack taylor desseyn talks about for working with recruiters?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends finding five to ten recruiters you trust and putting them all on a bcc email once a month. send them updates on where you're interviewing, what you're thinking, and attach your resume to cover ground at scale. he explains that recruiters handle so much they might not remember you, so a monthly bcc keeps you visible without spamming individual inboxes.
