ep 391 · jul 29, 2024

gamecocks, recruiting, content, and the job search w/ corey jones

guest: corey jones

  • if you're stuck finding a developer role try adjacent paths like qa, support, or account management to build business knowledge first
  • follow up on interviews and recruiter conversations since timing and responsiveness often beat being the most qualified candidate
  • try the bcc hack by emailing a small group of recruiters you trust once a month with updates on your search and resume
  • add a short hobby line to your resume since shared interests can create real connections with hiring managers
  • stay pleasant and curious in interviews, even on tough questions, since energy and attitude are read closely right now
