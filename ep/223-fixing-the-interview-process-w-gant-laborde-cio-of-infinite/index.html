fixing the interview process w/gant laborde, cio of infinite red a react native first consulting company

guest: gant laborde, cio of infinite red

  • learning react native is not hard but different, since web assumptions like the dom do not carry over directly
  • job seekers often send one resume everywhere instead of researching and tailoring per company, leading to worst fit not best fit
  • watch out for companies that hire to hit growth metrics rather than because they actually need the role filled

questions this episode answers

what does gant laborde say is the biggest mistake job seekers make in the interview process?

on guidance counselor 2.0, gant laborde says most job seekers want to make one resume and hand it out to every company without reading the position or researching the role. they think one-dimensionally about just needing a job and a number, ignoring whether the role fits them as a person. this hunger leads to the fault of first availability rather than best fit.

what does gant laborde say happens when companies prioritize metrics over values?

on guidance counselor 2.0, gant laborde says when companies rely on metrics instead of values, people learn to game the system instead of doing good work. hiring managers chase metrics like headcount to get promoted rather than hiring because they need the role, leading to overstaffing. employees who do great work get passed over for promotion because a metric did not reflect their impact, and those who just know how to not get fired stay employed.
