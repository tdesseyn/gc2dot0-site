ep 439 · apr 9, 2025

from sales to first engineering job at netflix w/ shaundai person

guest: shaundai person, senior software engineer, ui engineer at netflix on the developer productivity team

  • build internal relationships before you need them by genuinely connecting with people outside your immediate team
  • post what you are learning publicly so a community forms around your progress and vouches for you later
  • when messaging someone senior for networking, keep it short, specific, and ask for a clear small thing like advice on skills to build
  • come to a networking call fully prepared with your own questions since you are asking for someone else's time
  • use short personalized video in applications and thank you notes to stand out as a real person in a remote hiring process
