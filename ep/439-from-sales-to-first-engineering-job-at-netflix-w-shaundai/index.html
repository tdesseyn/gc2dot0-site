from sales to first engineering job at netflix w/ shaundai person

guest: shaundai person, senior software engineer, ui engineer at netflix on the developer productivity team

  • build internal relationships before you need them by genuinely connecting with people outside your immediate team
  • post what you are learning publicly so a community forms around your progress and vouches for you later
  • when messaging someone senior for networking, keep it short, specific, and ask for a clear small thing like advice on skills to build
  • come to a networking call fully prepared with your own questions since you are asking for someone else's time
  • use short personalized video in applications and thank you notes to stand out as a real person in a remote hiring process

questions this episode answers

what does shaundai person say about messaging senior people for networking?

on guidance counselor 2.0, shaundai person says to keep networking messages short, informal and specific with a clear ask up front. she avoids formal greetings like 'dear' and long paragraphs, instead opening with a direct request like asking for advice on skills to build. she emphasizes asking for something small and finite like a brief conversation, and never wasting time with pleasantries that sound like a sales pitch.

how did shaundai person build relationships at sales loft before switching to engineering?

on guidance counselor 2.0, shaundai person explains she used internal networking tools like slack donut to schedule coffee chats with engineers while she was still in sales. she aimed to make genuine connections and have her name come up positively in every room, without asking for anything immediately. simultaneously, she posted everything she was learning about coding on twitter to build a public community that would vouch for her later.

what does shaundai person recommend when you reach out to someone for a networking call?

on guidance counselor 2.0, shaundai person and taylor desseyn agree that when you ask for someone senior's time, you must come fully prepared to lead the meeting with your own questions. shaundai prepared specific questions for her call with ryan, asking about team gaps and future hiring needs rather than expecting him to fill the time. she says letting the other person lead when you requested the meeting is offensive and wastes their most precious resource.
