questions this episode answers

what does bill israel say about growing senior developers instead of hiring them?

on guidance counselor 2.0, bill israel, engineering manager at built technologies, says it is better to take mid-level developers and train them up to senior rather than only hiring senior talent externally. he aims for a balanced team mix, avoiding being too top heavy with seniors or too bottom heavy with juniors. a couple of seniors can help those underneath them while juniors learn on their way up, creating an effective team dynamic.

how does bill israel recommend managing senior versus junior developers?

on guidance counselor 2.0, bill israel, engineering manager at built technologies, says to let senior people own their decisions and use yourself as a sounding board rather than micromanaging them. for junior developers, he recommends letting them struggle on their own for about an hour to figure things out, but not letting them drift for days at a time. he emphasizes letting juniors get repetitions and learn how to find information rather than feeding them every answer.

what does bill israel say junior developers should focus on in interviews?

on guidance counselor 2.0, bill israel, engineering manager at built technologies, says he is way less interested in the code junior developers write and far more interested in their enthusiasm, attitude, and the questions they ask. he urges candidates to be honest instead of bluffing, saying it is better to admit you don't know something than to pretend, because he can tell when someone is faking knowledge. showing excitement to learn and asking questions matters more than perfect technical answers for junior roles.
