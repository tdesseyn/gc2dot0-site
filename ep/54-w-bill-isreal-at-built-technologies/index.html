ep 54 · jul 14, 2021

w/bill isreal at built technologies

guest: bill israel, engineering manager at built technologies

  • grow your own seniors by training mid-level devs instead of only hiring senior talent externally
  • aim for a balanced team mix so you are not too top heavy with seniors or too bottom heavy with juniors
  • let senior people own their decisions and use yourself as a sounding board rather than micromanaging
  • for junior devs let them struggle for a bit on their own before stepping in to help, but dont let them stall for days
  • in interviews honesty beats bluffing, say you dont know something and show enthusiasm to learn instead of guessing
