interview with chris spintzyk

guest: chris spintzyk, direct placement recruiter, vaco

  • before job hunting, figure out exactly what you want to do instead of just taking anything for a paycheck
  • build a list of target companies, then work backwards to find a way in instead of applying online
  • look for a recruiter who has been with the same firm and local market for at least two years
  • work with two to three recruiters at most to get a well rounded view of the market without creating chaos
  • stay in touch with your recruiter and update them on changes since the relationship works best as a two way street

questions this episode answers

what does chris spintzyk say about applying to jobs online?

on guidance counselor 2.0, chris spintzyk says do not apply online because your resume goes into a black hole and you will wonder why nobody reaches back out to you. in 20 years of recruiting, he has heard only one person say they applied online, got interviewed, and got a job. instead, use your network or partner with a recruiter to get into target companies.

how many recruiters should a job seeker work with according to chris spintzyk?

on guidance counselor 2.0, chris spintzyk recommends working with two to three recruiters at most. two to three recruiters can give an honest assessment of the market and provide context around different companies because not every firm has the same clients. anything more than that invites chaos to the job search.

what is the two and two rule for finding a recruiter?

on guidance counselor 2.0, chris spintzyk confirms that the two and two rule is spot on: look for a recruiter who has been with the same company for at least two years and within the local market for two years. someone with two years of experience can give context, advice and encouragement because they understand the market dynamics, salary trends and which roles are becoming more or less popular.
