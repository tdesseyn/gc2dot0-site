interview with chris spintzyk

guest: chris spintzyk, direct placement recruiter, vaco

  • before job hunting, figure out exactly what you want to do instead of just taking anything for a paycheck
  • build a list of target companies, then work backwards to find a way in instead of applying online
  • look for a recruiter who has been with the same firm and local market for at least two years
  • work with two to three recruiters at most to get a well rounded view of the market without creating chaos
  • stay in touch with your recruiter and update them on changes since the relationship works best as a two way street
