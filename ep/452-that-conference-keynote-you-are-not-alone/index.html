ep 452 · may 9, 2025

that conference keynote - you are not alone

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • remember that feeling alone in the job search is common, even for people you admire on social media
  • build your network before you need it by attending meetups, live streams, and conferences
  • seek out a mentor and take ownership of driving that relationship instead of waiting to be guided
  • push for regular one-on-ones with your manager since feeling heard reduces isolation at work
  • as a leader, give feedback during interviews and onboarding since candidates remember how they were treated even after rejection

questions this episode answers

how does taylor desseyn recommend building your network before you need it?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn says you should build your network before you need it by regularly attending meetups, live streams, and conferences. he explains that this network fills the void and makes the shock of job searching more manageable. he emphasizes that not hearing back from recruiters and hiring managers is isolating, but consistent community involvement reduces anxiety to manageable levels.

what does taylor desseyn say about driving a mentorship relationship?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says that if you seek out a mentor, you have to drive that conversation yourself. he explains that it becomes awkward when someone asks to be mentored but then has no direction when asked what they want to talk about. the mentee must take ownership and drive the relationship forward.

why does taylor desseyn recommend regular one-on-ones with your manager?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn says regular one-on-ones with your manager make you feel heard and seen, which most people do not feel in their job. he recommends that if your organization has no one-on-ones, you should reach out to your manager to set them up, or if you are a manager you should establish them with your employees. when people go through one-on-one sessions, it reduces isolation at work.
