navigating the job search (solo show)

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • build a savings runway since some folks are taking six to nine months to land their next job
  • big tech layoffs are happening while startups and high growth companies are still hiring
  • get plugged into your local startup ecosystem through entrepreneur centers and founder communities
  • aim to have one to two real networking conversations a day during your job search
  • check for common connections on linkedin before applying so someone can refer or vouch for you
