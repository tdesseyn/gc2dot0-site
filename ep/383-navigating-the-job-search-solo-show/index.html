questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about how long it takes to find a job right now?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says people in the current job search are taking six to nine months to find their next job. he recommends having a savings runway to cover that period because anyone could get laid off at any time. his first priority after finding a new job would be to replenish the savings account.

how does taylor desseyn recommend networking during a job search without being salesy?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends aiming for one to two real networking conversations per day during the job search. to avoid being salesy, he says to ask questions and be genuinely interested in the other person rather than immediately needing something. he suggests starting with flattery, noticing something specific they do, then asking for a short three-minute conversation with one question.

where does taylor desseyn say people should look for jobs when big tech is laying off?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says startups and high-growth companies are still hiring even though big tech is laying people off. he recommends getting plugged into local startup ecosystems by looking up entrepreneur centers like the national entrepreneur center in nashville. before applying to any job, he suggests checking linkedin for common connections who can refer or vouch for you.
