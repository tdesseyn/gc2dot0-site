finding a job through community w/ appwrites nishant jain

guest: nishant jain, integration engineer at appwrite

  • join a community early since it can expose you to opportunities and knowledge you did not know existed
  • share your work in public through blogs and videos because small projects can catch a founder or hiring manager's attention
  • hackathons help you bond with a team fast and simulate real work pressure in a short timeframe
  • hackathons do not have to be complex or competitive, a simple idea with passion behind it can win and make an impact
  • dont be afraid to dm people for one on one conversations since most people in tech communities genuinely want to help

questions this episode answers

what does nishant jain say about building in public to get job opportunities?

on guidance counselor 2.0, nishant jain says sharing your work publicly through blog posts and videos on social media can directly lead to job offers. his first job came from a single video he posted about creating a login form in five minutes, which caught the attention of a startup founder who messaged him in the evening and sent an offer letter by night. the founder also checked his github contributions and appreciated his public activity on social media.

what does nishant jain recommend about reaching out for one on one conversations?

on guidance counselor 2.0, nishant jain says you should never be shy about sending direct messages to people in tech communities asking for one on one calls. if you see someone who can help with career guidance or mentoring, just hit them with a dm and ask to get on a call to discuss things. people genuinely want to help because they remember being in your position seeking mentorship, so they will not deny your request for guidance.
