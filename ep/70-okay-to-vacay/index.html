questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about unlimited pto policies?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says unlimited pto policies can backfire because people need guidelines. when companies offer unlimited pto, employees often take less than 15 days off, whereas giving a specific number like 15 days leads people to actually take that amount. he personally is fine with unlimited pto and will test the limits, but he warns companies to track usage and encourage employees to take time off.

how should contractors handle time off according to taylor desseyn?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says if you are a 1099 or w2 contractor, you need to build time off into your hourly rate since you have no paid pto. you have to know your costs and budget your rate to cover some vacation time, because as a contractor you will not receive paid time off from the company.

what does taylor desseyn say is a sign of bad workplace culture?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says if taking time off is frowned upon at your organization, that is poor culture and you need to move on. he clarifies you should be strategic about when you take time off and not disappear before big deadlines, but a workplace that looks down on employees taking vacation is displaying bad culture.
