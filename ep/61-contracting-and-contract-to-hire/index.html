ep 61 · jul 30, 2021

contracting and contract to hire

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • know your full compensation package including base, bonus, pto, and 401k match before comparing it to a contract hourly rate
  • ask recruiters to build pto and two weeks of bench pay into long term contracts since they wont offer it unless you ask
  • build relationships and create content around the team or department you want to move into for internal transfers
  • bring up your performance reviews and market research on pay when negotiating a contract to hire conversion
  • being kind, communicative, and reliable on a contract job greatly increases your odds of getting converted to full time
