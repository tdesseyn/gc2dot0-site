grab bag episode w/tessa kriesel

guest: tessa kriesel

  • developers need about four to five touch points before they truly recognize and trust you
  • plan social events early in a conference so people see you multiple times before the final days
  • track every engagement in a tooling stack so you can show how devrel work contributed to a sale even if it was not the direct lead
  • use birds of a feather sessions at conferences to find and connect with your specific niche audience
  • build genuine relationships with developers instead of selling to them since they can tell when they are being pitched
