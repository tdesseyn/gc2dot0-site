questions this episode answers

what does tessa kriesel say about how many touchpoints developers need before they trust you?

on guidance counselor 2.0, tessa kriesel says developers need at least four to five touchpoints before they truly recognize and trust you. she recommends planning social events early in a conference so people see you multiple times before the final days. by the time developers encounter you on day three, they already think of you as a friend because they have seen you several times.

how does tessa kriesel track developer relations work that leads to sales?

on guidance counselor 2.0, tessa kriesel says she tracks every single engagement in her tooling stack so she can show how devrel work impacted revenue even if she was not the direct lead. when a big client signs, she can point to her place in the conversion path and demonstrate how her relationship-building contributed. this lets her report on revenue impact rather than just conversion numbers.

what does tessa kriesel recommend for finding your niche audience at conferences?

on guidance counselor 2.0, tessa kriesel recommends using birds of a feather sessions at conferences to find and connect with your specific niche audience. these are informal sessions where you invite conference attendees to talk about a particular topic, either through official conference setups or by tweeting the event hashtag and claiming a social space. she uses them to discover people already working in her field that she does not yet know.
