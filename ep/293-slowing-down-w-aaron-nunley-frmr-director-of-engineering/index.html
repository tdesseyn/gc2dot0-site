ep 293 · nov 13, 2023

slowing down w/aaron nunley frmr director of engineering

guest: aaron nunley, former director of engineering

  • treat a career break like a startup runway and know your burn rate before you stop working
  • schedule leisure time on your calendar the same way you schedule workouts or meetings
  • networking after you leave a job still counts, posting an update and reconnecting with old coworkers can surface opportunities
  • build relationships with cross functional people like sales reps, not just engineers, since they often hear about openings first
  • do not fall in love with a job opportunity before you finish interviewing or it can hurt your judgment and your negotiation
