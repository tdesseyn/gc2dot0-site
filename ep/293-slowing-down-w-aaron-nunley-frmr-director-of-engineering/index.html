guest: aaron nunley, former director of engineering

  • treat a career break like a startup runway and know your burn rate before you stop working

questions this episode answers

what does aaron nunley say about planning a career break?

on guidance counselor 2.0, aaron nunley says you should treat yourself or your household like a startup and understand your burn rate before taking a career break. he explains that you need to know how long you can keep going while depleting your savings, recognizing that you are spending money to save or rebuild your sanity. this approach requires either privilege, planning, or the right circumstances to have that financial cushion.

what does aaron nunley say about networking after leaving a job?

on guidance counselor 2.0, aaron nunley says networking after you leave a job still counts and can surface opportunities without needing a resume. he made one post on linkedin announcing his departure, which led to people reaching out via imessage, including former coworkers he had not talked to in years. he also emphasizes building relationships with cross-functional people like sales representatives, not just engineers, because they often hear about job openings first at their own organizations.
