questions this episode answers

how does greg lasseter measure progress in a job search?

on guidance counselor 2.0, greg lasseter says to measure progress by how many applications move to a screen, not by how many you send. volume alone tells you nothing, so track the rate at which applications turn into conversations.

how does greg lasseter use claude to prep for interviews?

on guidance counselor 2.0, greg lasseter explains that he finds out who is interviewing him, grabs their linkedin experience and feeds it into claude to prep. he also flips the fit question from am i a fit for this role to is this role a fit for me.
