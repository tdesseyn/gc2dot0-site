questions this episode answers

what does heather downing say contractors should do when they don't know something?

on guidance counselor 2.0, heather downing says contractors should be willing to say they don't know something instead of pretending. she recommends telling the client you haven't done it before and offering to research it if they're willing to pay for that research time. heather has never said no to a contractor who asked to be paid for research hours.

what does heather downing recommend for job seekers overwhelmed by interviews?

on guidance counselor 2.0, heather downing recommends time boxing your interview schedule instead of letting it take over every day. she suggests picking specific days and times, like mondays and wednesdays after five, and sticking to those boundaries. nobody worth working for will care that you only have limited availability.

how does heather downing say you should manage up with your manager?

on guidance counselor 2.0, heather downing says managing up means telling your boss what you need to be effective, not telling them what to do. she recommends coming to one on ones with what you've already done and what you plan to do next, so your manager only has to say yes or no. this approach makes you easier to manage because you're giving them actionable information instead of forcing them to figure everything out.
