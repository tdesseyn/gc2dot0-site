with taylor desseyn (solo)

questions this episode answers

how does taylor desseyn recommend freelancers get clients in a tough market?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends offering free tangible work to small businesses to get your foot in the door. for freelancers doing photography, videography, design or website work, he suggests doing the first project free to become sticky with a client. he cautions not to overload yourself with free work where you make no money, but to pick and choose strategically, and advises looking at businesses in your own life like your church or community for opportunities.

what does taylor desseyn say about contract work for job seekers right now?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says job seekers should be open to contracting, contract to hire and even part time work. he mentions that one community member landed a contract gig that turned into full time in two to three months. taylor himself is placing people in part time roles to get them in the door and sticky with companies, which can eventually turn into more work.

how should candidates prepare for interviews in the current job market according to taylor desseyn?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says candidates must show up prepared and can no longer be lazy in interviews. he explains that in the last two years people could skip researching the company and not ask questions, but now employers are being picky and have raised the bar. he shares an example of a candidate who answered why he wanted the job with just because i don't have one, which is not good enough anymore, and emphasizes candidates must research the company and ask questions.
