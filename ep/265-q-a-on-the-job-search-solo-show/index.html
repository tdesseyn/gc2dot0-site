ep 265 · aug 28, 2023

q&a on the job search (solo show)

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • offer free tangible work like design, photography or video to small businesses to build trust and get your foot in the door
  • don't overload yourself with free work, pick and choose where you give your time so you can still make money
  • look at your own life, your church, your pets, your community for small free projects you can use to build a portfolio
  • be willing to take contract or part time work since it can turn into a full time job within a few months
  • show up prepared to interviews, research the company and ask real questions since employers are being more selective now
