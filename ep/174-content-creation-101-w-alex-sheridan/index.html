content creation 101 w/ alex sheridan

guest: alex sheridan, founder of impact media marketing

  • start simple by recording yourself on a webcam or smartphone during normal conversations and turn that footage into posts
  • pick two or three content pillars tied to what your audience cares about instead of posting about everything
  • separate personal content from private content so you share real experience without oversharing your life
  • if you are camera shy use text overlays and voiceover instead of talking straight into the camera
  • download tiktok and spend time learning basic recording and auto captions as the easiest way to start making video

questions this episode answers

what does alex sheridan recommend as the easiest way to start creating video content?

on guidance counselor 2.0, alex sheridan says the easiest video strategy is to record yourself during normal zoom calls or conversations using just your smartphone or webcam. you press record when a conversation starts, then chop up the recording afterward into short clips for posting. this lets you create content without setting aside extra time or trying to manufacture something artificial, because you're just documenting what you're already doing throughout your day.

what does alex sheridan say about choosing what to post on social media?

on guidance counselor 2.0, alex sheridan says you need to pick one to three content pillars focused on what your target audience cares about, rather than posting about everything in your life. a big mistake beginners make is trying to share every random thing that happens to them. instead, decide who you want to reach and what you want to accomplish, then focus your posts on topics that matter to that specific audience and that you're also excited about.

what does alex sheridan say if you are nervous about being on camera?

on guidance counselor 2.0, alex sheridan says you do not have to look directly into the camera and talk. you can record yourself writing in a notepad and add a voiceover explaining your message, or film yourself walking into work with text overlays and audio added later. he also recommends downloading tiktok and learning its auto captions feature, which lets you add titles and captions without delivering your message directly to the camera.
