ep 101 · jan 12, 2022

interview feedback

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • expect no interview feedback most of the time since over half of interviews end with little to no response from hiring teams
  • wait about 24 to 48 hours before following up with a recruiter and 48 to 72 hours before following up with a hiring manager
  • always work through your recruiter instead of dming the hiring manager directly or you risk getting rejected on the spot
  • email or text for follow ups since those get seen faster than dms or phone calls
  • never bash a previous employer in an interview since getting defensive about feedback can get you rejected on the spot

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about expecting interview feedback?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says candidates should go into interviews expecting not to get feedback because more than 50 percent of the time there is almost no feedback from hiring managers. he acknowledges this is a broken part of the hiring process but emphasizes it is the reality. recruiters are often scared to give feedback, and when they do, it is mostly vague.

how long should you wait before following up after an interview, according to taylor desseyn?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn says if you are working with a recruiter, wait 24 hours before following up, and at most 48 hours. if you are working directly with a hiring manager, give them 48 hours or maybe 72 hours before you follow up. he recommends using email or text rather than direct messages or phone calls because those get seen faster.

what does taylor desseyn say about contacting hiring managers directly during the interview process?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says if you are working with a recruiter, you should only work through the recruiter and never go around them to message hiring managers directly. he warns that the moment you bypass the recruiter and contact the hiring manager yourself, you could be immediately rejected. always work through the proper channels with your recruiter.
