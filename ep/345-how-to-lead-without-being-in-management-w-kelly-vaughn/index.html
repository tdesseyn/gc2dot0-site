how to lead without being in management w/ kelly vaughn, director of eng

guest: kelly vaughn, director of engineering

  • think beyond your assigned task and consider the impact your work has on the wider organization
  • amplify and support your teammates instead of only promoting your own work to build real influence
  • ask for feedback often and be willing to accept it even when it is delivered poorly or bluntly
  • work on genuine weaknesses like being defensive or abrasive instead of writing them off as just your personality
  • build emotional intelligence by managing your own reactions and stepping away before responding in heated moments
