using ai for mock interviews

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • upload your resume into an ai project so it has context before you ask it questions
  • ask ai what skills or tools are missing from your resume based on your actual day to day work
  • use voice chat with ai to brain dump your background when you dont know how to start your resume
  • run mock interviews with ai as the hiring manager and have it critique what landed and what was weak after each answer
  • use the star method with ai by answering situation task action and result one at a time to get a polished answer

questions this episode answers

how does taylor desseyn recommend using ai voice chat for resume writing?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to upload your resume into an ai project first so it has context, then use voice chat to have ai interview you about your current role and accomplishments. he explains this helps people who struggle to start writing their resume because they can brain dump their background conversationally instead of staring at a blank page. he demonstrates having claude ask him questions about his tech stack, metrics, and responsibilities one at a time to build out missing resume sections.

what does taylor desseyn recommend for mock interview practice with ai?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to set up ai as a hiring manager for a specific role and have it ask interview questions one at a time, then critique each answer for what landed well, what was weak, and how to improve. he emphasizes asking ai to point out when you skip parts of behavioral questions, especially the action portion of star method answers. taylor says most people think they can step into interviews and crush them, but only a few can, so practicing five to ten minutes daily with ai could be monumental.

how does taylor desseyn suggest using ai for career exploration and job targeting?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to use ai voice chat to talk through what you want to do next when you feel torn between different paths. he demonstrates asking claude whether to pursue head of community, vp of marketing, or other roles, and having it analyze which titles match his actual work scope versus how companies structure those positions. taylor also recommends asking ai to pull job descriptions and cross-reference them against your resume to identify skill gaps, then using voice to discuss where you're lacking rather than just reading text output.
