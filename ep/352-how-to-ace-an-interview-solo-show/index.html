how to ace an interview - solo show

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • bring printed resume and pen and paper to interviews so you are not distracted by typing on screen
  • research the company and the people interviewing you so you can turn the interview into a conversation
  • prepare three to four clear bullet points on your background tied directly to the company's specific needs
  • quantify your accomplishments with real numbers and be ready to explain details, since vague claims get challenged
  • after each interview keep a journal of what went well and what to improve to build your own study guide

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about taking notes during virtual interviews?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says you should use pen and paper instead of typing on screen during video interviews. multiple engineering managers have told him that watching someone type while talking is incredibly distracting, but writing notes by hand is less so. he recommends having a printed resume, pen and paper ready for every interview.

what does taylor desseyn recommend doing after every interview to improve?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to create a google doc journal after every single interview. write down what went well, like staying calm or asking good questions, and what didn't go well, like missing a question about a specific technology. the things you didn't do well become your study guide for the next interview, and over time you build an ongoing brain dump document that helps you process and improve.
