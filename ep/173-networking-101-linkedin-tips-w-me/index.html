networking 101 & linkedin tips w/ me :)

guest: me :)

  • use a dot card or qr code at conferences to cut friction instead of relying on traditional business cards
  • before a conference get your linkedin, twitter, or instagram fully set up so people can connect with you right away
  • pick a handful of sessions, speakers, or people you want to meet at a conference instead of trying to do everything
  • on linkedin fix your banner photo, title, and featured section since these are what recruiters and connections see first
  • your linkedin profile is searchable by recruiters using keywords, while your resume usually is not

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about using business cards at conferences?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says traditional business cards don't work anymore because people throw them away or ignore them. he recommends using a dot card instead, which is a 20 dollar card you can bump against someone's phone to share all your social media profiles. he believes this eliminates the friction of awkwardly exchanging contact information and lets people connect on their own terms.

what does taylor desseyn say is the difference between linkedin and your resume for job searching?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says your linkedin profile is searchable by recruiters while your resume usually is not. he explains that recruiters can type in keywords like dotnet developer and a location to find candidates on linkedin, making it top of the funnel for marketing yourself. your resume might be on dice or other sites but recruiters may never find it, so linkedin is the most searchable tool you have.
