questions this episode answers

how does chance strickland say early career job seekers should approach opportunities?

on guidance counselor 2.0, chance strickland says early career people should take opportunities that pop up instead of being systematic. he explains that when you are newer in your career people won't seek you out as much, so you have to take risks and not be picky. being systematic about career moves only becomes possible later once you have experience and understand your value.

what does chance strickland say about knowing your reason for leaving a job?

on guidance counselor 2.0, chance strickland says you need to know specifically what you are looking for before you leave a role. he explains that when he left remix he wanted to feel more connected to consumer apps, ship faster with real deadlines, and work in the messier environment of a startup. having clear goals about what you need helps you make the right move instead of just jumping blindly.

how does chance strickland gauge the current tech job market conditions?

on guidance counselor 2.0, chance strickland says he gauges the job market by tracking how many recruiter emails he gets each week. he noticed that during the worst of the layoffs those emails completely dried up, but recently they have started coming back. he also believes the market is not as bad as the vibes suggest, noting that most layoffs hit giant tech companies or unprofitable venture backed startups while smaller sustainable businesses kept hiring quietly.
