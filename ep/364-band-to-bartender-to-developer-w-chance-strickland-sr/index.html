ep 364 · may 8, 2024

band to bartender to developer w/ chance strickland sr engineer at replo

guest: chance strickland, sr engineer at replo

  • treat early career moves like gigs you dont know how a show will go but you learn by talking to people and showing up
  • when recruiter outreach dries up or picks back up thats a real signal for gauging how the job market is shifting
  • layoffs hit mostly giant tech companies and unprofitable venture backed startups while smaller sustainable companies kept hiring quietly
  • if youre early career expect to take opportunities that pop up instead of being picky since experience is what lets you get systematic later
  • know your specific reason for leaving a role before you jump so you dont make a move without a clear why
