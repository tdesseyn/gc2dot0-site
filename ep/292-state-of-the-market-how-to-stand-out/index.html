ep 292 · nov 10, 2023

state of the market & how to stand out

guest: dan digangi, software engineering manager, velocity team at activecampaign

  • connect with every person you interview with, even after a rejection, since you could move up the list if other candidates fall through
  • when job searching, reach out to second and third degree connections instead of only asking first degree contacts for referrals
  • look beyond big name companies since startups often hire people with less experience and offer fast skill growth
  • during interviews, turn the q&a into a conversation by tying your answers and questions back to your own relevant experience
  • connect with recruiters at target companies with no note, then post content regularly so they see your work in their feed over time
