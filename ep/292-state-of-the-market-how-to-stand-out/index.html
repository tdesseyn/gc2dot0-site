ep 292 · nov 10, 2023

state of the market & how to stand out

guest: dan digangi, software engineering manager, velocity team at activecampaign

  • connect with every person you interview with, even after a rejection, since you could move up the list if other candidates fall through
  • when job searching, reach out to second and third degree connections instead of only asking first degree contacts for referrals
  • look beyond big name companies since startups often hire people with less experience and offer fast skill growth
  • during interviews, turn the q&a into a conversation by tying your answers and questions back to your own relevant experience
  • connect with recruiters at target companies with no note, then post content regularly so they see your work in their feed over time

questions this episode answers

how does dan digangi recommend reaching out to your network when job searching?

on guidance counselor 2.0, dan digangi recommends reaching out to second and third degree connections instead of only asking your immediate network for help. he explains that your first connections are friends and family, but you should keep hopping the bubble outward by asking for introductions to people in your target area. casting multiple lines increases your chances of finding opportunities.

what does dan digangi say about looking at startups versus big tech companies?

on guidance counselor 2.0, dan digangi says job seekers should look beyond big name companies like fang because startups offer different advantages. startups typically hire people with less experience because it costs less, and when they succeed and raise more money they build out their engineering teams. working at a startup gives you faster skill growth and more hands-on experience than you would get at a larger company.
