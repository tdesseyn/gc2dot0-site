questions this episode answers

what does brittany garrett say about unconventional career paths in tech?

on guidance counselor 2.0, brittany garrett says people should gather their talents and press forward, knowing their different experiences create unique value. she emphasizes that taking varied paths creates amazing stories and allows you to apply yourself in ways people who took straight paths cannot. she tells job seekers to use their unconventional journey to their advantage because it makes them extremely unique and special.

how does brittany garrett recommend networking for junior developers?

on guidance counselor 2.0, brittany garrett says you have to put yourself out there even if it feels uncomfortable, because growth only happens outside your comfort zone. she recommends adding people on linkedin, introducing yourself, and following up to ask for a meeting to hear their advice or journey. she explains that creating dialogue allows you to become a three-dimensional person, letting others hear how you think and who you are beyond a profile.

what do taylor and brittany say about being a people person in tech roles?

on guidance counselor 2.0, brittany garrett and taylor desseyn agree that being a people person is actually an asset in tech, not a limitation. brittany explains that understanding different personalities, conflict resolution styles, and cultures makes you a stronger candidate because you will collaborate with different teammates and functions across the company. taylor adds that companies need diverse thinking and that hiring only one type of person limits what gets accomplished.
