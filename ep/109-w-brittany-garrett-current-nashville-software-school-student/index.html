guest: brittany garrett, current student at nashville software school

  • treat an unconventional career path as a unique asset, not something to hide from employers
  • if you are a people person, that skill transfers well into tech roles that need collaboration across teams
  • go into hiring conversations with an open mind and set aside assumptions about someone's background
  • network by reaching out on linkedin, introducing yourself, and following up to ask for a real conversation
  • build a personal vehicle around something you love to consistently meet new people in tech
