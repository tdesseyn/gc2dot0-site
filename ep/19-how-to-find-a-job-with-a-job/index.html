how to find a job with a job

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • find recruiters on linkedin by searching the word recruiter and favor those with two years at their company and two years recruiting
  • once you find a few good recruiters bcc them together on job leads so they all know what you are seeing
  • ask every recruiter you talk to for one or two more recruiter contacts to keep expanding your network
  • log into job board profiles like indeed and dice every single day since recent activity pushes you to the top of recruiter searches
  • spend at least 15 to 30 minutes a day networking on linkedin by posting, commenting, and engaging to stay visible to your community

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn recommend for finding good recruiters to work with?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends using the search bar on linkedin and typing the word recruiter, then looking for people with the most recommendations who appear to be seasoned professionals. he uses the two and two rule: recruiters who have been at the same company for two years and have been recruiting for two years. once you find one good recruiter, ask them to connect you with one or two other recruiters to expand your network.

how does taylor desseyn suggest working with multiple recruiters efficiently?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends putting all your recruiters into a blind carbon copy email group and sending them job leads you find. this way all the recruiters see what opportunities you are looking at without knowing they are all being contacted together. he suggests keeping the message general, asking if they know anyone or if they are working on those specific jobs.
