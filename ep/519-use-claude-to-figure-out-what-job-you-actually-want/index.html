use claude to figure out what job you actually want

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • ask claude to figure out what role you actually want instead of just what you are qualified for
  • start by asking claude to assume it knows nothing about you, then have it ask what it needs to build your job search background
  • have claude ask you five to seven questions about your values, priorities and non-negotiables one at a time before moving on
  • once you have a target role profile, paste in real job descriptions and ask claude to score the fit one to ten and flag yellow flags
  • ask claude what job boards or niche communities fit your background since most people do not know where to start looking

questions this episode answers

how does taylor recommend figuring out what job role you actually want using ai?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends asking ai to help you figure out what you actually want in your next role, not just what you're qualified for. he says to have the ai ask you five to seven questions about your values, priorities and non-negotiables, answering them one at a time. these questions should cover things like when you felt most alive at work, what drained you, your ideal reporting structure, compensation priorities, team size preferences and immediate dealbreakers.

what does taylor say about using ai to evaluate job descriptions?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to paste real job descriptions into ai after building your target role profile and ask it to score the fit on a scale of one to ten. he recommends asking the ai to flag any yellow flags or red flags based on your stated priorities and non-negotiables. this helps you see where a job falls short before you waste time applying, like when a role has the right title but the actual work doesn't match what you want to do day to day.
