working with recruiters (solo show)

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • reach out to agency recruiters first since they work across many clients and often know the market better than corporate recruiters
  • remember agency recruiters only get paid when they place someone, which shapes how responsive they are
  • text or email recruiters instead of calling since most are on calls with other candidates
  • if a recruiter goes quiet, send a direct message asking for honest feedback so you can move on in your search
  • when connecting with recruiters on linkedin always include a personal note instead of a blank request

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about reaching out to agency versus corporate recruiters first?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says job seekers should spray and pray with agency recruiters first rather than corporate recruiters. agency recruiters have better market awareness because they work with 10, 15, 20, or 25 clients at one time, while corporate recruiters only work for one company. he recommends searching for technical recruiters in your area on linkedin and messaging every one of them to start your job search.

how should you follow up with a recruiter who went silent, according to taylor desseyn?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn recommends sending a direct message saying that you understand you are probably not a fit and asking them to confirm so you can move on with your job search. he explains that recruiters are sometimes just scared to give feedback, and this word track gives them permission to be honest. he also says to text or email recruiters instead of calling because most are on calls with other candidates and will not answer.

what does taylor desseyn say about connecting with recruiters on linkedin?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says you should always send a personalized note when connecting with recruiters on linkedin instead of sending a blank connection request. he explains that if you connect without a note, you disappear into their followers and they will not notice you. he also notes that sending a note with each connection request prevents linkedin from flagging you as spammy, allowing you to connect with more people.
