ep 119 · feb 28, 2022

the importance of partnering with a recruiting firm w/ jessica kashyap

guest: jessica kashyap, recruiting leader focused on accounting and finance hiring initiatives at vaco

  • partnering exclusively with one recruiting firm creates accountability since there is no one else to point fingers at if hiring goes wrong
  • mixed messaging across multiple agencies confuses candidates and makes it hard to control the narrative in the marketplace
  • candidates have options and will only commit to two or so opportunities that sound most enticing, so slow processes lose talent
  • speed matters, some hiring cycles moved from friday interview to offer within about five business days
  • a good recruiter will tell you honestly when they are not the right fit instead of forcing a square peg into a round hole

questions this episode answers

how fast do hiring processes need to move in 2022 according to jessica kashyap?

on guidance counselor 2.0, jessica kashyap says candidates will only commit to the two most enticing opportunities because their time is valuable, so slow processes lose talent. she has seen successful hires happen in five business days, with companies interviewing on a friday and extending an offer by the following week. candidates have so many options that if a company cannot move quickly within about one week maximum, they will lose strong people to faster-moving employers.

what does jessica kashyap say about recruiters who force bad job fits?

on guidance counselor 2.0, jessica kashyap says a good recruiter will honestly tell you when they are not the right fit for your needs instead of forcing a square peg into a round hole. she advises job seekers to run away from recruiters who corner them and pressure them to take a position, because those recruiters only care about the placement fee. recruiters who are willing to walk away from a bad fit actually demonstrate self-awareness and are worth leaning into more.
