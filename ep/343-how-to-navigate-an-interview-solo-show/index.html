how to navigate an interview (solo show)

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • research the company and tie your own experiences or personal connection into why you care about their mission
  • research your interviewers on linkedin and google so you can find common ground and shift the conversation
  • bring a one page sizzle sheet with three to four bullet points connecting your background to the role
  • keep a pen and paper in front of you to take notes instead of typing, since hiring managers find typing more distracting and suspicious
  • print your resume, close extra tabs, and silence your phone so you stay focused and avoid blanking during the interview
