ep 405 · jan 20, 2025

the importance of the follow up w/ liz schwerd, sr. recruiter

guest: liz schwerd, sr. recruiter, founder of a recruiting consulting firm

  • apply to fewer jobs but follow up on each one, aiming for a higher hit rate instead of just blasting applications
  • after applying, message the recruiter directly and include the job or rec id number so they can route you to the right person fast
  • if you get no response after a cold application, give it a week or two before reaching out to the likely hiring manager, not before
  • once a recruiter has actually contacted you, never jump over their head to the hiring manager, that move backfires
  • when networking feels awkward, pick learning or industry events over pure networking mixers so conversation happens naturally around shared interest
