networking, becoming an independent, and paypal w/ kent c. dodds

guest: kent c. dodds, remix

  • build a public reputation through speaking and content creation so opportunities come to you instead of chasing applications

questions this episode answers

what does kent c. dodds say about how he got jobs without applying?

on guidance counselor 2.0, kent c. dodds from remix explains that he only applied to one job in his career, his first role at domo. every other opportunity came through networking, primarily from speaking at meetups and conferences. companies reached out to him directly or coworkers recommended him because they saw him demonstrate his skills publicly. he got his paypal role after tweeting about react and a paypal employee invited him to apply.

what does kent dodds recommend for preparing for technical interviews?

on guidance counselor 2.0, kent c. dodds from remix advises candidates to ask the recruiter what the interview will be like, since recruiters want you to succeed and won't waste time hoping you fail. if the company uses leetcode style challenges, he says you should study books on data structures even though he thinks those interviews are stupid and don't predict job performance. for better interviews, he recommends preparing to show what you are genuinely best at rather than memorizing answers.

why does kent dodds say open source contributions help your career?

on guidance counselor 2.0, kent c. dodds from remix says open source makes you a subject matter expert on the library you built or contributed to, which changes how people perceive your skill level. you get free code reviews from experienced developers and exposure to different coding styles, which builds experience quickly. he adds that you can put concrete numbers on your resume like millions of weekly downloads, and his paypal director spent their entire lunch interview talking about kent's open source projects.
