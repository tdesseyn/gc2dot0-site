wild a** market w/ jermaine murray

guest: jermaine murray, technical recruiter at wealthsimple and career coach

  • ats rejection emails are automated but a real recruiter made the decision to reject you
  • if you keep getting rejected after many applications, revisit your resume and the roles you are targeting instead of just applying more
  • look beyond software development roles like data analyst, business analyst, or project coordinator to break into tech
  • use boolean string searches with your core skills on job sites to find relevant postings even without knowing the exact job title
  • when rejecting candidates after multiple interviews, offer a short feedback call instead of just a rejection email
