questions this episode answers

what does jermaine murray say about ats systems rejecting resumes automatically?

on guidance counselor 2.0, jermaine murray says the myth that ats systems use ai to auto-reject resumes is false. he explains that while rejection emails may be automated, a real recruiter reviews every resume and manually pushes the button to send the rejection. most companies use standard systems like greenhouse, lever, or jazz hr that require human decision-making, not automatic filtering.

what does jermaine murray recommend if you get rejected from hundreds of jobs?

on guidance counselor 2.0, jermaine murray says if you are getting rejected after 800 applications, the problem is likely your resume or the roles you are targeting, not the volume of applications. he quotes jay z saying there is nothing wrong with your aim, sometimes you just have to change the target. instead of applying to more jobs, job seekers should revisit how they present their skills or look at different types of roles.

how can job seekers find tech roles without knowing the exact job title?

on guidance counselor 2.0, jermaine murray and taylor desseyn recommend using boolean string searches with your core skills on job sites to discover relevant postings. murray explains that recruiters use these x-ray searches to find candidates throughout the web, and job seekers can use the same technique on indeed or google to find roles that match their skills even if they do not know the specific job title. desseyn adds that people should look beyond software development at roles like data analyst, business analyst, or project coordinator.
