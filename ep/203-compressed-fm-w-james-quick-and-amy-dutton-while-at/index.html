questions this episode answers

what does james quick say about connecting with people before a conference?

on guidance counselor 2.0, james quick says to find people on twitter before a conference and reach out to set up a coffee or meetup in advance. he recommends finding two to three people to text constantly during the event so you have someone to navigate awkward situations with. this way you already know a few people when you arrive, which makes the whole conference experience much easier.

why does james quick say consistent social media posting matters for networking?

on guidance counselor 2.0, james quick says it takes eight different touch points before someone actually becomes a fan and cares about what you do. if you keep posting consistently, people see your tweets, videos, pictures and livestreams repeatedly until they follow and engage with you. when james posted about losing his job, he got at least a hundred messages because he had built that network through consistent social media presence, with twenty to thirty companies saying they wanted to hire him.
