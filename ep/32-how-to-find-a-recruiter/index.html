how to find a recruiter

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • use the linkedin search bar with terms like recruiter or technical recruiter plus your skill set, then filter by city
  • consider skipping recruiters with under two years experience since it often takes about two years for a recruiter to find their footing in a market
  • check a recruiter's linkedin profile first and reach out to ones who show personality rather than a boring profile
  • always customize your connection note every time you message a recruiter on linkedin
  • ask a recruiter you already trust for referrals to other good recruiters in your market or city

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about using linkedin to find a recruiter?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to use the linkedin search bar with terms like recruiter, it recruiter, technical recruiter, or recruiter plus your specific skill set, then filter by your city. he recommends setting aside an afternoon to go through profiles carefully. he also advises checking each recruiter's profile and only reaching out to those who show personality, not boring profiles, and to always customize your connection note.

how much experience should a recruiter have before reaching out, according to taylor desseyn?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends not messaging recruiters who have under two years of experience. he explains that it typically takes about two years for recruiters to find their footing and figure out all the moving parts within a city and market.

what does taylor desseyn say about asking recruiters for referrals to other recruiters?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends asking a recruiter you already like and trust for referrals to other good recruiters they know in your market. he explains that if you have a recruiter who operates well, you should ask them who else they recommend in the area and get those introductions, which helps you build a network of quality recruiters.
