questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about six month guarantees in hiring?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says that if a company wants a six month guarantee on a hire, they should use contract to hire instead of direct hire. he explains that a recruiting firm's job is to find good talent, not to keep that person at the company for six months, which is the employer's responsibility. he argues that six months of working together reveals far more than a two hour interview ever could.

how does taylor desseyn say companies should structure their interview process?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends keeping the hiring process to two interviews within two weeks or companies risk losing the candidate. he suggests spending the first 15 to 20 minutes of the first interview selling the opportunity and the project before diving into technical questions. the second round should be a one to two hour technical screen that also judges culture fit, followed by an offer.

what is the difference between contracting and consulting according to taylor desseyn?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn explains that contracting is used to fill specific skill gaps or cover projects when you have holes in your organization, like needing a senior developer for a year to bounce between projects. consulting is when you need someone to manage an entire project because you don't have the bandwidth to hire and manage a whole team yourself. contracting fills roles while consulting takes over project management.
