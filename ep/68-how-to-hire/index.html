ep 68 · aug 16, 2021

how to hire

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • if a company wants a six month guarantee on a hire, push for contract to hire instead of direct hire
  • contract to hire works as a working interview since six months reveals more than a two hour interview ever could
  • use contracting to fill specific skill gaps or projects, use consulting when you need someone to manage an entire project for you
  • sell the opportunity in the first 15 to 20 minutes of an interview before diving into technical questions
  • keep the hiring process to two interviews within two weeks or you risk losing the candidate
