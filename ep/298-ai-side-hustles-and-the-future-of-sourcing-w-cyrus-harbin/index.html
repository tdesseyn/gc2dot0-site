questions this episode answers

what does cyrus harbin say about pre-sales roles like sales engineering for people struggling with coding?

on guidance counselor 2.0, cyrus harbin says if you become impatient trying to learn software engineering, consider the pre-sales space like sales engineering or solutions consulting. he did a sales engineering bootcamp and got into the industry as a solutions engineer conducting technical demos and presentations to executives. his background as a speaker made solutions engineering a better fit than pure coding, and the role still pays well.

what does cyrus harbin's tool optipli do for job seekers?

on guidance counselor 2.0, cyrus harbin explains that optipli is an ai tool that finds companies hiring, tailors the keywords in your resume to match each specific job description, and applies to roles automatically. it creates a dashboard to track applications, rejections and scheduled interviews so you only have to focus on showing up for interviews. the tool handles the administrative grind of job searching, from applying to ten companies daily up to higher tiers.

what does cyrus harbin say about posting content consistently without worrying about likes?

on guidance counselor 2.0, cyrus harbin says stop checking likes and comments and just put content out to help people because your content is for the world, not you. he notes that his viral posts were never the ones he expected and often took time to blow up later as sleeper content. he compares it to going to the gym where the compound effect of consistency matters more than immediate results, and says focus on adding value to your community rather than chasing dopamine hits or comparing yourself to others.
