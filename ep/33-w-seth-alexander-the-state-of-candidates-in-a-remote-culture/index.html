ep 33 · mar 19, 2021

w/seth alexander & the state of candidates in a remote culture

guest: seth alexander, software engineer at evidence care

  • expect recruiter outreach to come through every channel, even emails not listed on your public profiles or linkedin
  • set a personal filter for how much more pay would justify the risk of switching jobs before engaging with offers
  • ask about compensation even when happy in your role so you know your market value for future raises or promotions
  • location based pay is shifting as companies pay for the value an engineer provides, not where they live
  • share market outreach data with your leadership so the company stays aware of retention risk and can respond with transparency

questions this episode answers

what does seth alexander say about how much extra pay justifies switching jobs?

on guidance counselor 2.0, seth alexander says he uses a personal filter of 20k more than his current salary before he'll even discuss an opportunity. he explains that a 10k bump he'd likely get internally anyway over time, so it's not worth the risk of switching companies. the 20k threshold makes the risk of leaving a company he likes worthwhile to explore.

why does seth alexander ask about compensation even when he's happy at his job?

on guidance counselor 2.0, seth alexander explains he asks about market offers even when not job seeking to understand his worth for internal discussions about promotions and raises. he also shares the outreach data with his cto and ceo so the company stays aware of retention risk and can be competitive. he believes this transparency helps the company understand what recruiters are offering their engineers.
