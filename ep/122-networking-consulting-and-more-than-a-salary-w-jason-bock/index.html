questions this episode answers

what does jason bock say about independent contracting versus working for a consulting firm?

on guidance counselor 2.0, jason bock says independent contractors get all the income but must handle their own taxes and insurance, so they need a financial buffer in case a client suddenly ends the contract. working for a consulting firm offers a middle ground because the firm provides benefits like 401k and health insurance while still giving exposure to different projects and industries. he worked for consulting companies for 23 years and appreciated not having to do all the administrative work himself.

how does jason bock recommend networking at conferences as an introvert?

on guidance counselor 2.0, jason bock says introverts should aim to make one or two genuine connections at a conference instead of trying to meet fifty people. he explains that trying to work the room feels fake and draining for introverts, but a single meaningful conversation can lead to big opportunities. he shares that one half-hour conversation at a conference lunch led to a huge consulting gig, and a relationship he maintained with ted neward over fifteen years eventually helped him land his current job at rocket mortgage.

what does jason bock say about evaluating culture and salary when choosing a job?

on guidance counselor 2.0, jason bock says people should weigh salary against other variables like health insurance, 401k, time off and company culture. he explains that salary is important but it is just one part of an equation that should include what matters most to you personally. he recommends asking for equal interview time so candidates can ask questions and vet the company just as much as the company vets them, suggesting a 30-30 split instead of leaving only fifteen minutes at the end.
