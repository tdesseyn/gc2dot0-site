ep 122 · mar 7, 2022

networking, consulting, and more than a salary w/ jason bock

guest: jason bock, developer advocate at rocket mortgage

  • weigh salary against things like insurance, 401k, time off, and culture when choosing a job
  • independent contracting means more income but you handle your own taxes and insurance, so build a financial buffer
  • working through a consulting firm can offer a middle ground between full freelance and a single full-time role
  • as an introvert, aim to make one or two real connections at an event instead of trying to meet everyone
  • ask for equal time in interviews so you can ask questions and vet the company just as much as they vet you
