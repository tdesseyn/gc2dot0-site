with taylor desseyn (solo)

questions this episode answers

how many recruiters should a job seeker work with right now, according to taylor desseyn?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says job seekers should partner with one or two local recruiters plus three or four recruiters in hot tech cities across the country. local recruiters provide eyes and ears for networking and company reputation information in your city. recruiters in cities like nashville, atlanta, new york, and san francisco can connect you to remote opportunities nationally.

what does taylor desseyn recommend for quickly screening recruiters before partnering with them?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends having short calls with many recruiters to find ones you actually like working with. he suggests three to ten minute calls, nothing longer than ten minutes, to quickly determine which recruiters are a good fit. he acknowledges you have to kiss a lot of frogs and go through duds, but finding the right recruiters is worth the effort for career success.

what does taylor desseyn tell hiring managers about candidate lead times right now?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn tells hiring managers the candidate lead time is two to four weeks right now, especially for senior technical talent. he advises hiring managers to engage recruiting firms as soon as possible because of these longer timelines. he also notes that hiring managers no longer need to work with recruiting firms only in their own city since remote recruiting now works nationally.
