ep 57 · jul 21, 2021

hiring manager and job seeker strategy right now

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • job seekers should keep one or two trusted local recruiters plus three or four recruiters in other hot tech cities
  • use short calls of three to ten minutes to quickly figure out which recruiters you actually like working with
  • local recruiters give you eyes and ears in your city, so lean on them for local networking and reputation info
  • hiring managers no longer need to use a recruiting firm in their own city since remote recruiting now works nationally
  • expect candidate lead time of two to four weeks right now, especially for senior technical talent, so start the search early
