ep 104 · jan 21, 2022

w/stephon parker, csm & engineering manager at eventbrite

guest: stephon parker, csm and engineering manager at eventbrite

  • before hiring junior engineers make sure your team has the structure and mentorship to support them or you risk setting them up to fail
  • leaders can build buy in for hiring junior talent by showing it creates loyalty and grows future staff level and leadership talent from within
  • directors and vps should regularly talk directly with individual contributors instead of relying only on managers to relay information
  • ship work more often in smaller pieces and iterate rather than waiting until everything is finished before releasing it
  • a good performance review or termination should never surprise an employee if the manager has been consistently honest about expectations
