the benefits of changing companies w/ james q quick, content creator

guest: james q quick, content creator

  • when someone offers to help or says reach out, actually follow up since most people never do
  • invest in your community through meetups, livestreams, and conferences instead of thinking of it as networking
  • research salary ranges, required skills, and experience levels for target roles, then work to fill the gaps
  • staying at one company can cap your salary and growth, so compare offers and be open to moving
  • after a layoff give yourself grace and a day to breathe before starting to research and apply again

questions this episode answers

what does james q quick say about reaching out to people who offer help?

on guidance counselor 2.0, james q quick says that when speakers at panels, podcasts or boot camps tell you to reach out if you have questions, only about 2 percent of people actually do it. he emphasizes that he sincerely means it when he says reach out, and that people who do follow up are the ones who get him most excited to help. this small effort of reaching out after someone offers can create real opportunities, but almost nobody does it.

how does james q quick define networking?

on guidance counselor 2.0, james q quick says he doesn't like the word networking and prefers calling it investing in your community. he explains this means showing up to linkedin live streams, twitter, discord, local meetups in person, and conferences. the key is networking starts before you need it, not when you need a job. you build relationships by just being present and being part of the conversation over time.

what does james q quick recommend doing when you feel stuck in your current job?

on guidance counselor 2.0, james q quick advises doing research on job opportunities that interest you, including salary ranges, required skill sets, years of experience and portfolio projects employers want. then work backwards to identify and fill the gaps in your own skills. he emphasizes this doesn't happen overnight but you need to start investing energy into getting to that next point. he also says there's almost always another job where you'd be just as happy and make more money.
