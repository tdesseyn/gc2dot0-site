ep 154 · jul 13, 2022

job hunting thoughts w/ john-michael mccasland

guest: john-michael mccasland, front end engineer at upscribe

  • set a clear salary goal before taking calls so you can gauge offers against something concrete
  • use a job specific email plus a trello board to track every touch point with recruiters
  • keep taking calls even when stretched thin because the right fit often shows up late in the process
  • ask interviewers directly if they have concerns about you to quickly learn where you stand
  • be upfront about your stress and interest level with a company if you want them to move fast and be honest back

questions this episode answers

what does john-michael mccasland say about setting salary expectations before a job search?

on guidance counselor 2.0, john-michael mccasland says he set a clear goal before his search, telling taylor he wanted to double his income. while he didn't double it, having that goal helped him and taylor set realistic expectations immediately. he also talked to many recruiters to understand his range, getting slightly different responses from each that helped him calibrate.

how does john-michael mccasland recommend organizing a job search with many recruiters?

on guidance counselor 2.0, john-michael mccasland says he created a job-specific email address and set up a trello board as a kanban-style system. he tracked every touch point with each recruiter and company through a flow he designed, treating it almost like a customer relationship management tool. he took around fifty recruiting calls during his search.

what does john-michael mccasland say about continuing to interview when you already have offers?

on guidance counselor 2.0, john-michael mccasland says a recruiter advised him to keep taking calls even when stretched thin if he hadn't found the perfect fit yet. he almost dropped the call with his current manager at upscribe because he was so stressed, but it turned out to be the absolute best match. he says the next two or three weeks might be stressful, but you might find the right role late in the process.
