ep 154 · jul 13, 2022

job hunting thoughts w/ john-michael mccasland

guest: john-michael mccasland, front end engineer at upscribe

  • set a clear salary goal before taking calls so you can gauge offers against something concrete
  • use a job specific email plus a trello board to track every touch point with recruiters
  • keep taking calls even when stretched thin because the right fit often shows up late in the process
  • ask interviewers directly if they have concerns about you to quickly learn where you stand
  • be upfront about your stress and interest level with a company if you want them to move fast and be honest back
