ep 334 · feb 19, 2024

tackling interviews w/ vitor oliveira, talent success manager at gun.io

guest: vitor oliveira, talent success manager at gun.io

  • treat interviewing as a learning journey where you apply to many jobs, fail sometimes, and adjust rather than expecting one interview to land the role
  • study three to five job descriptions for similar roles and mirror that language in your resume so recruiters see a fast match
  • build a company research doc for your top target companies so you can explain what they do and why you want in
  • answer tell me about yourself in under two minutes, tie it directly to the role, and find a personal connection with the interviewer
  • use the star method, situation task action result, and always close with a measurable outcome when describing projects or challenges
