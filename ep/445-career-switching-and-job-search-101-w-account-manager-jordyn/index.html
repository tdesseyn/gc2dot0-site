career switching and job search 101 w/ account manager, jordyn wallace

guest: jordyn wallace, account manager

  • join a job search community so you can see real wins and interviews happening daily instead of sitting in negativity
  • get someone else to review your resume and linkedin since you cannot see your own blind spots or weak signals
  • write your resume so a non technical reader can understand your title company and impact in under six seconds
  • show up consistently to the same meetup or community so people start recognizing you and familiarity builds naturally
  • set small daily goals like a number of people to engage with instead of only focusing on the end goal of getting hired
