career switching and job search 101 w/ account manager, jordyn wallace

guest: jordyn wallace, account manager

  • internal recruiters hire only for their own company, while agency recruiters work across many clients, so their net is cast much wider
  • talk to at least one agency recruiter and one internal recruiter a week, and come at a company you want from every angle you can
  • when a recruiter posts a job on linkedin you can usually see who posted it, so go to their profile and reach out directly
  • if a recruiter can't help right now, ask for one resume tip, one linkedin tip and how your salary ask compares to the market, then stay in touch
  • switching careers is scary: commit fully, learn the basics of the roles around you, and let people tell you about their work

questions this episode answers

what is the difference between an agency recruiter and an internal recruiter, according to jordyn wallace?

on guidance counselor 2.0, jordyn wallace, an account manager in agency recruiting, explains that an internal recruiter works for one company and recruits only for the roles that company assigns them. an agency recruiter has access to many clients, so their net is cast much wider across jobs, areas and industries. even when agency recruiters are assigned to one industry like tech and engineering, they can reach every client within it.

how does jordyn wallace suggest finding recruiters to reach out to?

jordyn wallace says to research recruiters on linkedin the same way you would research a company you want to work for: find people who work there and the recruiters linked to its job postings. when a recruiter posts a job on linkedin, you can usually see the internal or agency recruiter who posted it, go to their profile, and see other potential contacts at that company. she adds that the more you put on your own profile, like where you went to college, the more linkedin shows you people in common at a company.

what advice does jordyn wallace give people switching careers?

jordyn wallace says career transitioning is terrifying, but when she left real estate for recruiting she committed fully and set out to learn everything she could. she watched youtube explainers on roles like project manager and solutions architect, then learned by picking up the phone and letting people talk about their work. her advice to her younger self is to stop being so afraid and not make yourself smaller to fit in.
