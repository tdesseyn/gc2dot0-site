ep 303 · dec 6, 2023

the funnel of networking (solo show)

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • when you meet someone new, skip the long pitch and just ask their name and what they do
  • find out which social app someone actually uses and connect with them there right away
  • treat networking like a funnel: connect first, engage with their posts for months, then ask for something
  • if you avoid social media, you need daily coffees or lunches instead to keep meeting people
  • keep posting even when no one engages at first, it can take months before people start responding

questions this episode answers

how long should you engage with someone's posts before asking for help, according to taylor desseyn?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to engage with someone's content for three to six months before asking for anything. he describes networking as a funnel: connect with people first, engage with their posts consistently, and only then slide into direct messages to ask for something or schedule a conversation. he emphasizes that asking comes way down the funnel after months of engagement.

what does taylor desseyn say about posting content when no one is engaging?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says not to worry if no one engages with your posts for the first six months. he tells people to use that time to figure out their cadence and messaging without pressure. he shares that his own vlog took a year before people started messaging him about it, and advises treating the initial period as practice time to work out the kinks.
