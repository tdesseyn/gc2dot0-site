questions this episode answers

what does shauna kimball say about deciding whether to leave a bad job situation?

on guidance counselor 2.0, shauna kimball, senior manager of diversity inclusion at 2k, says you need to distinguish whether the problem is the company, yourself, or just the current situation before reacting. she recommends keeping a running list of frustrations alongside your wins so you can identify real patterns instead of making big decisions based on one emotional moment. shauna also suggests asking how you got to feeling your manager sucks, because often the real issue is an underlying feeling rather than the person themselves.

what does shauna kimball say about job market rejections right now?

on guidance counselor 2.0, shauna kimball says the current tech market rejections and delays are not personal, even though they feel that way. she notes people are going from one week to six months between roles, and emphasizes this is an industry-wide problem caused by macro economics, not something wrong with individual candidates. shauna urges job seekers with flexibility to be patient and remember the rejections are not about them.
