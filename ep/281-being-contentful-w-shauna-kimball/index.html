being contentful w/shauna kimball

guest: shauna kimball, senior manager, diversity inclusion for recruiting programs and diversity initiatives at 2k, and founder of everybody eats

  • separate whether a bad work situation is about the company, about you, or just the current moment before reacting
  • keep a running list of what frustrates you at work alongside your wins so you can spot real patterns, not just one bad day
  • when considering a new job, look at total offer like benefits, flexibility and stock, not just the cash bump
  • weigh how much real influence and leadership buy-in a role offers before chasing a title or a bigger paycheck
  • in a tough job market, remember rejections and delays are often about the industry, not a reflection of you personally
