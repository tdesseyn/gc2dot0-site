doing the job before you get the job w/ jason torres

guest: jason torres, host of the tech commute, former filmmaker now in developer advocacy and job search

  • earn the job by doing the work before you get hired, just like apprenticeships in film or trade industries
  • build genuine relationships early so people are willing to help you when you actually need it
  • lead interviews by asking questions and building rapport instead of waiting to be asked things
  • if you keep losing out on roles for the same reason, change your approach instead of repeating it
  • find a personal reason to keep going since that will outweigh the frustration of rejection
