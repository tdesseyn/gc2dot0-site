ep 426 · mar 10, 2025

community chats and meetup.com alternatives w/ founder, taz singh

guest: taz singh, founder of guild

  • treat community building like the creator economy, where the ecosystem pays through sponsors, not the organizers or members
  • founders misalign incentives when they start a community just to hire developers, then let it die after one event
  • mix event formats like tech talks, workshops, socials, and hack nights, each on its own natural schedule
  • surround yourself with strong performers in a community since who you spend time with shapes how much you improve
  • push any project past the easy first 90 percent, since the final 10 percent is where the real learning and growth happens

questions this episode answers

what does taz singh say about funding a tech community without losing money?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taz singh says he put seven thousand dollars of his own money into running toronto javascript, which he calls backwards. he built guild to work like youtube or twitch, where the ecosystem pays through sponsorship instead of charging community organizers or members. guild makes money from sponsors and recruitment, never from the community itself, so organizers can run events without personal financial loss.

what does taz singh say about never having to look for a job?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taz singh says he has never had to look for a job in his entire life because of community involvement. the community has known him well over the years and always wanted to work with him, giving him stable employment. he explains that if you surround yourself with well performers in a community you are more likely to perform fifteen percent better, while poor performers make you perform thirty five percent worse, so joining a community helps you improve alongside others.
