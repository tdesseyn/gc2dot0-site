restaurant, factory worker, hotel manager, software developer

guest: ray morrison, front-end software engineer at oc tanner

  • if you're waiting for the right time to pivot careers, that time will never feel comfortable, just start
  • keep applying even after a great interview, a dead week in your pipeline now means no calls later
  • when networking with distant connections, ask if they know anyone hiring instead of asking for a referral directly

questions this episode answers

what does ray morrison say about asking distant connections for job help?

on guidance counselor 2.0, ray morrison says when he reached out to a cousin he had never really talked to, he asked if the cousin knew anybody hiring instead of directly asking for a referral. ray explains that if you don't know someone well, asking do you know who's hiring? instead of give me a job is a much better approach. he mentions he would not even ask random people if they know who is hiring, only people he felt comfortable with like family.

what interview question does ray morrison recommend instead of asking about culture?

on guidance counselor 2.0, ray morrison says instead of asking what the company culture is like, ask if they have any top performers on the team and what makes them stand out. if they respond by talking about someone always dropping everything to work overtime or being on call, that tells you they value long hours. if they talk about mentoring juniors or saving the company money, you know they value skills and contributions rather than just time spent working.

what does ray morrison say about stopping applications after a good interview?

on guidance counselor 2.0, ray morrison says after his first panel interview went well in december, he stopped applying because he felt the job was in the bag, but he did not hear back until january eighth. he warns that if you apply today you might not get a call for a week, so if you skip a week of applying, you will have a dead week in the future where you are not getting any calls.
