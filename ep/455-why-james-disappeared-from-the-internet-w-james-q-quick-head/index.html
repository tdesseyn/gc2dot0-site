why james disappeared from the internet w/ james q quick, head of dev exp

guest: james q quick, head of developer experience at makeswift (bigcommerce)

  • expect burnout from full time content creation even when you control your own schedule and income
  • treat networking as showing up and investing in your community instead of cold introductions at a booth
  • job leads can start from casual conversations in a lunch line rather than formal pitches at a booth

questions this episode answers

what does james q quick say about burnout from full-time content creation?

on guidance counselor 2.0, james q quick says he burned out from full-time content creation even though he controlled his schedule and made good money. the constant pressure to earn a living and be responsible for every aspect of creating, marketing, and producing content took a toll he did not initially realize. he found the stress of maintaining income and visibility while numbers dropped mentally exhausting, and the burnout only became clear after starting a full-time job with parental leave and set boundaries.

what does james q quick say about networking at conferences?

on guidance counselor 2.0, james q quick says networking is not about cold introductions at booths with firm handshakes but about showing up and investing in your community. he advises bringing a friend or two to make sitting at random tables less intimidating, volunteering at conferences to get free or discounted tickets while still attending talks, and accepting that almost everyone feels uncomfortable so you should not let awkwardness hold you back from starting conversations.
