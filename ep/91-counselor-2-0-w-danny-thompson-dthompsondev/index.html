questions this episode answers

what does danny thompson say about reaching out to hiring managers on linkedin?

on guidance counselor 2.0, danny thompson says you should message hiring managers directly on linkedin with three specific reasons why they need to talk to you. nine times out of ten you can see who posted the job ad, and if not you can research the company to find the hiring managers. messaging them directly bypasses the ats software that filters out resumes, and one conversation can change the trajectory of your career.

why does danny thompson recommend volunteering for nonprofits instead of just building clone projects?

on guidance counselor 2.0, danny thompson says volunteer experience never expires, so you can talk about work you did five years ago. volunteering is a win-win-win because you gain resume experience, you network with people who have actual careers, and you help your community. he thinks instagram or twitter clones are bad interview projects because hiring managers already know what those apps do and won't ask clarifying questions that let you showcase your skills.
