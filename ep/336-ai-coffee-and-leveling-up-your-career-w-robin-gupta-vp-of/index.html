ep 336 · feb 23, 2024

ai, coffee, and leveling up your career w/robin gupta, vp of eng

guest: robin gupta, vp of engineering

  • treat prompt engineering like talking to a seven or eight year old, give clear but not overly detailed instructions
  • on scripting languages aim copilot at small chunks of about five to seven lines of code for best results
  • don't be a naysayer on ai tools, pro coder or no coder, this is the time to learn and experiment
  • build a mentor mentee habit, be both a mentor and a mentee, and keep catch ups to roughly once every two weeks to start
  • when stepping into a new or undefined role, spend the first 90 days having conversations to understand pain points before building solutions
