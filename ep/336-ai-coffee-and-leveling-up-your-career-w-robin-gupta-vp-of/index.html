questions this episode answers

what does robin gupta say about prompt engineering with ai tools?

on guidance counselor 2.0, robin gupta says to treat prompt engineering like talking to a seven or eight year old child. give clear, specific instructions without being overly detailed, then you can ask for creative enhancements. start with the high level intent in your comments, then mention the specific components you want to build out.

what does robin gupta recommend for using github copilot effectively?

on guidance counselor 2.0, robin gupta recommends aiming github copilot at small chunks of about five to seven lines of code for best results. he finds it works better on scripting languages like typescript, javascript or python rather than procedural languages like java. ask copilot to do something that produces five to seven lines, because if the task is too big it goes in unwanted directions.

what does robin gupta say about mentor mentee relationships at work?

on guidance counselor 2.0, robin gupta says you can be both a mentor and a mentee at the same time, which keeps you growing. he recommends starting with catch ups once every two weeks, though the cadence can adjust based on the person's situation and workload. for employee retention, having a sophisticated mentor mentee program empowers senior leaders and helps future leaders grow without much cost, just a zoom call and thirty minutes.
