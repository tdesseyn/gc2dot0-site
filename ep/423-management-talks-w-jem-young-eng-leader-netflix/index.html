management talks w/ jem young, eng leader @ netflix

guest: jem young, engineering manager at netflix

  • believe in the power of the individual and invest in empowering people to grow
  • hire and look for curiosity, since curious people proactively find and solve problems instead of just closing tickets
  • make it safe to fail on your team, since failures teach more than successes ever do
  • culture beats execution, but execution beats vision, so build trust and then get things done
  • as a manager your job is to repeat the goal and priorities over and over until it feels boring

questions this episode answers

what does jem young look for when hiring engineers at netflix?

on guidance counselor 2.0, jem young, engineering manager at netflix, says the number one trait he looks for is curiosity. curious engineers proactively identify problems and solve them rather than just closing tickets. he explains that someone who asks why things work a certain way will find solutions before you even know a problem exists, versus someone who is purely task-focused.

what is jem young's advice for engineering managers on communicating goals?

on guidance counselor 2.0, jem young says a manager's job is to repeat the goal and priorities over and over again until it feels boring. because engineers sit at desks making thousands of independent decisions every day, you need to communicate the same goal constantly so all those small decisions orient toward the right outcome. he admits it sounds repetitive but it's one of the most important things a leader does.
