questions this episode answers

how can a text-based ai assistant help with a job search?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn says claude or chatgpt is the brain, and a text-based ai assistant is the eyes and ears of your job search. set up a job scan that texts you what it finds, then ask your assistant who you're connected to at that company.

how do you keep an ai assistant from feeling spammy?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn says to always give it guardrails, like don't send anything until i approve. have it draft a specific dm from your background using star (situation, task, action, result), and review it before anything goes out.
