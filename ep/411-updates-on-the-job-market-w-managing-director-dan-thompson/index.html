questions this episode answers

what does dan thompson say about when software development hiring will pick back up?

on guidance counselor 2.0, dan thompson, a managing director at veo, says software development has been the hardest hit area with companies scaling back research and development. he expects hiring in software development to pick back up in the next three to eight months because work is still piling up and has to get done. companies are feeling the pain of not having people in those roles.

how does dan thompson recommend explaining your experience on a resume?

on guidance counselor 2.0, dan thompson says you should explain the problem you were trying to solve, how you built the solution, and what technology you used instead of just throwing tech jargon on a resume to fill space. he warns against listing every skill from a job description because interviewers will ask random questions to test your actual experience and catch you if you only sat in on a demo or played with something for an hour.

what interview preparation does dan thompson recommend before an interview?

on guidance counselor 2.0, dan thompson says to research the company's industry, products, and what problems they solve by checking their linkedin, website, or crunchbase. he also says to know your own resume well enough to answer questions about any bullet point without fumbling around looking for it. you should test your tech setup before the interview, log in early to make sure your microphone and camera work, and limit distractions behind you.
