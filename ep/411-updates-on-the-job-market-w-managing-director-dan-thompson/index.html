ep 411 · feb 3, 2025

updates on the job market w/ managing director, dan thompson

guest: dan thompson, managing director

  • expect software development hiring to pick back up over the next few months as companies still have real work piling up
  • on your resume explain the problem you solved and how you built the solution instead of just listing tech jargon
  • do not stuff your resume with every skill in the job description or interviewers will probe and catch inflated experience
  • before an interview research the company's business and products and know your own resume well enough to discuss any bullet point
  • test your tech, limit distractions, and show up as the person you would actually be in the office to make a strong impression
