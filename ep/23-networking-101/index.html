networking 101

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • when you dm someone new, ask for a specific short amount of time and name the exact topic you want to discuss
  • stop trying to sell yourself at networking events and instead get genuinely curious about the other person
  • at conferences, pick a handful of people you actually want to connect with and leave once you have met them
  • treat networking as making friends, not making a pitch, and keep outreach messages short and real
  • if you want to get serious about using linkedin for networking, cut distracting apps and put your focus there instead

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about reaching out to someone new for networking?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says you should ask for a specific short amount of time and name the exact topic you want to discuss. he explains that if you message him saying you want two minutes to talk about networking on linkedin, he will respond. this approach worked when he messaged a founder named eddie, asking for six minutes to discuss how copywriting and the job search come hand in hand.

what is taylor desseyn's advice for behaving at networking events?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says you should stop trying to sell yourself and instead be genuinely interested in other people. he recommends picking five specific people you want to connect with at a conference, then leaving after you meet them. he emphasizes that networking is about becoming friends, not selling, and says his biggest tip is to just be there and be curious about somebody else.
