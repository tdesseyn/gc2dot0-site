ep 390 · jul 26, 2024

ai, women in tech, and your career isn't linear w/ apurva misra, sr. ml eng

guest: apurva misra, senior machine learning engineer and ai strategy consultant

  • before investing in ai, get your data organized, cleaned up, and well maintained first
  • remember generative ai is probabilistic not deterministic, so design interfaces that set expectations and collect user feedback like thumbs up or down
  • dont force ai into every problem just because its popular, use it where it actually improves your day to day workflow
  • a step back early in your career, like an unpaid internship exploring new fields, can set up the path that leads to your real passion
  • dont take job rejections personally, most of the time it has nothing to do with you and expecting rejection can make it hurt less
