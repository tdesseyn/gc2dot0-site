questions this episode answers

what does will king say about how junior developers should use ai in their job search?

on guidance counselor 2.0, will king says junior developers should use ai as rocket fuel to level themselves up, not as a crutch that writes all their code. he recommends using ai in mentor mode to teach you more about code and to help you practice decision-making by asking for three variations of a solution plus an argument for the best one. instead of relying on ai to solve problems, use it to understand how to think through solving problems and to build more projects faster.

how does will king recommend approaching the job search instead of mass applying?

on guidance counselor 2.0, will king says to pick specific companies you want to work for instead of mass applying everywhere. understand what type of work they do, what they care about, and what type of person they want to hire, then build something with their product that shows you understand their business. he emphasizes building a resume or website that tells them you are the engineer for them specifically, not just a good engineer in general.

what does will king say about learning new skills like vibe coding as a junior developer?

on guidance counselor 2.0, will king says that before investing time in learning any new skill, you need to be able to answer how it makes you more valuable to a business. if you cannot answer that question about a tool like lovable or vibe coding, find something else to learn first or learn it in your free time. he explains that the value of tools like lovable for engineers is that they can shortcut design and prototype experiences faster, which helps get ideas in the hands of customers quicker.
