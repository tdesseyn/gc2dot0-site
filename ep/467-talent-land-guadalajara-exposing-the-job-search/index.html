questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about writing a resume professional summary?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode from talent land in guadalajara, taylor desseyn says the professional summary should be bullet points listing years of experience with each technology, not long paragraphs. he recommends writing something like 10 years of dot net experience, 7 plus years of angular experience, 3 years of azure experience, 2 years of leadership experience managing a team of 10 people. he emphasizes that recruiters only look at the first third of your resume and they skip paragraphs entirely.

how does taylor desseyn recommend using linkedin for job search?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says there are three ways to use linkedin successfully: post content documenting what you learn each day, slide into hiring managers' direct messages professionally to introduce yourself, and like and comment on posts to engage authentically without using ai. he says posting about something you learned and tagging the person exposes you to their entire follower network, and he encourages people to be comfortable reaching out directly.

what does taylor desseyn say about following up after a job interview?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says follow-up is the single most important part of the job search because checking back in can turn a soft no into a yes. he shares that he once followed up with a founder who said not right now, and by checking in a week later instead of waiting until the end of the month as suggested, he turned that into a job offer. he recommends sending thank you messages on linkedin after every interview as another touch point.
