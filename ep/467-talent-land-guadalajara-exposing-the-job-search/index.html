talent land - guadalajara - exposing the job search

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • write resume bullet points instead of long paragraphs since recruiters skim the first third of your resume
  • include specific technical keywords in your resume because hiring managers often search for them directly
  • quantify your work with real numbers like users supported or revenue impact instead of listing tasks
  • before an interview find commonalities with the interviewer and research what problem the company is trying to solve
  • always follow up after an interview since checking back in can turn a soft no into a yes
