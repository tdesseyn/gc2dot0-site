questions this episode answers

how does taylor desseyn use claude to build a target company list?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to ask claude to act as a career coach and generate fifteen to twenty types of companies that fit your target role, including company size, industry and values. claude then creates a prioritized tracker with forty-plus companies ranked in tiers, plus columns for warm intros, how each company would value you, estimated size, and objections you might face. before reaching out, ask claude to research each specific company so you understand their business, customers and possible red flags.

what does taylor say about using ai to draft outreach messages?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to ask claude to craft an outreach dm based on your background and the company research, then punch it up yourself instead of copying it as is. he recommends using ai to get you eighty percent of the way there, then add the remaining twenty percent with your own personality, data or how you want to frame the conversation. the mistake most people make is just pasting and copying without editing to make it punchier and more personal.

how does taylor use claude to prepare for job interviews?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to paste a real job description into claude to pressure test your fit, surface gaps, and generate interview questions to prep with. claude will tell you where you match, identify two or three specific gaps like domain experience or seniority, and create six or seven questions the interviewer will likely ask so you can address weak spots head on. you can also upload your resume alongside the job description to see what you should expand on before submitting.
