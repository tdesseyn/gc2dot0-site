ep 434 · mar 28, 2025

how to navigate large conferences w/ nick taylor, dev advocate

guest: nick taylor, developer advocate at pomerium

  • set a clear personal goal before a big conference or you will feel like you accomplished nothing
  • smaller settings like dinners and intimate conversations build stronger connections than trying to meet everyone
  • aim for a handful of intentional conversations rather than spreading yourself thin across thousands of attendees
  • if company swag is cheap or scratchy skip it, people only wear high quality items that double as free promotion
  • create content from talks you attend and tag the speaker, most speakers will repost or appreciate the shoutout

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about creating content at conferences?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn says attendees should create content from talks they watch and tag the speaker on social media. if you take pictures and live tweet during a session, the speaker will almost certainly repost it and may even turn it into their own content while giving you credit. no speaker will be upset that you promoted their talk, and this is one of the best networking hacks for conferences.

what does nick taylor say about conference swag quality?

on guidance counselor 2.0, nick taylor says most company swag is terrible and a waste of money if it is low quality. if you give out scratchy uncomfortable t-shirts, people will not wear them, but high quality comfortable shirts become free promotion because people wear them constantly. he recommends spending more per item on fewer high quality pieces rather than buying cheap swag in bulk, and notes that useful items like quality usb cables also get used regularly.
