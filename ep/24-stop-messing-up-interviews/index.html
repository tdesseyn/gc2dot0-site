stop messing up interviews

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • research the company before any interview so you can actually answer what they do
  • take phone interviews in a quiet place and have your resume in front of you for talking points
  • keep pen and paper handy during interviews to jot down questions so you dont forget them
  • write down key accomplishments tied to the specific role before you go into the interview
  • skip the thank you letter and instead send a personalized connection note on linkedin after the interview
