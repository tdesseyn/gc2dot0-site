ep 190 · dec 7, 2022

branding, content, and creating your own luck

guest: christina aldan, brand strategy consultant and fractional cmo

  • start networking before you need a job, since your personal brand already exists whether you shape it or not
  • pick one or two content formats and stay consistent instead of trying to be everywhere at once
  • if you can't share work details, write about your own journey, struggles, or lessons instead
  • summarizing a talk or article you learned from counts as content and speakers appreciate it
  • at conferences, pick one or two people to connect with and offer to volunteer or live tweet to attend cheaply

questions this episode answers

what does christina aldan say about creating content when you can't share work details?

on guidance counselor 2.0, christina aldan says you can still talk about fundamental topics because there are always newbies learning your stack. you can also write about your own journey navigating office politics, going back to the office, or struggles you faced early in your career. another approach is to summarize talks or articles you learned from, which counts as valuable content and speakers will often retweet it.

what does christina aldan suggest for attending conferences on a budget?

on guidance counselor 2.0, christina aldan says conferences desperately need volunteers and will typically waive your ticket if you volunteer the whole time or even just one day. you can offer to work registration or sit at the workshop day table in exchange for attending the conference days. another option is to offer to live tweet talks, since conferences don't have social media people in every room and would really appreciate that help.
