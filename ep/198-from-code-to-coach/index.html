ep 198 · dec 26, 2022

from code to coach

guest: jonny scharer, technical career coach at pathrise

  • treat every job application like a tiered system and spend most of your time on roles you truly want, not easy apply jobs
  • a referral from an engineer on the team carries more weight than a recruiter forwarding your resume
  • find common ground before you message someone, like a shared bootcamp or shared industry interest, to make your outreach feel human
  • before asking for a referral or recommendation, add value first, think of it like a social bank where you invest before you withdraw
  • if software development isn't clicking, stay open to roles like qa testing or low code work since internal promotion is easier than restarting a search

questions this episode answers

how does johnny scharer recommend adding value before asking for a referral?

on guidance counselor 2.0, johnny scharer says to treat networking like a social bank where you invest before you withdraw. he suggests finding common ground first (like a shared bootcamp or industry interest) to make outreach feel human, then asking good questions or offering a fresh perspective on their work. he also recommends drafting a recommendation for someone before asking them to write one for you, which makes them 85 percent more likely to help.
