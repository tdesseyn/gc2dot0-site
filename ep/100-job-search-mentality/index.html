job search mentality

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • being willing to jump through interview hoops like coding evals or extra rounds sets top candidates apart
  • staying kind and positive with recruiters and hiring managers goes further than being negative or defensive
  • treat rejection as a new connection that could lead to an opportunity down the road
  • flipping your mindset from dreading the job search to being curious and eager changes how interviews go
  • sending a genuine thank you note after an interview, even a bad one, shows gratitude that pays off later

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about willingness to jump through interview hoops?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says the candidates who are willing to take coding evaluations, send references, meet on zoom, and even go through six interview rounds are the ones who come out on top. he emphasizes that willingness to jump through hoops, combined with kindness, makes the difference in landing jobs. companies are not getting their first choices right now, so being cooperative gives you an advantage.

how does taylor desseyn recommend handling rejection in the job search?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to flip your mentality and treat rejection as gaining a new connection rather than a failure. he explains that a hiring manager who rejects you today could reach out in five years with your dream job, so staying kind and grateful during rejection matters. how you deal with rejection will define your job search and career more than the rejection itself.
