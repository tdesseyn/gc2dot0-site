questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about who pays the recruiter?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says the company pays the recruiter, not the job seeker. he explains that recruiters do not work for candidates even though they help find jobs. this means if a recruiter is not meeting your needs, remember they are serving the company that pays them and juggling many other candidates at the same time.

how often should you check in with your recruiter according to taylor desseyn?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn says job seekers should actively check in with their recruiter instead of assuming they have been ghosted. he explains that recruiters meet 10 to 15 people per week, which adds up to 780 people per year, so they need candidates to send regular touchpoints via text, email or call. if you think a recruiter ghosted you, ask yourself when you last reached out to them.

what does taylor desseyn say about negotiating contract rates with recruiters?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says candidates need to research their current pay, benefits and market rate before negotiating. he advises being firm on your worth but staying flexible, because being too rigid on rate can cost you a good opportunity. for example, if you want 100 dollars per hour but the recruiter says the highest placement was 75, that 25 dollar gap is a 50,000 dollar annual difference you need to seriously consider.
