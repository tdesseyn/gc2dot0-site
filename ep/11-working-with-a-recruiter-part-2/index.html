working with a recruiter part 2

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • remember the company pays the recruiter, not the job seeker, so expectations should be set accordingly
  • recruiters juggle many candidates at once, so check in regularly instead of assuming you are being ignored
  • have grace with recruiters since a lot of paperwork and coordination happens behind the scenes after every meeting
  • research your current pay, benefits and market rate before negotiating so you can talk real numbers with a recruiter
  • be firm on your worth but stay flexible, since being too rigid on rate can cost you an otherwise good opportunity
