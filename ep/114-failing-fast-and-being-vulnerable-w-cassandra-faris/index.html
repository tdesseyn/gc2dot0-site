questions this episode answers

what does cassandra faris say about being vulnerable at work when you're struggling?

on guidance counselor 2.0, cassandra faris says being open and honest with your managers and coworkers when you're struggling personally actually builds support instead of damaging your reputation. she learned this during a difficult period in 2018 when she decided to stop hiding that everything was falling apart and instead shared respectfully with her team. by being vulnerable, she received more support and accommodations that helped her get through it.

how does cassandra faris recommend testing new workplace experiments?

on guidance counselor 2.0, cassandra faris recommends trying any new program or initiative for a set period like three months, then doing a proper retrospective. she sits down with her team after sponsoring events or launching programs and asks what went well, what went poorly, and what to improve. this methodical approach helps determine what actually works and what to stop doing, rather than continuing failed experiments.

what does cassandra faris suggest if your whole company isn't safe for vulnerability?

on guidance counselor 2.0, cassandra faris says you need to identify one or two trusted people at work who can hold space for you, even if the entire company culture isn't open to vulnerability. she emphasizes that you don't need everyone to support you, just a couple of friends at work where the relationship is reciprocal and safe. she also recommends asking whether someone wants advice or just needs to be heard before responding to their struggles.
