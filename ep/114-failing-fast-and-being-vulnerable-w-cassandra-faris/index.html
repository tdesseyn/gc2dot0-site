failing fast and being vulnerable w/ cassandra faris

guest: cassandra faris, technical community manager at rocket

  • being open about personal struggles with managers and coworkers can build trust instead of damaging your reputation
  • run experiments for a set time then do a real retrospective on what worked and what did not
  • find a couple trusted people you can be vulnerable with instead of expecting the whole company to hold space for you
  • before giving advice ask the other person if they want advice or just want to be heard
  • ask someone if now is a good time to talk before diving into a serious conversation so they can actually listen
