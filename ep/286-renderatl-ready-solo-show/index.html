renderatl ready (solo show)

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • before any conference decide your goal networking learning or just meeting people so you know how to spend your time
  • pick your sessions ahead of time since you cant attend everything so be strategic about who you want to see
  • live tweet or summarize talks you attend because speakers notice and remember people who create content for them
  • schedule downtime at conferences since treating it like a marathon not a sprint helps you avoid burning out
  • follow up with everyone you meet soon after the event because most people never do and it makes you stand out

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about creating content at tech conferences?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says the biggest hack to get noticed by speakers is to create content for them during their talks. he recommends live tweeting or summarizing sessions because speakers immediately check their phones after presenting to see who posted about them. when you approach a speaker afterward, they will already know who you are because you created content for them.

what does taylor say about rest and pacing at conferences?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says conferences are a marathon, not a sprint, and you need to schedule downtime. he recommends blocking out an hour in the afternoon to go to your hotel room and rest, especially since conferences take a toll on introverts. he warns against overextending yourself the first day or two because you'll be exhausted and unable to maximize the last days of the event.
