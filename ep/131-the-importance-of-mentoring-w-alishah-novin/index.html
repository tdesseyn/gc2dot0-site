ep 131 · apr 6, 2022

the importance of mentoring w/ alishah novin

guest: alishah novin, director of engineering

  • look for a mentor who has no personal stake in your success so they can give an outside, honest perspective
  • when reaching out to a potential mentor, lead with situation, ask, and background so busy people can respond quickly
  • the mentee should drive the relationship by coming with a clear, specific ask instead of a vague open-ended question
  • use the what else question repeatedly in mentoring conversations to get past surface answers and uncover real issues
  • companies should avoid pairing people with their direct manager as a mentor since that creates bias and limits honest feedback

questions this episode answers

what does alishah novin say about how to approach a potential mentor in a dm?

on guidance counselor 2.0, alishah novin says to use a situation-ask-background formula when reaching out. start with the situation you're facing, then state your specific ask clearly, and finally provide background information only if needed. this structure respects the mentor's time and lets them quickly see what you need without having to read through a long narrative first.

according to alishah novin, why should you pick a mentor who has no stake in your success?

on guidance counselor 2.0, alishah novin explains that a good mentor has zero stake in your success and does not care whether you take their advice. friends, family and managers all have bias because they want you to succeed or need you to do certain things. an outside mentor will give you a stranger's perspective and tell you the honest truth without emotional investment or personal agenda.

what technique does alishah novin recommend for mentors to deepen a conversation?

on guidance counselor 2.0, alishah novin recommends using the what else question repeatedly during mentoring conversations. after the mentee shares what is on their mind, asking what else forces them to dig past the surface level and get into what is really important. he says this technique from the book the coaching habit works consistently to uncover deeper issues.
