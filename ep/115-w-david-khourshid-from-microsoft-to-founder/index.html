w/david khourshid + from microsoft to founder

guest: david khourshid, founder, stately

  • big tech interviews often test memorized algorithms that have little to do with the actual job

questions this episode answers

what does david khourshid say about open source contributions helping you get hired?

on guidance counselor 2.0, david khourshid says he was recruited by microsoft specifically because of his open source work in react and react redux. he explains that open source contributions help you build a community and gain visibility with recruiters without ever applying directly. he also notes that his first three startup hires were contributors from his open source project, so he skipped technical interviews entirely because he already knew their work.

what does david khourshid say about microsoft's interview process versus the actual job?

on guidance counselor 2.0, david khourshid says microsoft's interview process involved brutal whiteboard coding problems and algorithm practice that required nights and weekends of studying leetcode. he calls it hazing because once he got the job, the actual work had almost nothing to do with any of the interview questions. he notes this disconnect is typical of big tech interviews at google, facebook and microsoft.

what does david khourshid say hiring managers should focus on instead of just salary?

on guidance counselor 2.0, david khourshid says hiring managers need to have real conversations about what candidates want to achieve in their careers, not just list cookie cutter benefits. he explains that any developer can find a hundred companies offering the same benefits, so salary alone does not differentiate you. he says candidates will turn down five hundred thousand dollars if the work is uninteresting and take a fraction of that for something they are passionate about.
