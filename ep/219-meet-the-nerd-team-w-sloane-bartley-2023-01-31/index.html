ep 219 · jan 31, 2023

meet the nerd team w/sloane bartley

guest: sloane bartley, recruiter on taylor's team

  • treat recruiting like helping people through transitions, not just filling roles
  • stay consistent every week since results from your outreach show up two to three weeks later
  • use a tool like trello to organize candidates by skill set and track where they are in the process
  • be upfront with candidates when you have nothing for them instead of ghosting them
  • ask more questions early in a new role instead of pretending to already know everything
