fractional work and ai w/ reed tomlinson, cto

guest: reed tomlinson, fractional cto

  • treat fractional work as crisis response as much as strategy, since most calls come when something has already gone wrong
  • stay indispensable and easy to work with, since that is what keeps you employed whether you are full time or on contract
  • if you start contracting, update linkedin, get active on it, try marketplaces, and network in person or at co-working spaces
  • differentiate yourself with niche subject matter knowledge plus coding skill instead of trying to be the best coder in the room
  • when using ai coding tools, treat code review and understanding why code works as the real skill, not just accepting autocomplete

questions this episode answers

what does reed tomlinson say about when fractional ctos get called in?

on guidance counselor 2.0, reed tomlinson says most of his fractional cto work comes when something has already gone horribly wrong at a company. he explains that people don't realize fractional work often means jumping into a crisis to redirect things and get them pointed in the right direction. he notes that turning around a crisis situation is stressful but really rewarding.

how does reed tomlinson say contractors and full-time employees stay employed?

on guidance counselor 2.0, reed tomlinson says the thing that keeps you last on the list to get cut at a full-time job is the same thing that gets you repeat contract work: make yourself indispensable and be somebody people like working with. he explains that when you have three people with equivalent resumes, you'll choose the person you enjoy working with, the person who's helpful and who responds quickly. reed emphasizes that interpersonal skills go a very long way.

what does reed tomlinson recommend for developers using ai coding tools?

on guidance counselor 2.0, reed tomlinson says the real skill with ai coding tools is code review, not just accepting autocomplete. he explains that ai will spit out code but it may or may not actually work, so for every line it generates you need to ask does this make sense and why is it doing what it's doing. reed warns that if you just tab autocomplete without understanding, it'll never work and you'll spend forever debugging it.
