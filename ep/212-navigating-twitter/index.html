navigating twitter

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • set a strong twitter banner and profile picture since they create the first impression for a hiring manager
  • write your title to cover who you are, what you do, and what you tweet about
  • use lists to find and join communities instead of just posting random tweets
  • engage by liking and commenting even if you never tweet, since twitter rewards consistent interaction
  • pin a tweet that shows your mission or who you are to invite follows right away

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about twitter profile pictures for job seekers?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says your headshot is the single most important part of your virtual identity because it creates the first impression a hiring manager will have of you. he recommends putting yourself in a hiring manager's shoes and asking what they would think when they see your picture. if you want to use twitter professionally to talk about career topics, you should absolutely have a profile picture.

how does taylor desseyn recommend writing a twitter profile title for job seekers?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn says your twitter title should talk about who you are, what you do, or what you tweet about. he uses his own title as an example: it mentions meeting five thousand software developers, states his mission to make hiring easier for candidates and companies, then invites follows by offering secrets. this structure catches attention and tells people why they should follow you.

what does taylor say about using twitter lists to find job search communities?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 episode, taylor desseyn says lists are incredibly important and a wonderful way to get started with a community on twitter. instead of just tweeting randomly, you should use lists to find and join communities relevant to your field. he explains that twitter is about finding communities, and lists are the best tool for that purpose, especially for people who don't know how to start using twitter.
