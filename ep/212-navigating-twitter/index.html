ep 212 · jan 27, 2023

navigating twitter

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • set a strong twitter banner and profile picture since they create the first impression for a hiring manager
  • write your title to cover who you are, what you do, and what you tweet about
  • use lists to find and join communities instead of just posting random tweets
  • engage by liking and commenting even if you never tweet, since twitter rewards consistent interaction
  • pin a tweet that shows your mission or who you are to invite follows right away
