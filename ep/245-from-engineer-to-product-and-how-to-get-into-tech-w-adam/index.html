ep 245 · apr 22, 2023

from engineer to product and how to get into tech w/adam murray who is a sr. product manager at cloudflare

guest: adam murray, senior product manager at cloudflare

  • try moving from engineering to product by flexing product skills on a side project before making a full switch
  • get involved in open source to practice working with a community the way product managers work with customers
  • do not limit your tech job search to developer roles, consider product, project management, qa, customer success or business analyst paths
  • be careful accepting inflated titles early since you may later have to walk them back
  • in interviews it is fine to admit you do not know something rather than giving a long answer to dodge it
