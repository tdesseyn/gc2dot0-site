questions this episode answers

what does jeff torbeck say about networking when you need help?

on guidance counselor 2.0, jeff torbeck, vp of revenue at gun.io, says you should focus on being of service to others rather than immediately asking for help. he keeps his door open for people who reach out on linkedin with questions about building a career, even if they are strangers. someone who is selfless during interactions will build a stronger reputation than someone who only asks for favors, and that reputation will help when people back-channel you during job searches.

how small can a networking meetup be and still be valuable, according to jeff torbeck?

on guidance counselor 2.0, jeff torbeck says a meetup with six or seven people is perfectly fine and can be more valuable than a huge event. if there is no local group in your field, start one yourself with just three or four people and meet monthly or bimonthly. post about these small gatherings and eventually they will grow to five, then six, then ten, but the number does not need to be large for real relationship building to happen.

what does jeff torbeck recommend before pursuing a leadership title?

on guidance counselor 2.0, jeff torbeck recommends talking to people who already hold the leadership role you think you want, then asking yourself why you actually want it. many people say they want leadership for a better title or because they think it is the only growth path, but some highly successful people remain individual contributors their entire careers, make a lot of money, and have great lives. figuring out what you truly want matters more than chasing a title.
