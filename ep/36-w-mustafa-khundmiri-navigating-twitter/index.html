questions this episode answers

what does mustafa khundmiri say about cold emailing for job seekers?

on guidance counselor 2.0, mustafa khundmiri says cold emailing should be a prospecting game, not a numbers game. he recommends researching the right people and reaching out to them as human beings with emotions and aspirations, rather than blasting the same message to thousands. the key is to stop selling in your first email and instead focus on starting a real relationship, not trying to extract something from them right away.

how does mustafa khundmiri recommend using twitter to find a job?

on guidance counselor 2.0, mustafa khundmiri says twitter lets you showcase your skills publicly so people can start reading your work from day one, unlike a blog that no one reads for years. he recommends following key hiring people like company founders, building relationships by filling their timeline with great content for a few months, and then messaging them about a job. this way you become the top candidate in their mind because they already know you are capable.
