layoff to offer letter in 89 days w/ sr. dev rel, lawrence lockhart

guest: lawrence lockhart, senior developer advocate at be free

questions this episode answers

what does lawrence lockhart say about applying for jobs right after getting laid off?

on guidance counselor 2.0, lawrence lockhart says you should wait at least a week after a layoff before applying anywhere. he compares it to grocery shopping when you're hungry: you make bad decisions when you're emotionally strung out. he wanted to come from a calm place, not fight or flight mode, so he could make better decisions and present his best self to employers instead of showing up desperate.

how did lawrence lockhart write a smart goal for his job search?

on guidance counselor 2.0, lawrence lockhart says he wrote a specific smart goal instead of just get hired. his goal was to be hired as a senior developer advocate within 90 days. he chose 90 days because he believed his brand and public work were strong enough to support that timeline, even though he was hearing about people being unemployed for six to nine months or longer.

what does lawrence lockhart mean by impact in public instead of building in public?

on guidance counselor 2.0, lawrence lockhart says building in public is often just daily coding updates like today i learned a for loop, which doesn't get you hired. instead, impact in public means sharing things that show how you move the needle: innovative projects you built, problems you solved, or people you helped. this gives companies social proof that you can deliver results before they even interview you, which matters more than a perfect resume in the age of ai-generated resumes.
