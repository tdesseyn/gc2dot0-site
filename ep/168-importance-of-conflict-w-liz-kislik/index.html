importance of conflict w/ liz kislik

guest: liz kislik, management consultant and executive coach

  • irrational anger usually signals a boundary violation, not a character flaw
  • calm your body first by noticing your feet, surroundings, or what you can hear to stop a stress spiral
  • rebuild routines gradually, like meeting a colleague for coffee before jumping back into full social schedules
  • talk to yourself in second person to create distance from harsh self judgment
  • look back at what you have already accomplished before assuming you are failing or an imposter
