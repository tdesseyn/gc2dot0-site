getting to where your going w/michael liendo

guest: michael liendo, senior developer advocate at aws

questions this episode answers

what does michael liendo say about finding community as a self-taught developer?

on guidance counselor 2.0, michael liendo says self-taught developers need to make sure they have their community in place, or they will fall through the cracks very easily. he emphasizes that even though he calls himself self-taught, he had mentors, books, and meetup groups that supported him. liendo believes boot camps are valuable mainly for finding pockets of community and folks who will stick with you through the learning journey.

what does michael liendo say about who needs a college degree in tech?

on guidance counselor 2.0, michael liendo says he would never recommend going to college for front-end development if someone wants to design websites or mobile apps. however, he notes that some people need to build the tools and abstractions that others use, which requires intensive knowledge of algorithms and data structures. liendo emphasizes not to confuse education with a degree, and that boot camps or free meetups can provide the same community and learning for much less money.

what does michael liendo say about showing up and being memorable?

on guidance counselor 2.0, michael liendo says he tells people, including his daughter, to let people know you're in the room when you walk in. he believes you never want to be forgettable, and showing up everywhere while making sure people know who you are opens opportunities. liendo says this approach helps because maybe you have something to offer or can help each other out, but if you're just another blip on someone's radar, nothing will happen.
