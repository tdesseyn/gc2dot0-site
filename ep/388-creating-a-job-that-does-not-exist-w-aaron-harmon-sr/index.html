creating a job that does not exist w/aaron harmon, sr. recruiter

guest: aaron harmon, sr. recruiter

  • file for unemployment benefits immediately after a layoff since the process can take weeks to kick in
  • after panic applying for a few days, switch to a more strategic approach like targeting specific people instead of mass applying
  • reach out to peers and individual contributors at target companies, not just talent acquisition managers, when networking for referrals
  • personalize your outreach messages based on the person's role and how they communicate on linkedin, matching their tone
  • accept help when people offer it during a job search, even small gestures like an introduction can open doors

questions this episode answers

what does aaron harmon say to do immediately after being laid off?

on guidance counselor 2.0, aaron harmon says to file for unemployment benefits immediately after a layoff because the process can take multiple weeks to kick in. he filed right away and it took about three weeks for his benefits to start. while waiting, he also turned on the linkedin green open-to-work banner and started applying to jobs.

what does aaron harmon recommend doing instead of mass applying after a layoff?

on guidance counselor 2.0, aaron harmon says that after panic applying for two or three days, he shifted to a more strategic approach. instead of messaging talent acquisition managers, he reached out to peers and individual contributors at target companies asking about openings. he used boolean searches on linkedin to find people in specific departments and personalized his messages based on how those people communicated on linkedin.

how did aaron harmon create a job that did not exist?

on guidance counselor 2.0, aaron harmon explains that many recruiting jobs are filled before they are posted publicly because managers ask current employees for referrals first. he connected with someone a few months earlier by offering referrals, and that person later reached out when they were discussing creating a remote position. the relationship built through helping others and staying visible on linkedin led to a role being shaped around him.
