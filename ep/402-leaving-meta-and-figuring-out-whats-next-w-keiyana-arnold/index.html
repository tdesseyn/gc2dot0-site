questions this episode answers

what does keiyana arnold say about when it's time to leave a job?

on guidance counselor 2.0, keiyana arnold says you need to reflect on your personal values and see whether your day-to-day work still aligns with them. she recommends making a pros and cons list of leaving, then checking which items align or don't align with your values. when you notice a gut feeling or déjà vu that it might be time to move on, have conversations with trusted people in your network before making a decision.

how does keiyana arnold recommend standing out in job applications?

on guidance counselor 2.0, keiyana arnold says you need to be like one flower in a sea of grass by doing something different than the rest of the crowd. she recommends creating content that demonstrates your expertise instead of just applying and praying, because recruiters will look at your linkedin to see you're an expert in the field. offer value in your posts by sharing the impact you made at past jobs or the steps you took after a layoff, not just announcing you're looking for work.

what does keiyana arnold say about structuring your day during a job search?

on guidance counselor 2.0, keiyana arnold says she lives and dies by her calendar, blocking off everything including bathroom breaks and movement time. she sets specific boundaries like never bringing her laptop into her bedroom and shutting it down by six o'clock every evening. she recommends setting just a few daily goals like drinking eighty ounces of water and moving your body for twenty minutes, rather than overwhelming yourself with too many tasks.
