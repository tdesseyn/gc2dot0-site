ep 487 · sep 19, 2025

the art of networking...internally w/ dania luc, cyber security program manager

guest: dania luc, cyber security program manager at digital realty

  • keep a brag sheet of your projects so you can quickly explain impact when a manager or interviewer asks for specifics
  • when interviewing, talk about what you personally did on a project, not just what the team accomplished
  • research a company's financials and strategic focus before interviews so you can connect your skills to where they are headed
  • build real relationships at internships and conferences since those connections can lead to referrals and support years later
  • when networking for help, ask specific questions like requesting feedback on a resume or about particular job openings instead of a vague ask
