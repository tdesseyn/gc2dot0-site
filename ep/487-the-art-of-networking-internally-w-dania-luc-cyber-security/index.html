ep 487 · sep 19, 2025

the art of networking...internally w/ dania luc, cyber security program manager

guest: dania luc, cyber security program manager at digital realty

  • keep a brag sheet of your projects so you can quickly explain impact when a manager or interviewer asks for specifics
  • when interviewing, talk about what you personally did on a project, not just what the team accomplished
  • research a company's financials and strategic focus before interviews so you can connect your skills to where they are headed
  • build real relationships at internships and conferences since those connections can lead to referrals and support years later
  • when networking for help, ask specific questions like requesting feedback on a resume or about particular job openings instead of a vague ask

questions this episode answers

what does dania luc say about talking about projects in interviews?

on guidance counselor 2.0, dania luc says people tend to talk about a project's impact at large instead of what they did specifically. when interviewing people, she asks them to break down their specific role in a project and the impact that had. many candidates cannot answer this question, which makes it hard to say they can do the same work on a new team.

how should you cold message on linkedin when job hunting, according to dania luc?

on guidance counselor 2.0, dania luc says to make linkedin messages super specific by giving people options. she asks either for feedback on her resume or about two to three specific jobs she pulled from the company. she includes a short spiel about why she would be a good candidate and her resume. this works because if someone is not comfortable with a referral, they can still give resume feedback or tell you about a skill you are missing.

what does dania luc say about researching companies before interviews?

on guidance counselor 2.0, dania luc says to go beyond the company website and dig into their financials and reporting if available. seeing where a company is spending money sounds really good in an interview. you can tell them you have been reading they are putting money towards ai or infrastructure, then connect your skills to that focus. this shows you understand not just what they do but where they are trying to go.
