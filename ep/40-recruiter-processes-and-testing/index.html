ep 40 · jun 11, 2021

recruiter processes and testing

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • expect recruiters to test you and ask for references before submitting you to a client
  • always ask your recruiter to share the actual test results with you, not just a pass or fail
  • if you bomb a skills test, ask the recruiter to let you retake it rather than giving up
  • stick with a small handful of recruiters you trust instead of testing with every recruiter you meet
  • while in school or bootcamp, do free work for your network to gain real experience for your resume

questions this episode answers

why do recruiters send skills tests to candidates before submitting them to clients?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says recruiters test candidates to prove due diligence to hiring managers. when a hiring manager receives two resumes and one candidate has completed testing and an informal reference while the other has not, they will screen the tested candidate first. this makes testing a way to improve your chances with the hiring manager.

what does taylor desseyn say to do if you fail a recruiter's skills test?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says you should ask the recruiter to let you retake the test. sometimes you just need to get the feel of the test they send, and if you bomb it, just ask them to send it again. he has had candidates take the test one, two, or three times.

what does taylor desseyn recommend students do to gain experience for their resume?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says students in boot camp or college should ask their network if they need anything built or any data evaluated. you should take on this work as an internship to gain experience and put it on your resume. he mentions that someone on his team volunteered their time to build out their portfolio and has already landed a job because of that experience on their resume.
