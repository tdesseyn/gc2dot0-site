finding a job through a friend and a dm w/ andrew gilliland

guest: andrew gilliland, full stack engineer at elevate

  • when job hunting, look at small agencies and niche shops since they take chances on people without a formal track record
  • get in front of people face to face instead of only submitting applications online, it builds real connections faster
  • starting a local meetup with no marketing can grow steadily and even lead directly to a job offer through a referral
  • when juggling two interview processes, be upfront with the company you prefer instead of using offers just to negotiate
  • use ai tools to learn unfamiliar tech by asking questions back and forth, but always validate what they tell you
