questions this episode answers

what does andrew gilliland say about where junior developers should look for their first job?

on guidance counselor 2.0, andrew gilliland says junior developers should look at small agencies and niche shops because they take chances on people without formal experience. he found his first developer job at a small application development company in his town after the vice president of a marketing agency referred him there. these smaller companies are more willing to give opportunities to people breaking into tech than larger corporations that filter out applicants without traditional backgrounds.

how did andrew gilliland get his first tech job through meetups?

on guidance counselor 2.0, andrew gilliland says he started his own local tech meetup at a brewery after finding no existing meetups in his area. he chose breweries because they are relaxed spaces where people can linger without bothering servers. within a few months he was getting fifteen to twenty people showing up through just meetup dot com with no other marketing. his most recent job came when a co-organizer of his meetup told him to reach out directly to his boss who was hiring.

what does andrew gilliland recommend when juggling multiple job offers?

on guidance counselor 2.0, andrew gilliland says to be upfront with the company you prefer instead of using offers to negotiate salary. when he had two offers, he told his preferred employer during the interview process that another company had extended an offer but he was prioritizing them. he chose the company with the lower salary and smaller title because he liked the people and his friend already worked there, emphasizing working with people over chasing compensation.
