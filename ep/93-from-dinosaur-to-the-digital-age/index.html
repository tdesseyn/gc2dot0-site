ep 93 · oct 20, 2021

from dinosaur to the digital age

guest: alex dale, runs the technology practice at vaco

  • build real relationships with recruiters instead of treating hiring as purely transactional
  • a good recruiting partner should push back and explain their reasoning, not just nod along
  • virtual meetings are fine but face to face or video still matters most early in a relationship
  • be honest about expectations and capabilities on both the candidate and client side
  • the job market is moving fast so slow down, think through your plan, and avoid chasing money blindly

questions this episode answers

what does alex dale say about choosing a recruiting partner?

on guidance counselor 2.0, alex dale says job seekers should look for a recruiter who will build a real relationship, not just do transactions. a good recruiting partner should be willing to push back, disagree professionally, and explain their reasoning instead of just nodding along to everything you say. he adds that if a recruiter is doing nothing but head-bobbing and saying yes, they're either not listening or not being honest.

how does alex dale describe the current job market in 2021?

on guidance counselor 2.0, alex dale describes the 2021 job market as being at ludicrous speed, driven partly by pent-up demand from covid. however, he warns job seekers not to chase money blindly just because they can make 30 grand more somewhere. he advises people to slow down even when the market is fast, do some soul searching, and think through a well-planned approach instead of running around frantically.

what does alex dale say about remote recruiting relationships?

on guidance counselor 2.0, alex dale says you can build rapport and trust even when you're a thousand miles apart from a candidate or client. the key is being intentional about using video calls through teams or zoom instead of always defaulting to phone, text or email. he emphasizes that seeing someone face to face, even virtually, still matters most when you're first getting to know each other and building the relationship.
