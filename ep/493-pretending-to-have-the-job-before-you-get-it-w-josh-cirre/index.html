ep 493 · oct 3, 2025

pretending to have the job before you get it w/ josh cirre, devrel @ laravel

guest: josh cirre, devrel at laravel

  • start doing the job you want before you have the title by making content or solving problems nobody asked you to solve
  • build small side projects in different tools to find the stack that lets you ship fastest, then lean into that specialty
  • going above and beyond usually means working on your own time outside your actual job description, not during work hours
  • find out where your target companies and future coworkers actually hang out online instead of assuming everyone is on linkedin
  • treat creating content as first round interviewing at scale rather than only applying to job listings
