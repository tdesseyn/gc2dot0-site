questions this episode answers

what does aaron francis recommend for getting noticed by employers?

on guidance counselor 2.0, aaron francis says to publish your work publicly long before you need it, because blog posts, tweets and videos act as distributed advocates championing you. when he got laid off and tweeted about it, that single tweet got five hundred thousand impressions and led to thirty calls the next day, which he attributes to three years of prior public work. he explains that public work shows employers you can already do the job they are hiring for, which removes risk.

how does aaron francis say to stand out when applying for jobs?

on guidance counselor 2.0, aaron francis says to follow the exact instructions in the job posting and include links to public examples of your work. he notes that only five to ten percent of applicants actually follow the stated directions, so doing that simple step already sets you apart from ninety percent of candidates. instead of just sending a resume, write a short note explaining what you think the role needs and point to examples of you having already done that work publicly.
