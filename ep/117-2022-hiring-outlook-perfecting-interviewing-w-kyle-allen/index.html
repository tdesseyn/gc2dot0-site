ep 117 · feb 23, 2022

2022 hiring outlook & perfecting interviewing w/ kyle allen

guest: kyle allen, managing partner, nashville office

  • focus on finding talent instead of just filling a role, know the two or three things that make someone successful in the job
  • spend more interview time selling the opportunity, aim for roughly 20 minutes on the candidate and 40 minutes on the role
  • keep the process to about three rounds within ten business days so you do not lose candidates to slow decisions
  • do technical assessments live as a paired exercise during the interview instead of giving take home tests
  • candidates often undervalue flexibility and autonomy and overvalue a raise once you factor in taxes it is smaller than it looks

questions this episode answers

what does kyle allen recommend for interview process length and speed?

on guidance counselor 2.0, kyle allen says companies should complete their hiring process in about three rounds within ten business days maximum. he argues that organizations waste time with slow processes involving multiple rounds and long waits for key decision makers, which causes them to lose candidates. speed and making hiring a priority rather than an afterthought is essential in the current market.

what does kyle allen say about how much interview time should be spent on the candidate versus the company?

on guidance counselor 2.0, kyle allen says the first interview should spend roughly 20 minutes talking about the candidate and 40 minutes talking about the opportunity and selling the company. he explains that companies need to focus more on selling themselves to candidates rather than just evaluating what the candidate can do for them, especially in the current competitive market.
