the power of the dm w/ conner bush

guest: conner bush, software engineer at netflix

  • rank target companies with a weighted scorecard using prestige pay purpose and people to see what you actually value
  • skip the generic application and dm engineering managers directly but expect a low response rate so send a lot of them
  • check a person's recent linkedin activity before messaging since inactive profiles rarely respond
  • keep outreach short specific and time boxed like asking for eight minutes and tie it to something you both have in common
  • when starting a new remote role spend the first few weeks scheduling one on ones across teams before diving into the work itself

questions this episode answers

how does conner bush suggest ranking target companies when job searching?

on guidance counselor 2.0, conner bush, a software engineer at netflix, says to create a weighted scorecard with four categories: prestige, pay, purpose, and people or culture. assign each category a percentage weight based on what matters to you, then score each company from one to 100 in each category and calculate the weighted average to get a final ranking. this helped him narrow down to 10 to 12 target companies.

what does conner bush recommend doing in the first few weeks of a remote job?

on guidance counselor 2.0, conner bush says to schedule one on ones with everyone on your core team and cross-functional teams before diving into the code work. he told his manager he wanted to focus on meeting people for the first three weeks to build context and connections. in remote work every conversation must be intentional, and this approach helped him know who to ask when technical questions came up later.
