ep 165 · aug 8, 2022

the power of the dm w/ conner bush

guest: conner bush, software engineer at netflix

  • rank target companies with a weighted scorecard using prestige pay purpose and people to see what you actually value
  • skip the generic application and dm engineering managers directly but expect a low response rate so send a lot of them
  • check a person's recent linkedin activity before messaging since inactive profiles rarely respond
  • keep outreach short specific and time boxed like asking for eight minutes and tie it to something you both have in common
  • when starting a new remote role spend the first few weeks scheduling one on ones across teams before diving into the work itself
