finding a job in this tough job market w/ barrett blake ms mvp & architect

guest: barrett blake, microsoft mvp and architect at nimble pros

  • connect with two or three new people every time you watch a live show or attend a meetup to slowly build your network
  • treat linkedin strictly as a professional space and skip posts about family or hot takes if you want it to work for you
  • ignore recruiter messages that skip salary range and job details since real opportunities will include that upfront
  • create content and document what you learn even if you think no one cares, since views and interest often build slowly over years
  • getting any foot in the door, like support or qa roles, can lead to a dev job faster than waiting for your first dev offer
