finding a job in this tough job market w/ barrett blake ms mvp & architect

guest: barrett blake, microsoft mvp and architect at nimble pros

  • connect with two or three new people every time you watch a live show or attend a meetup to slowly build your network
  • treat linkedin strictly as a professional space and skip posts about family or hot takes if you want it to work for you
  • ignore recruiter messages that skip salary range and job details since real opportunities will include that upfront
  • create content and document what you learn even if you think no one cares, since views and interest often build slowly over years
  • getting any foot in the door, like support or qa roles, can lead to a dev job faster than waiting for your first dev offer

questions this episode answers

what does barrett blake say about networking at meetups and user groups?

on guidance counselor 2.0, barrett blake says to connect with two or three new people every time you attend a meetup or watch a live show, even if you just send a quick linkedin message saying you saw their comment. he emphasizes you don't have to participate actively at meetups—just showing up consistently and sitting quietly is fine, and nobody will judge you. over time, simply being present builds connections without pressure.

what advice does barrett blake give about creating content when you think nobody cares?

on guidance counselor 2.0, barrett blake says to create content primarily for yourself as documentation of what you learn, so you can reference it later when you forget. he posted on youtube for three years with only a hundred total views before it suddenly picked up to nearly ten thousand views in the last year. barrett emphasizes you should never expect quick results—it took his blog about ten years to gain real traction—but documenting your work helps both your memory and eventual career opportunities.
