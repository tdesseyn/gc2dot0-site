questions this episode answers

how does ken schumacher say companies are using ai in technical assessments now?

on guidance counselor 2.0, ken schumacher explains that some companies now want ai open assessments where candidates openly use tools like claude or github copilot, and hiring managers can see how fast they drive them. instead of locking down the browser to prevent ai use, these assessments let candidates go all in with ai tools, then evaluate how quickly and effectively they can accomplish tasks. the focus shifts from testing knowledge without help to testing how well someone can use modern tools.

what does ken schumacher advise junior engineers about using ai tools to learn?

on guidance counselor 2.0, ken schumacher says junior engineers should lean fully into ai tools without holding back because it accelerates learning. however, he adds that while ai implements things quickly, engineers still need to learn by understanding why something works instead of just accepting the output. he emphasizes showing up every day trying to learn as much as possible, and when ai does something you don't understand, taking the time to figure out why it worked.
