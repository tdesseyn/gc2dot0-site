how to find a job on linkedin - solo show

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • when a job posting lists required skills, mirror that exact language in your resume's professional summary
  • search linkedin for your target role, then go to that company's page and click into people to find who to contact
  • message someone a level below the founder, like a director, since they are more likely to respond than top leadership
  • always click connect instead of follow, since follow does not open a direct way to message someone
  • craft dms that name a specific connection point, state your fit for the role, and end by asking if they want your resume or who else to contact
