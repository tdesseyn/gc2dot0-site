ep 338 · feb 28, 2024

navigating this job market w/ dan thompson, managing dir at vaco

guest: dan thompson, managing director at vaco

  • expect salary growth to stay flat for a while since the huge jumps of 2021 and 2022 were not sustainable
  • if you negotiate near the top of a salary range be ready to explain why you are worth it, not just point to your old pay
  • junior developers should build real experience through startups, hackathons, meetups, or helping small businesses with projects
  • reach out to other juniors, mids, and seniors for advice since they get asked less often than managers and directors
  • be purposeful in your job search by researching a company's actual products and services before interviewing, not just their website
