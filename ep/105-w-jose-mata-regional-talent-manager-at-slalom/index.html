questions this episode answers

what does jose mata say about the difference between agency and internal recruiters?

on guidance counselor 2.0, jose mata, regional talent manager at slalom, says the biggest difference from a job seeker perspective is the relationship with the client. with an agency, you could be one of forty companies attacking a position and one of a hundred candidates, or you could have an exclusive one-on-one relationship. understanding how much influence the agency actually has in presenting you and helping you get an offer is crucial.

what does jose mata say about putting skills on your resume?

on guidance counselor 2.0, jose mata says if something is on your resume, make sure you can talk about it in detail. when a good recruiter sees something interesting, they will ask you to go into more detail about how you did it, why you did it, and what the business challenge was. people often believe their current experience is not good enough, but it mostly is.

how should you calculate your hourly rate for a contract role according to taylor desseyn?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn says you need to take into account your pto, hsa or other contributions, and 401k match to come up with a total lump sum, then divide it by 2,080 hours (the typical yearly work hours). as a shortcut, divide your salary by two and then add ten to fifteen dollars an hour, which covers two or three weeks pto, hsa, and 401k match.
