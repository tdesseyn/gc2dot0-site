ep 373 · may 29, 2024

it's all fun and games until... w/ will johnson, sr dev advocate at auth0

guest: will johnson, sr developer advocate at auth0

  • get into tech by showing up at meetups even before you have a job title, it signals initiative more than a resume line
  • build community by doing the work before you have the title, like moderating a discord or answering questions for free
  • giving back without expecting anything in return, like sharing links or translating content, can open doors you never planned on
  • devrel is shifting from chasing big numbers like conference attendance to bringing back real product feedback from users
  • bond with people over shared interests outside of tech like sports or hobbies, those connections often turn into career opportunities

questions this episode answers

what does will johnson recommend for breaking into tech without experience?

on guidance counselor 2.0, will johnson says he attended meetups constantly while still working in factories and learning to code, even before he had a tech job. he volunteered at coding workshops for kids and women learning angular, which helped him learn and get known in the community. he went to angular, vue, and javascript meetups for 18 months straight while teaching himself, showing initiative that a resume from factory work could not convey.

how can someone get into community roles in tech according to will johnson?

on guidance counselor 2.0, will johnson recommends finding a company's discord and actively facilitating conversation, answering questions, and recommending resources before you have a job title. he says to do the work before you have the role because that becomes your resume and interview. companies notice when someone is managing a discord well and will bring them in officially since they are already acting as an ambassador.

what does will johnson say about giving back without expecting anything?

on guidance counselor 2.0, will johnson says he would find links to resources mentioned in james quick's live streams and drop them in chat instantly, competing to be the fastest. he never expected anything in return and did it for fun, but james remembered and later convinced will to apply at auth0, which changed his career. he emphasizes giving without intention of return, whether translating content, making transcripts, or helping in chat.
