ep 507 · dec 12, 2025

what to do if you were laid off today w/ will king, design engineer

guest: will king, design engineer at snowflake

  • if you get laid off, pick a few target companies and build something that shows you can do the job day one instead of mass applying
  • hiring is a trust exercise so your goal is to make yourself the least risky choice a company can make
  • use ai to speed up research and prep, not to generate your resume or project for you since that is easy to spot
  • contributing real fixes to open source tools you actually use heavily can be a stronger signal than a cold application
  • say yes to ambitious side projects while still employed since those unexpected efforts tend to open the most career doors
