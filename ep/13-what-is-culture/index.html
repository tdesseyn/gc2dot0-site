ep 13 · feb 1, 2021

what is 'culture'

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • build culture around we not me, make team success the shared win instead of individual stats
  • run short daily huddles, five to ten minutes, to get everyone aligned before the work starts
  • create a safe space where people can be open and vulnerable without fear of judgment
  • when someone brings you a problem, listen and affirm first before jumping to solve it
  • promote an open door policy so your team feels comfortable bringing issues directly to you
