job search tips with jake and dan

guest: jake shepard and dan thompson, senior director at vaco (raleigh) and vaco technology practice lead (tampa)

  • update your resume now and tailor it with specific technical details, not vague soft skills claims
  • use any downtime to learn new skills or certifications so you stand out when hiring picks back up
  • skip the generic skills section and instead show real projects or github work to prove what you can actually do
  • work with more than one recruiter and favor ones who have stayed at their company for several years
  • know your own priorities in a job search, whether that's pay, team size, technology, or company culture, before you start interviewing

questions this episode answers

how does dan thompson recommend using downtime during a slow job market?

on guidance counselor 2.0, dan thompson says job seekers should use any downtime to learn new certifications or skills through free services like skillshare or lynda.com. he notes the market will change drastically when things rebound, so upskilling in cloud technologies, infrastructure, or new programming languages will make you more competitive. he also advises taking time to decompress and avoid burnout, because when the economy recovers companies will be in overdrive and you'll wish you had rested.
