ep 257 · aug 9, 2023

tips and tricks of the job search from a meta engineer w/andronica klaas

guest: andronica klaas

  • treat free work like design or video help as a foot in the door, but dont overload yourself with unpaid projects
  • stop mass applying and expect results, you have to do real research on companies before interviews
  • contract and part time roles can turn into full time work, dont be afraid to take one as a starting point
  • if you want time with a busy manager be willing to sweeten the ask with something like a coffee or lunch delivery
  • showing up consistently and commenting on networking content can directly lead to real job offers
