ep 172 · sep 28, 2022

recruiter life w/ hunter mitchem

guest: hunter mitchem, agency recruiter focused on software development

  • ask about company financials and funding before accepting a job to gauge stability
  • find a recruiter with tenure who knows the local market and specific tech stacks
  • keep your linkedin profile updated since recruiters rely on it to match you to roles
  • check in with recruiters every week or two so you stay top of mind for new roles
  • give recruiters a salary range and trust their read on where you stack up in the market
