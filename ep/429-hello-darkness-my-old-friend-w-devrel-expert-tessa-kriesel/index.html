questions this episode answers

what does tessa kriesel say about isolation after a layoff?

on guidance counselor 2.0, tessa kriesel says that after her layoff she isolated herself and tried to grind alone while building her business, which made things worse. she didn't realize how sad she was until she attended commit your code in dallas and spent time with other developers. being around peers who understand you is essential, and isolation during a career transition can be harmful even when you think you're being productive.

what does taylor desseyn say is a sign you are too busy?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn says that if you find yourself skipping workouts, that is a clear sign you are too busy and not prioritizing yourself. he believes that if you cannot make time to move for at least thirty minutes, whether it's crossfit, biking, or walking, you need to reassess your schedule. taking back control of your day includes protecting time for physical health, not just work tasks.
