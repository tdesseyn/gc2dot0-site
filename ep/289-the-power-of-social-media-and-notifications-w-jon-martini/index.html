the power of social media and notifications w/jon martini

guest: jon martini, senior leader in tech, product, and marketing

  • save searches on linkedin with specific titles, locations and onsite or remote filters so new postings trigger alerts right away
  • skip checking experience level filters on linkedin since job posters rarely fill them out accurately
  • message every single new linkedin connection with a genuine thank you to build a real network, not just a contact list
  • post on linkedin regularly and track when your audience actually engages so your updates get seen
  • build a daily routine with set work blocks and real breaks so a long job search does not wreck your mental health
