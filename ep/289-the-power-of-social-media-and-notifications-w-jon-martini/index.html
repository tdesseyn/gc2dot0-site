questions this episode answers

what does jon martini say about linkedin saved searches for job hunting?

on guidance counselor 2.0, jon martini says you should create saved searches with specific titles, locations, and filters like on-site or remote, but skip the experience level filters because job posters rarely fill them out accurately. he recommends setting alerts to daily or as soon as possible so you get push notifications when new matching jobs post, often within minutes. his own job came from a saved search alert that notified him within five minutes of the posting going live, when zero other applicants had seen it yet.

what does jon martini recommend doing with every new linkedin connection?

on guidance counselor 2.0, jon martini says he messages every single new connection immediately with a genuine thank you, something like he appreciates the outreach and looks forward to learning from them and hopes he has things to teach them too. he filters people by who responds, giving attention only to those who reply. this turns a contact list into a real network where people will make introductions and refer you to open roles.

how does jon martini say to structure your day during a job search?

on guidance counselor 2.0, jon martini says he posted on linkedin before six in the morning every day, then checked his saved searches by clicking through all fourteen to sixteen of them. he took real breaks including walking his dog, eating lunch away from job hunting between noon and one thirty, and playing video games with friends at night for an hour or two. he warns that without resetting you will grind yourself into dust before you even get the job, so taking care of your mental health with breaks and hobbies is essential.
