ep 99 · jan 7, 2022

what to give when remote is not enough

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • set a clear pto policy instead of vague unlimited pto so employees actually know what they get
  • build a defined mentorship program for junior devs instead of leaving them to figure it out alone
  • let employees use part of their time for side hustles or volunteering since it can boost motivation at work
  • give spot raises so people don't have to quit just to get paid fairly
  • consider offering things like a work from home budget, childcare support, or paid parental leave to stand out

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about unlimited pto versus set pto policies?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says companies should offer set pto amounts like 30 days or 45 days instead of unlimited pto. he believes unlimited pto creates ambiguity about what employees actually get. a clear policy tells people exactly how many days they have, such as 15 days plus all government holidays.

what does taylor desseyn say about mentorship programs for junior developers?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says he does not understand why companies lack defined mentorship programs for junior developers. he asks any company with a good junior dev mentorship program to contact him so he can highlight how they built it. one commenter mentioned their company has established intern cohorts and an entire mentorship program.

what does taylor desseyn say about side hustles and work motivation?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says companies should encourage employees to have side hustles. he believes people who stay busy with work outside their main job are more motivated at work because the side hustle gives them a break. most companies fear side hustles will hurt productivity, but he has noticed the opposite effect.
