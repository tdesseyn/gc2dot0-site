laid off twice in a year...now what? + gc 2.0 w/emmett naughton

guest: emmett naughton, technical support engineer, creator of coder dads

  • build genuine relationships on twitter by cheering on others and sharing what you find, not just self-promoting
  • when job hunting, book coffee chats and interviews quickly so you stay top of mind with hiring managers
  • expect some companies to pull job offers or close positions late in the process during tough markets
  • read the room on salary negotiation, sometimes taking a job that keeps you in tech matters more than matching past pay
  • a support or non-coding tech role can still keep you in the industry and valuable to a company
