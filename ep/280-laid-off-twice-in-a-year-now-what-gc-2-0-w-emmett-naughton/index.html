laid off twice in a year...now what? + gc 2.0 w/emmett naughton

guest: emmett naughton, technical support engineer, creator of coder dads

  • build genuine relationships on twitter by cheering on others and sharing what you find, not just self-promoting
  • when job hunting, book coffee chats and interviews quickly so you stay top of mind with hiring managers
  • expect some companies to pull job offers or close positions late in the process during tough markets
  • read the room on salary negotiation, sometimes taking a job that keeps you in tech matters more than matching past pay
  • a support or non-coding tech role can still keep you in the industry and valuable to a company

questions this episode answers

what does emmett naughton say about networking on twitter for job search?

on guidance counselor 2.0, emmett naughton says he landed both his first tech job with kelly and his second role at luro by sharing podcast episodes on twitter and tagging the creators. he explains that cheering other people on and sharing what you find, rather than just self-promoting, gets people to notice your profile and follow you. his current job at no commerce also came from a ceo who had been following his activity on social media for over a year.

how does emmett naughton recommend scheduling interviews during a job search?

on guidance counselor 2.0, emmett naughton says he scheduled all interview calls and coffee chats between 10 am and 2 pm because that was the quietest time in his house with young kids. he also recommends booking calls same day or within 48 hours whenever possible so you stay top of mind with the hiring manager, rather than letting weeks pass between initial contact and the interview.
