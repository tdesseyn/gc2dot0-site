ep 227 · mar 3, 2023

obstacles in the job search w/jacob herrington a sr. engineer

guest: jacob herrington, senior software engineer

  • if you lack a degree or connections, take rough early jobs and prove you can learn fast and work hard
  • list short job stints on your resume if you can explain why and what you delivered in that time
  • when a recruiter wont share full details on a role, ask for an unrealistic rate first to gauge real market pay
  • before doing a lengthy take home test, offer your public code history as proof of skill instead
  • build real relationships with people in your field instead of only reaching out when you need something
