no mans land out of coding bootcamp w/drew robbins who is a recent graduate of a bootcamp that now is at meta

guest: drew robbins, contractor at meta working on xr/vr tech, recent coding bootcamp graduate

  • expect a no mans land after bootcamp where you have to build your own structure for job search, networking and projects
  • choose a bootcamp with real structure and accountability instead of picking the cheapest option
  • send personalized linkedin connection requests referencing something specific from the persons profile to boost response rates
  • always reply to recruiters even to decline, since responding back is a small effort that builds goodwill and future opportunities
  • track every job you apply to in a spreadsheet so you can revisit rejections and reapply when new openings come up
