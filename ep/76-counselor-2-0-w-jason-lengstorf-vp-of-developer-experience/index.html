questions this episode answers

what does jason lengstorf recommend for preventing burnout and retaining senior engineers?

on guidance counselor 2.0, jason lengstorf from netlify says companies should schedule company-wide days off instead of relying on individuals to coordinate their own time off when everyone is burnt out. netlify added juneteenth as a holiday plus an extra company-wide day for a four-day weekend, and they schedule one friday off per month through summer. he explains that if you lose senior engineers, you lose mentoring capacity for junior talent and contextual knowledge, which is astronomically costly in the most competitive hiring market he has ever seen.

how does jason lengstorf say smaller companies should compete with big tech salaries?

on guidance counselor 2.0, jason lengstorf says netlify cannot pay netflix-level salaries or half-million-dollar packages, so they compete on lifestyle and work-life balance instead. netlify offers four weeks of paid time off plus fourteen company holidays for roughly six weeks off per year, stays fully remote with no headquarters, and provides work-from-home budgets. he emphasizes that leadership will message employees who work weekends to tell them to take care of themselves rather than praising extra hours.

what interview questions does jason lengstorf recommend to spot red flags in candidates?

on guidance counselor 2.0, jason lengstorf recommends asking story-based questions instead of asking candidates to estimate what they will do. he suggests asking candidates to tell you about a time they had a conflict with a coworker or a time they had a better idea than their manager who did not believe them. if someone says they got data, wrote a proposal and overcame objections, that is a good sign, but if they say they hated their manager and quit, that is a red flag.
