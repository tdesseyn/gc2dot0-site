counselor 2.0 w/jason lengstorf, vp of developer experience at netlify

guest: jason lengstorf, vp of developer experience at netlify

  • schedule company wide days off instead of relying on people to use individual pto during burnout periods
  • compete on lifestyle and work life balance when you cannot match big company salaries
  • give senior engineers real time off since losing them costs you mentoring capacity for junior talent
  • ask story based interview questions like tell me about a conflict with a coworker to spot red flags early
  • batch interview stages close together so you do not lose candidates to faster moving companies
