mastering the technical interviews w/ anthony mays

guest: anthony mays, software engineer and interview coach, creator of mastering technical interviews

  • treat interviewing like a marathon by first figuring out exactly where the finish line is for each specific company and role

questions this episode answers

what does anthony mays say about what job seekers should focus on instead of just listing tools and technologies?

on guidance counselor 2.0, anthony mays says candidates should stop focusing only on the tools and technologies they know. instead, they need to show how they make money, save money, save time, or drive efficiency for the company. listing skills without demonstrating results is not enough to get hired.

what does anthony mays say about how candidates should think about the interview process?

on guidance counselor 2.0, anthony mays says the interview is not about you, it is about what you can do for the company. he explains that candidates need to show the company and the interviewer what is in it for them, not just expect their skills to shine through. treat interviewing like a marathon where you first need to know where the finish line is for each specific company and role.
