questions this episode answers

how does taylor recommend using chatgpt to find job boards?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends asking chatgpt what job boards you should use for your specific role. he demonstrates typing questions like what job boards should a software engineer be on or what ai-specific job boards exist, and chatgpt returns niche boards like ai jobs dot net, machine learning jobs, and others. he says this helps you discover specialized boards you may never have heard of instead of only using general sites.

what is taylor's advice about applying through job boards versus company websites?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says you should create your profile on job boards so recruiters can find you, but apply directly to jobs on the company's own career page rather than through the job board. he explains that companies get so much spam from linkedin and other aggregators that many engineering leaders now only look at applicants who apply on their own site. use job boards like dice for discovery, then go to the company website to submit your actual application.
