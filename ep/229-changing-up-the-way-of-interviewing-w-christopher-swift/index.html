questions this episode answers

what does christopher swift recommend asking in technical interviews instead of basic definition questions?

on guidance counselor 2.0, christopher swift, senior software engineer at live oak bank, says interviewers should ask candidates to walk through the specific details of what they built rather than objective right-or-wrong questions. he listens for depth of experience the same way you would listen to someone who actually lived through a deployment versus someone who just read about war. swift wants to hear candidates explain problems they ran into while building features, like styling a button, to gauge if they really know what they are talking about.

what are christopher swift's top turnoffs when interviewing candidates?

on guidance counselor 2.0, christopher swift, senior software engineer at live oak bank, says his top three interview turnoffs are disengagement and lack of energy, dishonesty or trying to fake answers, and unwillingness to admit when you don't know something. he emphasizes that the interviewer probably knows the answer to their own question, so faking it is pointless. swift values honesty and says admitting you don't know something is critical.
