changing up the way of interviewing w/christopher swift, senior software engineer at live oak bank in raleigh

guest: christopher swift, senior software engineer at live oak bank in raleigh

  • ask candidates to walk through specific details of what they built instead of asking basic definition questions
  • listen for depth of experience the same way you would listen to someone who actually lived through a hard situation versus someone who just read about it
  • watch for disengagement in an interview since lack of energy and care is a major red flag
  • value honesty and willingness to admit you dont know something over trying to fake an answer
  • build real connections with peers since those relationships often lead to future job opportunities more than recruiters do
