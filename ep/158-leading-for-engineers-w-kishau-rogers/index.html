ep 158 · jul 22, 2022

leading for engineers w/ kishau rogers

guest: kishau rogers, ceo at time study

  • define success metrics with your engineering team so everyone aligns on what matters and can work their own way
  • build rest into project planning because burnout shows up as slower, less creative work if you dont
  • create sandbox time so teams can tinker and break things without judgment before building the real thing
  • use transparent hiring steps and prep candidates on what to expect so interviews feel conversational, not an interrogation
  • in job interviews explain the impact of your work, not just the skills, including customer feedback and team context

what does kishau rogers say about defining success metrics for engineering teams?

on guidance counselor 2.0, kishau rogers says she makes a deliberate effort to define how success is measured and align the entire engineering team around those metrics. everyone leads team meetings by going through their metrics, which keeps the team aligned quickly. this approach allows engineers to work the way they want to work, so she does not pester them on weekends because she understands engineering flow state. she also measures customer feedback as a success metric so engineers can build portfolios that explain not just their skills but the impact of their work on customers.
