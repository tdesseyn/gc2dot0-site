questions this episode answers

what does clark sell say about the tech talent shortage in 2023?

on guidance counselor 2.0, clark sell says there is still a gigantic amount of opportunity and companies can't fill roles even after big tech layoffs. he points out that facebook, amazon, microsoft, apple, and google represent such a small segment of the tech job market that people forget about all the other opportunities. he has been recruiting for 25 years and says there has always been a deficit of people coming into tech.

what advice does clark sell have for someone starting their own business in tech?

on guidance counselor 2.0, clark sell says don't solicit generic advice but instead solicit other people's experiences and ask how they've done certain situations to gather evidence for your own story. he warns that you'll see a lot of highlight reels and lies online but not the countless hours, panic attacks, loss of money, or capital that goes into building something. he says to fail fast and that entrepreneurship isn't for everybody, which is okay.

what does clark sell say about working for companies outside the united states?

on guidance counselor 2.0, clark sell says he worked for a bulgarian company and it was some of the best years he ever had in his professional career. he points out that while people in the united states worry about competing with the global market, it goes the other way too and americans can work for global companies.
