ep 49 · jul 2, 2021

recruiter ama

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • when a recruiter dms you, ask them to answer at least half of a short list of screening questions before engaging further
  • only invest time in recruiters who have been at their company and in the industry for at least two years
  • if you dont want more job pitches from a recruiter just stop responding instead of saying no thanks
  • junior devs stand out with a clean consistent resume listing specific tech like react and node instead of vague acronyms
  • before accepting a contract to hire role ask the recruiter if its a true contract to hire and get the conversion salary in writing

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say to do when a recruiter dms you on linkedin?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to go to his website and download the 10 questions to ask a recruiter, then send them to any recruiter who dms you. he says the recruiter should at least answer half of those questions before you invest time in a call. if they won't answer, just move on because most recruiter contacts will not go anywhere.

how does taylor desseyn say to find a good recruiter on linkedin?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends using a two and two rule he created. only message recruiters who have been at their current company for at least two years and have been in the recruiting industry for longer than two years. anything less than that and you risk working with someone who is not very good at their job.

what does taylor desseyn say about stopping recruiters from contacting you about irrelevant jobs?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to just stop responding to recruiters who contact you about jobs you are not qualified for. he says not to even send a message saying no thanks, because when you reply at all they know they still have your attention. just do not message them back, similar to how you would stop communicating with someone you are not interested in dating.
