burnout and hanging on (solo show)

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • set a hard stop time for your workday instead of letting work bleed into evenings
  • get outside and move your body daily, even short walks or workouts help with burnout
  • limit how many meetings you take in a day so you have mental energy left for family
  • find people in your industry you can vent to since friends outside the field won't fully get it
  • ask yourself honestly if you'd feel relieved or sad if you got laid off today, it tells you something

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn suggest for preventing work from taking over your life?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says he stops work at five o'clock now after working 12 to 14 hour days during covid. he also limits himself to only three meetings per day because he found that 14 meetings in a day left him mentally unable to be present with his family. he channels all his meetings through a calendar tool to enforce this boundary.

what does taylor recommend for dealing with burnout besides time off?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn recommends getting outside in sunlight within the first 30 minutes of your morning and working out regularly. he also suggests finding people in your industry who you can vent to, because friends outside your field won't fully understand the specific problems you're facing. he says not to put so much pressure on yourself at work since there's only so much you can control.

what does taylor suggest asking yourself to know if you should leave your job?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn suggests asking yourself whether you would feel thankful and relieved or sad and disappointed if you were laid off today. he says many people have told him they were actually relieved when they got laid off. he encourages people to look in the mirror and ask why they're still at a job if it's taking a toll on their mental health.
