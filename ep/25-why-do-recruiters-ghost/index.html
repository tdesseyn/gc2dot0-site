why do recruiters ghost

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • recruiters often ghost because they are juggling hundreds of candidates and heavy admin work, not because they do not care
  • follow up with your recruiter yourself, do not assume they will always reach out first
  • check in once or twice a week for the first few weeks after meeting a recruiter, then ease off
  • text or email recruiters instead of calling since most are in meetings all day and will not pick up
  • remember the hiring company pays the recruiter, not the job seeker, so communication is not always guaranteed

questions this episode answers

what does taylor say about how often job seekers should check in with recruiters?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says job seekers should check in with recruiters once or twice a week for the first few weeks after meeting them. after a month, candidates can back off a little bit or wait for the recruiter to tell them to ease up. he emphasizes checking in via text or email rather than calling, since recruiters are in meetings all day and won't pick up the phone.

why do recruiters ghost candidates according to taylor desseyn?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn explains that recruiters ghost candidates primarily because they are overwhelmed with volume, not because they don't care. he notes that recruiters meet 520 to 700 people per year and have heavy administrative work for each person, including data entry, tech evaluations, references, and managing current consultants. taylor also points out that the hiring company pays the recruiter's fee, not the job seeker, so recruiters prioritize company communication over candidate updates.
