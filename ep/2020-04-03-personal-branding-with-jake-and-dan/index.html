personal branding with jake and dan

guest: jake and dan, recruiters, vaco

  • start building your personal brand now instead of waiting, like planting a tree today instead of years ago
  • document your work and share real wins or lessons instead of trying to create perfect content
  • be yourself online, don't copy other influencers, your own voice and passions build trust faster
  • use social media to extend your reach if you're introverted so you don't burn out from constant networking
  • find your own way to stand out, whether it's meetups, community boards, or something as simple as a signature style

questions this episode answers

what does dan say about when to start building your personal brand?

on guidance counselor 2.0, dan from vaco says the best time to create a brand was a while ago, but the second best time is right now. he compares it to planting a tree: you should have done it 20 years ago, but today is the next best time. waiting longer only lets other people fill the gap or void you could have occupied.

what does taylor say about documenting your work instead of creating perfect content?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn says you should just document your day rather than trying to create perfect content. he mentions posting about finding someone a job after they were laid off, and that post went viral with 23,000 views even though he didn't think much of it. the key is to share real wins and educational moments without overthinking it.
