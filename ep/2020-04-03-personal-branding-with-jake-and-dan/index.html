personal branding with jake and dan

guest: jake and dan, recruiters, vaco

  • start building your personal brand now instead of waiting, like planting a tree today instead of years ago
  • document your work and share real wins or lessons instead of trying to create perfect content
  • be yourself online, don't copy other influencers, your own voice and passions build trust faster
  • use social media to extend your reach if you're introverted so you don't burn out from constant networking
  • find your own way to stand out, whether it's meetups, community boards, or something as simple as a signature style
