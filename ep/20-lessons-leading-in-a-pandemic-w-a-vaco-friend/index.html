lessons leading in a pandemic w/a vaco friend

guest: taylor roberts, leads a team in raleigh doing sales and client delivery for technology

  • pay attention to what people say with their hands and their hearts, not just their words
  • start meetings by checking in on the person before jumping into tasks
  • let go of controlling every detail and trust your team at a deeper level
  • notice when team members start proactively checking in on each other and build that into a habit
  • as a leader, talk to your people about where they want to go and align that with business strategy instead of just dictating direction
