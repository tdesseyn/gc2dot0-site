questions this episode answers

what does lawrence lockhart say about how many applications you need to send right now?

on guidance counselor 2.0, lawrence lockhart says you need to apply in high volume because competition is fierce in the current market. he personally put out over 100 applications before getting his first callback when conditions were better, and over 200 before landing his first actual job. referrals are crucial now because they bump your application up in stacks that may contain hundreds or thousands of candidates for a single role.

what adjacent roles does lawrence lockhart recommend for breaking into tech?

on guidance counselor 2.0, lawrence lockhart recommends considering roles like systems administrator, quality assurance engineer, or business analyst as strategic stepping stones toward your target job. he specifically highlights that business analyst is a common path to product manager, and that sys admin with a red hat certification can lead to cloud, devops, or cybersecurity roles. these adjacent positions have smaller candidate pools, making it easier to stand out and gain domain knowledge.

what does lawrence lockhart say about building a portfolio for web developers?

on guidance counselor 2.0, lawrence lockhart says your portfolio should include varied projects that show technical depth, not three identical clones or todo apps. he recommends one static website, one project consuming or writing an api, and one full stack application with persistent data and backend logic. at least one project should be personal and meaningful to you, something that solves a real problem in your own life, because passion for your own work signals you will care about an employer's work.
