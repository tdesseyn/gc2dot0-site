ep 312 · dec 27, 2023

is devrel going away w/ tracy lee, ceo of this dot labs

guest: tracy lee, ceo of this dot labs

  • a good engineer cares about the work and understands how it connects to business impact, not just collecting a paycheck
  • communication is the most repeated trait of strong engineers, from explaining your work to building empathy with your team
  • devrel is not going away but it needs time, sometimes a year or more, to prove value which clashes with short term business metrics
  • before applying to a company, engage with people who work there on social media so you are not a stranger when you reach out
  • follow up more than once when job searching, most busy people do not respond until the second or third message
