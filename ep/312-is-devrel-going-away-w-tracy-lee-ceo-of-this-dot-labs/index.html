questions this episode answers

what does tracy lee say makes a good engineer?

on guidance counselor 2.0, tracy lee says a good engineer actually cares about their work and understands how it connects to business value, not just clocking in and out for a paycheck. she emphasizes that engineers should know why their ticket or sprint matters to the business so they have a reason to show up excited. communication is the skill everyone in the conversation agreed is critical, from explaining your work to pairing with juniors without just grabbing their keyboard.

is devrel going away according to tracy lee?

on guidance counselor 2.0, tracy lee says devrel is not going away but it is getting cut right now because businesses are short-sighted. devrel takes at least a year, sometimes two or three, to prove value, which clashes with companies wanting to see results this month. she argues that if you start with devrel and build community first you get retention, but you do not see the effects until one to three years down the road.

what does tracy lee say about following up when job searching?

on guidance counselor 2.0, tracy lee says you should bug people and follow up multiple times because busy people often will not respond until the second or third message. she and taylor agree that most busy people see the first message and forget it, type something after the second, then finally respond on the third. she emphasizes that you should not worry about annoying someone because they will tell you if you are.
