ep 262 · aug 21, 2023

dealing with this downturn (solo show)

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • in a tough market, mix mass applying with one or two intentional networking calls or messages a day
  • stand out to recruiters by communicating consistently, following up, and staying flexible when they ask you to do something
  • text or email recruiters instead of calling since they are in meetings most of the day
  • post content and engage publicly even as a junior developer so recruiters and hiring managers can find you
  • if you keep getting rejected, ask interviewers directly for feedback and how many candidates are in the process

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about mass applying versus networking in a tough job market?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says that in the current tough market, job seekers without a job should do both mass applying and intentional networking. he recommends submitting many applications but also having one or two intentional phone or video calls per day to connect with people in your network. he acknowledges this is a change from his previous advice, but the difficult market requires both approaches.

what does taylor say about posting content as a job seeker?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 episode, taylor desseyn says job seekers must post content and be active publicly, even junior developers. he explains that hiring managers and recruiters need to find you, and being known for something helps even if you are just asking questions about react as a junior. he states that not creating content will make the job search take longer because people need social proof beyond just a resume.
