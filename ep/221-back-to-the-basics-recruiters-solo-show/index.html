questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about the two types of recruiters?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn explains there are agency recruiters and corporate recruiters. agency recruiters work with many different companies at once, while corporate recruiters work in-house for just one company. he says agency recruiters are more focused on quick placements because they only get paid on commission, whereas internal recruiters can be more intentional and take better care of candidates since they're not commission-based.

what is the two and two rule for choosing a recruiter according to taylor desseyn?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn recommends the two and two rule when picking a recruiter to trust. look for a recruiter who has been in the recruiting industry for at least two years total and has been at their current company for at least two years. he says first-year recruiters are still figuring things out, and good recruiters who care about their work typically stay at one company for at least two years rather than jumping around for salary bumps.

how does taylor desseyn recommend keeping in touch with agency recruiters?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to build a list of around ten agency recruiters you like and keep them on a mass email list. he suggests checking in with them once a month on a bcc email reminding them of your skill set and asking if they see anything interesting. he notes that no job seeker does this consistently and that it's a huge way to stand out, since agency recruiters all work with different clients.
