music city code 2018

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • meet your recruiter in person or at least on video before trusting them with your job search
  • a good recruiter knows the basics of tech and stays active in the local developer community
  • only work with two or three recruiters at a time so your resume does not get overexposed in the market
  • look for recruiters who have stayed at the same company and in the same local market for at least two years
  • build your network on twitter and linkedin and add side projects to your resume to stand out as a junior developer
