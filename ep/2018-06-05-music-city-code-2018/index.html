questions this episode answers

what does taylor say about how many recruiters you should work with at once?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode from music city code 2018, taylor desseyn says you should only work with two recruiters maximum, three at most if they are all respectful of your time. working with more than that causes your resume to get overexposed in the market. he wants developers to have multiple options but not so many that their candidacy gets diluted.

how does taylor desseyn recommend vetting a recruiter before working with them?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says you should meet your recruiter in person or at least on video before trusting them with your job search. he also recommends checking their linkedin to confirm they have been with the same company for at least two years and in the same local market for at least two years, because recruiters who job-hop every eighteen months do not know the market well. look for recruiters who have references from developers on their linkedin profile.

what does taylor recommend for junior developers to stand out to employers?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says junior developers should build their network on twitter and linkedin by directly messaging people who work at companies they want to join. he also recommends adding side projects to your resume, including volunteer work or free projects you built, and making sure your github has code visible. he points out that at a career panel, only two national software school students who got jobs had actually direct messaged developers on twitter.
