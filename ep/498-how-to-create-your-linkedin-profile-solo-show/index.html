how to create your linkedin profile - solo show

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • use your linkedin banner to show a personal detail like a hobby so you look human and memorable, not just another ai-style profile
  • pick a profile picture that is current, happy, and professional looking so you don't catfish someone who later meets you on a call
  • put your specific skills and target job title in the words under your name since that section is what recruiters actually search
  • write the first few sentences of your about section before the see more cutoff with real impact and keywords, since many people never click to expand it
  • ask for linkedin recommendations directly from people you worked with since multiple recent recommendations help build trust the same way restaurant reviews do

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about using the linkedin banner section at the top of your profile?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says you should use the banner to show something personal alongside professional details, like a hobby or interest, so you look more human in a sea of ai-generated profiles. he gives the example of someone who did audio engineering for the backstreet boys putting that in their banner, or an outdoor enthusiast showing hiking photos. this helps you stand out and gives recruiters a conversation starter beyond just technical skills.

what should you put in the title section under your name on linkedin according to taylor desseyn?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn says the words under your name are what recruiters actually search for, so you need to list your specific technical skills and target job title there. he explains that when recruiters search for a dot net developer, the title section is what shows up in results, so instead of just saying software developer, you should write something like three plus year dot net developer with angular c sharp microsoft sql server. recruiters will not sift through forty pages of search results, so this section determines whether you get found.

how should you write the about section on linkedin before the see more button, according to taylor desseyn?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says you need to make the first few sentences before the see more button highly impactful with your key skills, because many people never click to expand the section. he recommends writing something like you are an experienced leader with a proven track record using technologies like x y and z, rather than waiting until after the see more to list technical skills. those visible words also get caught in linkedin keyword searches that recruiters run.
