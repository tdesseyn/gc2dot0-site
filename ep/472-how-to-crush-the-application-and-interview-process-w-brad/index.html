how to crush the application and interview process w/ brad garropy

guest: brad garropy, front end developer at cloudflare

  • practice talking through your code out loud while solving problems, not just solving them silently
  • record yourself coding and watch it back to catch nervous habits before real interviews
  • ask interviewers about team rituals, planning, and performance reviews to spot real culture
  • apply directly on company career pages instead of linkedin easy apply to stand out from the flood of applicants
  • keep a tracker of every interview contact including shared background details to build rapport fast
