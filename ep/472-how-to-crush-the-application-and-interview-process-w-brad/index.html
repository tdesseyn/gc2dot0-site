questions this episode answers

what does brad garropy say about practicing for coding interviews?

on guidance counselor 2.0, brad garropy, front end developer at cloudflare, says developers should record themselves coding and talking through problems out loud, then watch it back to catch nervous habits. he recommends going on twitch or turning on your camera to practice thinking out loud while solving leetcode or hacker rank problems. the key is getting comfortable talking and coding at the same time, which is what interviewers want to see, not just silent problem solving.

how does brad garropy track his job applications and interview contacts?

on guidance counselor 2.0, brad garropy says he uses a spreadsheet to track every application with the date applied, company, position, and who he talked to. for each interview, he links out to a notion doc that tracks the interviewer's github and linkedin, and he tries to find something in common with them beforehand. this lets him break the ice in the conversation by mentioning shared background like the same university, which completely changes the dynamic of the interview.

what does brad garropy recommend about applying on linkedin versus company websites?

on guidance counselor 2.0, brad garropy says never apply on linkedin and instead apply directly on the company's job website. taylor desseyn agrees, saying he texted fifteen engineering leaders in nashville and almost all of them said they post to the career page first because linkedin easy apply brings too much ai slop and fake resumes. applying directly on the company site gives you a much higher chance of actually getting noticed by the hiring team.
