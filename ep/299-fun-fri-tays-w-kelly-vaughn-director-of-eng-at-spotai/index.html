ep 299 · nov 27, 2023

fun fri-tays w/kelly vaughn, director of eng at spotai

guest: kelly vaughn, director of engineering at spotai

  • every time you say yes to something you are saying no to something else, so protect your time with intention
  • try the positive no by offering a smaller scope or a later date instead of a flat refusal
  • figure out when you personally do your best thinking and push meetings to the other part of the day
  • before taking a meeting ask what it is about so you can mentally prepare instead of walking in blind
  • decide at the start of the week what you are simply not going to get to, since not everything can fit
