living life to the fullest w/ alex friedman, investor & entrepreneur

guest: alex friedman, investor and entrepreneur

  • build small, vetted events instead of big flashy ones if you want real connection over brand awareness
  • start content by talking to the version of yourself from years ago, not by proving your authority
  • own your audience through things like a newsletter instead of relying only on borrowed social reach
  • set clear work boundaries like fixed start and stop times so family and health dont get sacrificed for output
  • practice gratitude on purpose and remember failing early and often makes rejection easier to handle later

questions this episode answers

what does alex friedman say about building small versus big events for networking?

on guidance counselor 2.0, alex friedman says she prefers hosting small events with 30 to 40 people where she vets every attendee instead of big flashy events. she explains that big brand events focus on awareness and social media posts rather than real connection. small vetted events create better conversations and relationships because everyone is genuinely building something or working in the same space.

how does alex friedman recommend starting content creation on social media?

on guidance counselor 2.0, alex friedman says she started by talking to the version of herself from five to ten years ago when she was sitting in her bedroom trying to figure out her first product. she avoids leading with authority or proving accomplishments and instead focuses on showing people how to go from zero to one. she believes this approach is more helpful than content where people try to prove how smart or accomplished they are.
