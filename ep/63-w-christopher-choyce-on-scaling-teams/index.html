questions this episode answers

what does christopher choyce say about screening candidates when hiring fast?

on guidance counselor 2.0, christopher choyce says when scaling fast you should pick one or two key attributes that predict success in your company and screen hard for those. he recommends looking at your best current employees, analyzing what makes them successful, and then screening backward for those same traits. he emphasizes building a structured onboarding plan so new hires aren't thrown into chaos after you hire them quickly.

what does christopher choyce say about hiring for soft skills versus technical skills?

on guidance counselor 2.0, christopher choyce argues you should hire for so-called soft skills like empathy and communication because those are harder to teach than technical skills. he says it's easier to teach someone how to write a python script or use kubernetes than to teach them how to treat people fairly and be compassionate. he notes that most people enjoy the ritual of technical screening because that's how they were hired, but it screens for the wrong things.

what does christopher choyce say about contract to hire arrangements?

on guidance counselor 2.0, christopher choyce says contract to hire works well as a mutual trial period where both sides can opt in after two to three months. he explains it lets you see if someone is truly additive to your culture without the awkward process of terminating an employee if it doesn't work out. from the candidate side, if the company turns out to be a bad fit, they can list it as a consulting gig on their resume instead of explaining a short stint at a permanent job.
