how to stand out in your search to a founder w/ nizar zaher

guest: nizar zaher, founder of mail zero

  • skip the ai-generated resume and instead do real research on the company you're applying to
  • find a way to dm the founder directly or meet them at a meetup instead of just submitting an application
  • build something people actually use since that shows you can act like a founder, not just follow instructions
  • post what you're building on x consistently so companies can see your work before you ever apply
  • practice explaining your work out loud since communicating clearly is as valuable as the coding itself
