questions this episode answers

what does nizar zaher say about standing out when applying to startups?

on guidance counselor 2.0, nizar zaher says job seekers should skip ai-generated resumes and instead research the company they're applying to, then dm the founder directly or meet them at a meetup instead of just submitting an application. he looks for people who can act like founders themselves, meaning they build things on their own without handholding. when hiring at mail zero, he prioritizes people who post what they're building on twitter so he can see their work before they ever formally apply.

how did nizar zaher get his first engineering job at cal.com?

on guidance counselor 2.0, nizar zaher explains that cal.com's application required a one-minute video instead of a resume, but he discovered the system didn't enforce the time limit. he recorded a three-minute loom video showing all the projects he'd built, his youtube channel with fifteen thousand subscribers, and research he'd done on the founders' interest in open source. he beat out candidates from microsoft and google because the founders liked that he built things and was interesting, even though he was only a few months into learning development.

what does nizar zaher recommend building to get hired at startups?

on guidance counselor 2.0, nizar zaher says job seekers should build projects that real people actually use, like getting neighbors to use an app, because that shows they can act independently like a founder rather than just follow instructions. he recommends posting demo videos, build logs and screenshots on twitter every single day to show what you're working on. he emphasizes that the specific project matters less than demonstrating you can learn on your own and don't need constant direction, and he's hired people based on seeing their work on twitter without ever posting a job listing.
