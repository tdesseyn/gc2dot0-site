ep 50 · jul 5, 2021

the importance of recruiting companies

guest: kyle allen, head of the vaco office

  • a good recruiter does more than find talent, they also push the process to get you to the finish line with offers and onboarding
  • if your hiring process moves too slow in a hot market, candidates and recruiters will move on to someone else
  • recruiters often know about company moves and market shifts before people inside those companies do
  • vet a recruiter by checking their tenure, online presence, and whether you actually click with how they work
  • keep a relationship with one or two local recruiters plus a few more outside your area since remote work is here to stay
