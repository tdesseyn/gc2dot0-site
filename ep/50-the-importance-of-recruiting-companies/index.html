ep 50 · jul 5, 2021

the importance of recruiting companies

guest: kyle allen, head of the vaco office

  • a good recruiter does more than find talent, they also push the process to get you to the finish line with offers and onboarding
  • if your hiring process moves too slow in a hot market, candidates and recruiters will move on to someone else
  • recruiters often know about company moves and market shifts before people inside those companies do
  • vet a recruiter by checking their tenure, online presence, and whether you actually click with how they work
  • keep a relationship with one or two local recruiters plus a few more outside your area since remote work is here to stay

questions this episode answers

what does kyle allen say a good recruiter does beyond finding candidates?

on guidance counselor 2.0, kyle allen says finding talent is only a third of a recruiter's job. the other two thirds involve pushing the process forward to get candidates to the finish line with competing offers, managing interview speed, pushing hr teams, and handling onboarding timelines. recruiters also provide market intelligence about company moves and culture before those organizations even know internally.

how does kyle allen recommend vetting a recruiter before working with them?

on guidance counselor 2.0, kyle allen recommends checking a recruiter's tenure in the business, their team structure and consistency, and their brand presence online including content they push out. he also emphasizes the importance of personality fit and whether you actually vibe with the recruiter, because if you don't click with how they work, the relationship won't be productive.
