ep 250 · may 5, 2023

interviewing as a principal engineer as a first timer w/joe flateau, technical lead/staff engineer at density

guest: joe flateau, technical lead/staff engineer at density

  • when moving to a new job after many years in one place, practice talking about what you personally did, not just what your team did
  • trim resume debt by cutting skills that aren't front and center or relevant to the job you're applying for
  • message a referral contact before you submit your application so they can actually advocate for you with hr
  • keep cover letters short, around four to five sentences, each tied directly to something in the job description
  • treat yourself like a business you run, not just a dependent of your employer, and keep learning and selling your skills
