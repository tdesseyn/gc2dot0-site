questions this episode answers

what does steven evans say about ghost jobs?

on guidance counselor 2.0, steven evans from marathon consulting says ghost jobs usually are not fake postings where the job does not exist. instead, companies often keep certain roles posted continuously because they hire the same types of positions on an ongoing basis, or they receive thousands of applications and only move forward with top candidates while others sit unreviewed. if you applied and heard nothing, evans recommends sending a simple follow-up email rather than assuming the job is dead, because sometimes timing just did not line up when you first applied.

how should a resume list technical skills according to steven evans?

on guidance counselor 2.0, steven evans says put your tangible skills and years of experience with each technology at the top of your resume in a tech skills box. taylor desseyn and evans both agree you should avoid vague lines like used react or used aws without any context. instead, describe what you actually built with those tools, such as built this application using xyz technologies to solve a specific problem, because recruiters search for concrete skills and years, not phrases like problem solving.

what does steven evans recommend to appear lower risk to employers?

on guidance counselor 2.0, steven evans from marathon consulting says shrink your geographic search area and tell employers you are willing to come into the office. companies right now are risk averse and have noticed that employees in their geographic areas who come in tend to stay longer than fully remote workers. showing flexibility on location and in-office presence makes you feel like a safer bet because the company can build real in-person connections with you, which theoretically makes it harder for you to leave.
