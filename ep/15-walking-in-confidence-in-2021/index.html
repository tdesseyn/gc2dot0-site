questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about carrying 2020 setbacks into 2021?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says people should not bring 2020 struggles into 2021 as a victim card or excuse for a slow start. he warns that holding onto 2020 as a reason you can't get going will torpedo your successes in the new year. instead, he urges viewers to recognize that 2021 is a new year with its own challenges and to move forward with confidence.

how does taylor desseyn suggest building confidence to do scary things like going live?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says he started by recording himself on his phone around march of the previous year when the pandemic hit. he explains that taking that first small step eventually built up to doing live broadcasts in front of viewers. he emphasizes that while going live is scary and terrifying, starting with something simpler like phone videos can help you work up to bigger challenges.
