questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about the current state of tech hiring in 2025?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says tech hiring is not dead despite negative headlines. he notes that 7 million tech jobs were posted, up 16 percent year over year from 2023, and it unemployment dropped to 2.8 percent from 3.5 percent. he believes people still compare the market to 2021-2022, which was an anomaly, and the current state is actually normal.

how does taylor desseyn recommend developers prepare for ai in their careers?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says developers must start using ai, researching it, and understanding how to implement it in their workflow now rather than waiting. he compares the shift to when react came out and developers who ignored it consistently had trouble finding jobs afterward. he says you do not need to pivot immediately, but you cannot stick your head in the sand anymore because ai job postings are up seven times.

what does taylor desseyn say about getting involved in local tech meetups for finding jobs?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says getting involved in a local tech meetup should be the first step because that is where your next opportunity actually comes from. he notes that almost every meetup in the nashville area has folded except three or four, and stresses that being present at in-person events is not normal anymore but is critical. he says this is where jobs happen, not just online applications.
