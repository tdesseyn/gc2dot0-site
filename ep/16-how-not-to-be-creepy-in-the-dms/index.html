how not to be creepy (in the dm's)

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • before dming someone, comment on and engage with their posts for a while so you are not a stranger
  • when you do dm, reference something specific the person did or posted instead of just saying hi or whats up
  • use the jab jab jab right hook approach, give value through comments and likes before you ask for anything
  • when someone changes their job status on linkedin, comment and dm them asking how they found that job to start a conversation
  • dont open a recruiting or sales dm with claims about being different or promising huge results, just be yourself and stay low key

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about messaging someone on linkedin before you know them?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says you should follow someone and comment on their posts for a while before dming them, so you're not a complete stranger. when you do send a dm, reference something specific they recently did or posted rather than just saying hi or what's up. he recommends using flattery or commenting on their work to start the conversation.

what is taylor desseyn's jab jab jab right hook approach to networking on linkedin?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends using gary vaynerchuk's jab jab jab right hook framework for networking. the approach means you should keep giving value through likes and comments repeatedly before you ask for anything. his suggested cadence is comment, like, comment, like, comment, like, then finally dm to ask for something after 30 to 45 days of engagement.
