ep 38 · jun 7, 2021

let's hang

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • instead of applying through a company website, find people you know on the company's linkedin page and dm them directly
  • when you dm someone at a target company, ask for a quick virtual coffee to learn about the role rather than asking for a job
  • build a small mock-up or proof of concept using the company's own product or api before reaching out
  • use that mock-up as an excuse to message a developer advocate or senior engineer at the company for feedback
  • creating something real for a hiring manager gets more attention than offering to work for free or sending a generic greeting

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about applying through company websites?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says job seekers should not submit resumes through company websites. instead, he recommends taking information from the job posting, going to the company's linkedin page, finding people you're connected to who work there, and directly messaging those individuals. this approach bypasses applicant tracking systems and gets you in front of real people.

how does taylor desseyn suggest getting attention from a hiring manager?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends creating a mock-up or proof of concept using the target company's own software or api. he then advises messaging a developer advocate or senior engineer at the company to ask for feedback on what you built, which creates an excuse for a conversation. he emphasizes that creating something real gets immediate attention, while offering to work for free or asking for coffee meetings does not work at scale.
