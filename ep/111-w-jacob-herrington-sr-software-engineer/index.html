ep 111 · feb 9, 2022

w/jacob herrington, sr. software engineer

guest: jacob herrington, sr. software engineer

  • rank companies into tiers like a, b, c, and absolutely not to stay organized during a busy search
  • ask companies directly how they ensure pay bands are fair so people doing the same work aren't paid unequally
  • be wary of recruiters who ask for a long call right away since that can signal a long drawn out process
  • put yourself out there on social media and content creation to become discoverable instead of relying only on applications
  • don't be intimidated by job postings since most requirements are a wish list and no candidate checks every box
