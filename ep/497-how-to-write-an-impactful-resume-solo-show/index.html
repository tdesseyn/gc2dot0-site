how to write an impactful resume - solo show

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • keep your resume header small, skip the address, and use a dedicated job search email instead of your personal inbox
  • write a bullet pointed professional summary matching the job description's must have and nice to have skills so recruiters call you faster
  • start every bullet with an action verb and make it quantitative, showing what you did, what tools you used, and the result
  • add a technologies used bullet to each role so recruiters can keyword search and find you in applicant tracking systems
  • keep formatting simple and boring with no colors or double columns, since content and results are what set a resume apart
