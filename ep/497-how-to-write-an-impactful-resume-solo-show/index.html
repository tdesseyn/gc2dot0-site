how to write an impactful resume - solo show

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • keep your resume header small, skip the address, and use a dedicated job search email instead of your personal inbox
  • write a bullet pointed professional summary matching the job description's must have and nice to have skills so recruiters call you faster
  • start every bullet with an action verb and make it quantitative, showing what you did, what tools you used, and the result
  • add a technologies used bullet to each role so recruiters can keyword search and find you in applicant tracking systems
  • keep formatting simple and boring with no colors or double columns, since content and results are what set a resume apart

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about the resume header and contact information?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to keep your header small and avoid making your name a huge font that takes up a third of the page. he recommends skipping your physical address unless you need to show you are local, and using a job search specific email instead of your personal inbox so you do not mix holiday invites and spam with recruiter messages. include your name, email, linkedin profile, and github or portfolio link.

how does taylor desseyn recommend writing the professional summary section?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says the professional summary should be five to seven bullet points matching the must have and nice to have skills from the job description you are applying to. he explains that when he was a recruiter, if the professional summary had the required skills, he would call the candidate without reading the rest of the resume. reverse engineer what the job wants and put those specific technologies and years of experience in your summary.

what does taylor say about writing bullet points for each job on a resume?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says every bullet point should start with an action verb and follow the structure of what you did, what tools you used, and what the result was. he emphasizes making bullets quantitative, showing how you increased revenue, users, or decreased runtime. the first three bullet points of your latest position should be the most impactful, and at the end of each role add a technologies used bullet so recruiters can control f and find your resume in keyword searches.
