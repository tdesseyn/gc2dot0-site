resume writing. then what?

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • if you work in it, put your resume on dice since only it professionals use it, which cuts down on noise
  • know that recruiters search by last activity, so logging into dice to edit your resume can trigger calls even if you are not job hunting
  • use career builder if you are outside it since it has a broad reach and is easy to use
  • expect more recruiter contact the more job boards your resume sits on, so post selectively if you want fewer calls
  • on linkedin use a professional photo and describe your job in one simple sentence plus a second sentence listing your technologies
