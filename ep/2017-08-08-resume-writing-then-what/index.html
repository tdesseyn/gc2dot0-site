resume writing. then what?

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • if you work in it, put your resume on dice since only it professionals use it, which cuts down on noise
  • know that recruiters search by last activity, so logging into dice to edit your resume can trigger calls even if you are not job hunting
  • use career builder if you are outside it since it has a broad reach and is easy to use
  • expect more recruiter contact the more job boards your resume sits on, so post selectively if you want fewer calls
  • on linkedin use a professional photo and describe your job in one simple sentence plus a second sentence listing your technologies

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about which job board it professionals should use?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says it professionals should use dice because only people in the technology field can be on it, which reduces noise compared to career builder where anybody can post. he spends most of his own recruiting time on dice for this reason. for people outside it, he recommends career builder because it has broad national reach and is easy to use.

why does taylor desseyn warn against editing your dice resume when you're not job hunting?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn warns that recruiters search by last activity on dice, so logging in to make any edit triggers your profile to appear in searches even if you are not looking for work. this means you will get recruiter emails and calls simply from updating your resume. his senior developer friends sometimes accidentally do this and get flooded with contact immediately.

what does taylor desseyn recommend putting in your linkedin summary as a developer?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends writing just two sentences in your linkedin summary. the first sentence should describe your job in simple terms as if explaining it to your grandmother, like saying you develop software that helps field technicians communicate with back office. the second sentence should list the specific technologies you use, which lets recruiters search for your skills without reading long paragraphs.
