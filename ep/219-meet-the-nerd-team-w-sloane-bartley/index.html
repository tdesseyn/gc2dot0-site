meet the nerd team w/sloane bartley

guest: sloane bartley, recruiter on taylor's team

  • treat recruiters as an extra set of eyes watching the market for you, even if they cannot place you right now
  • when a recruiter has nothing open for your skill set, ask for honesty instead of silence so you can keep searching elsewhere
  • use a simple board to track candidates or job leads by skill set so you can match quickly when something opens up
  • set up regular check ins with your network so you know who is still looking and who has moved on
  • focus less on finding one perfect calling and more on what in your current job brings you joy and lines up with your values

questions this episode answers

what does sloane bartley say about honesty when a recruiter has no open roles for you?

on guidance counselor 2.0, sloane bartley says recruiters should tell candidates honestly when they have nothing in their skill set right now instead of ghosting them. she lets front-end developers know she meets many of them but currently has few front-end roles, so they should keep searching elsewhere. she positions herself as an extra set of eyes watching the market who will reach out the moment something opens up.

how does sloane bartley track candidates by skill set so she can match them quickly?

on guidance counselor 2.0, sloane bartley explains she uses a trello board organized by skill set columns like dot net, javascript and ruby to track active candidates. when a new job comes in for a react developer, she goes straight to the react column to see who fits the salary range, years of experience and nice-to-have skills. this board streamlines matching candidates to jobs instead of searching from scratch every time.
