ep 195 · dec 19, 2022

from opera singer to software developer

guest: anna, director of product and engineering, axel springer national media and tech

  • treat your job search like a three step process: learn technical skills, build a community, then apply with tailored materials
  • use a germany style education voucher program as inspiration to research free or subsidized reskilling options in your area
  • post your learning journey publicly, even small topics like the difference between class and id help beginners and build your social proof
  • record a short video cover letter answering the questions employers usually ask, like your tech stack and work authorization status
  • pick one or two polished, complete portfolio projects instead of many small ones so you can show you can handle real world complexity

questions this episode answers

what does anna say about building a portfolio as a junior developer?

on guidance counselor 2.0, anna, director of product and engineering at axel springer national media and tech, says it is better to have one to two excellent, totally finished projects than ten small code snippets. this shows you can work at scale and handle the complexity of a real code base. juniors should get used to modularizing code and organizing tasks through larger projects rather than simple to-do lists or clocks.

what does anna recommend for breaking into tech without experience?

on guidance counselor 2.0, anna says job seekers should treat the transition as a three step process. first, learn the technical skills through courses or self-teaching. second, build a community and create social proof by blogging, tweeting your learning journey, or attending meetups to show you have those skills. third, apply for jobs with tailored materials like customized cover letters and research on each company.

what does anna say about using video in a job application?

on guidance counselor 2.0, anna created a two-minute video cover letter for her portfolio that answered all the questions german employers typically have about candidates. she covered her experience with digital marketing and project management, her tech stack, her ability to speak german, and her work authorization status. the video got 25,000 views in 24 hours on twitter and brought her 12 job leads in her inbox within that time.
