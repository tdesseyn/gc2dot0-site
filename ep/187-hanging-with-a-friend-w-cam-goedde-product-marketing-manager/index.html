hanging with a friend w/cam goedde, product marketing manager at dell

guest: cam goedde, product marketing manager at dell

  • when switching into a new role, lean into your actual strengths instead of comparing yourself to specialists around you
  • give yourself a few months to step back from day-to-day tasks and figure out what unique value you bring
  • stop waiting for content to be perfect, post the rough version now and let the idea evolve over time
  • build a tangible hobby or physical habit outside of work so you can see real output and avoid burnout
  • when picking communities or projects to join, look for people and teams that are public and transparent rather than anonymous
