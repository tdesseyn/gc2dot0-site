ep 464 · jun 11, 2025

hiring trends and the power of community w/ andy hutsell

guest: andy hutsell, leads permanent placement sales at ronstad digital

  • core tech skills like system engineering, network engineering, and devops are still in demand despite ai buzzwords dominating headlines
  • job seekers should expect different companies to want different things, from cover letters to ai-showcasing, so stay flexible and true to your style
  • get your resume reviewed often since agency recruiters see macro market trends and can help you avoid overpricing yourself
  • when reaching out to a recruiter for the first time, ask how they prefer to interact with talent instead of just asking for a job
  • build a community of people to do life with professionally, since human connection and trust can't be replaced by any process or technology
