ep 180 · oct 17, 2022

so you think you're a senior engineer w/ andrew wyborski

guest: andrew wyborski, engineering manager at kroger

  • separate the question of skill level from title and salary band since every company labels seniority differently
  • senior engineers impact a wider group of teams, not just their own, by setting direction and standards
  • know why you call yourself senior and back it up with how you adapted, mentored, and solved harder problems, not just years of experience
  • keep a running log of your wins in cause, action, result format so review time and resume updates are easy
  • ask hiring managers why a requirement like a degree exists so you can judge if it is a real need or just a checkbox
