questions this episode answers

what does anthony mays say about breaking into big tech companies like google early in your career?

on guidance counselor 2.0, anthony mays says slow down before targeting big tech companies. he explains you need both the right information and the right work ethic; if you have one but not the other, you will fail. he compares it to professional sports: you cannot do six months of learning basketball and expect to join the lakers; you need to understand the mindset, work ethic, and interview process that big tech demands.

how does anthony mays recommend building your professional network on linkedin?

on guidance counselor 2.0, anthony mays says be genuinely social on linkedin instead of just sending connection requests or cold messages with your resume. he stresses that people forget the social part of social networking; you need to introduce yourself, express interest in others, and have real conversations. the most important skill he learned in computer science was writing and communicating clearly, which helps you build relationships and open doors.

what does anthony mays say about negotiating a job offer?

on guidance counselor 2.0, anthony mays says always negotiate every offer and never just sign the first offer you receive. he recommends doing your homework by finding someone in a similar position and asking if the offer looks fair. he also says realize that everything is negotiable if the company wants you enough, and never let anyone rush your decision on something that will impact the next three to five years of your life.
