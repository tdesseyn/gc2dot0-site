ep 149 · jun 10, 2022

setting boundaries w/ shaundai person

guest: shaundai person, senior software engineer at netflix

  • cut out streaming apps and social media when you are trying to learn to code or job search on the side
  • pick a few non-negotiables like family and your current job, then build your schedule around protecting those first
  • before saying yes to a new ask, check if it benefits the other person, benefits you, and benefits your company or goals
  • set a hard stop time for work each day and actually shut the computer down to protect your personal time
  • networking through speaking, twitter and genuine conversations can lead directly to job opportunities without ever cold pitching for a role

questions this episode answers

how does shaundai person decide whether to say yes to new opportunities?

on guidance counselor 2.0, shaundai person explains she first asks herself if the opportunity sparks joy. if it does not, she says no immediately. if maybe, she asks whether it aligns with one of her big six-month goals or benefits a group she cares about. her manager ryan burgess at netflix uses a three-question framework: does it benefit the other person, does it benefit me, and does it benefit the company.

how did shaundai person get her job at netflix?

on guidance counselor 2.0, shaundai person says she met her manager ryan burgess on twitter by being active in the community and speaking at conferences. she never sent a cold pitch or asked how to get into netflix. when ryan posted a job, she replied saying she might not be ready but asked if he would be hiring in six months, and he suggested they talk anyway, which led to her getting hired.
