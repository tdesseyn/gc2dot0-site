ep 414 · feb 10, 2025

promotion by layoff w/ ai staff eng, john vandivier

guest: john vandivier, staff software engineer

  • treat your job search like a full time job and track every application in a spreadsheet with a real human point of contact
  • follow a rule of three, don't apply to more than three roles at one company so recruiters and referrers aren't confused
  • use the snowball method, start interviewing at smaller companies to build confidence before tackling your dream tier one companies
  • reach out for a referral before you apply, not after, and message multiple contacts at a company for better odds
  • split your job search day evenly between applying, networking, and interview prep, then adjust weekly based on what results you're seeing

questions this episode answers

what does john vandivier say about timing referral requests during a job search?

on guidance counselor 2.0, john vandivier says to seek a referral before you apply to a job, not after. he recommends waiting up to three business days after finding a posting to contact multiple people at the company for referrals before submitting your application. this approach worked much better for him than applying first and then reaching out for support afterward.

how did john vandivier structure his daily job search routine after being laid off?

on guidance counselor 2.0, john vandivier says he treated the job search like a full-time job, working twelve hours a day six days a week. he divided his time evenly at first between applying to jobs, social networking and interview prep, giving each activity at least one hour. for interview prep he spent one hour each on coding, system design and behavioral practice, then adjusted weekly based on results and where he was failing.
