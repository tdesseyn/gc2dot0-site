staying positive in your job search w/ bree hall, developer advocate

guest: bree hall, developer advocate

  • when job searching keep a running list of questions you missed in interviews and study those gaps before the next one
  • a non-dev first job like client support can still build skills like empathy and real examples that help you land a dev role later
  • mindset matters in a tough search, focus on doing one small improvement a day instead of trying to do everything at once
  • don't be afraid to ask interviewers questions about the process or to negotiate things beyond salary like remote days or tools
  • don't search alone, surround yourself with a positive community since comparing yourself to others online can hurt your confidence

questions this episode answers

what does bree hall say about tracking interview mistakes during a job search?

on guidance counselor 2.0, bree hall says she kept a running list of questions she missed or didn't know in interviews, then went home and researched those gaps so she could study them before the next interview. she would write down all the questions as best she could remember after leaving each in-person interview. this helped her identify specific weaknesses like not knowing what frameworks were and prepare more effectively over time.

how did bree hall's non-dev support job help her land a developer role?

on guidance counselor 2.0, bree hall says her three years in client support gave her empathy and real examples she could use in interviews. when asked behavioral questions about conflicts or working with different people, she could pull from actual support experiences instead of invented scenarios. she says client support shaped how she thinks about code and customers, teaching her to develop with empathy and meet users where they are.

what does bree hall recommend for staying positive during a long job search?

on guidance counselor 2.0, bree hall says to focus on doing one small thing better each day instead of overwhelming yourself trying to do everything at once, like just updating your resume summary one night. she also says don't search alone because comparing yourself to others online will hurt your confidence and make you question if you deserve to be there. surrounding yourself with a positive community is essential because being around inspired people is contagious and she has never regretted attending an in-person event.
