the tactical part of community w/ pauline narvas, community @ vercel

guest: pauline narvas, staff community engineer at vercel

  • build one central hub for community conversations instead of scattering updates across every channel
  • run regular live sessions or office hours so customers and internal people can show their faces and share what they are building
  • consistency beats a single big push, post and engage on a repeating schedule across multiple channels
  • when picking conferences, go where your actual users already gather, and ask customers directly where they spend their development budget
  • meetups often beat large conferences for roi because they create direct relationships at a much smaller, cheaper scale
