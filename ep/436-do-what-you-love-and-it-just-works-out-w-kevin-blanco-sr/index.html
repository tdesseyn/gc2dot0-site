do what you love and it just works out w/ kevin blanco, sr. devrel

guest: kevin blanco, sr. developer relations advocate at appsmith

  • build your career on community involvement, not just applying through hr or job boards
  • show up and contribute to communities to help others first, not to immediately get a job out of it
  • skip a formal degree in tech and instead spend that time and money building real projects and a portfolio
  • invest in communication and soft skills since connecting with people can matter as much as technical ability in interviews
  • keep applying to speak or contribute even after rejection, since consistency and showing up is what separates people over time
