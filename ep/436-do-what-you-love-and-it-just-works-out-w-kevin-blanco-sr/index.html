do what you love and it just works out w/ kevin blanco, sr. devrel

guest: kevin blanco, sr. developer relations advocate at appsmith

  • build your career on community involvement, not just applying through hr or job boards
  • show up and contribute to communities to help others first, not to immediately get a job out of it
  • skip a formal degree in tech and instead spend that time and money building real projects and a portfolio
  • invest in communication and soft skills since connecting with people can matter as much as technical ability in interviews

questions this episode answers

what does kevin blanco say about getting involved in tech communities for job opportunities?

on guidance counselor 2.0, kevin blanco says you should join communities to help other people first, not to get something for yourself. he spent ten years contributing to drupal, javascript, and google communities without any pay or developer advocate job, but eventually about eighty percent of his new jobs came from people reaching out after meeting him at community events. he says the community will pay you back over time if you focus on supporting others instead of looking for immediate career benefits.

what does kevin blanco say about soft skills and communication in tech interviews?

on guidance counselor 2.0, kevin blanco says investing in communication and soft skills is just as important as technical ability because hiring decisions often come from human connection. he learned this late in his career but realized that while other candidates interviewing for the same positions could code as well or better than him, he got hired because he connected with interviewers through empathy and strong communication. he recommends investing in how you speak and communicate, not just in technical certifications and coding skills.
