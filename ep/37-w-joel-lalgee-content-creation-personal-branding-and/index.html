w/joel lalgee & content creation, personal branding, and clubhouse

guest: joel lalgee, human headhunter, helps recruitment agencies with personal branding on linkedin

  • tie personal stories into business lessons on linkedin, overcoming a struggle resonates more than a resume
  • treat clubhouse as a way to start conversations, but always move the relationship to a real one on one call
