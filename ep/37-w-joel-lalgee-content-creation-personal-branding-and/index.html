w/joel lalgee & content creation, personal branding, and clubhouse

guest: joel lalgee, human headhunter, helps recruitment agencies with personal branding on linkedin

  • focus on quality over quantity in job search, a few deep connections beat mass applying every time
  • commenting on other people's linkedin posts counts as content and can help you build real connections
  • you lose out on jobs mostly to internal referrals, so network your way into becoming one
  • tie personal stories into business lessons on linkedin, overcoming a struggle resonates more than a resume
  • treat clubhouse as a way to start conversations, but always move the relationship to a real one on one call

questions this episode answers

what does joel lalgee say about commenting on linkedin as a job seeker?

on guidance counselor 2.0, joel lalgee says commenting on linkedin is content and vastly underrated for job seekers. instead of worrying about what to post, he recommends spending time supporting people in your industry by commenting on their content, because everyone wants views and comments. this strategy helps you make connections because you become that person who supports others, which can give you an advantage over other job seekers.

what does joel lalgee recommend posting on linkedin as a job seeker?

on guidance counselor 2.0, joel lalgee recommends two buckets of content for job seekers. first, post about the problems you solve and how you solve them, thinking of employers as clients who need value. second, tell personal stories about overcoming something like fear or hardship and tie them into a business lesson, because people connect with those stories and this combination wins on linkedin.
