3 things to do when you are laid off

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • update your resume first and tell a story with project details instead of a long list of bullet points
  • make sure your linkedin has an updated title, banner, and photo since it exists for recruiters and hiring managers to find you
  • collect recommendations on linkedin to help you stand out from other applicants
  • work with recruiters before applying elsewhere since they can do a lot of the heavy lifting for you
  • get your resume uploaded to job boards and consider ones like dice and indeed for tech roles
