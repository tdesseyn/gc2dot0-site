ep 29 · mar 10, 2021

chat w/webflow's quality engineer manager - josh cypher - on hiring

guest: josh cypher, quality engineering manager at webflow

  • global remote hiring means you compete on pay with candidates everywhere, not just your local market
  • location based pay tiers can lose you good candidates once remote work becomes common instead of rare
  • before planning a return to office ask if the job truly requires a body in a seat every day
  • think about succession planning for hires who may only stay a short time during uncertain job markets
  • in interviews explain your reasoning out loud since communicating how you think matters as much as the right answer
