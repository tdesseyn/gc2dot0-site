ep 98 · jan 5, 2022

dealing with rejection

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • if you haven't heard back from a recruiter, reach out and simply ask for a yes or no either way
  • recruiters often sit on bad news because they struggle to deliver it, so following up helps both sides
  • rejection usually reflects fit or timing, not your worth, so try not to take it personally
  • keep taking interviews even when you're not actively looking so rejection stops feeling so heavy
  • building real relationships instead of mass applying can cut down how often you face rejection

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn recommend saying to a recruiter who hasn't responded?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends reaching out with a simple message that acknowledges you might have been rejected and asks for confirmation either way. he suggests saying something like you haven't heard back in a while, which is probably not the best sign, and you'd love to know one way or the other. taylor admits recruiters often sit on bad news because they struggle to deliver it, so this approach makes it easier for them to respond.

how does taylor desseyn say you should view rejection in a job search?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says rejection is not personal and doesn't reflect your worth. he explains the hiring process involves imperfect people on both sides and rejection often comes down to timing or fit rather than your qualifications. taylor recommends continuing to interview even when you're not actively looking so rejection stops feeling so heavy, and he emphasizes that building relationships reduces rejection compared to mass applying.
