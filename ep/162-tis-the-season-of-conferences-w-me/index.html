ep 162 · aug 1, 2022

tis the season of conferences w/ me :)

guest: me :)

  • before a conference pick five to ten speakers you actually want to meet instead of trying to see everyone
  • you dont have to attend every session, choose a handful of must see talks and give yourself breaks
  • connect with every person you meet on social media right away since a business card alone usually gets forgotten
  • stick near one or two people you already know at a conference to get introduced into new circles in the hallway track
  • its fine to skip sessions and rest in your hotel room if a conference starts feeling overwhelming
