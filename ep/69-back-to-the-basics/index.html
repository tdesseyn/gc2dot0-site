questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say to do first when you get laid off?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to spend time editing your resume first, making it specific around project work. he recommends getting your resume, linkedin profile, and job board accounts set up within 24 to 48 hours of getting laid off. after the resume, update your linkedin because recruiters and hiring managers check it to judge your validity, like how people check a company's website or a restaurant's instagram.

how does taylor desseyn recommend finding recruiters to message on linkedin?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to search for technical recruiters in your area using linkedin filters, then only message recruiters who have been at the same company for at least two years because recruiters bounce around a lot. he recommends spending an entire day going through profiles to find the right people. when messaging them, use three parts: flattery about their company, a specific short time request like six minutes, and what you want to ask.

what does taylor desseyn say about using dice and indeed for job searches?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to use job board sites like dice and indeed, and to log into them once a day because that triggers the search feature with recruiters to find you. he explains that recruiters run searches off job sites for 24 hours of activity. he emphasizes that you should not approach the job search by applying to jobs, but instead by building relationships with recruiters, sending at least 50 customizable direct messages per day.
