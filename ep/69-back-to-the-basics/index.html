back to the basics

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • update your resume first, focused on project work, before anything else in the job search
  • build out your linkedin presence since recruiters and hiring managers check it to judge your validity
  • use job board sites like dice and indeed and log in daily to trigger recruiter search visibility
  • find recruiters who have been at the same company for at least two years before dming them
  • when dming recruiters use flattery, a specific ask, and a short time request like six minutes
