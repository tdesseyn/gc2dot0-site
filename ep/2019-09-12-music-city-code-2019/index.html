questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about screening recruiters before working with them?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode at music city code 2019, taylor desseyn recommends using a two and two rule to screen recruiters: they should have at least two years of experience in your market and two years at their current company. he also advises working with only two, at most three, recruiters at a time so you stay locked in step with them and they don't price gouge you by marking up your rate everywhere. ask how many people the recruiter has placed with the specific company or team before trusting their contract to hire pitch.

what questions does taylor desseyn recommend asking recruiters about contract to hire roles?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode at music city code 2019, taylor desseyn says to ask how many people the recruiting team has placed with the whole company and how many people that specific recruiter has placed there. ask how long the position has been open, why it's open, and if there's budget approval to bring you on full time. he recommends asking for the conversion salary upfront and even requesting the recruiter forward you the submittal summary showing the salary in the hiring manager's inbox.

how does taylor desseyn recommend structuring a linkedin profile for developer job searches?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode at music city code 2019, taylor desseyn says your linkedin should have three sentences at minimum. the first sentence explains what you do in plain terms that you would use with someone non-technical. the second sentence lists your primary technologies you're currently using. the third sentence should be your entire tech stack, because recruiters search linkedin by technology keywords and zip code, so this structure directs them to call you about the right positions.
