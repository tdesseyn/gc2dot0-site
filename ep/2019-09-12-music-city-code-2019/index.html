music city code 2019

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • screen recruiters with the two and two rule, two years in the market and two years at their current company
  • work with only two or three recruiters at a time so they stay locked in step with you and don't price gouge you
  • ask a recruiter how many people they have placed with a specific team before trusting their contract to hire pitch
  • quantify your resume with real numbers and keep it to two or three pages instead of forcing everything onto one page
  • structure your linkedin in three sentences, what you do in plain terms, your main technologies, then your full tech stack
