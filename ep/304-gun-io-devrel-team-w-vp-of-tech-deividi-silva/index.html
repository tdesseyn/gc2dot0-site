gun.io devrel team w/vp of tech, deividi silva

guest: deividi silva, vp of technology at gun.io

  • build your freelance profile with real impact details, not just a resume dump, since results-oriented info gets you noticed by clients
  • expect a conversational approval interview focused on your career and technical judgment instead of coding tests or hackerrank style challenges
  • strong communication matters most as a contractor since you are responsible for keeping client projects clear and on track from start to finish
  • going freelance does not mean going alone, ongoing support from a team can help you navigate tough calls and trade offs on client projects
  • stay curious and keep asking questions throughout your career, looking at the data yourself instead of being afraid to look uninformed
