ep 304 · dec 8, 2023

gun.io devrel team w/vp of tech, deividi silva

guest: deividi silva, vp of technology at gun.io

  • build your freelance profile with real impact details, not just a resume dump, since results-oriented info gets you noticed by clients
  • expect a conversational approval interview focused on your career and technical judgment instead of coding tests or hackerrank style challenges
  • strong communication matters most as a contractor since you are responsible for keeping client projects clear and on track from start to finish
  • going freelance does not mean going alone, ongoing support from a team can help you navigate tough calls and trade offs on client projects
  • stay curious and keep asking questions throughout your career, looking at the data yourself instead of being afraid to look uninformed

questions this episode answers

how does gun.io support freelance engineers during client projects according to deividi silva?

on guidance counselor 2.0, deividi silva explains that gun.io runs a talent success management program where the team checks in with freelancers weekly or biweekly throughout an engagement. freelancers can reach out for technical conversations when they face tough decisions or trade-offs on solo contracts. the goal is to ensure contractors never feel alone, especially on their first freelance project, by giving them access to former ctos and engineering leaders who can help them navigate client work.

what does deividi silva recommend about profile details when applying to gun.io?

on guidance counselor 2.0, deividi silva says engineers should not just submit a resume to gun.io but instead fill out a detailed profile showing the specific impact they had in previous roles. he advises writing information in a results-oriented way so the team can highlight candidates properly to clients. victoria, the community leader, helps applicants write their profiles to demonstrate real outcomes rather than generic job descriptions.
