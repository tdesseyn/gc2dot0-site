questions this episode answers

what does stephen shary recommend for comparing technical career ladders across companies?

on guidance counselor 2.0, stephen shary, principal engineer at acv auctions, recommends using levels.fyi to compare technical career ladders across different companies. he explains that titles like staff engineer, principal, architect, and fellow mean completely different things at different organizations, so the site helps you understand what these levels actually represent. shary notes the industry lacks standardization, making this comparison tool valuable for planning your next move.

what book does stephen shary recommend for senior engineers considering leadership without management?

on guidance counselor 2.0, stephen shary recommends the book staff engineer by will larson for senior engineers wondering what comes next without becoming a manager. the book is subtitled leadership beyond the management track and specifically addresses software engineers who want to advance technically. shary says larson interviewed dozens of people at large companies and identifies different leadership archetypes beyond management.

what does stephen shary say about writing reference implementations as a technical leader?

on guidance counselor 2.0, stephen shary says that when moving into leadership roles, you should write reference implementations, not just working code. a reference implementation is the perfect example others can copy, something good enough to post on the internet showing the correct way to build that type of application. shary explains this means understanding all the edge cases and being able to answer every question about why things work a certain way, not just getting experimental features to function.
