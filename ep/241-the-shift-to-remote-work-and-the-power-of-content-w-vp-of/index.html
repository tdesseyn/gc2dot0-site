ep 241 · apr 11, 2023

the shift to remote work and the power of content w/vp of software engineering, andrew moore

guest: andrew moore, vp of software engineering at nations lending

  • apply within the first few hours of a job posting since high volume roles fill up fast with applicants
  • skip generic easy apply templates and send a direct message explaining why you fit the role
  • when networking or applying, open with something personal or a shared connection to the work to stand out
  • if you lead remote teams, be intentional about one on ones and check ins since casual hallway contact is gone
  • build your online presence consistently on linkedin since an active profile gets noticed faster than a silent one
