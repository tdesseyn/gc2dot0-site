ep 193 · dec 14, 2022

from long nights at the er to software developer

guest: emmett, software developer at an early stage startup, founder of coder dads

  • build a network by consistently sharing and summarizing content from podcasts you actually listen to on twitter
  • if you cannot attend conferences or take months off due to family or work, use small pockets of time to engage online
  • take every call you get during a job search, even rejections can lead to referrals for other roles
  • if you get laid off, ask for a letter of reference from your ceo or manager explaining it was not about your performance
  • cheer on other people's wins in the job search instead of feeling discouraged by their progress
