break the glass (solo show)

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • if you get laid off, take the next day off to process before you start job searching so you are not acting on emotion
  • rewrite your resume to be achievement and quantitative based instead of just listing tasks like react or aws
  • if you are open to both front end and back end roles, build two different resumes each focused on that specific track
  • treat your job search like an eight to five job with a routine, including resume work, linkedin engagement, and messaging recruiters and companies daily
  • use a tool like trello to track every application by stage so you know exactly when to follow up

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say to do the first day after getting laid off?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to take the next day off completely to process the layoff before starting your job search. he emphasizes you should not act on emotion, because most cuts right now are due to budget and overhiring, not performance. taking a day to breathe and think prevents you from making emotional decisions in your search.

how does taylor desseyn recommend writing a tech resume after a layoff?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn says your resume must be achievement-based with quantitative data, not just task lists. instead of writing i wrote react code, explain what you built, how many users it served, how much money it saved, or how many transactions per second it handled. if you are open to both front end and back end roles, create two different resumes with each focused on the relevant projects and skills for that track.
