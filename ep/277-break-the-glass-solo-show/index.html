break the glass (solo show)

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • if you get laid off, take the next day off to process before you start job searching so you are not acting on emotion
  • rewrite your resume to be achievement and quantitative based instead of just listing tasks like react or aws
  • if you are open to both front end and back end roles, build two different resumes each focused on that specific track
  • treat your job search like an eight to five job with a routine, including resume work, linkedin engagement, and messaging recruiters and companies daily
  • use a tool like trello to track every application by stage so you know exactly when to follow up
