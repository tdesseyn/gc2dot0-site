ep 145 · may 27, 2022

work life balance w/ gabe greenberg

guest: gabe greenberg, founder of g2i

  • experiment with a four day or reduced hour work week without cutting pay to boost retention and output
  • write job descriptions that are simple, fun and transparent instead of dry and generic
  • use a short video from the hiring manager to give candidates real context on the role
  • when raising work life balance with a manager, frame it as how you feel rather than what you need
  • watch for workaholism as a reliance on adrenaline, not just long hours, and address the underlying habit
