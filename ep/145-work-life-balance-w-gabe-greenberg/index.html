questions this episode answers

what does gabe greenberg say about implementing a four-day work week without cutting pay?

on guidance counselor 2.0, gabe greenberg says founders should experiment with a four-day work week without reducing salary because it attracts better talent, reduces turnover, and leads to higher quality work when people are more rested. he explains that working less should not mean less productivity, and companies can try variations like every other friday off, split team schedules, or a 32-hour week with half-day fridays. you should never pay employees less for a four-day work week.

how does gabe greenberg recommend making job descriptions stand out?

on guidance counselor 2.0, gabe greenberg says companies should make job descriptions simple, fun, and transparent instead of dry and boring. he recommends including a short loom video from the hiring manager explaining the role and sharing internal vision videos to give candidates real context. he personally writes job descriptions for important roles and requires a loom video for every posting because it gives candidates a transparent view into the company and helps them understand who will be interviewing them.

how should an employee ask their manager for better work-life balance according to gabe greenberg?

on guidance counselor 2.0, gabe greenberg says employees should frame the conversation around how they feel and how it affects their productivity rather than just stating what they need. he recommends saying that you are feeling overwhelmed and that it is getting in the way of producing quality work for the team. he warns that research shows people who directly ask for better work-life balance sometimes get passed over for promotion, so framing it as a performance issue rather than a personal demand can be more effective.
