why meet a recruiter

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • meet in person with a recruiter instead of relying only on phone calls when job searching
  • partner with one or two recruiters you trust rather than spreading yourself across many
  • visit the recruiter's office so you can also meet the team who may handle your opportunities
  • know who is representing you by meeting both the recruiter and the sales side of the team
  • dress business professional when meeting a recruiter, even though a full suit is not required
