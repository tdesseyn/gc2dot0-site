questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about how long job searches are taking right now?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn says some job searches are currently taking six to nine months before people find their next role. he emphasizes the importance of having a savings runway to survive this extended timeline. taylor recommends that anyone who currently has a job should prioritize building up their savings account to prepare for potential layoffs.

what does taylor desseyn recommend instead of applying to big tech companies?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn recommends targeting startups and high growth companies because they are still hiring even while big tech continues layoffs. he suggests following startup focused sources like product hunt and angel list, and getting involved with your local entrepreneur center. taylor explains that big tech over hired during covid and is now cutting back, while smaller startups that raised funding are still actively hiring engineers.

what does taylor desseyn say about using linkedin connections before applying to a job?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn says to check linkedin for mutual connections at a company before you submit your application. he recommends messaging those connections to ask about their experience at the company and what you should keep in mind, but only ask them to be a reference if they truly know you. taylor emphasizes that you need to have an actual advocate at the company who can speak for you, not just a random mutual connection.
