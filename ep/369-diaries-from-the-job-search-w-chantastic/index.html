ep 369 · may 20, 2024

diaries from the job search w/ chantastic

guest: chantastic

  • build a savings runway since some job searches are taking six to nine months right now
  • startups and high growth companies are still hiring even as big tech continues layoffs
  • follow startup focused sources like product hunt and angel list and plug into your local entrepreneur community
  • aim to have one to two real conversations a day during your search instead of just applying online
  • before you apply check linkedin for mutual connections at the company and ask them for insight, not a reference, unless they truly know you
