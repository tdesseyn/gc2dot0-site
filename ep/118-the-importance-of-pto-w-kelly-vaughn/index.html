ep 118 · feb 25, 2022

the importance of pto w/ kelly vaughn

guest: kelly vaughn, business owner, the taproom and gavalo

  • offer unlimited pto with a required minimum, like 20 days a year, so people actually take time off
  • as a leader, model taking time off yourself so your team feels safe doing the same
  • use scheduling tools so messages and emails don't land on people's phones late at night and cause anxiety
  • in interviews, be upfront about what you know and don't know instead of pretending to have experience you lack
  • job seekers should ask hard questions back and talk to current employees to research a company before accepting an offer
