ep 220 · feb 17, 2023

back to the basics (solo show)

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • treat your resume and linkedin as the building blocks of your job search and be obsessed about getting them right
  • keep linkedin as a short summary with just three or four bullet points per role, not your full work history
  • keep your resume simple, especially for creative roles, so reviewers focus on content instead of design choices
  • google your target job title and pull common buzzwords from job descriptions to add to your resume and linkedin
  • message recruiters and ask for one piece of feedback on your resume or linkedin to improve it for free
