questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about how much detail to put on linkedin versus a resume?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says your linkedin should be a short summary with just three or four bullet points per role, not your entire work history in major detail. your resume needs to be longer form content with a more complete work history. he emphasizes that linkedin is your primary place to be discovered on the internet, so it needs key buzzwords, but should remain a summary like a baseball card with key stats.

why does taylor desseyn recommend keeping resumes simple for creative roles?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn shares advice from a creative recruiter who told his wife that if you get creative on your resume when applying for creative jobs, hiring managers will start picking apart your design choices like color schemes and fonts instead of focusing on your actual content. keeping your resume super simple ensures reviewers focus on your qualifications rather than critiquing your design decisions.

how does taylor desseyn suggest getting free resume and linkedin advice from recruiters?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends messaging recruiters on linkedin and asking them for one piece of advice on your resume or one piece of advice on your linkedin. if you do this ten to fifteen times with different recruiters, you will end up with a really well-written resume and linkedin profile for free. this works even if you are a junior developer or switching industries.
