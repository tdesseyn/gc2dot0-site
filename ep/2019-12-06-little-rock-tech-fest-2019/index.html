questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about submitting resumes directly to job postings?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode from little rock tech fest 2019, taylor desseyn says to stop submitting your resume directly to job postings because it disqualifies recruiters from working with you. when you submit your resume to a client, recruiters can no longer represent you for that position. he tells the audience that re-engineering your job search means partnering with a recruiter first, interviewing them, and then letting them do the work for you.

what is the two and two rule for choosing recruiters according to taylor desseyn?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode from little rock tech fest 2019, taylor desseyn recommends the two and two rule for picking recruiters. look for recruiters who have been at the same company for at least two years, because bad recruiters usually jump ship every eighteen months to two years to get their base salary raised. the second part is that the recruiter should have been working in your local market for at least two years, because it takes time to learn companies and build real relationships.

how should a developer format their resume according to taylor desseyn?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode from little rock tech fest 2019, taylor desseyn says a resume should start with a high level summary of your years of experience and primary technologies. for each job, write three to four bullet points: first describe specific projects you worked on as if explaining to your mom or dad, then list technologies used, then include your complete tech stack from front to back. he stresses that recruiters use control f to search for buzzwords, so you need to list every technology even if you only had some interaction with it.
