dec 6, 2019

little rock tech fest 2019

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • partner with two or three recruiters and interview them using specific questions before trusting them with your job search
  • stop submitting resumes directly to job postings since it disqualifies recruiters from working on your behalf
  • use the two and two rule: look for recruiters who have stayed at their company two years and worked in the local market two years
  • format your resume with a high level summary, specific project bullet points, and a full technology stack recruiters can search
  • keep linkedin brief with three sentences per role covering the application, technologies used, and full tech stack
