ep 263 · aug 23, 2023

let me help you (solo show)

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • use twitter lists to narrow thousands of users down to the people you actually want to network with
  • treat networking dms like an interview of the other person, not a pitch about your own value
  • good cold dms include a specific time ask, a specific question, and genuine admiration for the company or person
  • always ask for another introduction at the end of a good conversation, only if the chat is going well
  • if social media feels overwhelming, pick one or two spots like linkedin and discord servers instead of trying to be everywhere
