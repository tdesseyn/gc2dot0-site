ep 192 · dec 12, 2022

tips from a former apple hiring manager

guest: tiago ghisi, former apple hiring manager, engineering director

  • interview every year even when you are happy in your current job so you are ready for great opportunities
  • ask a connection inside a company to submit you as a referral but write your own recommendation for them to use
  • avoid wasting referral energy on a generic resume and instead write specific points on why you are above the bar for the role
  • prepare a bank of star method stories ahead of time so you are not caught off guard in behavior interviews
  • practice system design interviews with time boxing so you do not get stuck on one section like data design and run out of time

questions this episode answers

what does tiago ghisi recommend for getting a referral at a company?

on guidance counselor 2.0, tiago ghisi says you should write your own recommendation and ask a connection inside the company to submit it as your referral. he recommends writing three or four paragraphs following the star method that explain why you are above the bar for the role, highlighting your superpowers from a hiring manager's perspective. this gives you a much higher chance of getting visibility than applying through the company website or working with a recruiter.

how should engineers prepare for behavioral interviews according to tiago ghisi?

on guidance counselor 2.0, tiago ghisi says you need to create a bank of star method stories ahead of time so you are not caught off guard during behavioral interviews. he recommends writing down scripts of your best stories in a table format so you can quickly pick the right example for any question, such as conflict with a peer or a challenging project. you should ask peers or mentors to review your answers to the top ten most common behavioral questions and polish them until you know them by heart.
