austin cumberlander live + finding a job as a developer by putting out content

guest: austin cumberlander, junior software developer

  • post real projects with a short description of what you built and what you learned to start building visibility
  • sharing your honest job search journey and feelings often resonates more than polished project updates
  • end posts with a question to invite comments, that engagement signals and conversation help posts travel further
  • reply to comments and engage with other people's posts or your own audience will stop engaging back
  • stay consistent with posting and don't take it too seriously, focus on your goal instead of fear of judgment
