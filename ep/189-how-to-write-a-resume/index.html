how to write a resume

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • put only your name and a work specific email at the top of your resume, skip phone number and address
  • write a summary section with four or five bullet points on years of experience and top skills so recruiters can decide fast
  • list each job with company, start and end date by month and year, position, location, and a quick summary of what the company does
  • break out professional experience by project with a short overview, bullet points on your tasks, and the technologies used
  • keep your resume simple with no pictures, colors, or double columns so your experience is easy to scan
