faang & the job search w/ andy wong

guest: andy wong, senior software engineer at block

  • treat coding bootcamp as only worth it if you put in serious effort, not because of a placement rate promise
  • expect to fail big tech interviews at least once, learn from it, and try again since the process can reopen in six to twelve months
  • lean into your interviewer during fang style interviews, ask questions, and treat it like a conversation instead of a solo test
  • if you dont know an answer in an interview say so and work through it together instead of freezing up
  • in salary talks assume you have negotiating power, know your options, and be willing to walk away

questions this episode answers

what does andy wong say about coding bootcamps and effort?

on guidance counselor 2.0, andy wong says what you put into a bootcamp is what you get out. he explains that he worked extremely hard during and after his bootcamp, studying hundreds of pages per week, which made the difference. candidates who struggle after bootcamp often had unrealistic expectations about the effort required before, during, and after the program.

how does andy wong recommend handling fang interviews?

on guidance counselor 2.0, andy wong says to lean into your interviewer during fang style interviews and treat it like a conversation, not a solo test. interviewers expect you to ask questions and get unstuck by working together. if you don't know something, say so but add that you want to work together to find the solution, because showing how you respond to hints matters more than knowing the answer immediately.

what does andy wong say about failing big tech interviews?

on guidance counselor 2.0, andy wong says he never passed big tech interviews on the first try but that failing taught him where the bar was set. he explains that if you fail an interview, you can typically reapply in six to twelve months, so take advantage of that chance. experiencing rejection helps you understand what to work on and become more confident for the next round.
