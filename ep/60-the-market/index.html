the market

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • if your team is getting recruiter dms every week, sit down and learn what actually motivates each person to stay

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say hiring managers should do when their team is getting recruiter messages?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says hiring managers need to sit down with each person on their team to understand what motivates them beyond just money. he explains it could be leadership opportunities, title changes, or working with specific technology, and managers need to know their people more intimately than ever before. employees are getting direct messages from recruiters three or four times a week in this market, so retention requires understanding individual intrinsic motivations.

what does taylor desseyn say about companies lowballing candidates in 2021?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says if a candidate asks for a certain salary that is not completely out of line and the hiring manager thinks they are worth it, give them that offer. he describes a situation where a candidate stated their number but the company offered twenty thousand dollars under it, which he says is a mistake. when companies lowball candidates, those candidates will probably keep looking for other opportunities.

how does taylor desseyn say junior developers should gain experience instead of paying for bootcamp?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says junior developers should volunteer their time doing unpaid intern work to gain real world experience instead of spending money on bootcamp or college coursework. he argues candidates should take the time and money they would spend on education and instead do free work to build actual experience. he believes this approach could leave candidates further along in their career than spending twenty thousand dollars on formal education.
