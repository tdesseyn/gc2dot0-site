ep 71 · aug 23, 2021

hiring better

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • hire your leadership team first before bringing on a wave of developers at a small company or startup
  • agree on rate expectations with your recruiter upfront so you are not backtracking after an offer goes out
  • do not reject a candidate solely because of resume formatting or typos, talk to them first to see if they have the skills
  • stay open to contract, contract to hire, and direct hire options since the tight market wont always give you the perfect direct hire candidate
  • stick to the interview process you promised a candidate, adding extra rounds damages trust and makes candidates think the company is dragging them along
