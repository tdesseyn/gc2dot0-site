virtual interview etiquette

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • set up your interview space with a clean well lit background instead of a messy room
  • get a 4k or 1080p camera a ring light and a basic usb mic to look and sound better on calls
  • position your ring light above you instead of directly in your face for better lighting
  • keep your camera on by default during interviews and work calls to show you are engaged
  • skip fake virtual backgrounds since they tend to look unnatural and distracting

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about cameras and lighting for virtual interviews?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says you should get a logitech 4k or 1080p camera, a ring light, and a basic usb microphone like a yeti to look and sound better on video calls. he recommends positioning the ring light above you rather than directly in your face. he emphasizes that good lighting, camera quality, and audio actually make a difference in the interview process.

what does taylor desseyn recommend about using your camera during virtual interviews and work calls?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says the default should be cameras on during interviews and work calls because it shows you are engaged and present. he always does video calls instead of phone calls or text messages because he believes it builds rapport much better. he is not a fan of fake virtual backgrounds because they make you look funny and unnatural, and he has seen very few good ones.
