questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say are the three basics to focus on first in a job search?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says the three basics for a job search are an updated resume, a solid linkedin profile, and a good professional headshot. he considers the resume as top of funnel marketing to get people interested, while linkedin is bottom of funnel to close the deal. if job seekers are going to spend money on the job search, taylor recommends investing in help with the resume and linkedin, plus getting a quality headshot.

how does taylor desseyn recommend using polywork badges to find hiring managers?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends using polywork badges to directly message people who are open to specific topics. he explains that clicking on the hiring badge shows hundreds of people who have marked themselves as open to hiring conversations, and job seekers can message all of them. taylor emphasizes that the direct message feature on polywork is where the real value lies, allowing job seekers to reach out with a personalized message about seeing someone's hiring badge.

what does taylor desseyn tell hiring managers about interviewing during the holidays?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn tells hiring managers to keep interviewing and hiring through the holidays instead of pausing. he explains that most other companies slow down during this time, so continuing to interview gives hiring managers a competitive advantage to attract the best talent. taylor notes that the typical timeline from first interview to start date is five to six weeks, so hiring managers who want people starting in the first quarter need to keep moving through the holiday period.
