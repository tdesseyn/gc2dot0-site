w/john fly, director of se at smiledirectclub

guest: john fly, director of se at smiledirectclub

  • define your company culture early, waiting until you feel the need for it usually means you are already in trouble
  • avoid silos by making sure every new hire understands the company mission, not just their job role
  • when hiring a highly skilled person, be clear in the interview about who the company is so they do not pull culture off track
  • build a standardized interview process so every candidate is evaluated through the same steps and skills criteria
  • skip the canned job description and think through why the role exists so you can speak to it honestly in the interview
