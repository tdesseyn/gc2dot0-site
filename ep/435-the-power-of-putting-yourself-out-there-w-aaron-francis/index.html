the power of putting yourself out there w/ aaron francis

guest: aaron francis, co-founder at tryhard studios

  • lower the bar for what you share by posting a novel to you insight instead of waiting for a universal insight
  • frame your posts as i did this instead of you should always do this so you avoid defending a position you cannot back up
  • build content brain by writing down clever moments while you work so you never run out of things to share
  • ask for the opportunity you want since the worst case is the same no but the best case opens a new door
  • do your best work even when the audience is tiny because you never know who is watching or who they will tell later
