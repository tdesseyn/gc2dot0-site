ep 355 · apr 17, 2024

why you should consider freelance & contracting w/ founder dan mall

guest: dan mall, founder, designer, and creative director

  • build cash savings and in-demand skills before a layoff hits so you have options instead of desperation
  • decide in advance what job or income you would accept if your main career path dried up, so you're not deciding under stress
  • when freelancing, learn your clients buying cycles and plan slow months around them instead of fighting them
  • get your first freelance clients through friends of friends and overflow work, then trade a great testimonial for future higher paying projects
  • frame your freelance or contract work as crucial to a client's business, not a nice to have, so it survives budget cuts

questions this episode answers

how does dan mall recommend getting your first freelance clients?

on guidance counselor 2.0, dan mall says he started by asking 300 people he'd worked with before for overflow work or anything too small for them, sending personal emails to each one. he took scraps like powerpoint decks and one-pagers from friends of friends to build cash in the bank. then he used those early projects to get testimonials, even offering to write the testimonial himself for the client to approve, because testimonials break objections for the next higher-paying client.

what does dan mall say about dealing with slow months when freelancing?

on guidance counselor 2.0, dan mall says he learned to accept the feast or famine cycle instead of trying to fix it. he tracked his clients' buying cycles and realized june and july were always slow, so he stopped fighting it and planned family vacations then. he also created a policy not to start new projects in december because that was another predictable down period, and knowing the pattern in advance let him work it into his schedule.
