questions this episode answers

what does gant laborde say about treating ai as more than a trend?

on guidance counselor 2.0, gant laborde, cio of infinite red, says to stop trying to memorize all the ai models and instead focus on real-world problems that raise quality of life. he argues ai is a new power source, not a fad like nfts, and warns against shoving technology into spots just to cut costs rather than making people's lives better. the real value comes from using ai to solve actual needs, not from forcing it into products where it frustrates users.

what does gant laborde say about saying yes to too many things?

on guidance counselor 2.0, gant laborde, cio of infinite red, says every time you say yes to something, you are saying no to twenty other things. he recommends guarding your yeses carefully and saying no more often, because you can almost always go back and say yes later if you change your mind. he traces this advice back to his ceo todd werth telling him to start saying no in 2023, which helped him begin healing from pandemic-era overwork.

what does gant laborde say about back to back meetings?

on guidance counselor 2.0, gant laborde, cio of infinite red, says contiguous meetings where one immediately follows another are dangerous because you carry fatigue into each subsequent meeting. he explains that by your third hour-long meeting in a row, you are giving people the worst version of yourself even though it is their first interaction with you that day. he compares it to writing code at two in the morning and stresses that you need breaks between meetings to reset and walk around.
