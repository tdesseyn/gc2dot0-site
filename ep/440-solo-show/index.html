questions this episode answers

how does taylor desseyn say networking on linkedin compares to in-person networking?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says posting on linkedin every day is the equivalent of showing up to a meetup or conference. liking and commenting on posts is the digital version of shaking hands and introducing yourself at an event. following up in direct messages is like the follow-up you would do after meeting someone at a conference.

what does taylor desseyn recommend for linkedin post length?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says most people overcomplicate linkedin posts and should keep them short, three to five sentences. he explains that people have zero attention spans, so while you can use linkedin for long-form blog-style content, most people just do not read it.

what does taylor desseyn suggest you do when meeting someone at a tech meetup?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn suggests going up to someone and asking simple questions like what do you do, what do you focus on, and what do you love about your job. he says the conversation should only last 90 to 120 seconds, then you say it was great chatting and move on. the key is to connect with them on linkedin immediately and follow up after the meetup.
