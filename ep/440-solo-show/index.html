with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • treat posting on linkedin like showing up to a meetup, do it consistently to build your network
  • liking and commenting on posts is the digital version of shaking hands and introducing yourself
  • keep linkedin posts short, three to five sentences, since most people have short attention spans
  • when networking in person, ask simple questions like what do you do and what do you love about it, then move on
  • connect on linkedin right after meeting someone and follow up afterward to make the relationship stick
