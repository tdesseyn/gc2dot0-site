it's okay to stay somewhere w/my dear friend stacey smith who is a sr. principal developer

guest: stacey smith, sr. principal developer

  • staying at one company for years is fine as long as you are still learning and growing
  • ask about tenure in interviews since long employee retention is a strong sign of a healthy team
  • figure out if a frustration is an industry wide issue or something specific to your organization before deciding to quit
  • dont expect your job to fulfill your whole life, build relationships and interests outside of work too
  • practice answering tell me about yourself since confidence there carries into the rest of the interview
