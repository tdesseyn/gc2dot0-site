questions this episode answers

what does stacey smith say about staying at one company for a long time?

on guidance counselor 2.0, stacey smith says it's okay to stay at one company as long as you're still learning and enjoying your job. he has been at his current company for 10 years and knows colleagues who have stayed for 20 to 30 years. stacey emphasizes that people worry too much about staying somewhere beyond three years, but if you're learning and happy, there's no need to jump.

how can you tell if a company has good retention during interviews?

on guidance counselor 2.0, stacey smith and taylor desseyn agree that you should directly ask about employee tenure during interviews. stacey recommends asking how long team members and managers have been there, and says anything more than two years is a great sign. this question can reveal whether the company has staying power and treats employees well.
