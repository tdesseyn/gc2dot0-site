questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about choosing which recruiters to work with?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode from devspace 2018, taylor desseyn says work with a recruiter who has been at the same company at least two years and in the local market at least a year. he says most recruiters are bad at their jobs and jump every 18 months to get a base salary raise because they cannot make enough commission. he also recommends checking a recruiter's linkedin for references from developers who can speak to their technical ability.

how can a developer test whether a recruiter really knows the local market, according to taylor?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn says ask a recruiter who their main clients are right now with your skill set, even if they have no open job for you at those clients. he says a good recruiter should be able to list off 12 to 15 bread and butter clients they get jobs from all the time, and if they stutter or cannot answer, you should run away.

what does taylor desseyn recommend about communication frequency with recruiters?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 episode, taylor desseyn says check in with your recruiter every single week by text or email if you are actively looking. he says the biggest complaint about recruiters is lack of communication, not lack of effort, but it is a two way street. he explains that recruiters meet 10 to 15 new people per week and over a year that means managing thousands of relationships, so keeping in touch regularly helps you stay top of mind.
