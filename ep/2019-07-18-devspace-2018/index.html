jul 18, 2019

devspace 2018

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • work with a recruiter who has been at the same company at least two years and in the local market at least a year
  • check a recruiter's linkedin for references from people who can speak to their technical ability
  • ask a recruiter who their main clients are right now, even if they have no open job for you, to see if they really work your market
  • check in with your recruiter every week since lack of communication is the biggest complaint, not lack of effort
  • keep your linkedin open to opportunities, list your tech stack clearly, and consider building a presence on twitter to network with developers
