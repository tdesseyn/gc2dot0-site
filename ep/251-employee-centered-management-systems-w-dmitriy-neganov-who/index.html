questions this episode answers

what does dmitriy neganov say about putting employees first in management?

on guidance counselor 2.0, dmitriy neganov says organizations should make employees the foundational priority rather than shareholder value, then build all decision processes and priorities from that starting point. he argues this shift leads to better financial results because happy, engaged employees create quality products and services that meet customer needs. neganov points to best buy as an example, where aligning individual employee goals with company goals (rather than making profit the stated goal) helped the company survive when circuit city failed.

what are the three employee needs dmitriy neganov identifies in his management framework?

on guidance counselor 2.0, dmitriy neganov identifies three core needs employees must have addressed in order to truly engage at work: purpose, direction, and engagement. he explains that once you put employees first, you must ask what people fundamentally need, and each of these three needs requires its own specific tool or approach to address properly.

what does dmitriy neganov say about recognizing employees?

on guidance counselor 2.0, dmitriy neganov says recognition must be sincere rather than treating it like a quick fix or bonus. he warns that management and hr often approach recognition as a bandaid, thinking they can just give a small bonus and move on. neganov emphasizes that people need more than that and real sincerity in recognition is not easy to achieve but very possible.
