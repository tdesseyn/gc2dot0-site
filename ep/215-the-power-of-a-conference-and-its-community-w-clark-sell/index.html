ep 215 · feb 3, 2023

the power of a conference and it's community w/clark sell, founder & ceo of 'that conference"

guest: clark sell, founder & ceo of that conference

  • showing up is most of the battle, so register, attend sessions, and put yourself in the room
  • volunteer at a conference, especially registration, because it puts you in front of everyone
  • invest a little money in yourself through courses or conference tickets, it pays off over time
  • after meeting someone at an event, wait a few days before reaching out so you stand out from the first-day flood of messages
  • don't be afraid to sit at a different table or approach strangers, almost everyone there wants to connect too
