questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about ai skills in the job search right now?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says he does not think you can ignore ai and be successful in the job search anymore. he notes that one hiring manager in the wes bos thread said if candidates say they don't use ai, he skips them, and taylor believes more managers will follow this approach. fundamentals still matter most, but pairing solid fundamentals with ai tools closes the gap to getting hired much faster.

what does taylor say about referrals versus cold applying in 2025?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says referrals are now the single most important thing to cut through the noise in hiring. after reading through the wes bos tweet thread, he notes that most people who got jobs mentioned finding them through referrals. he points out that auto-apply tools are flooding companies with applications, which is a big reason candidates don't hear back, so mass applying is not effective.

what does taylor recommend about building a developer brand online?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says building a micro developer brand in a specific niche can make you skip interview rounds and go straight to senior decision makers like the vp of engineering. he emphasizes that niche is better right now, recommending that if you are a java engineer, you should focus on a certain industry and subsection within that industry and talk about it online. this helps because people already know who you are and what you are building before interviews.
