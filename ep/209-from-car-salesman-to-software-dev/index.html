ep 209 · jan 20, 2023

from car salesman to software dev

guest: david, software developer, former car salesman

  • message hiring managers directly on linkedin instead of commenting on their posts, since commenting just creates more competition for you
  • if you have experience in an industry like car sales or healthcare, target tech companies serving that same industry for your first dev job
  • build a real support system or mentor outside social media who actually knows you, not just quick calls or viral advice
  • pace your job interviews and give yourself real rest, since burning out on back to back interviews hurts your performance
  • check linkedin for existing connections at a company before applying, since a real referral can speed up the process a lot
