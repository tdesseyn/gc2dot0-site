ep 83 · sep 27, 2021

hitting the high points

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • start your resume summary broad then get specific, naming your top years of experience and main tech skills
  • write your resume in project format under each job, naming the project and listing responsibilities as bullet points
  • keep summaries to about five or six bullet points covering your most relevant tech
  • if changing industries, rewrite past jobs in plain terms with numbers so someone outside your field understands your impact
  • build one to three targeted resumes for the specific roles you want instead of one generic version
