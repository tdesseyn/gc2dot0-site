ep 83 · sep 27, 2021

hitting the high points

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • start your resume summary broad then get specific, naming your top years of experience and main tech skills
  • write your resume in project format under each job, naming the project and listing responsibilities as bullet points
  • keep summaries to about five or six bullet points covering your most relevant tech
  • if changing industries, rewrite past jobs in plain terms with numbers so someone outside your field understands your impact
  • build one to three targeted resumes for the specific roles you want instead of one generic version

questions this episode answers

how does taylor desseyn say to structure a resume summary for software engineers?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to start your resume summary broad and then get specific, like an upside-down pyramid. he recommends stating your total years of experience first, then your degree, then your most relevant technology skills with years for each. for example, write that you have 10 years of software engineering experience, a computer science degree, five years of dot net experience and three years of angular experience if those are the jobs you're targeting.

what does taylor desseyn recommend for writing work experience when changing careers into tech?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to write your resume so that someone outside your industry can understand what you accomplished. he uses the example of serving at outback steakhouse: instead of just listing tasks like delivering food and cleaning tables, write that you worked at one of the top 10 restaurants in sales out of 300, hit six sales awards when most servers only hit three, and did fifteen hundred dollars in sales per night when most averaged five hundred. the key is to use numbers and accomplishments that show impact, not just job duties.

should you have one generic resume or multiple versions according to taylor desseyn?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says you should not have a generic resume. instead, create one to three specific resumes based on the types of jobs you are targeting. for example, if you are a react and node developer, make one resume highlighting react projects you have done and another highlighting node projects you have done, so you can emphasize the most relevant experience for each position.
