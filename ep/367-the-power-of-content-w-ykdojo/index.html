ep 367 · may 15, 2024

the power of content w/ @ykdojo

guest: yk, founder of cs dojo

  • start building an online presence by sending thoughtful dms before worrying about full blown content
  • post about what you did, like events or projects you built, rather than just sharing opinions when starting out
  • keep your linkedin profile complete and active since recruiters favor accounts that post consistently
  • tag people you meet at events in your posts to get engagement even with zero followers
  • treat posting content like giving a small talk to hundreds of people instead of a one on one conversation

questions this episode answers

what does yk say about getting started with content creation if you're overwhelmed?

on guidance counselor 2.0, yk dojo says to start with direct messages before worrying about full content. he explains that a dm is a piece of content and if you can't communicate well with one person, you can't communicate with hundreds. think of content as sending a message to multiple people at once instead of one at a time.

what does yk recommend posting about when you're new to linkedin?

on guidance counselor 2.0, yk dojo recommends posting about what you did rather than your opinions. go to a tech conference or event with a hundred people, then post about your experience and tag the ten people you met. even with zero followers you'll get engagement because you tagged specific people. he says posting about your actions works better than thought pieces for beginners.

why does yk say linkedin is important even if you have a nine to five job?

on guidance counselor 2.0, yk dojo says linkedin is important because recruiters favor accounts that are active when they search for candidates. the bare minimum is having a solid, publicly visible profile so people can find you. posting even a little bit helps with linkedin's search algorithm, and posting more with more connections makes you even more visible.
