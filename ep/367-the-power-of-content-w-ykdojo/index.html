ep 367 · may 15, 2024

the power of content w/ @ykdojo

guest: yk, founder of cs dojo

  • start building an online presence by sending thoughtful dms before worrying about full blown content
  • post about what you did, like events or projects you built, rather than just sharing opinions when starting out
  • keep your linkedin profile complete and active since recruiters favor accounts that post consistently
  • tag people you meet at events in your posts to get engagement even with zero followers
  • treat posting content like giving a small talk to hundreds of people instead of a one on one conversation
