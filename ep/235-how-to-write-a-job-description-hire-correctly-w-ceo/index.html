how to write a job description & hire correctly w/ ceo/cofounder of flightcontrol (yc w22) | creator of blitz.js

guest: brandon behr, co-founder and ceo of flightcontrol, creator of blitz.js

  • start a job description by getting clear on your company's real identity and core values before writing anything else
  • use a creative job title and photos of the actual team to help candidates picture who they would work with
  • keep required qualifications as short as possible so you do not scare off great candidates, especially underrepresented ones
  • run your job description through a bias checker to catch language that skews too masculine or feminine
  • design your application questions around values and growth mindset so you get a high signal to noise ratio of good fits

questions this episode answers

what does brandon behr say about writing requirements in a job description?

on guidance counselor 2.0, brandon behr says to keep your requirements list as short as possible, only the bare minimum you truly need. he explains that too many requirements will turn away great candidates, and disproportionately those who are underrepresented in tech. brandon suggests keeping hard requirements minimal (like years of experience) while making other desired qualifications nice-to-haves instead.

what does brandon behr include in job descriptions to help candidates picture working there?

on guidance counselor 2.0, brandon behr says he includes photos of the actual team with bios explaining who they are, where they are from, and what they do. he also includes a typical week section that details what meetings candidates will attend, who they will work with, and what their working hours will be. brandon explains that storytelling helps candidates picture themselves in the role and get excited about it.
