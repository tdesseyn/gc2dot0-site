ep 116 · feb 21, 2022

w/justin hoover + how to build out hiring processes

guest: justin hoover, it professional at alliance bernstein

  • skip pure computer science whiteboard challenges since they rarely reflect real day to day work and add undue stress
  • when screening candidates look for three tells: do they relieve stress, do they show perseverance, and do they show a spark of brilliance in solving new problems
  • pair program with candidates on a realistic business problem instead of arbitrary algorithm puzzles to see how you actually collaborate
  • have a small cross section of the team interview together so they can build on each others questions and judge culture fit
  • be transparent with candidates at every step since a cheap or dishonest interview process damages your reputation and scares off good candidates
