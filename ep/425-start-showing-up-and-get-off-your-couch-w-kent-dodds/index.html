start showing up and get off your couch w/ kent dodds

guest: kent c. dodds, teaches web development through epic web and epic react, runs epic web conf and epic web camp

  • treat conferences as a tool to build real relationships, not just a place to pick up education you can get online
  • if you want your company to send you to an event, frame it around solving a specific problem or finding talent, not just networking for yourself
  • sponsors get more value from creative booth activities like giveaways than from passive setups where employees just sit at a laptop
  • going to an event and paying your own way often makes you more engaged than when your company foots the bill
  • build your professional bridge before you need it by showing up to meetups and conferences while things are still going well

questions this episode answers

what does kent dodds say about convincing your company to send you to a conference?

on guidance counselor 2.0, kent c. dodds says you need to frame conference attendance around solving a specific business problem, not just personal networking. he suggests pointing to a technical challenge your team faces and saying you will find consulting help or potential hires at the event. companies used to send people as a perk, but now you must show how it benefits them directly since the burden of proof shifted from why not to why.
