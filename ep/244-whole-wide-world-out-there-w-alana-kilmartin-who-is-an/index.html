questions this episode answers

what does alana kilmartin say about traveling after high school or college?

on guidance counselor 2.0, alana kilmartin says a small investment in travel will pay off ten times over the next few years in life experience and personal growth acceleration. she argues that traveling abroad teaches problem-solving skills, helps you understand how other people live and work, and shows you what's possible beyond the prescribed path. you don't need twelve months overseas; even a few weeks in a place where the dollar stretches further can change your mindset.

what does alana kilmartin recommend if you feel stuck in a toxic job?

on guidance counselor 2.0, alana kilmartin says staying in a toxic job slowly erodes your confidence and makes you less sure of yourself. she recommends creating space to see something different before you quit—take a trip, meet people from a completely different industry, or do something to break the cycle you're in. you don't have to take extreme action immediately, but if you don't create the opportunity to consider what might be next, you'll stay stuck.

how does alana kilmartin suggest figuring out what career you want?

on guidance counselor 2.0, alana kilmartin says she spent most of her twenties having no idea what she wanted to do. by working out what she didn't want, she got closer and closer to working out what she did want. even if you don't know what you want, knowing what you don't want and avoiding that is a good start.
