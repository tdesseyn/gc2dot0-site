ep 475 · aug 6, 2025

career and community talks w/ domitrius clark

guest: domitrius clark, head of devrel at netlify

  • take real time off between jobs to decide what you want next before you start applying
  • ask interviewers directly why the role exists and what success looks like before you commit
  • reaching back out to former coworkers for help is just as valuable as building brand new connections
  • in person events help people remember you in a way that online presence alone cannot
  • be upfront about how you work and what you expect, even if it costs you some offers

questions this episode answers

what does domitrius clark say about taking time off between jobs?

on guidance counselor 2.0, domitrius clark says he took about two months to recover and figure out who he wanted to be in his next career phase before actively job searching. he used that time to decide whether he wanted to manage or be an individual contributor, which companies interested him, and who he wanted to work with rather than just where. clark emphasizes that nobody cares about your future like you do, so taking time to define what you want is worth it.

how does domitrius clark recommend asking about why a role exists in interviews?

on guidance counselor 2.0, domitrius clark says he starts interviews by directly asking why the company is hiring and what responsibilities they see the role fulfilling. when companies responded that they did not know and hoped he would help define it, he walked away because those roles force you to spend more time proving why you exist than actually doing the job. clark also asks each interviewer individually what they believe the role looks like, and if they all have different answers or no answer at all, that is a red flag.
