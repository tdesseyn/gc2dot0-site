ep 475 · aug 6, 2025

career and community talks w/ domitrius clark

guest: domitrius clark, head of devrel at netlify

  • take real time off between jobs to decide what you want next before you start applying
  • ask interviewers directly why the role exists and what success looks like before you commit
  • reaching back out to former coworkers for help is just as valuable as building brand new connections
  • in person events help people remember you in a way that online presence alone cannot
  • be upfront about how you work and what you expect, even if it costs you some offers
