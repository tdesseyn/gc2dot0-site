navigating a 20 year career in tech w/ sunil pai

guest: sunil pai, principal engineer at cloudflare

  • social media is adversarial so don't panic when it says everyone else has it figured out and you're behind
  • the ai tech is solid but unfamiliar, so your job is to build daily habits of testing new tools, not chase hype
  • being an absolutist about refusing to use ai tools can make you unemployable in a fast changing industry
  • write about what you're learning in public, even badly, since showing curiosity builds your reputation over time
  • before interviewing somewhere, try the product and send feedback or a quick demo directly to the hiring manager
