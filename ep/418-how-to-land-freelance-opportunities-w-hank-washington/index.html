how to land freelance opportunities w/ hank washington, founder

guest: hank washington, founder, hank design studios

  • build a visible body of work so clients can quickly see what you can do and want to hire you
  • when selling yourself focus on diagnosing a client's real problem before pitching your service
  • post consistently on linkedin since business decision makers live there, not on instagram or tiktok
  • be easy to reach with fast dm response since opportunities can disappear as quickly as they appear
  • sell your time and communication skills, not just your craft, to build long term client trust

questions this episode answers

what does hank washington say about selling yourself as a freelancer?

on guidance counselor 2.0, hank washington says not to prescribe before diagnosing what the client actually needs. he recommends asking high value questions to understand their real problems first, rather than immediately pitching your services. sometimes you can connect them to someone else who solves the problem, which makes you a valuable resource they remember. the key is selling yourself as a trustworthy person, not just your craft.

where should creative freelancers post their work according to hank washington?

on guidance counselor 2.0, hank washington says linkedin is the bread and butter for creative freelancers, not instagram or tiktok. business decision makers who hire and pay you are on linkedin, not scrolling through millions of creative pages on instagram. he kept a chrome window open just for linkedin direct messages because opportunities come fast but disappear just as quickly. showing your creative work on linkedin with context puts you in front of hiring conversations.

what does hank washington recommend having ready before pursuing freelance work?

on guidance counselor 2.0, hank washington says having a clear, easily accessible body of work is a huge priority. when he meets aspiring creatives, most either do not have a portfolio or are still working on it. people looking you up should be able to click one link from a linkedin post, see your work, and contact you immediately. a visible catalog of work you are proud of is essential for landing opportunities.
