from college student to speaking at conferences w/ dev agrawal dev advocate

guest: dev agrawal, dev advocate

  • look for student organizations and university funding to get to conferences you otherwise could not afford
  • volunteering at conferences gets you access to speaker dinners and parties that regular attendees never see
  • post your projects publicly and tell people about them instead of just building quietly in the shadows
  • submitting a conference talk proposal before the talk is finished can be treated as a chance to learn the topic
  • reach out directly when a company shows a product demo you are genuinely excited about, it can lead to a job
