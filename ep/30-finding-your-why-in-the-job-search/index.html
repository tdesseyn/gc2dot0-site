finding your why in the job search

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • figure out your actual why before starting a job search instead of applying just to see what happens
  • its fine if your why is simply chasing more money, just be honest about it and own that reason
  • write down and rank your top priorities like money, title, work life balance, and commute before you search
  • know your full compensation package including benefits and 401k costs before you start looking, not after
  • ask about benefits earlier in the process instead of waiting until the offer stage to find out they are bad
