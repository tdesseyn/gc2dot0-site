brandon arvay + landscape of marketing right now

guest: brandon arvay, marketing agency owner, branded78, lexington kentucky

  • post content without worrying about production value, especially when starting out on social media
  • focus your limited time and budget on the one or two social channels where your actual audience spends time
  • respond to every comment and message because community management is an overlooked part of marketing
  • find businesses with a similar customer base and collaborate so both sides gain exposure to new people
  • during tight budgets, try text message outreach since open rates run far higher than email

questions this episode answers

what does brandon arvay say about text message marketing versus email?

on guidance counselor 2.0, brandon arvay says text messages have a ninety-five percent open rate within fifteen minutes of receipt, while email marketing averages only twelve percent opens with two percent clicks. he recommends using text messaging to retarget customers who have already engaged with your business, the same people you would retarget with facebook ads or abandoned cart emails.

why does brandon arvay recommend community management as a marketing priority?

on guidance counselor 2.0, brandon arvay says community management is a poorly managed component of marketing that most brands overlook. he explains businesses receive daily messages asking questions about sizing, sourcing, or product details, and responding to every comment and direct message shows you care. he points to shopify support on twitter as an example of a company that built its entire customer support ecosystem through social media engagement.
