questions this episode answers

what does jarrett albritton say about networking at tech events instead of pitching yourself first?

on guidance counselor 2.0, jarrett albritton says to ask people what they are working on and how you can support them rather than leading with what you do. this changes the energy of the conversation and helps you understand how your skills or network can be valuable to them. people rarely get asked how you can support them at events, so it makes you stand out and they often reciprocate by asking what you're working on.

how does jarrett albritton recommend standing out when asking for someone's time on linkedin?

on guidance counselor 2.0, jarrett albritton recommends offering to buy someone a virtual coffee or lunch over zoom if you're asking for their time. he suggests leading with value first, like connecting them with someone in your network who fits a role they're hiring for, then mentioning your own interest and offering the virtual coffee or lunch. this approach makes you stand out because most people just ask for favors without offering anything in return.

what does jarrett albritton say about developers transitioning into sales roles?

on guidance counselor 2.0, jarrett albritton says developers should target products built for development teams or products that help developers with their workflows because they already understand the buyer's problems as subject matter experts. he also recommends going after series a, b, or c startups instead of competing for roles at microsoft, amazon or google, and showing founders that you resonate with their mission and want to have an impact on the culture.
