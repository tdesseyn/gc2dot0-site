why community matters w/ staff comm mgr at vercel, pauline narvas

guest: pauline narvas, staff community manager at vercel

  • treat community as its own discipline that touches growth, product, and engineering, not just marketing
  • build community before you get the job by volunteering, teaching, or running your own online groups
  • use a personal blog or portfolio site to document talks, posts, and projects so people can see your work over time
  • put yourself out there in dms, comments, and events the same way you would advocate for a community, because no one else will do it for you
  • stop hiding your authentic self at work, since showing up as you are opens doors instead of closing them

questions this episode answers

what does pauline narvas say about getting a job in community management?

on guidance counselor 2.0, pauline narvas says you have to do the work before you get the job. she recommends putting yourself out there by volunteering, teaching, running online communities, giving talks, writing blog posts, and showcasing all of it on a personal website or linkedin. she built her own community during the pandemic around devops and consistently shared her work for years before landing her first community role.

why does pauline narvas say companies should invest in community?

on guidance counselor 2.0, pauline narvas says a community first mindset helps companies build a loyal base that turns customers into advocates. she has seen community members become so invested they volunteer to run meetups or post content without being asked because they believe in the mission. this leads to organic growth through word of mouth and positive brand awareness that happens naturally as people connect and help each other.
