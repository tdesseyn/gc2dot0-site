ep 416 · feb 14, 2025

why community matters w/ staff comm mgr at vercel, pauline narvas

guest: pauline narvas, staff community manager at vercel

  • treat community as its own discipline that touches growth, product, and engineering, not just marketing
  • build community before you get the job by volunteering, teaching, or running your own online groups
  • use a personal blog or portfolio site to document talks, posts, and projects so people can see your work over time
  • put yourself out there in dms, comments, and events the same way you would advocate for a community, because no one else will do it for you
  • stop hiding your authentic self at work, since showing up as you are opens doors instead of closing them
