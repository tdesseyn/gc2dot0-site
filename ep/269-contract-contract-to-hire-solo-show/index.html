questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say is the difference between freelancing and contracting?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn explains that freelancing means you set up your own llc, do your own taxes, and hunt for your own clients. contracting means you work w2 through a recruiting firm, which handles taxes and can offer benefits. he notes that benefits through recruiting companies are typically decent for single people or couples but not great overall.

what questions should you ask a recruiter before accepting a contract to hire role according to taylor desseyn?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn says you should ask the recruiter how many people they have placed with that company before in contract to hire roles and how well they know the hiring manager. he emphasizes asking whether this is the recruiter's first time working with the manager or if they have worked together for years. most importantly, always ask upfront what the conversion salary will be, since your hourly contract rate will always be higher than the full-time salary.

why does taylor desseyn recommend looking at contract roles right now?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says he is seeing a lot more companies using contract and part-time arrangements because they do not want to bring on big salaries or keep salaries on their books right now. companies want to keep their staffing fluid, so if you have never looked into hourly contract work before, he highly recommends exploring it now because you will probably get more opportunities.
