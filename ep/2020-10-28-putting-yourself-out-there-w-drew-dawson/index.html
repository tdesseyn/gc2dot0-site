putting yourself out there w/drew dawson

guest: drew dawson, recruiter

  • posting one short video about your background and interests can replace weeks of applying to jobs one by one
  • a simple two minute video recorded on a phone with no editing or lighting can get strong response from recruiters and hiring managers
  • if video feels uncomfortable try live blogging on linkedin or recording audio instead to put yourself out there
  • watching yourself on video helps you clean up filler words and become a clearer communicator for real interviews
  • a short intro video that covers background and strengths can double as practice for the common tell me about yourself interview question
