questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about using downtime in december for your job search?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says december is the time to be reflective rather than immediately jumping ship. he recommends evaluating your current job by writing down pros and cons before deciding to leave, because the grass is not always greener. he warns people often get their bonuses in january or march and then bounce, but they should take time to really assess their situation first.

how does taylor desseyn recommend crowdsourcing your job search?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends creating a slack channel with people who have the same skillset or who have been laid off together. not everyone can take every job, so there will be leftovers to share. he points to his buddy emmett who created coder dads, a slack channel for dads sharing opportunities, as a model for this approach.

what is the bcc trick taylor desseyn recommends for working with recruiters?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn suggests finding multiple recruiters you trust in the same city and using bcc to send them all job leads you find. recruiters cannot remember everything, so this ensures at least one of them is working on that opportunity and remembers you. he also recommends asking recruiters on every call to take a quick look at your resume or linkedin and give you one thing to improve.
