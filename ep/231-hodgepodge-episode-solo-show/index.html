hodgepodge episode (solo show)

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • use downtime around the holidays to reflect on your job, listing pros and cons before deciding to make a move
  • crowdsource your job search by creating a shared slack channel with others who have the same skillset or layoff situation
  • bcc multiple recruiters you trust on job leads so at least one of them can act on it
  • always ask recruiters for quick feedback on your resume or linkedin during any call with them
  • practice upward communication with your manager so they stay aware of what you are doing and implementing
