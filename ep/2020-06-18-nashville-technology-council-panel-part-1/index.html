questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about vetting recruiters before working with them?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn recommends using the two and two rule to vet recruiters: check if they have been at their current recruiting company for at least two years and in the local market for at least two years. he explains that recruiters who are not successful at their job often bounce around between firms trying to get base raises because they cannot earn enough commission. even after six years in nashville, he says he is still learning about companies in town, so market experience matters.

how many recruiters should i work with when i am unemployed?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn says that if you do not have a job right now, you should work with up to four recruiters at once, whereas in better times he recommends working with only one or two. he explains that every recruiting company has its own book of business, so they do not all work with the same employers. working with multiple recruiters increases your access to different opportunities.
