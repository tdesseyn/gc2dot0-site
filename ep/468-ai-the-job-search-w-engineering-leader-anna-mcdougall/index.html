questions this episode answers

what does anna mcdougall say about learning ai tools as a developer right now?

on guidance counselor 2.0, anna mcdougall says developers should pick one ai agent like github copilot and get comfortable with it instead of jumping between many tools. she emphasizes that ai is like any other part of your developer tooling that you need to keep learning and updating. nobody needs to panic about falling behind right now because most teams expect this to matter more in a year or two.

how does anna mcdougall let candidates use ai in technical interviews?

on guidance counselor 2.0, anna mcdougall says her team tells candidates to use any tool they normally would, including ai, during the technical round to mimic their first week on the job. they give candidates a mini fake codebase one hour beforehand and ask them to solve a task like they would at work, using google, ai agents or whatever their normal workflow includes. the real signal is whether the candidate can explain and justify what the ai helped them build, not just whether they finished the task.

what does anna mcdougall say about creating psychological safety in technical interviews?

on guidance counselor 2.0, anna mcdougall says she trains her engineers to be approachable and friendly because how you come across influences whether candidates feel safe. she spends the first fifteen minutes of the technical round catching up on the candidate's experience to bring their stress level down. she also reassures candidates beforehand that the technical round is not pass fail but one criteria among many strengths and weaknesses they weigh up.
