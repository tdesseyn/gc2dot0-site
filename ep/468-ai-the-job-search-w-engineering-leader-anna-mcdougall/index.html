ep 468 · jun 25, 2025

ai & the job search w/ engineering leader anna mcdougall

guest: anna mcdougall, engineering manager at hello better

  • treat ai tools like any other part of your developer tooling that you need to keep learning and updating
  • pick one ai agent or tool and get comfortable with it instead of jumping between many
  • nobody needs to panic about falling behind on ai right now since most teams expect this to matter more in a year or two
  • in technical interviews let candidates use whatever tools they normally would, including ai, to mimic a real first week on the job
  • the real signal in interviews is whether a candidate can explain and justify what the ai helped them build, not just whether they finished the task
