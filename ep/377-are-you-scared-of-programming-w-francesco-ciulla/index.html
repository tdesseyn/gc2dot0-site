questions this episode answers

what does francesco ciulla say about switching careers into tech later in life?

on guidance counselor 2.0, francesco ciulla says switching to tech later in life is absolutely doable and he is living proof. he started coding professionally around age 34 or 35 after deciding at 32 to switch careers. his advice is to reset your mindset like a fresh casino bet: decide what you would do now if you were starting at 18 instead of being held back by past choices or sunk costs.

what does francesco ciulla say about learning rust as a first programming language?

on guidance counselor 2.0, francesco ciulla says rust should not be your first programming language even though he creates rust tutorials. he explains that many rust tutorials reference other programming languages like javascript, so if you do not know those languages you will be frustrated by the references. he recommends rust as a second or third language if you want to bet on the future, predicting it will jump into the top three or four most used languages in two to three years.
