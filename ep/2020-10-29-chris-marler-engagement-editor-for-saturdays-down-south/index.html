chris marler engagement editor for saturdays down south + building brand and being vulnerable over social media

guest: chris marler, engagement editor for saturdays down south

  • building a brand starts with knowing who you are and owning both the positives and the negatives
  • expect public pushback when you put opinions online and learn to take the losses without quitting
  • figure out your own lane instead of copying someone else's style, since there is room for different approaches

questions this episode answers

what does chris marler say about building a personal brand?

on guidance counselor 2.0, chris marler says building a brand really means knowing who you are and owning both the positives and negatives of that. he explains that you need to understand yourself or your company first, then build on those strengths while being able to own your weaknesses. marler learned this after initially buying into the idea that you just build your brand without that self-awareness foundation.

what does chris marler say about handling criticism on social media?

on guidance counselor 2.0, chris marler says you will fail on social media and you need to learn how to take a loss and move forward. he explains that in his role covering college football, he gets constant pushback on twitter when picking games or posting opinions. marler learned that owning the times you are wrong and just accepting the loss is crucial to continuing rather than quitting when people criticize you.

what does chris marler say about comparing yourself to others in your career?

on guidance counselor 2.0, chris marler says you need to stop comparing where you are to where your friends or peers are in their careers and salaries. he recalls being twenty six or twenty seven, bartending with eighty four dollars in his checking account while his friends made six figures, and feeling like his life was over. a friend told him this was all part of his journey, and marler learned that things take time and happen at different speeds for different people if you keep putting in the work.
