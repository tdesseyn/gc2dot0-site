questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about how many recruiters a job seeker should work with at once?

at code on the beach 2019, taylor desseyn says a job seeker should work with no more than two or three recruiters at the same time. he explains that two usually covers enough ground, and three is acceptable if you are really desperate or enjoy free lunches, since recruiters will always buy food for you. working with too many dilutes your search and makes it harder to build trust.

what two-and-two rule does taylor desseyn suggest for vetting recruiters?

at code on the beach 2019, taylor desseyn recommends the two-and-two rule: work with a recruiter who has been with the same company for more than two years and in your local market for more than two years. he explains that most recruiters bounce every two years to get compensation raises, so longevity at one firm signals they are performing well. recruiters who have been in a local market for multiple years also know accurate salary ranges and client relationships.
