why does colby fayock recommend creating content as a job seeker?

on guidance counselor 2.0, colby fayock says writing down what you just learned gives employers material to see you are actively learning and growing. creating content also reinforces your own learning because explaining a concept forces you to fully understand it. he emphasizes that content does not need to be perfect or popular, just something that shows your thinking and progress.
