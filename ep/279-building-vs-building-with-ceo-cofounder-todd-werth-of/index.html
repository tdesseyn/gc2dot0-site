questions this episode answers

what does todd werth say about getting customers before building a product?

on guidance counselor 2.0, todd werth says you can get customers to commit to paying before you start your business, even for a product. go to people in an industry you know and offer them a very good long-term discount if they help fund the initial build. this approach is easier than building on the side and ensures someone will actually pay for what you create.

why does todd werth recommend b2b over b2c for startups?

on guidance counselor 2.0, todd werth recommends business-to-business over business-to-consumer because you need far fewer customers to build a sustainable business. consumers require massive amounts of people to succeed, but with b2b you can get customers before you even start and focus on a smaller, more manageable client base.

how does todd werth say you should practice sales if you're not a natural salesperson?

on guidance counselor 2.0, todd werth says to treat sales as a skill you practice, not a talent you're born with. he and jamin would hold a retrospective immediately after every sales call where they pointed out what each person could improve and what they did well. this engineering approach let them find a sales style their personalities excelled at instead of imitating other people.
