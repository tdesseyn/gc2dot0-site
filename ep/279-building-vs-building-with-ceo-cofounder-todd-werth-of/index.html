building vs. building with ceo/cofounder todd werth of infinite red

guest: todd werth, ceo/co-founder of infinite red

  • get customers to commit to paying before you spend money building your product or business
  • choose b2b over b2c since you need far fewer customers to build a sustainable business
  • treat sales like a skill to practice, not a talent you either have or dont
  • after every sales call do a retrospective with your team to improve what you say and do
  • in interviews and at work just show up with something tangible done, thats more than most people do
