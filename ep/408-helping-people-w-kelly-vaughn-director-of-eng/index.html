helping people w/ kelly vaughn, director of eng

guest: kelly vaughn, director of engineering

  • stop using chatgpt to write your whole resume because recruiters can spot copy and pasted text word for word
  • when following up after applying keep it short and genuine instead of sending a generic auto filled linkedin message
  • write down bullet points before an interview so you dont blank, you only need to hit the key points not a perfect script
  • its okay to pause for a second before answering an interview question instead of rushing into a response
  • to get noticed reach out to the hiring manager or someone in your second level network instead of just applying and waiting
