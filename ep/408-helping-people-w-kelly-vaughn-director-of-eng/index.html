questions this episode answers

what does kelly vaughn say about using chatgpt to write resumes?

on guidance counselor 2.0, kelly vaughn says people need to stop using chatgpt to write their entire resumes because recruiters can spot copy and pasted text word for word. she sees the same descriptions and bullet points across multiple resumes from different companies, which tells her candidates are lying about doing identical work. it's fine to use chatgpt for format inspiration or brainstorming what's missing, but don't copy and paste directly.

what does kelly vaughn recommend for candidates who blank out during interviews?

on guidance counselor 2.0, kelly vaughn says candidates should write down bullet points and keywords before the interview rather than trying to memorize everything perfectly. she suggests recording yourself answering common questions and using keywords as reminders, because you only need to hit eighty-five percent of what you intended to say and nobody knows what you forgot. she also says it's completely okay to pause for a second before answering an interview question instead of immediately jumping into a response.
