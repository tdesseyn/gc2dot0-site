questions this episode answers

how does annyce davis recommend showing business impact on a resume?

on guidance counselor 2.0, annyce davis, vp of engineering at meetup, says to connect your resume bullets to business impact instead of just listing technologies you used. if you cannot find direct revenue numbers, estimate using team salary and headcount as a rough guess. for example, if three people on your team each make a hundred thousand dollars, your work must generate at least that much to justify the cost.

what does annyce davis say about becoming invaluable at your company?

on guidance counselor 2.0, annyce davis, vp of engineering at meetup, recommends becoming the go-to person for a specific topic so leadership knows your name and what you handle. she also advises giving busy stakeholders a direct yes or no answer first, then offering more detail if they need it. if you need more time to investigate, provide your best guess immediately and promise a more accurate response by end of day.

how can engineers find the business impact of their work according to annyce davis?

on guidance counselor 2.0, annyce davis, vp of engineering at meetup, says to ask your manager how your work moved the needle so you have concrete impact to put on your resume. she notes that most companies share high-level business metrics monthly or quarterly, and if you work for a public company that information is online. even without revenue access, you can use metrics like improving app store ratings from 4.3 to 4.5, which translates to a 75 percent increase.
