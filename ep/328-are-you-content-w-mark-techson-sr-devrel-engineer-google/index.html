questions this episode answers

what does mark techson say about breaking into devrel without a big social media following?

on guidance counselor 2.0, mark techson, sr. devrel engineer at google, says you do not need a big twitter following to break into devrel. he crossed 10k followers only last month but joined google in 2020, and many effective devrel professionals never appear on twitter at all. to break in, write blogs, contribute on github, help with documentation and issues, and focus on serving the developer community through multiple channels.

what does mark techson recommend about wanting more money or a bigger title?

on guidance counselor 2.0, mark techson, sr. devrel engineer at google, says make sure you want more money or a bigger title for yourself, not because you see someone else has it. chasing success by any means can leave you unfulfilled because you cannot control layoffs, economic conditions, or market shifts. it is okay to want more, but control those feelings and do not let external comparisons become your only driving factor.
