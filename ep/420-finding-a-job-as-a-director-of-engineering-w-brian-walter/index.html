finding a job as a director of engineering w/ brian walter, republic.com

guest: brian walter, director of software engineering at republic

  • expect a long search if you are a senior leader, one guest spent about 18 to 19 months finding his next role
  • a spray and pray approach of mass applying on linkedin produced almost no responses, so pivot after a couple months if its not working
  • joining a structured peer group that meets regularly can multiply your network and keep you accountable during a long search
  • cold outreach rarely gets a reply, but a warm introduction from someone who knows and trusts you changes the odds completely
  • always send a specific thank you note after interviews and keep following up with recruiters and old contacts over many months

questions this episode answers

what does brian walter say about cold applying on linkedin for senior roles?

on guidance counselor 2.0, brian walter, director of software engineering at republic, says his spray and pray approach of mass applying on linkedin produced almost no responses. he quickly realized after a couple months that cold applications do not work for leadership roles, and that you need warm introductions from people who know and trust you to get through the initial screen.

how did brian walter expand his network during his job search?

on guidance counselor 2.0, brian walter says he joined never search alone, a volunteer-run peer group that paired him with others at similar career stages who met twice a week. this quickly multiplied his network because each person could introduce him to their connections, and almost every interview he got came from a former connection or close relationship, not cold outreach.

what does brian walter recommend doing after every job interview?

on guidance counselor 2.0, brian walter says you should send a thank you note after every single interview, even though you usually will not get a response. he made sure to appreciate their time, mention what resonated with him, and talk about how his strengths could help them or add things he did not get to say during the interview.
