questions this episode answers

what does pariss athena say about resume design and applicant tracking systems?

on guidance counselor 2.0, pariss athena says job seekers should avoid putting colors, headshots, logos, tables and charts on their resumes because applicant tracking systems can create errors and rank those resumes really low. she explains that the ats matches your resume to a job description, and unnecessary design elements can prevent your resume from ever reaching a recruiter. keep resume design simple so the ats can parse it correctly.

how does pariss athena recommend using chatgpt for resumes?

on guidance counselor 2.0, pariss athena says chatgpt can give you a good outline if you don't know how to start a resume, and you can throw a job description in to help write initial content. however, she warns that just dumping everything into chatgpt and posting that directly is a slippery slope because the tool does not always follow directions correctly. always review and modify whatever chatgpt generates for you before submitting it.

what does pariss athena recommend for people trying to break into tech without debt?

on guidance counselor 2.0, pariss athena says bootcamps are a more equitable route to education than a four year degree because you avoid paying a lot of money. she urges people to look at free or cheap resources first before deciding to spend money on education, especially since even harvard master's grads are struggling to find jobs in the current market. she attended resilient coders, a free bootcamp that actually pays you a stipend while you learn and connects you with their network.
