black tech pipeline ai and learning to code again w/ pariss athena

guest: pariss athena, founder and ceo of black tech pipeline

  • avoid resume designs with colors, logos, tables, or charts since ats systems can rank them low or skip them entirely
  • use chatgpt to outline a resume draft but always review and personalize before submitting it anywhere
  • bootcamps can offer a paid, equitable path into tech so research free or low cost options before taking on big student debt
  • look for tech roles at small companies and consumer brands you already use by checking their careers pages directly
  • once you land your first tech job focus on getting your foot in the door rather than chasing the biggest salary right away
