questions this episode answers

what does teja yenamandra say about making mistakes at work?

on guidance counselor 2.0, teja yenamandra says the biggest mistake in a small company is not taking action out of fear of making tiny errors. he recommends building relationship capital and trust with your manager through casual check-ins, not just work talk, so you have space to make mistakes and grow. if your workplace punishes every small mistake harshly, that is a sign something deeper is wrong and you should consider looking elsewhere.

what does teja say is more important than resume and skills when hiring?

on guidance counselor 2.0, teja yenamandra says that resume, skill set and goals are baseline requirements when hiring, but the most important thing is the energy and vibe a person brings. he explains that managers go into 80 to 90 percent of interviews hoping to hire the person, and they want to be blown away by someone who brings accretive energy to the company, not someone who drains energy or shows up complaining.

what career advice does teja yenamandra give about taking risks?

on guidance counselor 2.0, teja yenamandra advises people to take more risk at every stage of their career. he says you should take so much risk that if you mess it up, you feel like you cannot come back from it, because that is probably the right level and you are still likely under-risking. he shares that his best career decisions were the risky ones, like not selling his company when he had money sitting there, because continuing was worth the risk.
