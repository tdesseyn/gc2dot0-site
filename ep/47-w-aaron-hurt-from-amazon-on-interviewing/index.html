w/aaron hurt from amazon on interviewing

guest: aaron hurt, systems development engineering manager at amazon

  • amazon's interview loop can run five hours total and may be split across multiple days instead of one sitting

questions this episode answers

what does aaron hurt say about how amazon's interview loop is structured?

on guidance counselor 2.0, aaron hurt, a systems development engineering manager at amazon, explains that the loop interview is a five-hour commitment that can now be split across multiple days instead of one sitting. each of the five one-hour sessions covers different competencies, with interviewers focusing on specific amazon leadership principles and asking behavioral questions about past situations. amazon tries to respond within two business days after initial contact and within five business days after loop interviews.

what does aaron hurt say about resume keywords for amazon applications?

on guidance counselor 2.0, aaron hurt explains that your resume needs to match the specific technology and skills mentioned in the job description to get flagged for a phone screen. if a role requires strong linux fundamentals but the word linux does not appear anywhere on your resume, you probably will not get a phone screen even if you list related tools like terraform or ansible. he also warns against using vague words like expert or massive on your resume because you will be expected to back up any such claims with specifics during the interview.

what does aaron hurt say hiring managers at amazon do for recruiting?

on guidance counselor 2.0, aaron hurt says that managers at amazon spend several hours a week on recruitment activities, not just relying on recruiters. he personally spends a few hours per week searching linkedin for candidates and reviewing resumes that either came through the internal recruiting team or were submitted directly, because managers have technical insight that recruiters may lack. he also does internal referrals for people who reach out to him on linkedin.
