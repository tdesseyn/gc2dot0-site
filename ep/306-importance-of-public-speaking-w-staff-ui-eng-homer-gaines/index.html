importance of public speaking w/ staff ui eng, homer gaines

guest: homer gaines, staff ui engineer, specializing in ui, ux and accessibility

  • treat accessibility as part of ux, not a separate add-on, since every user still interacts with the same product
  • learn accessibility fundamentals first, since tools and trends change but core principles stay the same
  • pick a topic you can talk about for 10 to 15 minutes with zero prep as your entry point into public speaking
  • expect burnout from the conference circuit and plan for the toll it takes on family and energy
  • public speaking builds real relationships and opens doors, so start small with lightning talks and build up

questions this episode answers

what does homer gaines say about getting started with public speaking at tech conferences?

on guidance counselor 2.0, homer gaines recommends picking a topic you can talk about for 10 to 15 minutes with zero preparation as your entry point into public speaking. he suggests starting with lightning talks at smaller groups to find your voice and build confidence before moving to longer 30 or 45 minute talks. this approach takes the stress off preparing slides and lets you focus on material you already know well.

how does homer gaines describe the burnout from speaking at tech conferences?

on guidance counselor 2.0, homer gaines explains that conference speaking takes a toll on family time and energy because preparing talks, traveling, and being away from home for weeks requires real sacrifice. he says it is easier working from home than attending conferences, and the grind can make you question if the effort is worth it. homer notes that your family is also impacted by your absence, so you need their support to sustain a speaking schedule.
