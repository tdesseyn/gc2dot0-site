how to get a job off of x/twitter w/ staff eng, jacob evans

guest: jacob evans, staff engineer at wop.com

  • treat tech twitter as a place for genuine connection, not just memes or drama
  • find mentors by engaging publicly with their posts instead of dming asking for mentorship
  • build your network by finding shared interests outside of tech like hobbies or background
  • when a post gets traction, be ready to say yes to opportunities like flying out to meet a team
  • job boards and easy apply can have a very low response rate, so build real connections elsewhere
