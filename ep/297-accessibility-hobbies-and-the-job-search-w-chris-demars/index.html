questions this episode answers

what does chris demars say about reaching developers in devrel work?

on guidance counselor 2.0, chris demars says developer advocates should focus on reaching developers directly, not the companies signing the checks. he explains that developers attend community events like that conference or kcdc, while product showcase events attract people with purchasing authority. the goal is to empathize with developers on the front lines where they actually are, not to sell to the company buying the product.

what does chris demars recommend for prepping for a first tech interview?

on guidance counselor 2.0, chris demars says job seekers should find role specific question lists on github, like front end interview questions covering css, javascript, html and accessibility. he also recommends the book cracking the coding interview. most importantly, he says to prepare your own questions in your back pocket because an interview is a two way street and the interviewer might not know the technology, so you can ask things they may need to pass along to someone more qualified.
