accessibility, hobbies, and the job search w/ chris demars

guest: chris demars, developer advocate

  • in devrel focus on reaching the developer directly, not the company signing the checks
  • track attribution with things like utm links so you can show which content or outreach drove engagement
  • devrel results take time, impact from a single conversation may not show up for a year
  • use erp (exposure and response therapy) style exposure and hobbies like fishing or paintball to manage work related anxiety and burnout
  • when prepping for a tech interview find role specific question lists on github and bring your own questions since interviews go both ways
