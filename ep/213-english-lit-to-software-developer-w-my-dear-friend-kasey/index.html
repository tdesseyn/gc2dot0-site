questions this episode answers

what does kasey wahl say about choosing a coding bootcamp?

on guidance counselor 2.0, kasey wahl says research bootcamps carefully and see if they interview you before accepting you, because a real screening process shows they invest in their students, not just take anyone who pays. he reached out to coder foundry graduates on linkedin and asked about their actual experience to get an honest forecast of what to expect. he also recommends asking what obligations the bootcamp has to you and what you have to them during any interview process.

how does kasey wahl suggest junior developers handle confusion while learning to code?

on guidance counselor 2.0, kasey wahl says the biggest mistake junior developers make is giving up too early instead of identifying exactly what they don't understand. he recommends writing down the specific concept, framework or context that's confusing you, then asking about that precise thing rather than just saying coding is hard and quitting. he recorded every lecture during his bootcamp and went back to rewatch parts he didn't understand.
