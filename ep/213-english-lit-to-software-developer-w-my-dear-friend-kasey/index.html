english lit to software developer w/my dear friend kasey wahl

guest: kasey wahl, junior developer

  • research bootcamps before committing and check if they interview you first, a real screening process signals they invest in students
  • ask bootcamp graduates on linkedin about their actual experience to get an honest forecast before enrolling
  • when stuck on a concept, write down exactly what you dont understand and ask about it instead of quitting
  • use mornings for job search and networking when your mind is freshest, save coding and content creation for afternoons
  • send recruiters and hiring managers content you created on topics discussed in interviews to show you followed up and learned
