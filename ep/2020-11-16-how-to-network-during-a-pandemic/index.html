how to network during a pandemic

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • like and comment on posts in your field consistently since that is the new way to network
  • stay on someone's radar by engaging with their posts long before you ever ask them for a favor
  • post on linkedin daily and aim for two or three posts a day if you can
  • if writing feels fake or salesy you can still network by liking commenting and dming people
  • follow accounts in your field on instagram and use it strategically for your career, not just friends
