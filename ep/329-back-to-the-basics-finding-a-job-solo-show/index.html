back to the basics (finding a job) + solo show

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • treat your resume and linkedin as the foundation everything else in your job search depends on
  • log into job board sites once or twice a week so your profile resurfaces at the top of recruiter searches
  • aim for two networking conversations a day, one brand new contact and one existing connection
  • use the three pillars of a good dm: admiration, a specific time ask, and a clear question
  • follow up consistently on applications since staying top of mind can change a hiring outcome

questions this episode answers

how many networking conversations per day does taylor desseyn recommend during a job search?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends having two networking conversations every day. one should be with a brand new person you're adding to your network, and one should be with someone you already know in your existing network. he emphasizes that networking takes energy and calls it deep work, so taking breaks between sessions is important.
