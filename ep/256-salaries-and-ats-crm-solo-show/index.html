questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about sharing salary expectations with recruiters?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to give recruiters three specific numbers: your absolute minimum base salary, your current base salary, and your ideal no-brainer offer. he emphasizes talking about base salary only, not total compensation, because things like hsa contributions, cell phone stipends, and gym reimbursements make total comp comparisons confusing. you should also update recruiters throughout the interview process if your salary expectations change, since many candidates start at one number but end up needing much more by the final stages.

how does taylor desseyn recommend getting into a target company?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends identifying your target company, then scanning the company's linkedin page for mutual connections. reach out to those mutual connections even if you haven't kept in touch with them, because people inherently want to help and many employees get referral fees for successful hires. having an internal advocate who can vouch for you gives you a much better chance of getting through the door than submitting your resume alone.

does the ats automatically reject resumes according to taylor desseyn?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says the ats does not reject your resume because no algorithm can perfectly match resumes to job descriptions. he explains that an ats is just software to keep track of resumes, and human beings always make the final hiring decisions. recruiters and hiring managers review applications manually rather than relying on automated searches or matching.
