ep 256 · aug 7, 2023

salaries and ats/crm (solo show)

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • give recruiters three salary numbers your absolute minimum, your current base, and your ideal no-brainer offer
  • talk base salary, not total comp, since things like hsa or stipends muddy the comparison
  • update recruiters throughout the process if your salary expectations change so they can recalibrate
  • to break into a target company, find mutual connections on linkedin and ask for a warm intro to recruiting
  • no algorithm or ats fully matches resumes to job descriptions, humans make the final hiring decisions
