don't know what to do in this market? neither do we.

guest: jake and dan, recruiters

  • keep your resume updated now so you are ready when hiring picks back up
  • check in with recruiters periodically but do not text every single day
  • treat linkedin like a networking event, post thoughtful content and record a quick video about yourself
  • stay patient and avoid pressing too hard, since desperation can hurt you in interviews
  • consider free or discounted work to build your skill set and portfolio while the market is slow
