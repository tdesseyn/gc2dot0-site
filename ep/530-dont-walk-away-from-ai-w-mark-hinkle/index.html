don't walk away from ai w/ mark hinkle

guest: mark hinkle, founder of peripety labs and organizer of all things ai

  • treat ai like a tool you actually drive, not a ferrari parked in the garage you never take out
  • look for the easy wins first, like cutting down time spent in email or copy pasting between tools
  • use one model to plan and a different model to check its work so mistakes get caught earlier
  • ask yourself not just what ai can do but what you should actually hand off to it
  • keep showing up to in person events since trust and empathy still get built face to face, not through a screen

questions this episode answers

what does mark hinkle say about starting with ai at work?

on guidance counselor 2.0, mark hinkle says knowledge workers spend 28 percent of their time in email and 50 percent cutting and pasting between systems, so those are easy places to start. he suggests looking at what you do every day and asking whether you add any value to that task. if the answer is no, that task is a good candidate for ai.

what does mark hinkle say about meeting people in person versus online?

on guidance counselor 2.0, mark hinkle says the relationships he made in the analog world have made it possible to survive in the digital world. he says empathy and trust are built in person, not as easily on a screen. he thinks showing up to events and shaking hands matters more now because it is hard to tell who is real online.
