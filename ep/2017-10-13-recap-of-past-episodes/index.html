with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • use font size 10 for your resume and keep it one page if you are a recent grad or have a year of experience

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn recommend for resume length based on experience?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends limiting your resume to one page if you just graduated college or have a year of experience. if you have three to four years of experience, expand to two pages. for very senior candidates, he suggests going up to five pages maximum and stopping there.

how does taylor desseyn suggest vetting a recruiter before working with them?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to interview recruiters like they are interviewing you. look for someone who has been at the same company for more than two years, since recruiters tend to chase money and bounce around every eighteen months. he also recommends looking for at least one to two years of recruiting experience, because someone fresh out of college without experience should be approached with caution.

what does taylor desseyn say about linkedin profiles for job seekers?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says you should absolutely have a linkedin profile because it shows you are a real person. he recommends having a professionally done photo and making sure all your positions are up to date. for each position, write just two or three sentences to summarize what you do, and if you are a developer, list your technology stack and a summary of the project or application you worked on.
