how to use ai to navigate offers

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • use ai to research market rate before accepting an offer by asking it what data sources and factors affect pay for your role
  • ask ai for bullet points to guide a negotiation call instead of a full script so you dont sound robotic if the conversation shifts
  • practice negotiation out loud using ai voice chat and ask it to critique you afterward on what you missed
  • when comparing multiple offers ask ai to evaluate total value beyond base salary against your own career goals
  • if equity is not mentioned in an offer ask specific questions about vesting shares and exercise windows before you negotiate further

questions this episode answers

how does taylor desseyn recommend using ai to research whether a job offer is competitive?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to ask ai what data sources and factors affect compensation for your specific role, rather than just looking up a single number. he demonstrates by inputting a community manager offer at a series a company and having claude analyze whether the base salary, equity, and bonus are competitive based on market data. the ai response included which benchmarking sites to check, what factors move compensation for that role, and eight specific questions to ask about equity before accepting.

what does taylor desseyn say about using scripts for salary negotiation calls?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says he is wary of scripts because if the phone call does not go exactly as planned, which it never does, you will get stumped and sound robotic. instead of asking ai for a full script, he recommends asking for high-level bullet points or things you absolutely must talk about so you can adapt as the conversation shifts.

how does taylor desseyn suggest practicing for salary negotiations using ai?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends using the voice chat feature in claude, chatgpt, or other ai tools to roleplay the negotiation conversation out loud before the actual call. he says to practice the full conversation and then ask the ai to critique you afterward on what you missed, because talking about money is stressful and if you do not rehearse, you will stumble and accidentally talk yourself down.
