questions this episode answers

why does taylor desseyn say you should stop comparing your career to peers?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says comparing your career timeline to peers will steal your joy and focus. he explains that everyone is on their own journey with different circumstances, so looking over your shoulder at others in your age group or skill level is exhausting and counterproductive. he stopped looking at his own weekly recruiting scorecard because he needed to focus on his unique path instead of constantly measuring himself against colleagues.

what does taylor desseyn say about asking questions during interviews?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says job seekers must ask enough questions and interview the company just as much as the company interviews them. he warns that many people jump at offers without asking detailed questions, then want to leave six months later because the role is not what was promised. he has seen a surge of candidates reaching out who took jobs last year without proper vetting and now regret it.

what does taylor desseyn say about pay when converting from contract to permanent?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says you should always ask the recruiter what the expected conversion salary is before accepting a contract to hire role. he explains that your salary will be lower than your hourly rate because the hourly rate typically accounts for benefits, paid time off, and other costs you cover yourself as a contractor. the dip should not be tremendous, but candidates should expect some decrease when the company starts providing benefits.
