ep 301 · dec 1, 2023

networking and the restaurant industry w/ head of eng, seth alexander

guest: seth alexander, head of engineering

  • skills from restaurant management like talking to upset customers and juggling priorities transfer directly into tech and project management
  • long-term networking pays off years later so stay in touch with former coworkers even after you change jobs or industries
  • joining a group chat with former coworkers after a layoff can provide job leads, salary info, and emotional support
  • pick a few meetups or slack and discord communities and show up consistently instead of spreading yourself across many
  • when you connect with someone after a podcast or meetup, include one line about why you want to connect so it is memorable
