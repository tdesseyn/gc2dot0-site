questions this episode answers

what does seth alexander say about transferring restaurant management skills to tech?

on guidance counselor 2.0, seth alexander says skills from restaurant management transfer directly into tech and project management. he explains that talking to angry, hungry customers taught him how to communicate with people, and managing multiple areas of a restaurant is essentially project management. he compares filling tables and planning week by week to running agile sprints.

how did seth alexander's networking lead to his head of engineering job?

on guidance counselor 2.0, seth alexander says his most recent head of engineering role came from long-term networking with former coworkers. after being laid off, he messaged a signal group of former evidence care colleagues he had stayed in touch with for over two years. a former coworker, ryan macy, then introduced him to the gracious team and got the conversation started, though seth still had to go through a full interview process.

what does seth alexander recommend about picking meetups and online communities?

on guidance counselor 2.0, seth alexander recommends picking a few meetups or slack and discord communities and showing up consistently instead of spreading yourself thin across many. he says people notice when you show up regularly even if they do not say it, and going deep on a few communities makes more impact than being surface level everywhere. he attended nash.js, nash.rb, and nash react monthly before getting into tech.
