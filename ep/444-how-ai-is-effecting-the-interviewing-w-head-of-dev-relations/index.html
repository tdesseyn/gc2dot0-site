how ai is effecting the interviewing w/ head of dev relations, angie jones

guest: angie jones, head of developer relations, block

  • treat ai use in interviews the same way you would on the job, since candidates will use it at work anyway
  • when reviewing a coding exercise done with ai help, ask the candidate to explain what the ai generated and what they would fix or improve
  • trying to secretly have ai answer interview questions for you is the real disqualifier, not ai use itself
  • posting publicly about tools you use and problems you solve helps opportunities find you, even without likes or comments
  • ask someone directly to refer you before applying, since a referral can help your resume get noticed among many applicants

questions this episode answers

what does angie jones say about using ai during coding interviews?

on guidance counselor 2.0, angie jones, head of developer relations at block, says candidates should be allowed to use ai tools during coding interviews the same way they would on the job. she argues that if you're going to let someone use ai after they're hired, it's hypocritical to forbid it during the interview. instead of banning ai, interviewers should ask candidates to explain the code ai generated and identify what parts need improvement, which shows whether they actually understand the work.

what does angie jones say is the real problem with ai in interviews?

on guidance counselor 2.0, angie jones says the real disqualifier is trying to deceive the interviewer with ai, not using ai itself. she explains that having an ai bot secretly answer questions or write code for you during the interview is not acceptable. however, using ai as a tool to assist you, then explaining and evaluating the output, demonstrates competence. the deceit will knock you out of the process more than the ai use, she says.
