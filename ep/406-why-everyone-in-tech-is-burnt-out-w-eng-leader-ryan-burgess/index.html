questions this episode answers

what does ryan burgess say causes burnout in tech right now?

on guidance counselor 2.0, ryan burgess says companies running lean after layoffs means fewer people absorb more work without anyone officially increasing their load. he also points out that remote and hybrid work removed natural boundaries like commute time, making it harder to fully disconnect at the end of the day. people fill evenings with one more meeting or one more slack message until they are exhausted.

how can you prevent burnout without quitting your job, according to ryan burgess?

on guidance counselor 2.0, ryan burgess says to block off time on your calendar for lunch and short walks or it will get filled with meetings and messages. he suggests turning off all notifications except text and calls from family. if you are burnt out in your current job, try setting real boundaries for 30 days before deciding to quit.
