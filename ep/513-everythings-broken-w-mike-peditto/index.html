everything's broken w/ mike peditto

guest: mike peditto, recruiter, director of talent, and creator known online as realistic recruiting

  • focus applications on roles where you are a true top match instead of mass applying to everything you are loosely qualified for
  • skip the generic resume summary and use a clear headline at the top that instantly shows your role, skills, and industry fit
  • make sure your most important skills and achievements appear in the first third of your resume since recruiters scan fast
  • the ats is not an ai robot auto rejecting people, its a filing system where a human still searches and decides
  • when reaching out to a recruiter or hiring manager, be specific about why you match that exact role instead of sending a generic message
