about me.

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • recruiters get a bad reputation, so look for ones who actually know the tech they recruit for
  • specializing in one space, like software development, helps a recruiter give better advice and connections
  • getting involved in local developer meetups and conferences builds real relationships, not just job leads
  • a recruiter who shifts from cold calling to in person relationships tends to understand candidates better
  • don't just dismiss recruiters, learn how to use them well since they can actually help your search

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about specializing as a tech recruiter?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says he started by recruiting for all types of roles across fortune 500 clients but realized he needed to shift his focus after developers kept hanging up on him. he moved to working only with developers in person, then narrowed further to only software developers in one city. he believes becoming more focused as his career progressed helped him serve candidates better.
