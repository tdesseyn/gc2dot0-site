ep 450 · may 5, 2025

jsconfmx conference talk - how content & community can impact your career

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • invite a couple of friends to something non technical like sports or a favorite restaurant instead of waiting for someone else to start a community
  • post on linkedin once a day or every other day simply documenting what you are learning right now
  • treat creating content as an ongoing first interview since hiring managers may follow your journey before ever talking to you
  • after a conference block time to follow up with everyone you met since the follow up matters more than the event itself
  • reach out to old contacts on linkedin even after many years since one message can lead directly to your next job

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about starting a tech community if you feel overwhelmed?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says you do not need to organize a large event or conference to start building community. he recommends inviting just two friends in tech to do something you already enjoy, like watching a sports team or going to a favorite restaurant. the activity does not need to be technical at all; taylor himself runs a monthly meetup for dads in tech where they never discuss technical topics, yet over 50 dads now attend and some told him they cried after the first session because they needed that community.
