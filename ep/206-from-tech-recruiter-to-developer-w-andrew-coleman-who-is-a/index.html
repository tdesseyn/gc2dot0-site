questions this episode answers

what does andrew coleman say about networking versus submitting online applications?

on guidance counselor 2.0, andrew coleman says only about 50 percent of all possible jobs are posted publicly, and only 50 percent of those posted jobs actually get filled through that posting. this means if you only apply online, you are limiting yourself to at best 25 percent of the jobs you could realistically get. he recommends spending more effort on networking and making connections than on mass applications.

what is the three c framework andrew coleman recommends for job searching?

on guidance counselor 2.0, andrew coleman describes the three c framework as code, content, and connection. code means building projects and keeping your skills sharp. content means writing about what you build and learn, even if nobody reads it at first, so you have a record for yourself and material to share. connection means networking with people who find your content or whom you reach out to directly, creating a flywheel where building leads to content, content leads to connections, and connections create more opportunities to build.

what does andrew coleman say about referrals and when to ask for them?

on guidance counselor 2.0, andrew coleman says you should connect with someone at your target company before you apply, not after. a referral is usually good enough for at least a first round interview, and most companies have referral bonuses for employees who refer successful hires. if you apply first and then reach out, the person you contact misses out on the referral bonus and you miss the advantage of having someone vouch for you from the start.
