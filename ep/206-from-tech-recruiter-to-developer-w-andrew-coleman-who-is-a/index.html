ep 206 · jan 13, 2023

from tech recruiter to developer w/andrew coleman who is a react recruiting lead at bitov

guest: andrew coleman, react recruiting lead at bitovi

  • focus on code, content, and connection instead of mass applying to jobs
  • write about what you build and learn daily, even if no one reads it at first, you still have a record for yourself
  • only a portion of jobs are posted publicly and even fewer get filled through that posting, so networking matters more than applications
  • avoid chasing new languages or frameworks right after a bootcamp, go deep in one skill instead of staying a generalist
  • connect with someone at your target company before you apply, since a referral can get you a first round interview
