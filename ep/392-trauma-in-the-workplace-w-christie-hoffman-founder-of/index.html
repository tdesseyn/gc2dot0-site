ep 392 · jul 31, 2024

trauma in the workplace w/ christie hoffman, founder of engagement academy

guest: christie hoffman, founder of engagement academy

  • figure out your personal values so you have a framework for decisions instead of relying on a company that may not have any
  • generic recognition like saying good job means little, teach managers to explain the specific impact an employee's work actually had
  • burnout often comes from feeling you have no control over your goals, so set personal goals outside of work to regain a sense of progress
  • pay attention to how you react when stressed or angry and ask if it mirrors a parent or caretaker, that awareness can change bad leadership habits
  • small daily habits like walking, joining a gym even without a plan, and watching nutrition can meaningfully reduce anxiety and burnout
