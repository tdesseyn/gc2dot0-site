navigating your career and job search w/ leon noel

guest: leon noel, founder of 100devs and resilient coders

  • get an outside perspective on your resume and linkedin because you cannot smell your own weak spots
  • put the most important info in the top third of your resume since most readers never get past it
  • name the size and type of companies you worked for so non-technical recruiters understand your experience
  • join a community so you can see peers getting interviews and jobs instead of sitting in a negativity bubble
  • build real relationships through small consistent actions like commenting online or showing up to the same meetup repeatedly
