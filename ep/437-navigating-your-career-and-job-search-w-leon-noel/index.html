navigating your career and job search w/ leon noel

guest: leon noel, founder of 100devs and resilient coders

  • get an outside perspective on your resume and linkedin because you cannot smell your own weak spots
  • put the most important info in the top third of your resume since most readers never get past it
  • name the size and type of companies you worked for so non-technical recruiters understand your experience
  • join a community so you can see peers getting interviews and jobs instead of sitting in a negativity bubble
  • build real relationships through small consistent actions like commenting online or showing up to the same meetup repeatedly

questions this episode answers

what does leon noel say about why job seekers should join a community?

on guidance counselor 2.0, leon noel says job seekers need to join a community so they can see other people getting interviews and jobs every single day instead of staying in a negativity bubble. when you watch peers putting in effort and getting results, you stay motivated and keep taking action. communities also give you real-time information about what's working right now, not just your own limited experience from a handful of interviews.

what does leon noel mean by saying you cannot smell yourself when job searching?

on guidance counselor 2.0, leon noel says you have a small thing on your resume or linkedin that makes recruiters pass on you, but you cannot see it yourself because you've been staring at it too long. he calls this your smell and says you need an outside perspective from someone else to spot what's wrong. in his one on ones, he found people had incorrect titles or weren't describing themselves in the best light.

how does leon noel say you should build relationships at meetups and online?

on guidance counselor 2.0, leon noel says relationships start with tiny repeated actions, not big impressive moves. at in person meetups, just make eye contact and nod at the same person three times over three months and you're already acquainted enough to connect on linkedin. online, like and comment on someone's posts a few times so they recognize you as someone who engages with their content. he also says reaching out about a shared hobby or interest gets way more replies than asking about jobs.
