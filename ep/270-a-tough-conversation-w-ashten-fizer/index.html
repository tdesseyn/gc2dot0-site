questions this episode answers

what does ashten fizer recommend doing during a tech hiring slowdown instead of just applying to jobs?

on guidance counselor 2.0, ashten fizer, talent brand go to market lead at dropbox, says job seekers should spend less time applying and more time upskilling during the current tech slowdown. she recommends figuring out what skills genuinely excite you before diving into trending areas like ai or machine learning, so you don't burn out. she suggests using free resources like youtube, linkedin learning through your library card, or udemy to build a more varied skill set that makes you a jack or jill of all trades.

how does ashten fizer say you should reach out to someone for mentorship on linkedin?

on guidance counselor 2.0, ashten fizer says to personalize every mentorship message and be specific about why you chose that particular person based on what they actually do. she warns never to send generic copy-paste messages because people can tell immediately. taylor desseyn adds a creative tip: offer to doordash the person coffee or lunch in exchange for 30 minutes of their time, which shows genuine interest and often gets a yes.

what does ashten fizer say about communicating upward to managers and executives?

on guidance counselor 2.0, ashten fizer says upward communication should be succinct, use bullet points instead of paragraphs, and happen early and often so your boss never has to ask what you're doing. if you're communicating to tech leaders, make it data driven and let the numbers tell the story. she emphasizes always being solution oriented: never bring a problem to your manager without proposing possible solutions or explaining what you're already trying to fix it.
