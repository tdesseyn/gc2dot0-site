building community

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • carry something like a tap card or qr code to cut networking friction at conferences and meetups
  • pick about five talks and five people to meet at a conference instead of trying to do everything
  • at local meetups narrow down to around ten people to connect with so you do not burn out
  • start networking before layoffs happen by sending one or two dms a day to people in your field
  • build community around something you are already passionate about and keep the first group small

questions this episode answers

how many people should you try to meet at a conference according to taylor desseyn?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn says to pick five talks and five people to meet at a conference instead of trying to do everything. for local meetups, narrow it down to around ten people. this approach prevents burnout and gives you permission to leave when you are exhausted, especially if you have social anxiety or are an introvert.

what does taylor desseyn say about networking before layoffs happen?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to start networking before you need a job, not after layoffs happen. he recommends sending one or two direct messages a day to people in your field just to talk tech and build relationships. one engineer he knows who landed at netflix was already networking and sending linkedin messages even while employed, getting ahead of potential layoffs.
