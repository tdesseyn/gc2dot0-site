ep 185 · nov 25, 2022

building community

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • carry something like a tap card or qr code to cut networking friction at conferences and meetups
  • pick about five talks and five people to meet at a conference instead of trying to do everything
  • at local meetups narrow down to around ten people to connect with so you do not burn out
  • start networking before layoffs happen by sending one or two dms a day to people in your field
  • build community around something you are already passionate about and keep the first group small
