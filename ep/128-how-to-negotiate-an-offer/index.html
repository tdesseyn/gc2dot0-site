how to negotiate an offer

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • when you get an offer, keep your mouth shut and let the hiring manager keep talking
  • ask detailed questions about pay schedule, bonus structure and benefits before responding to an offer
  • always ask for time to review an offer instead of deciding on the spot
  • if you counter an offer, always give a clear why backed by research like glassdoor data
  • confirm your salary range is dependent on seeing the full benefits package before locking in a number

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say is the most important negotiation tip?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says the most important negotiation tip is to keep your mouth shut when you receive an offer. when a company extends an offer, let the hiring manager keep talking instead of immediately accepting or rambling about what you might accept. you should respond with phrases like what else? or tell me more to keep them talking while you stay quiet and gather information.

how should you counter a job offer according to taylor desseyn?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says you should always provide a clear reason backed by research when countering an offer. do your homework on sites like glassdoor or salary.com to find the median salary for your skill set and industry, then state your desired number and explain why based on that data. after giving your counteroffer and explanation, keep your mouth shut and let the hiring manager respond instead of continuing to talk or backtracking.

what should you tell a hiring manager about your salary range according to taylor desseyn?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says you should always state that your salary range is dependent on seeing the full benefits package first. he warns that many candidates lock in a number, then see poor benefits and try to ask for more money, which creates problems. you should say something like my range is this, but i need to see the entire benefit package first before i can confirm that range to protect yourself.
