marketing & the job search

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • treat your job search like marketing by staying top of mind through consistent daily posting or engagement
  • post one piece of content regularly to document your job search or career instead of staying silent
  • comment and engage on other people's posts daily, it builds relationships that lead to referrals and job leads
  • skip putting your resume directly on your linkedin profile so people have to message you and start a conversation
  • write or blog about a topic you care about, even a niche one, since small posts can reach a surprisingly large audience over time
