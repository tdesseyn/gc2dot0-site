things to consider before leaving w/ me :)

guest: me :)

  • before jumping ship, check your base salary, bonus, 401k match, hsa contribution and pto policy first
  • benefit monthly costs matter a lot and can make a new offer look worse than your current job
  • ask if your problem is industry wide or specific to your company before assuming a new job fixes it
  • try a direct conversation with leadership before quitting, since many issues can be addressed in a meeting
  • when raising concerns with a manager, frame it as how you feel and bring suggestions instead of attacking

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say to check before accepting a new job offer?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn says to know your current base salary, bonus, 401k match, hsa contribution, and pto policy before jumping to a new job. he emphasizes that benefit monthly costs are huge and can make you realize you have really good benefits compared to what's available elsewhere. this financial comparison is critical because benefit costs affect your effective base salary and total compensation.

what does taylor desseyn recommend before quitting your job over workplace issues?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to ask yourself whether the issues you're dealing with are industry-wide problems or company-specific problems that could be fixed by a meeting with leadership. he acknowledges that having a direct conversation with your manager is hard and confrontation is uncomfortable, but many people are taking the easy out by jumping ship instead of addressing fixable issues. he suggests framing concerns as how you feel rather than attacking, and bringing suggestions to the table when you meet with leadership.
