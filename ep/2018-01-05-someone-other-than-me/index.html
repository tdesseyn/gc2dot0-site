someone other than me!

guest: david green, software developer at netapp

  • skip generic job boards like indeed, dice, and monster since leads rarely turn into real interviews and resumes get filtered by keyword systems
  • look beyond typical meetups for networking, try reverse job fairs and talking directly with internal recruiters
  • ask for a salary range early in the process so you avoid finding out you're 30 to 40k apart after a final interview
  • invest in a professional resume and linkedin rewrite so recruiters start reaching out to you instead of you chasing every lead
  • prep for interviews by researching the company and interviewer, practicing whiteboarding on sites like interview cake, and putting full effort into take-home assignments

questions this episode answers

what does david green say about using job boards like indeed and dice?

on guidance counselor 2.0, david green says job boards like indeed, dice and monster were a complete waste of time during his job search. he found that applying through aggregators or company websites directly led to no interviews because systems filter resumes by keywords automatically. he advises finding alternative approaches since you will likely hear nothing from these applications.

what does david green recommend about discussing salary during interviews?

on guidance counselor 2.0, david green says candidates should ask for the salary range early in the interview process instead of waiting. he went through two full interview processes all the way to final offers before discovering he was 30 to 40 thousand dollars off from what the company offered. his advice is to ask the recruiter or company upfront what range they are hiring at so you are not wasting everybody's time.

what does david green say about using a professional resume service?

on guidance counselor 2.0, david green says using a professional resume service that cost between 200 and 400 dollars changed everything for his job search. he used a service that rewrote both his resume and linkedin profile in an interactive process, and afterward he started getting quality inbound contacts from recruiters reaching out to him. he emphasizes choosing a service that specializes in technical resumes rather than a generic service.
