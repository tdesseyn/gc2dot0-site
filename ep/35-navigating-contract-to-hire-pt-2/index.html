ep 35 · mar 24, 2021

navigating contract to hire pt. 2

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • ask a recruiter how long a contract role has been open since months of vacancy is a red flag
  • always ask why the position is open, since turnover could be unrelated to the project or performance
  • ask if the hiring manager has budget to convert contractors to full time and if they have done it before
  • ask about conversion salary ranges so you are not lowballed when a contract converts to permanent
  • if you lack experience, offer free or volunteer hours to build a portfolio and get references

questions this episode answers

what questions does taylor desseyn say to ask recruiters about contract positions?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to ask how long the position has been open, because months of vacancy is a red flag. he says to ask why the position is open, whether the hiring manager has budget to convert contractors to full time, and whether the manager has converted people before. he also recommends asking what the conversion salary range is for your skill set, what the hiring manager's name is, whether the recruiter is a tier one vendor, and how many people the recruiter and their team have placed at that company.

how does taylor desseyn say to get experience when you have none?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to offer your time for free if you can afford it. he recommends volunteering one hour a day, five hours a week, for one client to gain experience. he explains that many people right now need work done but don't have money to pay, so this builds your portfolio and gets you references you can use later.
