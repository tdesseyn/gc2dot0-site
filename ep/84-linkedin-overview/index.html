linkedin overview

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • make your banner photo count since its the first thing viewers see on your linkedin profile
  • at minimum include your email in your banner since linkedin hides contact info behind a click
  • skip putting your resume in the featured section so people dm you directly instead
  • put specific keywords like exact languages or frameworks in your title since recruiters search by title not by scrolling profiles
  • add real numbers and comparisons to your accomplishments instead of vague claims like increased user retention
