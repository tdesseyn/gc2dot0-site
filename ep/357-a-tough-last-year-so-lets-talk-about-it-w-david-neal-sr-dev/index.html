ep 357 · apr 29, 2024

a tough last year so let's talk about it w/ david neal sr dev advocate

guest: david neal, sr dev advocate

  • expect interview requirements like unpaid take-home projects to sometimes be unreasonable and it is ok to decline them
  • take real time off to sit down and figure out what actually brings you joy before jumping back into a job search
  • if you are struggling after a layoff or tough year, consider talking to a counselor instead of pushing through alone
  • make a list of companies you would actually love to work for and reach out directly, even if they have no posted openings
  • getting involved in public speaking or community work builds skills and connections that can directly lead to future jobs

questions this episode answers

what does david neal say about unpaid take-home projects during interviews?

on guidance counselor 2.0, david neal says he received a 40-hour take-home project that paid only 25 dollars per hour during his job search. he declined it, telling the company that if they could not find a different way to assess his skills beyond a 40-hour project, they were done, especially since he had an entire body of work they could review.

how does david neal recommend taking time off after a layoff?

on guidance counselor 2.0, david neal says he took extra time off over the holidays to sit down with his wife and have long conversations about what brings him joy and what makes him come alive. he emphasizes the importance of taking inventory of past successes to identify what work was meaningful before jumping back into the job search.

what does david neal suggest about reaching out to companies without job postings?

on guidance counselor 2.0, david neal recommends making a list of companies you would absolutely love to work for, even if they have no job openings listed on their careers page. he says reaching out directly can lead to landing a job that never gets published, because you might connect with someone who has been thinking about hiring for a role and you get first shot at being that candidate.
