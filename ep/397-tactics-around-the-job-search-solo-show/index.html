tactics around the job search (solo show)

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • if you cannot land a developer role, consider qa, analyst, or customer support to get into a company and build business knowledge
  • practice explaining what you do in under 90 seconds so interviewers get a clear, confident answer right away
  • follow up quickly with recruiters, since slow responses can cost you the role to someone faster
  • stay positive and energetic in interviews, since hiring managers can sense negativity and it hurts your chances
  • join a slack or discord group with others in your field to share job leads and crowdsource the search
