tactics around the job search (solo show)

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • if you cannot land a developer role, consider qa, analyst, or customer support to get into a company and build business knowledge
  • practice explaining what you do in under 90 seconds so interviewers get a clear, confident answer right away
  • follow up quickly with recruiters, since slow responses can cost you the role to someone faster

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about pivoting when you can't land a developer role?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says if you're struggling to find a developer job, consider starting in qa, analyst work, or customer support to get into a company first. he emphasizes that organizations want people who understand the business, and starting in a role like customer support or account management gives you that business knowledge before moving to a technical role. he points out that having deep business understanding makes you more valuable than someone who can just code without context.

how quickly should you answer the tell me about yourself question, according to taylor desseyn?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says you need to answer the tell me about yourself question succinctly within 90 seconds. he explains that if you can't answer it clearly and concisely in that time frame, the interviewer's vibe will immediately turn negative and they'll doubt you. he adds that most job seekers ramble too much, so shorter is better, and you should end by asking if there's anything else they'd like to know.

what does taylor desseyn recommend for following up with recruiters during a job search?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says speed and follow-up are critical because if you don't respond within 24 hours, the company might move on to their second or third choice. he recommends the bcc hack: put 10 recruiters you like on a bcc email and send them monthly updates about where you're interviewing, your resume, and ask for their thoughts. he explains that recruiters handle so much at once that following up reminds them of you, and his own job at gun io happened because he followed up a week later.
