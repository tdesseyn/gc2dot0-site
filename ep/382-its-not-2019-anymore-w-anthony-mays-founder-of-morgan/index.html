questions this episode answers

what does anthony mays say bootcamp grads should do instead of grinding leetcode?

on guidance counselor 2.0, anthony mays says bootcamp grads should stop grinding leetcode alone and instead build real projects with one or two people. he tells students to focus on writing good commit messages, pull requests and documentation that show they can work on a team. when talking to hiring managers they can then point to these tangible projects as proof of their skills.

according to anthony mays who explains their work better, bootcamp juniors or self-taught juniors?

on guidance counselor 2.0, anthony mays says self-taught juniors communicate their work better than bootcamp grads on average. he explains that self-taught developers had to filter through a lot of bad information and documentation to learn, which forced them to develop the ability to hold conversations and explain what they are doing. they also tend to have more tenacity because they did not expect a shortcut to success.

what does anthony mays say about messaging someone when they view your linkedin profile?

on guidance counselor 2.0, anthony mays says if a hiring manager looks at your profile you should message them with specific questions, not generic ones. he recommends asking things like what three problems their team is facing right now or other specific details about the role. however taylor and anthony agree that if you are already working with a recruiter you should go through that recruiter instead of messaging the hiring manager directly.
