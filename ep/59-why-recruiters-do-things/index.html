ep 59 · jul 26, 2021

why recruiters do things

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • ask a recruiter how many people they placed last year to gauge experience and track record
  • ask if the recruiter has a specialty since focusing on one skill set leads to better matches than covering everything
  • ask how a recruiter tracks candidates and whether they will stay in touch even if the current role does not work out
  • if a company is getting a flood of resumes for one role the recruiting firm is not vetting candidates properly
  • recruiters who take time to test and reference check candidates often lose placements to faster firms but it leads to better quality hires
