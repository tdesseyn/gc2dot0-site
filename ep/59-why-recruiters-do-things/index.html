questions this episode answers

what questions does taylor desseyn say job seekers should ask recruiters?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says job seekers should ask three questions when a recruiter calls. first, ask how many people the recruiter placed last year, looking for around 50 placements as a good benchmark. second, ask if the recruiter has a specialty or focuses on a specific skill set, because recruiters who span all technology areas cannot learn enough to be effective. third, ask how the recruiter keeps track of candidates and whether they will stay in touch even if the current role does not work out.

why does taylor desseyn say most recruiters know nothing about company culture or team structure?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says recruiters know nothing about company culture and team structure because the industry is all about speed. most recruiting firms skip vetting steps like testing candidates, doing zoom calls, and checking references so they can submit resumes faster. taylor says his own team loses placements to competitors because they take time to do things right, while 99 percent of other firms just send out resumes quickly without proper screening.

what does taylor desseyn say about recruiting firms that send 10 resumes for one job?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says if a recruiting company is sending you 10 resumes for one role, they are doing it wrong and are not good at what they do. he says his own team struggles to get two to three qualified resumes per job order because they properly vet candidates. when firms flood clients with resumes, it shows they are not screening or qualifying candidates correctly.
