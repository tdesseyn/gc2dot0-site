hacking linkedin

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • use your linkedin cover photo to share contact info or a quick summary of what you do
  • pick a profile picture with good lighting and a pop of color so you stand out in a sea of tiny thumbnails
  • make your title specific with the exact tech or skill you use since recruiters search by keyword and skip vague titles like software developer
  • keep your about section to two or three lines since linkedin only previews a short snippet before cutting it off
  • spend time every day liking and commenting on posts since engagement through shared connections can get your profile and posts seen by hiring managers
