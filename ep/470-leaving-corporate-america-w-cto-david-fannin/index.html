questions this episode answers

what does david fannin look for when hiring engineers beyond technical skills?

on guidance counselor 2.0, david fannin says he looks for product engineers who understand the intent behind features and can solve problems, not just feature engineers who check off acceptance criteria boxes. he interviews for both competency and humility, testing whether someone can sit with a more junior person and still listen and learn. he also brings product managers into engineering interviews because engineers need to collaborate on discovery, not just execute tickets.

what career advice does david fannin give about staying at a job versus exploring other opportunities?

on guidance counselor 2.0, david fannin shares advice from his former manager doug at real track who told him to interview for jobs annually. the idea is that if you turn down other offers and stay, you know you are choosing to be there, not staying out of fear or because you lost interviewing skills. career moves do not have to be a straight line up, sometimes a step that looks smaller on paper gives you more room to grow and experiment with new approaches.

how does david fannin recommend engineers use ai tools in their day to day work?

on guidance counselor 2.0, david fannin says engineers should use ai to speed up busy work like summarizing user discovery calls or stubbing out code, but never trust it blindly. he lets ai handle the mundane tasks that would take hours, then goes in and writes the important code himself line by line. he warns against two extremes: going all in on vibe coding without security or refusing to use ai at all and falling so far behind you become obsolete in five years.
