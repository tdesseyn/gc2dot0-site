ep 9 · dec 3, 2020

if you are in a position of hiring...listen.

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • trust your recruiting firm enough to move fast once they send you a candidate instead of running three more rounds of interviews
  • recruiters typically charge around 20 percent of first year salary for average talent and 25 to 30 percent for top talent
  • consider contract to hire as a six month working interview so both sides can see if its a fit before committing
  • companies requiring full time on site work in tech right now will lose people to fully remote offers elsewhere
  • if you insist on in office work for your team ask whether that reflects a lack of trust in your leadership

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about how many interview rounds a company should do after a recruiter sends a candidate?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says companies paying a recruiting firm should trust them enough to either hire immediately or have one phone call and then hire. he argues that if a company is going to run three or four more rounds after the recruiter has already met, tested and referenced the candidate, they are not using the recruiter correctly. his team meets over 1,500 developers a year, and companies should trust that vetting instead of adding multiple interview rounds.

what does taylor desseyn recommend instead of traditional hiring for tech roles?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says contract to hire is the way to go for all tech positions. he describes it as a true working interview for six months where the candidate can see what the company is about and the company can evaluate the candidate. companies should integrate the contractor as if they will convert them full time, and if it does not work out after six months, both parties can part ways with no hard feelings.
