questions this episode answers

what does becca say about in-person conferences versus online interaction?

on guidance counselor 2.0, becca says that when you meet in person there is an authenticity that happens much quicker than online. she explains that people are used to curating their online persona, making it easier to present a specific version of themselves on zoom. in person creates more vulnerability and helps people create bonds and relationships in a more authentic way than what often happens in curated online environments.

what does becca say about treating your company as your community?

on guidance counselor 2.0, becca says that thinking of your company as your community is a bad idea because your company is not always there for you and they might fire you, leaving you without a community. she recommends that companies should promote external community building instead because it helps with diversity of ideas and opinions, helps developers grow through relationships, and can also help with recruiting by meeting people at conferences who would be perfect for open positions.
