questions this episode answers

how does taylor desseyn say employees should ask for feedback from their manager?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says employees need to drive the conversation and chase after their manager for feedback instead of waiting for the manager to initiate it. many leaders are scared to have radical candor conversations or get too focused on their own work to remember their team. employees should proactively ask how they are doing rather than expecting the manager to bring it up.

what does taylor desseyn recommend for leaders whose employees don't ask for feedback?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says leaders need to put time on their calendar specifically for following up with their team. he explains that people often create to-do lists but never complete them because they don't actually schedule the tasks. leaders should be intentional with their calendar and their employees' time to make sure team members feel seen and heard.

how does taylor desseyn say senior consultants should write resumes for long tenures at one company?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says the resume should be no more than three pages and should highlight renewals by big name clients along with high impact projects. he emphasizes removing filler words like great communicator or works well with others because the project history already demonstrates those skills. the focus should be on storytelling through clear, concise descriptions of important work rather than buzzwords.
