decentralization, interviewing w/ angie jones, vp of developer relations at block

guest: angie jones, vp of developer relations at tbd

  • referrals matter because they get your application a second look even in a market flooded with applicants
  • use open source contributions like fixing a typo in docs to get your name noticed by hiring teams
  • skip chatgpt for cover letters since hiring managers can tell and authenticity stands out more
  • in interviews admit what you dont know rather than making something up, then ask to learn the answer
  • invest in networking before you need it since who you know can open doors to roles and opportunities
