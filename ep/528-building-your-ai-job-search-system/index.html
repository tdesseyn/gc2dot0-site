building your ai job search system

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • success in job searching means conversations started not just applications sent, so focus on human replies that lead to real dialogue
  • break your ninety day job search into phases with different success metrics for weeks one through four versus five through nine versus ten through thirteen
  • run a weekly sunday review routine of thirty to forty five minutes where you log numbers, walk every live opportunity, and plan exactly five moves for next week

  • always ask yourself what objection you keep hearing in interviews and whether you have built an answer yet so you can workshop how you describe your value

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about measuring job search progress in claude?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn walks through a claude prompt that reverse engineers a 90-day job search timeline by breaking it into phases with specific success metrics for each week. the system tracks conversations started rather than applications sent, because success is measured by human replies and dialogue, not portal submissions. claude builds an excel tracker that logs eight key numbers weekly including conversation starts, first rounds, and pipeline stage, with automated calculations to show whether your pace matches the timeline.

how does taylor desseyn recommend structuring a weekly job search review with ai?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn demonstrates a claude co-work skill that schedules a 45-minute sunday routine divided into five blocks. you spend five minutes logging numbers, ten minutes walking through every live opportunity to identify who owes whom the next action, eight minutes answering diagnostic questions like what objection you keep hearing, seven minutes finding one leak to fix, and ten minutes planning exactly five moves for the coming week with a specific person and channel for each. the routine forces you to distinguish between where you are waiting on someone versus where someone is waiting on you.
