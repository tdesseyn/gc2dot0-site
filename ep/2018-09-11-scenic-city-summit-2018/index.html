questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about how long a recruiter should stay at one company?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn says look for recruiters who have worked at the same company for at least two years. frequent job hopping by recruiters often signals they are not great at their jobs and jump around every 18 months for commission bumps or higher base pay. a recruiter who has stayed in the same market and company for years brings market intelligence and stability.

how does taylor desseyn say to screen a recruiter before working with them?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn says ask any recruiter how they screen candidates and what methods they use. avoid working with recruiters who refuse to meet face to face or hop on a video call, because why would you trust someone for the next step in your career if they will not meet you. also check if the recruiter sends long stock job descriptions full of jargon, which signals they have no real relationship with the hiring manager.

what does taylor desseyn recommend about quantifying your resume?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn says get as quantitative as humanly possible on your resume with real numbers and comparisons. instead of writing vague phrases like good communication skills, state that you support a multi-million dollar application for a user base of 5,000 people. if you run a store that does five million in revenue, add that you rank in the top ten percent of 500 stores so the numbers have context.
