questions this episode answers

what does valarie regas say about waiting for a job to bring happiness?

on guidance counselor 2.0, valarie regas says waiting for the right job to bring happiness is a trap. she realized around christmas, after a dream job rejection, that she needed to be happy now instead of putting life on hold. she started teaching judo and jiu jitsu to find joy in the present rather than waiting for some external thing to make her happy.

what does valarie regas say about building self-esteem during a job search?

on guidance counselor 2.0, valarie regas says building genuine self-esteem matters more than just having a thick skin when facing rejection. she explains that while thick skin helps you mask hurt feelings, real self-esteem means valuing yourself and acknowledging both strengths and weaknesses. she notes that her greatest strength is being comfortable with her weaknesses and actively working on them.

what does valarie regas say about conference talk submissions and what audiences want?

on guidance counselor 2.0, valarie regas says she always submits three types of talks to conferences: a technical talk about a product, a hobby technical talk, and a human impact talk. for about a year and a half, conference organizers have been choosing the human impact talks, which she believes is telling about what people are actually hungry for right now.
