you're fired

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • get your resume and linkedin professionally written before anything else in your job search
  • try to meet or talk with one recruiter a day over coffee or lunch and ask who is hiring
  • look for recruiters with at least two years at their company and two years of recruiting experience
  • when you find a good job posting, bcc a few recruiters you trust and ask if they know the company
  • treat a full time job search as an actual full time job focused on relationships, not just applying

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say to invest money in during a job search?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says if you are going to spend money on your job search, the only thing worth paying for is getting your resume and linkedin professionally written. he emphasizes these two items are incredibly important and should be the front-end investment. while free resources and templates exist, sitting down with someone to get these right is the one expense he recommends.

how does taylor desseyn recommend using recruiters in a job search?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends trying to have lunch or coffee with one recruiter per day in your town and asking them who is hiring, who the best managers are, and which companies to avoid. he suggests following the two and two rule when picking recruiters: at least two years of experience at their current company and two years of recruiting experience overall. after meeting about five recruiters you can work with, send them job postings you find via bcc email to ask if they know anyone at those companies.

why does taylor desseyn say not to apply to jobs right away?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn points out that his entire job search strategy discussion did not once include applying to job postings. instead, he emphasizes building relationships first through creating your resume and linkedin, messaging recruiters for coffee meetings, and sending job leads to recruiters you trust. he stresses that relationships matter more than submitting resumes everywhere, saying depth over width in relationships will catapult you to the top of the list.
