ep 327 · feb 2, 2024

building courses and reputation w/ josh comeau

guest: josh comeau, software developer and educator

  • build an audience before you build a course so people already trust your teaching style
  • expect a course to take far longer than planned, often years, not months
  • start blogging or posting just to build your own reference and skills, not for an audience at first
  • teach at a local coding bootcamp to learn where beginners actually get stuck
  • treat your time and effort like a limited resource and spend it on work that serves your real goals

questions this episode answers

what does josh comeau recommend for beginners who want to start creating content?

on guidance counselor 2.0, josh comeau recommends starting on dev.to rather than building your own blog, because there's already a community and you can test if you enjoy writing before investing time in a custom site. he says his original motivation was just to build a reference for himself when he solved problems, not to build an audience. he published mostly for himself at first, which was perfect because it takes time to build the skill of writing and you want that practice before a big audience criticizes you.
