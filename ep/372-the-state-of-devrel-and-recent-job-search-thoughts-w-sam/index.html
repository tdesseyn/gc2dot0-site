ep 372 · may 27, 2024

the state of devrel and recent job search thoughts w/ sam julien

guest: sam julien, developer relations leader, starting a new devrel role at an enterprise generative ai company

  • treat your job search like a job but set clear work blocks instead of grinding nonstop all day
  • split your search day into focused time blocks so you still have room for family and rest
  • sort your job search tasks into green time like interviews and applying, yellow time like resume and linkedin upkeep, and red time like busywork, then prioritize green time
  • try to book two meetings or interviews every day during a search to keep momentum and feel progress
  • build your network and give back before you need a job, since that groundwork pays off if you're ever laid off

questions this episode answers

how does sam julien recommend structuring your day during a job search?

on guidance counselor 2.0, sam julien recommends working in focused blocks instead of grinding all day during a job search. he worked 9 to 11 am and 1 to 4 pm, which let him spend time with family for breakfast, lunch and dinner while still making progress. imposing these limits made him more efficient because he knew exactly when he had to schedule interviews and get work done.

what does sam julien mean by red yellow green time in a job search?

on guidance counselor 2.0, sam julien explains that green time is activities that directly move you forward like interviews and applying for jobs, yellow time is maintenance work like updating your resume and linkedin profile, and red time is busywork or time wasting tasks. he recommends prioritizing green time because interviews are the key performance indicator you are going for in a job search, since your odds improve dramatically once you get to that stage.

what daily goal does sam julien recommend during a job search?

on guidance counselor 2.0, sam julien recommends trying to schedule two meetings or interviews every day for sometime in the future during your job search. this practice came from his sales background and gives you a sense of accomplishment because it is hard to know when to stop for the day when you are endlessly applying. setting two appointments helps you feel like you have put in enough work and are setting yourself up for future success.
