ep 95 · oct 25, 2021

q&a and state of the market

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • offer top candidates more than what was submitted to build instant trust and buy-in
  • if you can't offer more money, get creative with personalized gifts like swag or a welcome book
  • candidates are moving fast right now so hiring managers should start hiring well before they actually need someone
  • sift through noise with empathy since both job seekers and hiring managers are overwhelmed right now
  • a shorter, simpler interview process respects candidates already working long hours and juggling family life
