questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about offering candidates more than their asking salary?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says that in his 10 years of recruiting, he has never had a hiring manager offer a candidate more than what was submitted. he argues that offering above the asking amount would build instant trust and buy-in, making the candidate excited to work at the company before they even start. he encourages hiring managers to do this when a candidate is really good and surprises them in interviews.

what does taylor desseyn recommend if a company cannot offer more money to a candidate?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn agrees with viewer suggestions that companies should get creative with personalized touches if they cannot offer more money. he mentions personalized welcome gifts like books specific to the role, swag, welcome videos, or even concert tickets and gift cards based on the candidate's interests. he stresses that anything personal goes a long way in a world that has become too impersonal.
