questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about volunteering time to gain job experience?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says job seekers should volunteer at least one hour per day or five hours per week on a specific project to build real skills when they lack experience. he emphasizes that churches and nonprofits often need website or digital help, and that volunteer work should be listed on your resume and linkedin as professional experience, not just side projects. taylor argues this approach helps junior developers and career changers get hired faster than those who don't volunteer their time.

how does taylor desseyn suggest asking for volunteer opportunities?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says job seekers should get comfortable directly asking people if they can help with projects for free. he recommends googling nonprofits in your city, finding their contact information or linkedin, and emailing them to say you're an up and coming developer or marketer trying to gain experience. taylor also suggests sliding into people's direct messages to offer volunteer services, noting that showing a small amount of free value upfront helps people see your worth and can lead to paid opportunities.
