ep 413 · feb 7, 2025

x's and o's of networking (solo show)

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • never message someone just because they posted a job, build rapport first around a shared topic
  • use linkedin job search filtered to the past day, then check the company for shared school connections
  • message people adjacent to a role about the skill or tool it mentions, not about the opening itself
  • show up consistently in slack and discord communities and comment before you ever need something
  • post on linkedin consistently and consider going live to interview people on topics you want to learn
