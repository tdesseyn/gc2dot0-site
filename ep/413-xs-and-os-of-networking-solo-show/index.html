ep 413 · feb 7, 2025

x's and o's of networking (solo show)

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • never message someone just because they posted a job, build rapport first around a shared topic
  • use linkedin job search filtered to the past day, then check the company for shared school connections
  • message people adjacent to a role about the skill or tool it mentions, not about the opening itself
  • show up consistently in slack and discord communities and comment before you ever need something
  • post on linkedin consistently and consider going live to interview people on topics you want to learn

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about messaging someone who posted a job on linkedin?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says never message someone directly about a job they posted if you don't know them, calling it slimy. instead, find people at that company and message them about a technical topic or skill mentioned in the job description, asking to learn from them. taylor shares the example of messaging someone about implementing test driven development in your environment when their company has a job listing that mentions it.

what does taylor desseyn suggest about starting a podcast or linkedin live for job seekers?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says job seekers should consider going live on linkedin to interview senior people about topics they want to learn. he suggests a show concept called learning me where a junior developer would interview senior engineers on topics in real time, which would help them learn while building relationships with those engineers. taylor explains this approach worked for him when starting his recruiting career, bringing decision makers onto his podcast to get to know him without pitching services directly.
