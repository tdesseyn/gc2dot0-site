questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about learning what jobs and salaries are available outside your current role?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says the best way to learn what jobs and salary ranges exist in the market is to talk to recruiters. he explains that many developers, especially those in smaller markets, have no idea what opportunities or compensation levels are available elsewhere. he encourages people to actively seek out and nurture relationships with good recruiters rather than dismissing all of them as spam.

how does taylor desseyn recommend networking in tech if you work remotely?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says developers need to go where tech people gather, just like musicians network at bars in nashville. he recommends joining discord channels, slack channels, conferences, and meetups. he also suggests connecting with everyone who comments on shows like his, connecting with every guest he has interviewed, and messaging strangers you want to talk to without fear.

what does taylor desseyn recommend for building a community around your interests in tech?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says starting a community is not hard if you pick something you are passionate about. he shares examples of starting a live show about tech careers, a recruiters anonymous meetup in nashville, and ideas for wine and tech gatherings or formula one brunch meetups. he emphasizes choosing things you genuinely care about and staying consistent rather than worrying about scale.
