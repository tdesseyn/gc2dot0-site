the path to principal w/ rich rader

guest: rich rader, principal engineer at evacore

  • a principal engineer role is still individual contributor work but carries director level responsibility and trust
  • staff and principal titles are often interchangeable, but senior and lead vary a lot by company so ask about actual responsibilities
  • extreme ownership means removing anything between you and a happy customer and fixing what you see even if it is not technically your job
  • healthy retention comes from rewarding teaching and growth, not rewarding people for working unpaid overtime during big launches
  • building strong remote culture takes intentional effort like regular culture discussions and team activities, not just being in an office

questions this episode answers

how does rich rader explain extreme ownership for engineers?

on guidance counselor 2.0, rich rader says extreme ownership means removing anything between you and a happy customer. if you see something the customer might not like, you fix it even if it is not technically your problem or someone else made the mistake. the mindset is that your customer is still experiencing a problem, so take ownership of their experience and make them happy regardless of whose job it was supposed to be.
