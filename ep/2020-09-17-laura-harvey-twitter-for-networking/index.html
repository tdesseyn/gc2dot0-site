laura harvey + leveraging twitter for networking

guest: laura harvey

  • start small on twitter by congratulating people on new jobs or birthdays to build real connections
  • use the hundred days of code hashtag to stay accountable and share your coding journey daily
  • keep hashtags relevant and minimal on twitter, unlike instagram where more hashtags can help
  • don't compare your early posts to others since most people only share their highlight reel
  • bring genuine enthusiasm to interviews, since attitude can matter as much as resume or portfolio
