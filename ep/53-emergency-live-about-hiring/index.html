questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say companies struggling to hire should do in 2021?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says companies struggling to find candidates are probably not offering full remote work and not paying enough. he recommends expecting lead times of two to four weeks to fill developer roles and being willing to pay above the typical 25 percent recruiting fee if you want top talent quickly. he also suggests considering all staffing models including consulting, contracting, contract to hire, and direct hire.

what does taylor desseyn recommend if you cannot find senior or principal engineers?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn suggests hiring managers who are struggling to find senior, principal, or staff level engineers should consider hiring mid level or junior developers instead. he recommends slowing down as a company, building out team responsibilities, and developing junior talent to scale up faster in the future rather than only searching for unicorn senior hires.

what does taylor desseyn tell job seekers to do during the competitive 2021 market?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn tells candidates to stay organized and responsible with their resumes and avoid being double submitted to companies by different recruiters. he says to be intentional about how you work with recruiters, check in with them consistently, and let them be your advocate since they are currently inundated with requests.
