questions this episode answers

how did attending chain react help mazen chami solve a work bug?

on guidance counselor 2.0, mazen chami says he attended chain react needing advice on bluetooth and react native for a startup building an injury prevention device. he heard a talk on bluetooth at the conference and on the plane ride back he fixed the bug he had been struggling with for a long time. the company then launched their product, and the relationships he built at the conference eventually led to his job at infinite red.

what does mazen chami recommend for balancing family and remote work?

on guidance counselor 2.0, mazen chami recommends doing a weekly standup with your partner to review the schedule and figure out who covers family responsibilities around important meetings. he and his wife also do a monthly standup to discuss higher-level family goals. he suggests using your remote work flexibility to fill your cup with short family time throughout the day, like coffee with your kid or covering lunch so your partner gets a break.
