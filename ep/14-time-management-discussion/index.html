time management discussion

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • clean out recurring calendar invites regularly to keep your schedule clutter free
  • block off an hour or so each day in your calendar just to work your to-do list instead of letting it sit untouched
  • try dragging emails straight into outlook to-do to turn messages into actionable tasks
  • use voice memos to respond faster to long emails like resume critiques instead of typing everything out
  • use color coded flags on emails to mark what needs a response, a follow up, or can wait

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about managing your to-do list in your calendar?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says he blocks off an hour to an hour and a half on his calendar specifically for working through his to-do list. he moves this calendar block around each day to make sure tasks actually get done instead of sitting on a list for weeks. he found that to-do lists without calendar time often go untouched because people don't work from them.

how does taylor desseyn use voice memos to save time on emails?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says he responds to long emails with voice memos instead of typing out full replies. for resume critiques, he looks at the resume while talking into his phone and sends the audio file so the person can listen when convenient. he credits his friend brandon for introducing him to this time-saving technique.

what does taylor desseyn recommend for keeping your calendar organized?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says he regularly deletes old recurring calendar invites and repurposes them at different times to keep his calendar clutter-free. he describes himself as very particular about calendar maintenance and does this cleanup at the end of each year. he also drags emails directly into outlook to-do to turn messages into actionable tasks.
