the power of self care, networking, and community w/annie t. bellefleur a software engineer at stitch fix

guest: annie t. bellefleur, software engineer at stitch fix

  • time block your days after bootcamp since the structure you had in class disappears once you graduate
  • split your week into focused chunks like networking days and project or coding days so nothing gets neglected
  • get visible on twitter by engaging with posts and sharing your projects so people start to know like and trust you

questions this episode answers

what does annie bellefleur say about structuring your time after bootcamp?

on guidance counselor 2.0, annie bellefleur says bootcamp gives you structure with a monday through friday schedule, but when you graduate you lose that and have to create it yourself. she recommends time blocking your entire day from wake-up to specific tasks. for example, she used monday, wednesday, friday for networking and interview prep, and tuesday, thursday for projects and data structures, with everything planned out so nothing gets neglected.

how did annie bellefleur use twitter during her job search?

on guidance counselor 2.0, annie bellefleur says she was heavily visible on twitter by commenting on and engaging with posts in the tech community. she posted daily snippets of her work, what she was learning, and her projects so people could see her work ethic. she explains there is a know, like and trust factor: the more people see you, the more they know you, like you and trust you, making it easier to ask for referrals later. hiring managers actually reached out to her on twitter because they saw her work.
