the power of self care, networking, and community w/annie t. bellefleur a software engineer at stitch fix

guest: annie t. bellefleur, software engineer at stitch fix

  • time block your days after bootcamp since the structure you had in class disappears once you graduate
  • split your week into focused chunks like networking days and project or coding days so nothing gets neglected
  • get visible on twitter by engaging with posts and sharing your projects so people start to know like and trust you
  • treat your job search with strategy and intentional conversations instead of just applying to a high volume of jobs
  • schedule real breaks and a weekly day off during your job search so you return with more energy and focus
