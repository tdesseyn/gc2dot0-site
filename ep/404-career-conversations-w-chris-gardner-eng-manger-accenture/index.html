career conversations w/ chris gardner, platform eng manger @ accenture

guest: chris gardner

  • blind applying can work but response rates are low so pair it with warm intros through friends and community
  • show up and do the job you want before asking for the promotion since titles usually follow proven work
  • when hiring, look past the resume and watch how a candidate talks through an unfamiliar problem out loud
  • treating teammates well and asking good questions builds a reputation that gets you referred into new roles
  • moving into management means stepping back from hands-on coding to focus on unblocking and developing your team

questions this episode answers

how does chris gardner recommend getting promoted according to accenture's approach?

on guidance counselor 2.0, chris gardner says at accenture you do not get promoted by waiting around. you get promoted by proving you are already doing the job at the next level, and eventually they give you the title to match. he advises showing leadership skills, handling client communication and doing the work of the role you want before asking for the promotion.

what does chris gardner look for when hiring junior developers?

on guidance counselor 2.0, chris gardner says he stops looking at the resume once he sees basic technical background. instead, he presents candidates with obscure problems the team took days to research and asks them to talk through their thinking out loud while he acts as their google search. he wants to see how candidates attack unfamiliar problems, ask questions and work collaboratively rather than check boxes on a skills list.
