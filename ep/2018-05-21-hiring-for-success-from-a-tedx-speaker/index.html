hiring for success from a tedx speaker!

guest: john rouda, it director and part-time professor at winthrop university

  • when hiring, weigh personality and team fit as much as technical skill since skill can be taught
  • find talent at local meetups by watching who speaks and who stays after to talk, not just who shows up
  • expect anyone in a senior role to mentor others, and ask candidates how they have mentored before
  • build a portfolio and a personal website with your own domain to stand out with no work experience yet
  • make your resume font and design slightly different from generic templates so it catches a hiring manager's eye

questions this episode answers

what does john rouda prioritize when hiring technical candidates?

on guidance counselor 2.0, john rouda says he weighs personality and team fit more heavily than technical skill when hiring. he explains that skills can be taught and ramped up, but how someone fits with the team is more important. he looks for people who are pleasant to work with and can communicate well, even if their technical abilities are only mediocre, because a mediocre developer with great soft skills who understands business problems is better than a superstar who is difficult to work with.

what does john rouda expect from senior engineers in terms of mentorship?

on guidance counselor 2.0, john rouda says he expects anyone in a senior role to mentor and lead other people. during interviews for senior positions, he asks candidates how they have mentored others in previous roles, and if they have not, he asks how they would approach mentoring. he wants to know that a senior engineer will help develop junior team members.
