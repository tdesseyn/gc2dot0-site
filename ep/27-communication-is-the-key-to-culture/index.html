questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about communicating time off to a new employer?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says you need to set expectations early by letting your manager know about any time off you've already planned before you start the job. many people start a new job with vacations or family time already scheduled but don't tell their managers, which creates problems. letting managers know from the beginning allows them to plan around your absence.

what does taylor desseyn recommend about after hours communication expectations?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends asking potential employers about their expectations for after hours communication before accepting a job. some organizations expect employees to be on call after five, which can wear people out. he says this is an important question for job seekers to ask during the interview process so there are no surprises later.

what does taylor desseyn say about video calls versus messaging at work?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says people need to get off direct messages and pick up the phone or do video calls instead, especially when there are frustrations. he explains that some people come across as brash over direct messages but their demeanor changes immediately on video. keeping your camera on during calls builds rapport, establishes culture, and is especially important if you started a new job during the pandemic.
