communication is the key to culture

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • set expectations with your manager early about time off so there are no surprises later
  • ask new employers what their expectations are for after hours communication before you accept
  • over communicate during the workday so your team always knows where you are
  • pick up the phone or hop on video instead of letting tension build over slack or dm
  • keep your camera on during calls to build rapport and trust with your team
