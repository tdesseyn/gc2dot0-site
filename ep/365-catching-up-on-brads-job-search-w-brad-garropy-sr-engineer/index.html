catching up on brad's job search w/ brad garropy sr engineer

guest: brad garropy, sr engineer

  • update your resume and linkedin on a regular schedule so you stay ready even before you start actively searching
  • let your network know you are looking since people amplify your search more than they hand you job leads directly
  • keep delivering at your current job while you search, then put in extra personal time in mornings and evenings
  • build a document outlining your compensation history and benefits so negotiation conversations go smoother
  • do not stay too long at a first job, aim for roughly two to five years to grow salary and experience faster

questions this episode answers

what does brad garropy say about keeping your resume and linkedin updated?

on guidance counselor 2.0, brad garropy says he sets a reminder every six months to update his resume and linkedin profile, even when he is not actively job searching. this habit kept him ready when docker reached out cold on linkedin because remix was in his headline. he also recommends going on practice interviews every six months to stay fresh.

how does brad garropy say networking helps a job search?

on guidance counselor 2.0, brad garropy says nobody in his network hands him a job directly, but people amplify that he is looking and plant seeds for him during their own interviews. a buddy interviewing at cloudflare mentioned brad to a senior vice president, which gave brad a warm introduction. he says the more people who know you are searching, the more they can keep their ear to the ground for opportunities.

what does brad garropy say about balancing a current job and a job search?

on guidance counselor 2.0, brad garropy says you need to continue delivering what you have been delivering at your current job so you do not burn the house down while you are still there. he handles personal job search tasks early in the morning before people are online and then puts in extra time at night, working late nights to wrap up what he needs to do. he admits scheduling interviews is tough but says you just have to do it.
