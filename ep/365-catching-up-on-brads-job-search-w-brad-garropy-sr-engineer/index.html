ep 365 · may 10, 2024

catching up on brad's job search w/ brad garropy sr engineer

guest: brad garropy, sr engineer

  • update your resume and linkedin on a regular schedule so you stay ready even before you start actively searching
  • let your network know you are looking since people amplify your search more than they hand you job leads directly
  • keep delivering at your current job while you search, then put in extra personal time in mornings and evenings
  • build a document outlining your compensation history and benefits so negotiation conversations go smoother
  • do not stay too long at a first job, aim for roughly two to five years to grow salary and experience faster
