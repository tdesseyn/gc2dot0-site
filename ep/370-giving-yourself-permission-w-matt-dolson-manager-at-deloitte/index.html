ep 370 · may 22, 2024

giving yourself permission w/ matt dolson, manager at deloitte

guest: matt dolson, manager, human capital at deloitte

  • look for ways to apprentice others or learn from someone with different experience, regardless of title or tenure
  • if your company lacks an apprenticeship or mentorship program, give yourself permission to start something small instead of waiting for approval
  • build in regular moments to celebrate wins on your team, no win is too small to acknowledge
  • leadership is about actions and skills anyone can show up with daily, not a title you wait to receive
  • before chasing a new role, get clear on your own values and who you are so you can tell if a culture fits
