questions this episode answers

what does will johns recommend for updating your resume regularly?

on guidance counselor 2.0, will johns recommends updating your resume every three months even when you are not actively job searching. he keeps a master resume in a google sheet with a skills section broken down by proficiency level. before submitting an application, he bolds the keywords relevant to that specific job and exports it as a pdf, so the same resume can be tailored quickly without rewriting it each time.

how does will johns suggest junior developers get started in tech?

on guidance counselor 2.0, will johns strongly advocates that juniors pick one framework or language and double down on it to get their foot in the door somewhere, then reach for mentors once inside. he also recommends github open source contributions because even small work like verifying bugs shows initiative, the ability to work on a team, and capability to do the work, which matters more to interviewers than the glamour of the contribution itself.

what daily note-taking habit does will johns recommend for tracking career growth?

on guidance counselor 2.0, will johns recommends jotting down a quick line at the end of each day about things you learned or worked on. at the end of the week, he reviews those five days and coalesces them into a slightly longer statement, then does the same monthly and yearly. this helps track personal growth, aggregates material for your resume, and identifies areas where you are not growing.
