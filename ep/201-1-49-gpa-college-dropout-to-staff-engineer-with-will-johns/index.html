1.49 gpa college dropout to staff engineer with will johns, staff software engineer at the butterfly network

guest: will johns, staff software engineer at the butterfly network

  • pick a framework or language, go deep, get your foot in the door, then look for mentors once you are in
  • check github for open source projects with first time contributor labels, even small bug verification work shows initiative to hiring managers
  • keep your resume updated every few months so you never have to cold recall your experience when you suddenly need it
  • keep a master resume in a doc with a skills section rated by proficiency, then bold the keywords relevant to each job before exporting it as a pdf
  • jot down a quick note each day on what you learned or worked on, then roll it up weekly and monthly to track growth and prep for reviews or interviews
