ep 175 · oct 5, 2022

onsite to fully remote

guest: sarah, head of business development at vault athletics

  • test a four day work week with a trial period and survey employees to see if productivity actually holds up, not just morale
  • build in forced rest between work sprints since burnout drops output just like it does for athletes in training
  • replace office water cooler moments with small structured things like random small group chats or casual team channels for non work talk
  • give people a personal development fund and a remote work stipend so they can choose what actually helps them, instead of generic happy hours
  • let employees choose their schedule and location instead of forcing onsite days, since forced choices breed resistance and autonomy builds buy in
