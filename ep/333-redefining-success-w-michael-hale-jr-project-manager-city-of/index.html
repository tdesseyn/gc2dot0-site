questions this episode answers

what does michael hale say about discovering who you are before job searching?

on guidance counselor 2.0, michael hale jr, project manager at the city of detroit, says you need to slow down and figure out what you actually like before chasing a job someone else loves. he recommends going back to your childhood to discover what made you tick and what made you excited or angry, because anger often points to real passion. then you can line that up with something you could do every day instead of copying what looks like success for someone else.

how does michael hale recommend redefining success in your career?

on guidance counselor 2.0, michael hale jr, project manager at the city of detroit, says you should ask yourself who told you that version of success in the first place. after both he and his wife lost their jobs this year, he had to shift from thinking success meant having whatever they wanted to asking why he wanted those things. he now defines success as being the best version of himself every day and serving people assigned to his life, and he says when you live that way the opportunities start coming to you.
