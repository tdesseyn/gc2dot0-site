questions this episode answers

what does rhia dixon recommend for getting comfortable talking about your accomplishments?

on guidance counselor 2.0, rhia dixon, associate director at vml, recommends writing about your accomplishments in third person, referring to yourself by name or as he, she, or they instead of using i or we. writing in third person turns feelings into facts because you step outside yourself and start listing what you did objectively. she also suggests keeping a brag book where you save screenshots of client praise and send them to your manager so they know your value.

what does rhia dixon say is the difference between moving from junior to mid level versus mid to senior?

on guidance counselor 2.0, rhia dixon says going from junior to mid level is about gaining technical skills and learning the tech stack, which is fairly transactional and education focused. moving from mid level to senior requires different skills like getting your team aligned to goals, spotting problems early, and explaining the why behind decisions to others. it is less about coding every day and more about applying your experience and getting people on the same page.
