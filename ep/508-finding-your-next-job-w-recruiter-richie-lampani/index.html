finding your next job w/ recruiter richie lampani

guest: richie lampani, recruiter and head of talent, rebel talent

  • react and node are so saturated that agencies rarely get hired to find react developers anymore, so consider building skills elsewhere
  • stay in touch with a recruiter about once a month, engage with their posts, and keep follow ups factual instead of emotional
  • don't let ai fully write your resume since reviewers can spot the same generic phrasing, write it yourself and use ai to check for blind spots
  • add a short italic description under unfamiliar company names on your resume so recruiters understand the context of your experience
  • after a rejection thank the recruiter and ask if they know anyone else hiring, since recruiters often pass good candidates to each other

questions this episode answers

what does richie lampani say about staying in touch with recruiters during a job search?

on guidance counselor 2.0, recruiter richie lampani says job seekers should check in with recruiters about once a month, not twice a week. he recommends dropping a direct message and engaging with their linkedin posts by commenting, which helps recruiters remember you. lampani emphasizes keeping follow-ups factual rather than emotional, avoiding messages about personal financial struggles since recruiters usually cannot speed up the process anyway.

what does richie lampani recommend about using ai to write your resume?

on guidance counselor 2.0, recruiter richie lampani warns against letting ai fully write your resume because he now sees the same chat gpt phrases over and over. he suggests writing the resume yourself first, then using ai to rewrite sections so it is not entirely ai-generated. lampani says he can spot ai resumes immediately because the phrasing is identical across candidates.
