ep 276 · sep 22, 2023

shashi lo - out here helping people

guest: shashi lo, senior software engineer at microsoft

  • treat mentorship as something you can get through tweets and groups, not just one on one relationships
  • shoot your shot when asking for mentorship or help, since the worst answer you get is no
  • stay intentional with your network by checking in on people even years after a rejection, since it can lead back to a job
  • keep a running doc of every interview question you missed so you can study and improve for the next one
  • if anxiety hits during an interview, ask for 30 seconds to collect your thoughts instead of rushing an answer

questions this episode answers

what does shashi lo say about finding a mentor?

on guidance counselor 2.0, shashi lo says you have to shoot your shot and ask people you align with, crafting a message that highlights what they've done and why you want to learn from them. he notes that not everyone has time for one-on-one mentoring, so he mentors through tweets and groups instead. the worst outcome of asking is a no, but someone might say yes and help shortcut your path to success.

how did shashi lo stay intentional with his network after a rejection?

on guidance counselor 2.0, shashi lo says he applied for a job and got rejected for lacking angular experience, but kept in touch with the hiring manager on linkedin. three years later he saw the manager was speaking at a conference and messaged to say he'd wanted to attend, and the manager replied asking if he was still looking for work because he was leaving and thought shashi would be perfect to replace him. shashi got one interview, killed it, and was hired because he stayed intentional about the connection.
