ep 276 · sep 22, 2023

shashi lo - out here helping people

guest: shashi lo, senior software engineer at microsoft

  • treat mentorship as something you can get through tweets and groups, not just one on one relationships
  • shoot your shot when asking for mentorship or help, since the worst answer you get is no
  • stay intentional with your network by checking in on people even years after a rejection, since it can lead back to a job
  • keep a running doc of every interview question you missed so you can study and improve for the next one
  • if anxiety hits during an interview, ask for 30 seconds to collect your thoughts instead of rushing an answer
