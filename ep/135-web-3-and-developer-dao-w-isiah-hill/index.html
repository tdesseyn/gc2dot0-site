questions this episode answers

what does isiah hill say about approaching web3 work and leadership?

on guidance counselor 2.0, isiah hill says treat web3 work as being a facilitator or liaison who can speak to multiple teams rather than always trying to be a leader or be in charge. he explains that in web3 it's better to operate from a sense of adding value and helping teams understand how they contribute to the bigger goal. this approach lets you work with more people, add more value, and learn more.

how does isiah hill say to choose which dao to join?

on guidance counselor 2.0, isiah hill says define very clearly what you want to do, what you're interested in, and what you hope to accomplish before joining a dao. he explains that different daos focus on different things like design (vectordao), social tokens (forefront dao), esports, or charity, so you need to know what you're interested in first. then people in web3 will be able to point you in the right direction based on your interests.
