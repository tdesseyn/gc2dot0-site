web 3 and developer_dao w/ isiah hill

guest: isiah hill, works with developer dao, contributing to get coin dao and the radical team

  • treat web3 work as being a facilitator or liaison who can speak to multiple teams rather than trying to be the leader in charge
  • approach web3 as a constant student of the game since the space changes fast and nobody stays an expert for long
  • before joining a dao, get clear on what you are interested in so people can point you toward the right community
  • look for daos matched to your interest, like design focused groups, social token projects, esports, or charity daos
  • before investing in crypto, start with the documentation and do real due diligence instead of jumping in blind
