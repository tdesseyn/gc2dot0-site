ep 516 · may 20, 2026

latam community recap

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • show up in person at engineering conferences instead of only staffing events if you want to actually meet talent face to face
  • free or low cost conferences tend to draw bigger and more engaged crowds than pricier ones
  • small thoughtful swag or gestures can mean a lot to people, so don't underestimate the impact of showing up prepared
  • consistent email and newsletter outreach compounds over time and helps grow an engaged community
  • booking private dinners around conferences is a simple way to build deeper connections with your community

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about staffing companies attending engineering conferences?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says he does not understand why staffing companies do not show up at engineering events. he notes that many staffing companies only attend other staffing conferences, which makes no sense to him. at talent land in mexico city, he believes his company was the only staffing or talent solutions firm present at a conference with thirty to forty thousand engineers.

what does taylor desseyn say about free conferences versus paid conferences in latin america?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn explains that nurdela in chile is a free conference where anybody in the community can sign up, and it drew between twelve and fifteen hundred people. he says his booth scanned eight hundred fifty-eight people, which was roughly half the conference attendees. he notes that at us conferences you typically see only ten to fifteen percent booth traffic, but they had fifty percent traffic at this free event.

what does taylor desseyn say about the impact of conference swag on attendees?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says people at the chile conference cried when they received swag like fanny packs and polaroid cameras. one woman had always wanted a polaroid camera but could never afford it, and she cried when they gave her one. he says you do not know the impact you have on people's lives and that even a simple polaroid camera can really affect people.
