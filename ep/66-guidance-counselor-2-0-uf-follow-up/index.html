questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn recommend when moving from direct hire to contract work for the first time?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says you should work with a recruiter for your first move into contracting. the recruiter can guide you through the client relationship, give you a heads up if things go wrong, and serve as your point person at the client. if you go freelance on your own, you are completely on an island with no support.

how does taylor desseyn explain the pay difference between contract and direct hire roles?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says with contracting you get paid for every hour you work, so if you work 60 hours one week you could take half the next week off. in a direct hire job, any hour over 40 hours a week immediately takes away from your salary with no extra pay. most contracts through recruiting firms are set at 40 hours a week because they have set budgets.

what does taylor desseyn say about contract ending notice and placement rates?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says for the most part he gets 30 days notice when a contract is ending, giving you time to find another job or get placed. his success rate at placing people on another contract after one ends is greater than 75 percent. the only reason he does not place people more often is they find jobs on their own.
