ep 259 · aug 14, 2023

how to get into tech w/ashten fizer

guest: ashten fizer, talent brand

  • reframe past jobs in resume language that fits tech, even a fast food job can show quality assurance skills
  • research bootcamps carefully since not all are equal, look for strong job placement rates and real company relationships
  • internships matter a lot for landing a job after school, aim for several summers of experience if you go the college route
  • if you dont know what you want to study dont feel pressured to enroll right away, explore trade school or free online courses first
  • if you wake up hating your job treat that discomfort as a signal its time to make a change
