ep 259 · aug 14, 2023

how to get into tech w/ashten fizer

guest: ashten fizer, talent brand

  • reframe past jobs in resume language that fits tech, even a fast food job can show quality assurance skills
  • research bootcamps carefully since not all are equal, look for strong job placement rates and real company relationships
  • internships matter a lot for landing a job after school, aim for several summers of experience if you go the college route
  • if you dont know what you want to study dont feel pressured to enroll right away, explore trade school or free online courses first
  • if you wake up hating your job treat that discomfort as a signal its time to make a change

questions this episode answers

what does ashten fizer say about reframing past jobs on a tech resume?

on guidance counselor 2.0, ashten fizer says every job you've held contains transferable skills that can be reworded for tech roles. she explains that a line cook at mcdonald's wasn't just cooking, but performing quality assurance to ensure customers received consistent product. the key is looking at what you actually did and rephrasing it to fit the industry you're targeting.

what does ashten fizer say about enrolling in college when you don't know what you want to do?

on guidance counselor 2.0, ashten fizer says if you don't know what you want to do, unenroll and go home to figure it out instead of wasting money. she believes you need at least an idea of your major and what you'll do with it after graduation, including how it will generate income. rather than spending years trying to figure it out in college, explore trade schools or free online courses first.
