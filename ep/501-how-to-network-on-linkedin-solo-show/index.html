questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about posting frequency on linkedin during a job search?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to post once a day on linkedin instead of three to five times daily. he recommends a weekly rhythm: mondays talk about what you are doing at work or building, tuesdays share something you are stuck on, wednesdays document a win or accomplishment, thursdays share a piece of content you consumed with your thoughts and tag the creator, and fridays can be a wildcard day. the goal is to document your journey rather than try to generate tons of content.

how does taylor desseyn recommend using linkedin likes and comments to network?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says liking and commenting on posts is like shaking hands at a meetup and can be scaled across the internet. when you consistently engage with someone's posts by leaving thoughtful comments, the algorithm shows you to more people and keeps you top of mind. if you consistently comment on posts about a specific technology like kubernetes, both the algorithm and those posting about kubernetes will see you more often. your name shows up more prominently than the original poster when you comment, helping you become known for that topic.

what does taylor desseyn say about writing connection requests and direct messages on linkedin?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to always add a personalized note when connecting, keeping it to three sentences maximum. start with admiration or flattery by mentioning something specific from their profile or activity feed, then make a clear and specific ask like requesting a call or advice. when you connect with someone and they accept, your message goes to the top of their inbox, creating friction that increases the chance they will respond. search for alumni from your school or bootcamp and filter by city to find warm contacts who share common ground and are more likely to help.
