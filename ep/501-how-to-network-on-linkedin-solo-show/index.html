how to network on linkedin - solo show

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • post once a day on linkedin and document your journey instead of trying to post multiple times daily
  • use a simple weekly rhythm of posts covering what you are doing at work, what you are stuck on, a win, and a piece of content you liked with your thoughts
  • engage with posts through likes and thoughtful comments since it works like shaking hands at a meetup and helps the algorithm show you to more people
  • when sending a connection request or dm always add a short personalized note with admiration or a specific shared detail, keep it to about three sentences, and end with a clear ask
  • search your school or bootcamp alumni on linkedin and filter by city to find warm contacts who are more likely to respond and help with your job search
