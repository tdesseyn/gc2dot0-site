questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about where to put technical skills on a resume?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 conference talk, taylor desseyn says to put specific skills and years of experience in the top third of your resume because most recruiters do a keyword search using control-f. if the technical skills are not visible at the top, recruiters will not call you even if you are qualified. he stresses that recruiters and hiring managers only read the top third of each page, spending no more than 90 seconds scanning.

why does taylor desseyn tell developers to avoid multiple column resume formats?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 conference talk, taylor desseyn says to stop using multiple column layouts because recruiters and hiring managers only spend about 90 seconds on a resume and get confused trying to follow text across two or three columns. he explains that when he opens a multi-column resume he does not understand what he is reading and has to work harder to scan it, so single-column formats make it easier for reviewers to process information quickly.

how often does taylor desseyn recommend following up during an interview process?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 conference talk, taylor desseyn says to follow up every few days, especially early in the process, because companies and hiring managers are slammed and busy. he shares that when a founder told him to follow up in a month, he followed up in a week instead and got hired because the other candidate did not work out. he emphasizes that your message tone is what can be annoying, not the act of following up itself.
