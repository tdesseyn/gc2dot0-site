how content can land you a job w/ chantastic

guest: chantastic, developer educator at workos

  • document a layoff publicly if it feels right, since being open and helpful can resonate well beyond your immediate circle
  • treat content as a trust-building exercise, not a highlight reel, since people hire who they trust
  • making a specific video for a company you want to work for can get you noticed faster than a standard application
  • it is okay to tell an interviewer a role is not the right fit for you, even at a company like netflix
  • if you have any interest in video editing, pursue it, since developer content and storytelling skills are increasingly valuable in tech careers
