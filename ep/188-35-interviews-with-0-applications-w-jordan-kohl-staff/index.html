35 interviews with 0 applications w/jordan kohl, staff software engineer at twilio

guest: jordan kohl, staff software engineer at twilio

  • turn on linkedin signals that show you're open so recruiters come to you instead of chasing applications
  • build a spreadsheet or trello board of target companies based on remote setup, size, and product you actually care about
  • ask every hiring manager and interviewer what their last vacation was to gauge real work-life balance

questions this episode answers

how did jordan kohl get 35 interviews without submitting applications?

on guidance counselor 2.0, jordan kohl explains that he turned on the linkedin setting showing he was open to work, which brought recruiters to him instead of requiring applications. he received ten to twenty recruiter messages per day once his profile was optimized. he also built a spreadsheet of target companies filtered by remote status, size, and product type so he could quickly evaluate inbound opportunities.

what vacation question does jordan kohl ask interviewers to gauge work-life balance?

on guidance counselor 2.0, jordan kohl says he asks every interviewer, including hiring managers and engineers, what their last vacation was. he finds that people give honest answers because they enjoy talking about time off, and the answers reveal differences in stress levels and actual work-life balance across roles. this simple question helps him understand how hard and how often people really work.

how did jordan kohl negotiate higher compensation with multiple offers?

on guidance counselor 2.0, jordan kohl explains that he pushed all his interviews to the final stages at the same time so he could get multiple competing offers. whenever he reached the final stage with any company, he immediately told every other recruiter he was talking to, which sped up their processes. he believes multiple offers are the key to unlocking the next tier of compensation because companies will pay market rate at the bottom, but competing offers make the numbers explode.
