35 interviews with 0 applications w/jordan kohl, staff software engineer at twilio

guest: jordan kohl, staff software engineer at twilio

  • turn on linkedin signals that show you're open so recruiters come to you instead of chasing applications
  • build a spreadsheet or trello board of target companies based on remote setup, size, and product you actually care about
  • ask every hiring manager and interviewer what their last vacation was to gauge real work-life balance
  • push interviews toward final stages in parallel so you can use competing offers to negotiate pay up
  • set a clear reply template listing comp, pto, and interests so you can filter out recruiters fast and stay consistent
