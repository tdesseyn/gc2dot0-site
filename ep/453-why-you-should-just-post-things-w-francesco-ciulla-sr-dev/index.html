ep 453 · may 12, 2025

why you should just post things w/ francesco ciulla, sr. dev rel

guest: francesco ciulla, senior developer advocate at daily dev

  • treat content creation like a long term commitment, try it for six months or a year before deciding it is not for you
  • post consistently even when nobody is watching, the feedback and connections often come later than expected
  • prioritize physical health and energy first, tired or stressed creators struggle to make good content
  • people will judge you whether or not you post, so you might as well put your work out there
  • build a backlog of content ideas so you can keep producing even during busy or low motivation periods

questions this episode answers

what does francesco ciulla say about how long you should try content creation before deciding it's not for you?

on guidance counselor 2.0, francesco ciulla says people should commit to content creation for at least six months or a year before deciding whether it works for them. he compares it to learning a skill like rust or docker, which took him years to really understand. trying something only a few times, like going to the gym three times, does not give you enough information to know if you actually like it or see its value.

how did francesco ciulla get started with content creation if he hated social media?

on guidance counselor 2.0, francesco ciulla explains that he originally hated social media and saw it as a waste of time. he only started using twitter in 2020 to promote an android app he built and get downloads. after joining tech twitter and publishing an article in february 2020, he felt the teaching vibe again even though nobody read it, which sparked his interest in creating content to help others learn.

what does francesco say about prioritizing health when creating content consistently?

on guidance counselor 2.0, francesco ciulla says physical health must come before content creation because you need to be in your best condition to create quality content. if you are tired or stressed, creating content becomes much harder, similar to trying to play piano or draw when exhausted. he goes for a run at five in the morning before recording, and even though it takes time away from content, the extra energy lets him accomplish more throughout the day.
