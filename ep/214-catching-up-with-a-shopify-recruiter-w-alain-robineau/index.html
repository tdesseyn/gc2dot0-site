ep 214 · feb 1, 2023

catching up with a shopify recruiter w/alain robineau

guest: alain robineau, recruiter at shopify focused on data science

  • when giving rejection feedback, offer candidates a quick call instead of just an email so they understand where they fell short
  • most candidates offered a feedback call will not book it, so dont expect high uptake even when you offer
  • before messaging someone at a company, check their profile so you ask about the right specialty instead of a generic question
  • stand out by sending a pointed message that connects your background to the specific role instead of a generic pitch
  • in a tough market be intentional with outreach, tailor your resume to the job description, and get active in slack or discord communities instead of lurking

questions this episode answers

what does alain robineau say about offering rejection feedback to candidates?

on guidance counselor 2.0, alain robineau says he gives candidates a link to book time with him immediately after sending a rejection email so they can discuss where they fell short. he finds that more than 50 percent of candidates offered this feedback call do not actually book it. he believes offering face-to-face feedback helps candidates understand what they need to improve and shows alignment with company values around receiving and acting on feedback.

how does alain robineau suggest job seekers message recruiters on linkedin?

on guidance counselor 2.0, alain robineau says candidates must find the right person and check their profile before reaching out. he received a message from a software engineer asking for advice even though his profile clearly states he only recruits for data science. he recommends sending a pointed message that highlights what in your background is relevant for that specific company and role, rather than a generic pitch asking what they look for.

what does alain robineau recommend for job seekers in a tough market?

on guidance counselor 2.0, alain robineau says job seekers need to be intentional in their outreach and craft a resume that addresses each specific job description rather than sending generic applications. he also emphasizes getting active in slack or discord communities rather than just lurking, saying engagement helps people notice you and allows you to ask questions and share different viewpoints.
