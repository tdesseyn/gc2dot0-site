ep 214 · feb 1, 2023

catching up with a shopify recruiter w/alain robineau

guest: alain robineau, recruiter at shopify focused on data science

  • when giving rejection feedback, offer candidates a quick call instead of just an email so they understand where they fell short
  • most candidates offered a feedback call will not book it, so dont expect high uptake even when you offer
  • before messaging someone at a company, check their profile so you ask about the right specialty instead of a generic question
  • stand out by sending a pointed message that connects your background to the specific role instead of a generic pitch
  • in a tough market be intentional with outreach, tailor your resume to the job description, and get active in slack or discord communities instead of lurking
