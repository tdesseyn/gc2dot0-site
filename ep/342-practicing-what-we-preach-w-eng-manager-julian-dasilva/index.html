ep 342 · mar 15, 2024

practicing what we preach w/ eng manager julian dasilva

guest: julian dasilva, engineering manager at misfits market

  • reach out to your network first when starting a job search, including old coworkers and recruiters you know
  • cold applying can still work but pair it with referrals and direct outreach to hiring managers on linkedin
  • time block your job search, spend focused hours each evening on quality applications instead of mass applying
  • research the actual people interviewing you, not just the job post, to tailor your examples to what they value
  • in your first 90 days at a new job spend real time learning how people actually work before changing processes

questions this episode answers

what does julian dasilva say about cold applying versus networking in a job search?

on guidance counselor 2.0, julian dasilva, engineering manager at misfits market, says cold applying can work but should not be your only strategy. in his recent job search, about 30 percent of his phone screens came from cold applies including the job he ultimately landed, while 40 percent came from referrals and another 30 percent from reaching out directly to recruiters in his network. he recommends casting bait in multiple pools rather than focusing on just one approach.

how does julian dasilva recommend structuring daily job search time?

on guidance counselor 2.0, julian dasilva says he time blocked two hours each evening while still working full time. his goal was five quality applications per night, not spam clicking but researching companies that aligned with his skills and mission, writing tailored cover letters, and adjusting his resume to highlight relevant keywords. he spent about one hour on applications and outreach and one hour studying and prepping for interviews.
