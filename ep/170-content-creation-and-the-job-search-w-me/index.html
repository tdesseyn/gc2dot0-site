ep 170 · aug 19, 2022

content creation and the job search w/ me :)

guest: me :)

  • document what you learn every day and post it, even short two or three sentence updates count as content
  • if someone corrects your post, dm them to say thanks and ask for a quick call, this builds your network fast
  • treat being wrong online as a chance to learn, not something to hide or delete
  • post video recaps on tiktok of what you learned that day, even with zero followers you can get real views
  • hiring managers should post about how they lead and what they believe to attract talent instead of relying only on job boards

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about posting wrong information on linkedin?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says being corrected on a post is actually a networking opportunity. when someone corrects you, send them a direct message thanking them and ask for a fifteen minute call to learn more. then ask that person if they know anyone else you can talk to, which can quickly build a network of thirty connections in that topic area.

how does taylor desseyn recommend using tiktok for a job search?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends posting daily video recaps on tiktok about what you learned that day or are struggling with. even with zero followers, every video can reach two to three hundred new viewers, so posting four videos a week means twelve hundred new eyeballs per week. he contrasts this with applying to jobs where maybe nobody sees your resume.

what does taylor desseyn tell hiring managers to do to attract talent?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn tells hiring managers to post content about how they manage teams, what they believe in, and their leadership philosophies on social media. he says companies should cut ad spending and instead invest marketing dollars in building content teams around hiring managers, because organic content from leaders attracts talent better than job board posts.
