ep 230 · mar 10, 2023

you belong in tech w/anna mcdougall

guest: anna mcdougall, director of product and engineering, author

  • break your career change into three steps: learn the technical skills, build a network, then apply to jobs with research instead of spray and pray
  • use a structure like scrum sprints to set weekly or monthly learning goals if you are teaching yourself to code
  • when networking, ask questions and listen instead of pitching, then jot down quick notes so you remember people at the next event
  • say yes to opportunities and actually show up, since consistency and time pay off within six months to a year
  • in applications use the names numbers quotes approach, naming people at the company, citing your achievements, and adding real recommendations
