ep 524 · aug 17, 2026

using ai in your job search - dms & cover letters

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • use ai to build a company hit list by giving it your target role and the type of companies you want, like community-first or saas companies
  • when reaching out to someone on linkedin, feed ai their profile and your background so it can find real commonalities for a dm, not generic flattery
  • short dm's beat long ones, ai tends to write wordy messages so trim them down to something that doesn't feel salesy
  • paste a job description into ai and ask honestly if it's a good fit for you, including salary, location and scope red flags
  • for cover letters tell ai to skip the cliche opener, write three short paragraphs on why the company, why you're a fit, and what you'd do in the first 90 days, then edit it to sound like you before sending

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn recommend when using ai to write linkedin messages?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn says to paste the person's linkedin profile into ai along with your background so it can identify real commonalities, not generic flattery. he emphasizes that ai tends to write overly long, wordy messages that feel salesy, so you should trim them down significantly. taylor consistently prefers the shortest message option ai generates because people don't read long messages and short notes get more responses.

how should you write a cover letter with ai according to taylor desseyn?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to tell ai to write three short paragraphs covering why the company interests you, why you fit the role, and what you would do in the first ninety days, plus a call to action. he specifically instructs ai not to start with cliche openers like i am writing to express my interest and to make it sound like you, not a form letter. taylor emphasizes that ai gets you eighty percent of the way there, then you must edit it yourself to sound authentic before sending.
