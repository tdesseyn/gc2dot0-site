ai and the job search w/ vp of eng, andrew moore

guest: andrew moore, vp of software engineering at a nationwide mortgage lender

  • dont blame ai for hiring slowdowns that are really just a tough economy and fewer openings
  • junior devs should still learn core fundamentals so they can vet and explain whatever ai generates for them
  • in big regulated companies ai coding tools are often banned or far from mainstream so dont assume everyone uses them
  • in fast moving startups speed and revenue matter more so being hands on with ai tools can help you move quicker
  • make your resume and cover letter sound like you since generic ai written content is obvious and frustrating to read
