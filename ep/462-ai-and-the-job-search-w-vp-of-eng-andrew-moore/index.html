ep 462 · jun 6, 2025

ai and the job search w/ vp of eng, andrew moore

guest: andrew moore, vp of software engineering at a nationwide mortgage lender

  • dont blame ai for hiring slowdowns that are really just a tough economy and fewer openings
  • junior devs should still learn core fundamentals so they can vet and explain whatever ai generates for them
  • in big regulated companies ai coding tools are often banned or far from mainstream so dont assume everyone uses them
  • in fast moving startups speed and revenue matter more so being hands on with ai tools can help you move quicker
  • make your resume and cover letter sound like you since generic ai written content is obvious and frustrating to read

questions this episode answers

does andrew moore think ai is actually taking junior developer jobs?

on guidance counselor 2.0, andrew moore says he has not seen companies state they are not hiring junior developers because of ai. he believes the real issue is bad timing: ai came to prominence at the same time the economy was not performing well and companies were scaling back. moore argues that people want something to blame, but the hiring slowdown is not caused by ai itself.

how can junior developers make their resumes stand out according to andrew moore?

on guidance counselor 2.0, andrew moore says candidates should make their resume and cover letter sound like themselves, not like ai generated content. he explains that when hiring managers read ai-written materials, it is obvious and frustrating because the candidate did not take the time to write it themselves. moore advises using ai as a starting point if needed, but making sure the final content is in your own words and feels human.
