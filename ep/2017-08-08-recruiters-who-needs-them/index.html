recruiters. who needs them?

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • after posting your resume to job boards, the real next step is learning how to work with a recruiter
  • look for a recruiter who has worked in your local market for at least one to two years
  • avoid recruiters who job hop every year or two just to chase a salary bump
  • favor recruiters who have stayed at the same company for an extended time, ideally two or more years
  • a recruiter with both local market tenure and company tenure is worth a closer look
