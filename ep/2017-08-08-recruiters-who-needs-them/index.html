recruiters. who needs them?

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • after posting your resume to job boards, the real next step is learning how to work with a recruiter
  • look for a recruiter who has worked in your local market for at least one to two years
  • avoid recruiters who job hop every year or two just to chase a salary bump
  • favor recruiters who have stayed at the same company for an extended time, ideally two or more years
  • a recruiter with both local market tenure and company tenure is worth a closer look

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say to look for when choosing a recruiter?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to look for a recruiter who has been working in your local market for at least one to two years because he still learns new clients after three and a half years. he also recommends finding a recruiter who has stayed with the same company for over two years, not someone who job hops every year or two for salary bumps. the ideal recruiter has both local market experience and company tenure of two or more years.

why does taylor desseyn say a recruiter needs local market experience?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn explains that recruiters need time to learn a local market because even after three and a half years recruiting nationally, he is still learning new clients and does not know the market one hundred percent. he recommends working with recruiters who have at least one to two years in your specific city.
