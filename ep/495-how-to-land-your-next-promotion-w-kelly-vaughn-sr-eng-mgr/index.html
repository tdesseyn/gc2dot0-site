how to land your next promotion w/ kelly vaughn, sr. eng mgr

guest: kelly vaughn, senior engineering manager at zapier

  • understand that a promotion needs both personal readiness and an open business case, and you can only control the first one
  • advocate for yourself by emailing or updating your manager regularly on what you accomplished that month, not just your job description tasks
  • if your company has a formal promotion document, start filling it out a year ahead to spot gaps and build a record of your work
  • when giving suggestions to leadership, present multiple options and clearly state which one you recommend and why to show ownership
  • if you get rejected for a role or promotion, ask why, work on the gaps, and reapply later since a no now is not a no forever

questions this episode answers

what does kelly vaughn say about the two components needed to get a promotion?

on guidance counselor 2.0, kelly vaughn says there are two components to getting a promotion: you have to be ready for that role, and there has to be a business case for the role to exist. you can only control the first component, which is your own readiness. if you want to move from ic to manager or senior to staff engineer, the opportunity has to exist at the company regardless of your qualifications.

what does kelly vaughn recommend doing with promotion documents a year in advance?

on guidance counselor 2.0, kelly vaughn says if your company has a structured promotion document you fill out, you should start filling it out a year before you want the promotion. this immediately identifies the gaps you currently have so you can work with your manager to gain the missing experience. it also creates a documentation trail of your accomplishments that you can use even if you leave the company.
