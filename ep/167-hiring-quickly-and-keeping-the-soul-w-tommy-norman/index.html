ep 167 · aug 12, 2022

hiring quickly and keeping the soul w/ tommy norman

guest: tommy norman, chief agility officer at aquanaevo

  • when scaling a company, know the people who thrived early on may not be the right fit for the next growth stage
  • define core values with specific examples, not buzzwords like integrity, so new hires know how to actually live them
  • let teams try to self correct conflict first through working agreements before escalating to formal coaching or a pip
  • hiring managers lose candidates mainly from lack of communication, so overcommunicate even when there is no new update
  • pay close enough to market rate that money stops being the deciding factor, then win on autonomy, purpose and mastery

questions this episode answers

what does tommy norman say about why candidates are lost during hiring?

on guidance counselor 2.0, tommy norman, chief agility officer at aquanaevo, says hiring managers lose candidates mainly because of lack of communication. he advises that even when there is no new information, managers should still reach out to let candidates know nothing has changed. in the time between calls, candidates may receive multiple offers and choose the one where they feel informed.

what does tommy norman recommend for salary strategy when competing for talent?

on guidance counselor 2.0, tommy norman says companies should pay close enough to market rate that money stops being the main decision factor, then compete on culture and values. he references daniel pink's framework of paying people enough so they do not worry about money, then focusing on autonomy, purpose and mastery to make candidates want to work there despite not matching the highest offers.
