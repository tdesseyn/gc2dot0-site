rest, the job search, and hiring for teachable skills w/paul gower who is an engineering team lead at kin insurance and conference organizer for little rock tech fest.

guest: paul gower, engineering team lead at kin insurance and conference organizer for little rock tech fest

  • treat rest as a skill you actively practice, not just laziness or downtime
  • experiment to find your own rest style since naps and routines work differently for everyone
  • build small recurring team moments like casual icebreakers to create connection and downtime
  • prepare your own thoughtful questions for interviews about culture and practices instead of just answering theirs
  • when hiring, prioritize teachable skills like collaboration and willingness to learn over exact tech stack experience
