questions this episode answers

what does paul gower say about rest and productivity?

on guidance counselor 2.0, paul gower, engineering team lead at kin insurance, says rest is a muscle you should actively work on, not a badge of laziness. research shows your brain is as active or more active when resting as when working, processing information differently and clearing out stress chemicals. he recommends experimenting to find what works for you, whether that's naps, walks, exercise, or deep play, since rest styles vary by person.

what does paul gower recommend job seekers ask in interviews?

on guidance counselor 2.0, paul gower says the biggest mistake job seekers make is not preparing their own thoughtful questions about the company's culture and practices. he recommends asking about pairing practices, how agile principles are applied, whether vacation is encouraged or just on paper, and what the day-to-day work environment looks like. coming with questions shows you're not just chasing a paycheck and that you're being thoughtful about fit.

what does paul gower prioritize when hiring engineers?

on guidance counselor 2.0, paul gower says hiring managers should focus less on exact tech stack experience and more on teachable skills like collaboration and willingness to learn. he explains that programming languages are teachable, but human skills like being collaborative, helpful, and encouraging are harder to train. he recommends including questions in the interview process that gauge aptitude and whether someone can learn, rather than fixating on whether they already know rails or python.
