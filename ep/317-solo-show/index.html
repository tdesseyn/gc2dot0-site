ep 317 · jan 10, 2024

solo show

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • treat cold applying as one tool in your job search, not the whole strategy, since hundreds of people often apply to the same posting
  • if you have applied to 500 jobs with no results, change your approach instead of repeating the same thing
  • follow up the day after a conference or meetup by connecting on linkedin and messaging people you met
  • if networking feels uncomfortable, bring a friend to events and follow them around until you warm up
  • for interviews, research the company and interviewer, review your resume against the job description, and prepare your own questions
