with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • treat cold applying as one tool in your job search, not the whole strategy, since hundreds of people often apply to the same posting
  • if you have applied to 500 jobs with no results, change your approach instead of repeating the same thing
  • follow up the day after a conference or meetup by connecting on linkedin and messaging people you met

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about following up after a conference or meetup?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says networking after the conference is almost more important than networking during the event. he recommends connecting with people you met on linkedin the next day and sending them a direct message saying it was nice to meet them. if you meet people at a conference or meetup and go home without following up the next day, you are missing out on most of the value.

what does taylor desseyn recommend if applying to hundreds of jobs isn't working?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says if you have applied to 500 jobs and only heard back from one company, you need to stop cold applying and change your approach. he calls the definition of insanity doing the same thing over and over and expecting different results. cold applying is just one tool in your toolkit, and you should spend less time mass applying and more time networking, sending direct messages, or trying to meet people for coffee.

how does taylor desseyn recommend getting comfortable with networking at events?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends bringing a friend or buddy to networking events and following them around until you warm up. he admits he is not naturally good at networking and needs someone with him at first events to feel comfortable. if you do not have a friend who works in tech, he suggests asking a non-technical friend to join you, even offering to buy them dinner in exchange for attending the meetup with you.
