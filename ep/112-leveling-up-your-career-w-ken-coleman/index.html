questions this episode answers

what does ken coleman say about changing jobs just for more money?

on guidance counselor 2.0, ken coleman warns that chasing a bigger paycheck without considering purpose will leave you disappointed. the good feeling from a raise wears off in about six to eight weeks, and then you're left with the same lack of fulfillment or worse, a toxic environment. he urges job seekers to chase purpose instead, which will naturally lead to better pay over time.

how does ken coleman define finding your professional purpose?

on guidance counselor 2.0, ken coleman defines professional purpose as using what you do best (talent) to do work you love (passion) to produce results that matter deeply to you (mission). when these three elements align, you're working on purpose and in your dream job. he emphasizes this is not mystical or only for lucky people, but a discoverable formula anyone can apply to multiple careers.

what does ken coleman say managers should do to retain employees right now?

on guidance counselor 2.0, ken coleman says leaders must make employees feel seen and known by asking two key questions regularly. first, ask how are you doing on a personal level, showing genuine interest in their family and life, not just a greeting. second, ask how can i help you win professionally. this personal and professional connection drives engagement and retention more than anything else.
