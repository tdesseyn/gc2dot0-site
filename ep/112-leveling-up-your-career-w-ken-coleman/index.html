leveling up your career w/ken coleman

guest: ken coleman, broadcaster, author, and career coach

  • don't change jobs just for a bigger paycheck since the good feeling fades in about six to eight weeks
  • find your purpose by matching what you do best with work you love and results that matter to you
  • as a leader your ability to grow is limited by the talent you bring onto your team
  • to retain people, check in on them personally and ask how you can help them win professionally
  • build soft skills like being likable, coachable, reliable, adaptable, and honorable to stand out in tech
