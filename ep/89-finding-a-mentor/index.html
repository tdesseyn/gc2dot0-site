questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about how to approach finding a mentor?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says you must go after mentorship yourself instead of waiting for a mentor to reach out to you. he recommends connecting with people you admire in your field on twitter and linkedin, then following, commenting on, and liking their posts before asking for anything. when you do reach out, be specific about what you want to learn rather than just asking someone to mentor you in general.

how does taylor desseyn recommend building rapport before asking for mentorship?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says you should offer something of value first so you end up in that person's ecosystem before asking for mentorship. he uses the example of andrew miller, who volunteered to help him set up his microphone at home, and now taylor feels grateful and willing to help andrew with his job search. taylor also suggests small gestures like sending virtual coffee or cookies when asking to meet.

what does taylor desseyn say about how to conduct mentorship meetings?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says you need to come prepared with good questions and drive the conversation yourself instead of expecting the mentor to lead it. he emphasizes that mentors do not have time to sit there and engage without direction, so you must bring the questions and topics. he warns against being the type of person who does not know what they want and expects the mentor to drive the conversation.
