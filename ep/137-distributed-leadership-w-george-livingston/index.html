ep 137 · may 9, 2022

distributed leadership w/ george livingston

guest: george livingston, leads product and software development at the escape game

  • build a culture of writing since distributed teams lose the easy in person context of a quick tap on the shoulder
  • carve out intentional unstructured time to replace water cooler moments that no longer happen by accident
  • bring distributed teams together in person when possible, even just a monthly lunch, to build real camaraderie
  • try structuring one on ones as a third chit chat, a third work talk, and a third how can i help
  • job seekers should follow up after interviews since strong written communication stands out in a distributed hiring process
