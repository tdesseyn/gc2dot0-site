too much powerpoint not enough code w/neil chaudhuri, president of vidya software

guest: neil chaudhuri, president of vidya software

  • developer productivity is nearly impossible to measure fairly because work happens in teams, not in isolation
  • leadership, not individual developers, is responsible for creating the conditions that let people focus and do good work
  • you dont have to be passionate about your job to be good at it, you just need an environment that lets you get satisfaction from doing it well
  • before going independent, make sure you have the skills, relationships, and safety net to cover healthcare and income if things dont work out
  • when hiring globally, think through time zone overlap, language, culture, and security practices before assuming it will save money
