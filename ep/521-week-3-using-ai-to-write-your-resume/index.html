week 3 + using ai to write your resume

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • start your resume summary broad then get specific, leading with years of experience before diving into scope
  • numbers are the biggest thing missing from most professional summaries so push ai to pressure test vague claims into real metrics
  • ask ai to interview you with questions about scope, budget, team size, and business impact before writing bullet points
  • use a project or dedicated workspace when job searching so ai keeps context and memory across your resume work
  • run an ats pressure check prompt on your resume to catch empty skills sections and missing keywords before you apply
