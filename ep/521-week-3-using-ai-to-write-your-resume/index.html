questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn recommend for structuring the professional summary section of a resume?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to start your professional summary really broad with total years of experience in your field, then get more specific with particular skills or focus areas. he emphasizes that numbers are the biggest missing element in most professional summaries, so you should push ai to turn vague claims into concrete metrics. for example, he starts with ten-plus years of staffing industry experience, then narrows to six-plus years of community building, then adds specific numbers like growing email sends from zero to 400,000 per month.

how does taylor desseyn suggest using ai to write better resume bullet points?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends asking ai to interview you with specific questions about your work before writing bullet points. he shows how claude asks questions about scope, budget size, team size, business impact, and what leadership actually cared about in reporting. this approach surfaces details you might not think to include on your own. he also suggests using the prompt pressure test frequently to find weaknesses and gaps in what you have written.

what ats check does taylor desseyn recommend running on your resume before applying?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to ask ai to pressure test your resume against applicant tracking systems to identify where you would get rejected. when he runs this check, claude flags an empty skills section as the biggest ats killer and points out missing keywords and vocabulary that job descriptions typically look for. the ai also notes when metrics are too marketing-focused instead of showing the specific outcomes the role requires, like member growth for community positions.
