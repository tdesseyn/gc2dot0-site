how to find a recruiter

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • search linkedin for technical recruiter plus your city to find relevant recruiters fast
  • check a recruiter's connection count and years of experience before reaching out, aim for at least two years in the industry
  • look for personality in a recruiter's profile, not just their title or company
  • when messaging a recruiter use flattery, a specific time ask like seven minutes, and one clear question
  • build relationships with recruiters before you need a job, then set reminders to check back in every few weeks

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about finding a technical recruiter on linkedin?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to search for technical recruiter plus your city in the linkedin search bar, then use filters to narrow results. he recommends looking for recruiters with over 500 connections and at least two years of recruiting experience. he also advises checking for personality in their profile, not just credentials or company name.

how should you message a recruiter according to taylor desseyn?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says your message should have three parts: flattery that shows you reviewed their profile, a specific odd time request like seven minutes rather than five, and one clear question. he recommends avoiding five minutes because people interpret that as fifteen minutes. he also emphasizes building relationships before you need a job and setting calendar reminders to check back in with recruiters every two to three weeks.
