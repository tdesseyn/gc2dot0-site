ep 72 · aug 25, 2021

why finding your why is important

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • write down your why on pen and paper before accepting any job offer
  • if an offer comes in fast, tell the company you are flattered but still interviewing and ask for a deadline
  • recruiters respect candidates who communicate honestly about continuing their search
  • know what you want to fix in your next job, like pay or promotion track, before you jump
  • few people stay at one company for ten years anymore, so dont put too much pressure on a single decision

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say to do before accepting a job offer?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to turn off all devices, sit down with pen and paper, and write down what you like about your current job, what you don't like, and what you want in your next role. he emphasizes pen and paper specifically, not typing on a phone or computer. this helps you understand your why before making a decision.

what should you tell a company if you get an offer very quickly according to taylor desseyn?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to tell the company you are flattered but just started your job search and are still interviewing other opportunities. ask them how long you have before you need to accept the offer. taylor says recruiters actually appreciate this honesty and direct communication rather than being strung along.

why does taylor desseyn say not to put too much pressure on a single job decision?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says the days of staying at one company for ten years are gone, and most people will only stay two to five years at a position. he tells job seekers to take the decision seriously but realize if a position doesn't work out after a year or two, you can move on. it's a big career step but not as permanent as people make it out to be.
