you're fired.

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • when you're unexpectedly job hunting, start by rebuilding your resume since most people haven't touched it since college
  • skip the one page resume rule if you have real experience, longer resumes can work for people with more years in the field
  • make sure your resume has enough detail tied to the job description or employers will pass on it
  • cut soft skills language like works well with others and replace it with quantitative data about your results
  • use specific numbers like sales figures or revenue generated to show impact instead of vague job duties

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about the one page resume rule?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to ignore the one page resume rule if you have professional experience. he suggests the number of years you've worked should roughly match the number of pages, so two years means two pages, three years means three pages, up to about five pages for seven plus years. employers need enough specific information tied to the job description or they will pass on your resume.
