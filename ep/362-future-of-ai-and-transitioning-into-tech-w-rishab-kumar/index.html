ep 362 · may 3, 2024

future of ai and transitioning into tech w/ rishab kumar staff eng twilio

guest: rishab kumar, staff engineer, twilio

  • treat unpaid internships as a strategic move if they clearly lead toward a full time job offer
  • go beyond assigned tasks during an internship to build trust and get included in bigger projects
  • nail interview fundamentals like networking and system design since big tech cares more about core knowledge than specific tools
  • practice soft skills by recording yourself talking through technical topics and reviewing the tapes over time
  • when reaching out for a job, skip the fluff and directly explain how your skills solve the company's specific problem

questions this episode answers

what does rishab kumar recommend for improving soft skills in tech?

on guidance counselor 2.0, rishab kumar, staff engineer at twilio, recommends recording yourself speaking about technical topics and then rewatching the tapes to identify where you stutter or leave long silences. he says practice is the only way to get better, whether through mock interviews with friends or just explaining concepts to non-technical people to see if they understand. he points to his own old videos from 2020 as proof that consistent practice leads to fluency over time.
