job search tips (solo)

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • stop chasing a dream job and instead look for the right job for the season of life you are in
  • build name recognition by consistently commenting and engaging on posts from people and companies you want to work with
  • being present and consistent at events like meetups or conferences counts as networking even without talking to people
  • try messaging people on sites where they have fewer followers since you are more likely to get a response
  • be fearless and message that hiring manager, recruiter, or senior developer you have been hesitant to reach out to
