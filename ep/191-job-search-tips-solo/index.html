job search tips (solo)

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • stop chasing a dream job and instead look for the right job for the season of life you are in
  • build name recognition by consistently commenting and engaging on posts from people and companies you want to work with
  • being present and consistent at events like meetups or conferences counts as networking even without talking to people
  • try messaging people on sites where they have fewer followers since you are more likely to get a response
  • be fearless and message that hiring manager, recruiter, or senior developer you have been hesitant to reach out to

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about finding a dream job?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says you should stop putting pressure on yourself to find your dream job and instead focus on finding the right job for the season of life you are in. he explains that what works when you can work 12-hour days may not work when you have kids or other life priorities. he believes there is no single dream job, only a dream job for your current season.

how does taylor desseyn recommend building name recognition during a job search?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn says you should comment and engage consistently on linkedin posts from people and companies you want to work with to build name recognition. he points out that he feels like he knows people he's never met in person because they regularly comment on his content. he emphasizes that liking and commenting on posts counts as networking in 2022.

what does taylor desseyn say about attending events without talking to people?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says that sometimes just being present and consistent at code conferences and meetups is networking enough, even without talking to people. he explains that you should go to the same conference every year in your city or the same meetup every time, and showing up consistently counts. he compares it to how he should have planted himself at music venues in nashville when he was a musician.
