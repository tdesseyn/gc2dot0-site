ep 126 · mar 16, 2022

a tale of hiring, devrel, and various other things w/ ted neward

guest: ted neward, technologist and author

  • look past checklist hiring and judge candidates by depth and transferable skills, not just exact tech matches
  • a cs degree is not required to succeed in tech, so don't let a job post requiring one stop you from applying
  • build your network and personal brand through local user groups, volunteering, and teaching, not just conferences
  • don't take job rejection personally since most of the time it comes down to fit or circumstances outside your control
  • when hiring juniors, pair them with a strong mentor, build in psychological safety, and invest time before expecting full productivity
