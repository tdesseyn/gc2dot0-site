ep 35 · mar 26, 2021

w/austin schlessinger (austinschless) & leveraging twitter to network

guest: austin schlessinger, strategy consultant and twitter content creator

  • use twitter threads instead of single tweets so people can engage at any point in the story instead of waiting until the end
  • build a content habit by writing short essays or threads consistently to develop both a writing skill and a visible portfolio of ideas
  • when starting out on twitter, define your core principles, share examples of people doing it well, then tell stories around those ideas
  • use twitter mainly to test ideas and network, since small but engaged niche communities matter more than follower count
  • use linkedin for job searching but turn to twitter if you want to learn content creation, join startups, or build an online audience

questions this episode answers

how does austin schlessinger recommend someone start creating content on twitter?

on guidance counselor 2.0, austin schlessinger recommends starting by defining your core principles in your area of interest, such as writing newsletters or starting podcasts if you care about the creator economy. next, give examples of successful people doing those things well. then tell stories around those principles and examples, embedding them into future threads you write.

what does austin schlessinger say about twitter versus linkedin for job searching?

on guidance counselor 2.0, austin schlessinger says if someone wants to find a traditional job, they should use linkedin because that is where those people and opportunities exist. however, if someone wants to learn content creation, join startups, or make money as a creator online, twitter is the better choice. he used linkedin to get his consulting job but now sees twitter as the superior networking tool for testing ideas and finding niche communities.
