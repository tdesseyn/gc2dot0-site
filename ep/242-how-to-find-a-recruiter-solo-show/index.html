ep 242 · apr 12, 2023

how to find a recruiter (solo show)

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • stop mass applying to job posts and instead message recruiters directly on linkedin to build real connections
  • search tech recruiters plus your city on linkedin and use the filters feature to narrow results
  • look for recruiters with at least two years in the industry and two years at their current company
  • when a recruiter cant help you directly ask for a quick favor like resume or linkedin feedback
  • if you find a job posting message a recruiter you know and ask if they can refer you or know someone there

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about applying to job postings versus messaging recruiters?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says job seekers should stop spamming their resume to online job posts and instead spam recruiters with calls and connections. he explains that if your resume doesn't arrive within the first 20 to 25 applicants on a linkedin job post, a hiring manager probably won't see it. recruiters know the market better than any hiring manager, tech labor report, or survey, so starting with recruiter conversations gives you better odds.

what should you ask a recruiter who can't help you directly, according to taylor desseyn?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says if a recruiter can't help you directly, ask for one quick favor like feedback on your resume or linkedin profile. he explains that if you talk to 10 to 15 recruiters and get 15 pieces of advice, that feedback can catapult you from no one looking at your materials to everyone looking. he also suggests asking where you should interview, where to work, which places to avoid, or the best way to navigate a specific company.
