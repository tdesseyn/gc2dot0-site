the mastermind of networking & community w/ christiaan heijmen

what christiaan heijmen covers in this episode

christiaan heijmen is an executive recruiter who has interviewed people for a living for about 15 years, by his count tens of thousands of interviews. he was on the interview team that hired taylor over a decade ago. he's also a dad of four and co-founded a business in the raleigh-durham area focused on community building with a real cross section of people. his line on that: if you're not intentional about diversifying your life, you're unintentional about not. this episode splits into two halves, interviewing and networking.

don't let the zoom link sink you

christiaan says he can't count how many tech candidates text him that the interview link didn't work. he's put that person forward for a cto or cio job, and now the ceo is asking how they can't get a video call working. his advice for tech folks: be on time, do your homework on the ceo and the company, check the link, show up five minutes early, and make sure your camera works. otherwise, he says, they won't even hear your experience. he notices that sales leaders warm up a room quickly, while many it people jump straight to what they've done. make the conversation easy first.

use your recruiter for real-time feedback

taylor asks about candidates who can't explain what they've actually done. christiaan's tip is to ask the person screening you directly: is the way i'm explaining my transitions making sense? he'll stop an interview himself when a career path doesn't add up, but many recruiters will just keep typing. the recruiter has a role to fill and wants to present a strong candidate, so you're on the same team.

that's also why he's surprised when candidates won't share their compensation. he wants to get them the best offer he can, and the client doesn't win if the candidate isn't excited about the package. he notes he works in a state where he can't ask for pay history, so when someone shares where they are and where they'd like to be, it's a sign of trust. the more he knows, the better he can advocate.

work yourself out of the connection

christiaan's networking philosophy is to do more for others and trust it comes back. a friend once told him he's a master at replacing himself as the connection point. instead of staying the link between two people, he introduces them and steps out. he compares it to a lego base plate: some pieces stay, the rest get to go build something. he admits this can feel scary when you're job searching and want to hold on to every connection, but he says it's how a network grows.

bring your own energy, and let others bring theirs

on interviews, christiaan's mindset has shifted from asking whether someone fits to asking what they'll add. he cites the common idea that most of an interview's impression is nonverbal: how you sit, how you lean in. get a good night's sleep, take a walk, and bring your authentic self. he's also learned that not everyone has to match his high energy to be a great fit. some of the best people he knows are introverted and level, and they deserve to be presented just as well.

questions this episode answers

what does christiaan heijmen say about technical issues during virtual interviews?

on guidance counselor 2.0, christiaan heijmen says candidates lose credibility when they can't manage basic tech setup for interviews. he notes that ceos question how a cto or cio candidate can handle the role if they text about zoom link problems or show up late. he recommends checking links early, showing up five minutes before, and ensuring camera and audio work to avoid creating barriers before the real conversation starts.

how does christiaan heijmen recommend using recruiters during the interview process?

on guidance counselor 2.0, christiaan heijmen recommends asking your recruiter for real-time feedback during screening calls. he suggests stopping to ask if your explanation of career transitions makes sense, since recruiters want to present strong candidates and are on your team. he also advises being transparent about compensation so recruiters can advocate better for you, noting that he can negotiate more effectively when candidates share their salary goals and personal circumstances.

what networking strategy does christiaan heijmen use to stay connected with thousands of contacts?

on guidance counselor 2.0, christiaan heijmen says he connects people directly to each other instead of staying as the middleman between them. he calls this working himself out of a connection, which lets his network grow organically while he keeps core relationships. he shares that commenting authentically on linkedin posts keeps him visible to people he cares about, and this habit has generated millions in business simply by showing genuine interest in others' milestones.
