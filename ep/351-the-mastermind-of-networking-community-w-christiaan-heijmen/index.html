questions this episode answers

what does christiaan heijmen say about technical issues during virtual interviews?

on guidance counselor 2.0, christiaan heijmen says candidates lose credibility when they can't manage basic tech setup for interviews. he notes that ceos question how a cto or cio candidate can handle the role if they text about zoom link problems or show up late. he recommends checking links early, showing up five minutes before, and ensuring camera and audio work to avoid creating barriers before the real conversation starts.

how does christiaan heijmen recommend using recruiters during the interview process?

on guidance counselor 2.0, christiaan heijmen recommends asking your recruiter for real-time feedback during screening calls. he suggests stopping to ask if your explanation of career transitions makes sense, since recruiters want to present strong candidates and are on your team. he also advises being transparent about compensation so recruiters can advocate better for you, noting that he can negotiate more effectively when candidates share their salary goals and personal circumstances.

what networking strategy does christiaan heijmen use to stay connected with thousands of contacts?

on guidance counselor 2.0, christiaan heijmen says he connects people directly to each other instead of staying as the middleman between them. he calls this working himself out of a connection, which lets his network grow organically while he keeps core relationships. he shares that commenting authentically on linkedin posts keeps him visible to people he cares about, and this habit has generated millions in business simply by showing genuine interest in others' milestones.
