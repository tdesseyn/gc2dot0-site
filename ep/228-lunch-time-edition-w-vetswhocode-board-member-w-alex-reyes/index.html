lunch time edition w/vetswhocode board member w/alex reyes, mba, who is an inclusive recruiting strategist @ meta

guest: alex reyes, inclusive recruiting strategist at meta

  • be intentional about your next role and highlight your real skills on your resume, linkedin, and in interviews
  • use impact bullets on your resume that show what you made, saved, or achieved at each position
  • if you were laid off be upfront and honest about it, then focus the conversation on your contributions and performance
  • before applying check linkedin for mutual connections and ask if they are comfortable being your referral
  • keep in touch with recruiters over time by engaging with their content or checking in every quarter
