gun.io mini series w/ victoria stahr, talent growth

guest: victoria stahr, talent growth, gun.io

  • fill out your profile with real detail since good data in means better job matches out
  • most rejections come down to years of experience or not having a focused, consistent tech background
  • being a generalist is valuable in life but clients hiring for contract work want proven consistency in specific skill areas
  • contract roles typically run three to six months or longer, with some engagements lasting well over a year
  • start contributing to a 401k early, especially if your employer matches, even if it feels unnecessary when you're young

questions this episode answers

what does victoria stahr say about filling out a gun.io profile?

on guidance counselor 2.0, victoria stahr from gun.io says the more context you provide in your profile, the better they can understand your work and match you with client expectations. she emphasizes listing specific tasks and impact within previous environments rather than high-level or generic information. clients look for consistency in how you have used technologies, not just that you have used them.

what does victoria stahr say about being a generalist when applying to gun.io?

on guidance counselor 2.0, victoria stahr from gun.io says that while being a generalist is valuable, it does not work well for contract hiring because clients are looking for consistency within certain areas. she explains that most contract roles do not have ramp-up time built in, so clients want proven expertise in specific skill areas rather than broad experience.

what career advice does victoria stahr give to her younger self?

on guidance counselor 2.0, victoria stahr says her biggest career advice to younger victoria would be to opt into a 401k, especially one that is matched. she notes this is not something covered in high school or college, and when you are young and making money you want to spend it, but later you realize it is good to save it somewhere you cannot reach.
