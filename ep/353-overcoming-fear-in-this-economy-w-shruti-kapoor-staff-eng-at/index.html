ep 353 · apr 12, 2024

overcoming fear in this economy w/ shruti kapoor, staff eng at slack

guest: shruti kapoor, staff engineer at slack

  • start building your network inside your company before layoffs hit, then move those connections to linkedin
  • ask coworkers for written or verbal recommendations now so you have them ready for future interviews
  • prepare for front end interviews by studying state management, css, and interactivity instead of pure leetcode style questions
  • ask recruiters whether an interview will focus on data structures and cs algorithms or practical javascript so you know how to prepare
  • update your resume and collect project details now so you are not writing it emotionally after a layoff
