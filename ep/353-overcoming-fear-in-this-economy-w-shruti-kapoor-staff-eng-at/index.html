overcoming fear in this economy w/ shruti kapoor, staff eng at slack

guest: shruti kapoor, staff engineer at slack

  • start building your network inside your company before layoffs hit, then move those connections to linkedin
  • ask coworkers for written or verbal recommendations now so you have them ready for future interviews
  • prepare for front end interviews by studying state management, css, and interactivity instead of pure leetcode style questions

questions this episode answers

what does shruti kapoor recommend doing before layoffs hit at your company?

on guidance counselor 2.0, shruti kapoor says you should start building your network inside your company before layoffs happen by adding coworkers on linkedin. she recommends asking for written recommendations through linkedin's feature or verbal recommendations you can write down to use later. preparing your resume and collecting project details now prevents you from writing it emotionally after a layoff when you're anxious.

how should a front end engineer prepare for interviews according to shruti kapoor?

on guidance counselor 2.0, shruti kapoor says front end interviews focus on state management, css and interactivity rather than pure leetcode questions. she recommends asking recruiters whether the interview will be data structures and cs algorithms or practical javascript so you know how to prepare. the themes in front end are so consistent that preparing for those specific areas will make you feel confident.
