a letter to recruiters (solo show)

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • recruiters should show up at local tech conferences in their own city, not just work job boards all day
  • you do not need to travel the country, just attend events in your hometown to start building relationships
  • if your company will not pay for a conference, ask to volunteer so you can still get in the door
  • after meeting someone, connect on their most active social channel right away instead of relying on a business card
  • keep networking simple, introduce yourself, ask what they do, trade contact info, and move on

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say recruiters should do instead of only working job boards?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says recruiters need to attend tech conferences in their own cities, not fly around the country. he argues that recruiting requires a two-sided approach: filling current positions through job boards while building long-term relationships at local conferences. taylor observed that over six days at two conferences, only one recruiter attended, and he believes recruiters must play the long game because relationships will pay back five to ten fold in future years.

how does taylor desseyn say to network at conferences if your company won't pay?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn says if your company will not pay for conference attendance, you should volunteer at the event to get in the door. he mentions that volunteering is a practical way to gain access and start building relationships even without company funding. taylor emphasizes that attending conferences is essential and volunteering removes the financial barrier.

what does taylor desseyn recommend doing immediately after meeting someone at a conference?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 episode, taylor desseyn recommends connecting with people on their most active social channel right away instead of relying on business cards. he says to ask what their preferred channel is, connect on linkedin or instagram immediately, and then move on. taylor admits he threw away business cards in the past and missed relationships because he waited until after the conference, so he now uses the linkedin qr code feature on his phone to connect instantly.
