ep 218 · feb 10, 2023

speaking to the pain & not apologizing w/jean leggett who is an amazing coach/speaker/resume writer

guest: jean leggett, career coach, speaker, and resume writer

  • build your resume around the hiring manager's pain points by pulling language straight from the job posting
  • group your resume into clear pillars like validation, impact, and people skills instead of a plain chronological list
  • use a cover letter to personalize your pitch since it probably won't win the job but can win you an interview
  • prep for interviews by reading each resume bullet aloud and making sure you can explain the story behind it
  • stop apologizing for your achievements on a resume, this is the one place you're supposed to brag
