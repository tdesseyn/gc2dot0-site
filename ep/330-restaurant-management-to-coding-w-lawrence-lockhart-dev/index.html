questions this episode answers

what does lawrence lockhart say about learning to code without a mentor?

on guidance counselor 2.0, lawrence lockhart says having a human mentor or subject matter expert you can refer to is invaluable when learning to code, far more helpful than just googling or using chat gpt. he spent two and a half years trying to self-teach without support, quitting and restarting multiple times because he had no one to ask why his code wasn't working. he now tells beginners to attach themselves to a community, meetup group, or person from lesson one to avoid wasted years.

should junior developers go to tech meetups before they have skills, according to lawrence lockhart?

on guidance counselor 2.0, lawrence lockhart says junior developers should absolutely attend meetups from day one, even before building deep technical skills. you should go to learn, listen, and observe rather than compete, ask open-ended questions about the speakers and their work, and offer to help clean up at the end to show you're useful. he explains that showing up consistently lets you build your personal brand as a technologist before you even have much code to show, which helps when you reach the interview stage.

what happened to lawrence lockhart's career after he started posting tech content on tiktok?

on guidance counselor 2.0, lawrence lockhart says posting tech content on tiktok led scott hanselman from microsoft to recognize him at render atl, which led to an all-expenses-paid invite to speak at the massive microsoft build conference in seattle. shortly after, anthony mayes from google invited him to speak at render atl, also expenses paid. these speaking opportunities, combined with his content creation and software development experience, directly boosted his candidacy to become a developer advocate at vaadin.
