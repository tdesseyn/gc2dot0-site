ep 322 · jan 22, 2024

engineering leader on the job search w/ dan digangi, engineering manager

guest: dan digangi, senior engineering manager

  • expect the application process across different job sites and logins to be frustrating, and plan extra time for it
  • if your usual network isn't producing leads right now, consider expanding it or approaching it differently instead of just mass applying
  • avoid blasting your resume out hundreds of times, focus instead on real conversations with connections and connections of connections
  • when emailing people to schedule time, give two to three specific days with clear time blocks so they can pick easily
  • in interviews, ask what success looks like in the first 30, 60, and 90 days to show you're focused on outcomes
