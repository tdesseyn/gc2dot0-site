ep 58 · jul 23, 2021

w/josh carroll + working for free

guest: josh carroll, cio at tag resources

  • build one project from start to finish instead of following a tutorial, so you can explain every decision you made
  • hiring managers sift through hundreds of resumes fast, so a generic bootcamp github project blends in and gets skipped
  • record a short video walking through your side project and post it on linkedin or twitter to stand out
  • putting in a little extra time consistently compounds over months into a much bigger skill gap than you expect
  • skip fancy colored resume templates since they slow hiring managers down and rarely contain better information
