questions this episode answers

what does taylor poindexter say about using your network during a job search?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor poindexter, engineering manager at spotify, says to use your network because there are so many people in the market right now and having that introduction can push you to the front of the line. she also recommends thinking about products you love and checking their competitors on sites like g2.com to find more roles. instead of focusing only on fang companies, remember that many non-tech companies are hiring software engineers and tech related roles.

how does taylor poindexter recommend approaching a hiring manager about an open role?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor poindexter, engineering manager at spotify, says the best way is to point her to the specific role you think you would be a good fit for and then tell her exactly why you think you would be a good fit for it, including the skills you could bring. she wants you to seem engaging and genuinely interested, not like you are just spraying and praying with minimal effort. if you have been consistently engaging with her on social media for months, that also helps when you apply.

what does taylor poindexter say about taking breaks during a job search?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor poindexter, engineering manager at spotify, says to be gentle with yourself because job hunting is exhausting and sometimes you will interview better if you give yourself a little bit of breathing room after a rejection. she compares it to driving while tired, explaining that when you interview you want to show your best self and taking time to rest can catapult you further than trudging through and potentially burning out. she also reminds people that as a hiring manager, she wants to hire you and it is okay to admit in an interview that you are nervous.
