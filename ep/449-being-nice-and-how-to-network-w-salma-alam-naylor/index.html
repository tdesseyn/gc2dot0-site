questions this episode answers

what does salma alam-naylor say about networking for a tech job?

on guidance counselor 2.0, salma alam-naylor says to treat networking as making friends rather than using corporate networking language, because the term sounds transactional and off-putting. she explains that real connections take time to build, just like any relationship, and you need to nurture them slowly instead of expecting instant results. she emphasizes that cold messaging people with job requests or open-ended questions like how do i get started without doing your own research first will make people ignore you.

what does salma alam-naylor recommend about admitting you don't know something at work?

on guidance counselor 2.0, salma alam-naylor says that saying you don't know something is actually a superpower because it removes tension and puts everyone on the same level. she points out that pretending to know everything is a confidence issue where people think they'll seem lesser if they admit gaps in knowledge. she argues that being honest about not knowing builds trust faster than faking expertise.

how long does salma alam-naylor say it takes to become a web developer?

on guidance counselor 2.0, salma alam-naylor says you cannot become a web developer in six, ten or twelve weeks because human brains don't learn that way. she explains that people asking how to earn six figures as fast as possible are chasing an impossible goal, and anyone claiming overnight success either did hidden work beforehand or is lying for engagement. she stresses that real careers require time investment in learning fundamentals, and trying to rush the process leads to a shallow skill set.
