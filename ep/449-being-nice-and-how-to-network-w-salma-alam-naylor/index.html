ep 449 · may 2, 2025

being nice and how to network w/ salma alam-naylor

guest: salma alam-naylor, head of developer education at nordcraft

  • treat networking as making friends, not a transaction, and build relationships slowly over time
  • admitting you dont know something builds trust faster than pretending to know everything
  • share your real life and vulnerabilities online, not just work content, to connect with people
  • avoid chasing follower counts or engagement, since real opportunities come from genuine connections
  • invest time in learning fundamentals instead of rushing to land a job in a few weeks
