blake lower + the hustle of freelancing

guest: blake lower, freelance web developer and founder of a freelance company

  • treat goal setting as an honest conversation with yourself about what you actually want, not generic promises
  • use facebook groups and meetup.com to find clients, introduce yourself directly and get on a call fast
  • offer a smaller discounted scope first to build trust instead of overpromising on a big budget
  • document your journey publicly on twitter or blogging to build a following and bring in freelance leads
  • expect rejection in outreach, keep sending messages and follow ups, and move on quickly when someone goes quiet

questions this episode answers

what does blake lower say about setting goals that stick?

on guidance counselor 2.0, blake lower says goal setting works when you have an honest conversation with yourself about what you truly want, not just generic promises. she says if you make goals just for the sake of it, you will feel resentful because you are making false promises to yourself. she emphasizes asking yourself deep questions about what you want your end result to be and where you see yourself.

what does blake lower recommend about pricing and scope for new freelancers?

on guidance counselor 2.0, blake lower says she offers a much smaller, discounted scope first rather than taking a client's full budget right away. when a client offered 1800 dollars for facebook ads, she suggested starting with just 300 dollars to build trust and scale up from there. she says this approach feels more authentic and prevents overpromising, and she researches what other people charge on google, then takes a few hundred dollars off when starting out.
