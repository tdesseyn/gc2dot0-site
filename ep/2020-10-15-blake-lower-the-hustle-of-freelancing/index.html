blake lower + the hustle of freelancing

guest: blake lower, freelance web developer and founder of a freelance company

  • treat goal setting as an honest conversation with yourself about what you actually want, not generic promises
  • use facebook groups and meetup.com to find clients, introduce yourself directly and get on a call fast
  • offer a smaller discounted scope first to build trust instead of overpromising on a big budget
  • document your journey publicly on twitter or blogging to build a following and bring in freelance leads
  • expect rejection in outreach, keep sending messages and follow ups, and move on quickly when someone goes quiet
