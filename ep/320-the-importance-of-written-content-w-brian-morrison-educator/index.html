ep 320 · jan 17, 2024

the importance of written content w/ brian morrison, educator at planetscale

guest: brian morrison, educator at planetscale

  • written content is often quicker to produce than video since you skip scripting, filming, and editing steps
  • pick a content format based on how you want to consume it, reading is faster for learning while video works well as background absorption
  • you dont have to start your tech career as a developer, networking and internal relationships can bridge you from it or other roles into software
  • look for tasks you can automate or problems you can solve at any job, that extra effort builds reputation with management over time
  • at conferences focus on meeting people over attending every session, keep introductions short and let the other person talk more than you do
