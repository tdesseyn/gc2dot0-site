ep 320 · jan 17, 2024

the importance of written content w/ brian morrison, educator at planetscale

guest: brian morrison, educator at planetscale

  • written content is often quicker to produce than video since you skip scripting, filming, and editing steps
  • pick a content format based on how you want to consume it, reading is faster for learning while video works well as background absorption
  • you dont have to start your tech career as a developer, networking and internal relationships can bridge you from it or other roles into software
  • look for tasks you can automate or problems you can solve at any job, that extra effort builds reputation with management over time
  • at conferences focus on meeting people over attending every session, keep introductions short and let the other person talk more than you do

questions this episode answers

what does brian morrison say about getting into software development from an it role?

on guidance counselor 2.0, brian morrison, educator at planetscale, says he moved from it help desk to developer by looking for tasks to automate at every job. he wrote scripts and programs that saved his company over a hundred thousand dollars in billable hours per year, which built his reputation with management. when an api developer position opened, his boss offered it to him because of the coding work he had already demonstrated.

how does brian morrison recommend networking at tech conferences?

on guidance counselor 2.0, brian morrison says the value of conferences is not the sessions but meeting people in person. he recommends sitting with strangers at lunch, introducing yourself briefly, and then letting the other person talk more than you do. he believes listening is more valuable than talking and that asking people to dig deeper into what they say helps build lasting connections beyond any specific technology.

what does brian morrison say about written content versus video content creation?

on guidance counselor 2.0, brian morrison says written content is faster to produce than video because you skip scripting, filming and editing steps. he can brainstorm an idea while cooking breakfast and publish a decent article within an hour. he also notes that written content lets you disconnect from your desk and write notes at a cafe, while video requires being tied to a specific location and screen.
