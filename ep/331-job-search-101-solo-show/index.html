ep 331 · feb 12, 2024

job search 101 + solo show

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • if you have to take a customer service job to pay the bills, keep coding or building on the side so you have something to show later
  • attention to detail matters more than ever, a typo or small mistake can get you rejected right now
  • if you cannot land a technical role, look at customer success, marketing, or support roles inside tech companies as a way in
  • before an interview, actually use the company's product or tool so you can speak to it instead of just talking about your own background
  • follow up matters a lot, checking back in a week instead of waiting a month helped taylor turn a stalled offer into a job
