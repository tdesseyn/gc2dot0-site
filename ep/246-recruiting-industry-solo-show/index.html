questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about chat gpt replacing recruiters?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says he doesn't believe chat gpt will replace the recruiting industry because relationships between people drive hiring. he thinks chat gpt will help automate manual processes in staffing and be integrated into certain areas of the work, but the core relationship-building aspect of recruiting cannot be replaced. he compares recruiters to football coaches who succeed by understanding the game without necessarily playing at the highest level themselves.

how does taylor desseyn recommend using career fairs for networking?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn says in-person career fairs are better than virtual ones, but they require substantial follow-up work. he recommends collecting business cards or connecting on linkedin with every booth person during the fair, then messaging everyone afterward to schedule 15-minute meetings. he explains that recruiters at career fairs meet hundreds or thousands of people and won't remember you, so the real value comes from following up online and getting on zoom calls after the event.

what does taylor say about networking when you're unemployed and job searching?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says if you're unemployed you should treat job searching as a full-time job and fill your entire eight to five schedule with networking activities. he recommends having at least one new conversation with someone every day, joining slack and discord communities, attending live events, and being consistently engaged. he emphasizes that your next job will most likely come from someone already in your network or phone contacts.
