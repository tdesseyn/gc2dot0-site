recruiting industry (solo show)

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • expect chat gpt to assist recruiting work rather than replace the industry since relationships still drive hiring
  • if you are stuck job searching, aim to have one new networking conversation every day
  • treat the job search like a full time job and fill your schedule with networking when unemployed
  • at career fairs collect contacts and connect on linkedin, then follow up with a short call afterward since people won't remember you otherwise
  • keep a short cover letter that highlights your career milestones even though many hiring managers skip reading them
