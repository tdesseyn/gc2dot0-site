the winding road w/ leslie greenwood, co-founder of women wednesday

guest: leslie greenwood, co-founder of women wednesday

  • start networking two years before you need a new job, not when you're already searching
  • before quitting a steady role, write an ideal life profile and build work around it instead of the reverse
  • pick one channel where your actual audience spends time instead of spreading yourself across every social app
  • people do business with people they trust, not with a company page, so build rapport and show up as yourself
  • your career path can zigzag through layoffs and side steps and still lead somewhere good, so walk through open doors when you see them
