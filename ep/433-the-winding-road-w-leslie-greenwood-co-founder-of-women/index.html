questions this episode answers

what does leslie greenwood say about starting to network before you need a job?

on guidance counselor 2.0, leslie greenwood says if you start networking when you are looking for a job you are already too late. she recommends starting two years before you need a new role because almost everyone will need a new job within two to five years. waiting until you are actively searching puts you at a significant disadvantage instead of starting from a position of strength.

how did leslie greenwood decide to leave her steady job at pavilion and go out on her own?

on guidance counselor 2.0, leslie greenwood says a mentor told her to create an ideal life profile where she wrote down what she wanted her life to be first, then worked on finding a job to fit into that life instead of fitting her life around work. she realized she had always done the reverse and this shift in thinking led her to consulting. she also discovered she is unemployable in structured environments because she thrives in early stage scrappy roles but pushes against structure once companies mature.

what does leslie greenwood say about choosing which social media channel to focus on?

on guidance counselor 2.0, leslie greenwood says she wasted time trying to be on tiktok and twitter when her actual audience of go to market leaders was only on linkedin. she recommends finding the one channel where your specific audience spends time instead of spreading yourself across every option. she also emphasizes that people do business with individuals they trust, not company pages, so she stopped posting on her company page and focused on building personal rapport.
