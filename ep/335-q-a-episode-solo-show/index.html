ep 335 · feb 21, 2024

q&a episode (solo show)

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • if you are a front end heavy developer, start learning back end skills since back end talent is harder to find right now
  • languages like python and java are in high demand for data processing, and messaging tools like kafka and rabbit mq are hot skills to pick up
  • if someone does not reply to your first message, follow up again since recruiters often need two or three messages before responding
  • networking does not require meeting everyone at an event, picking one or two people to intentionally connect with is enough
  • record a short selfie style video introducing yourself and add it to the featured section on linkedin to stand out to recruiters
