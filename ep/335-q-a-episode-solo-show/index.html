questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about learning back-end development skills right now?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says if you are a front-end heavy developer, you should start learning back-end skills because there is a shortage of back-end developers. recruiters struggle to find python, java, and ruby developers, while too many front-end developers flood the market. he notes that data processing roles favor java, and messaging tools like kafka and rabbit mq are extremely hot skills right now.

how many times should you message a recruiter before they respond, according to taylor desseyn?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says you should expect to send two or three messages before a recruiter responds. he explains that if someone sends only one message, it will get buried at the bottom of his inbox because he does not have time to respond immediately. two messages make him realize he should respond soon, and three messages make him feel he must follow up.

what does taylor desseyn recommend about networking at meetups and conferences?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says you do not need to meet everyone at a networking event. he recommends picking just one to three people to intentionally connect with, which is enough to get value from attending. he shares that he drove to a meetup only to shake hands with one speaker, josh long, and that single intentional connection was worthwhile even though he wanted to stay home.
