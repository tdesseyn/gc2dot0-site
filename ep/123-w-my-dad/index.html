ep 123 · mar 9, 2022

w/my dad

guest: dave desseyn, taylor's father, restaurant industry manager

  • when starting a new job, look at what needs to be done and just start doing it without waiting to be told
  • if you leave a long career to find something new, give yourself time to figure out if you miss the work itself or just the people
  • in interviews, show your character and integrity instead of letting the interviewer do all the talking
  • as a leader, check in on people's lives outside of work, not just their tasks, so they feel a real connection
  • when someone makes a mistake, want ownership and a plan to improve rather than excuses or blame

questions this episode answers

what does dave desseyn say about making a good first impression when starting a new job?

on guidance counselor 2.0, dave desseyn says his father told him that when you start a job, just start doing whatever you see needs to be done without waiting to be told. he explains you will quickly find out where your boundaries are because people will tell you when you've gone too far. the key is not to be the person who has to be told what to do.

what does dave desseyn say to show in a job interview beyond just your experience?

on guidance counselor 2.0, dave desseyn says you have to show your character and integrity during an interview to stand out. he explains that when he interviewed for his first restaurant job with only two years of college, he told them whatever he does will be the best they've ever seen, even if it's cleaning toilets. he warns that if the interviewer is doing all the talking, it's going to be tough for you to get the job.
