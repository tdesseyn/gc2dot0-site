w/my dad

guest: dave desseyn, taylor's father, restaurant industry manager

  • when starting a new job, look at what needs to be done and just start doing it without waiting to be told
  • if you leave a long career to find something new, give yourself time to figure out if you miss the work itself or just the people
  • in interviews, show your character and integrity instead of letting the interviewer do all the talking
  • as a leader, check in on people's lives outside of work, not just their tasks, so they feel a real connection
  • when someone makes a mistake, want ownership and a plan to improve rather than excuses or blame
