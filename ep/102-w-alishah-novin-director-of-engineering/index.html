questions this episode answers

what does alishah novin recommend instead of take-home coding tests?

on guidance counselor 2.0, alishah novin recommends asking candidates to walk through a project they actually built rather than giving take-home assessments. he suggests asking what they were trying to build, what problems they ran into, and what they would change if they did it again. he finds this conversational approach reveals more about how someone thinks and works than watching them complete an artificial coding challenge.

how should developers structure their resume according to alishah novin?

on guidance counselor 2.0, alishah novin says to put your programming languages and tools at the top of your resume, followed by quantifiable experience. he recommends avoiding vague skill ratings like javascript four out of five because reviewers have no idea what your scale means. in the summary, start general and get specific, like ten years overall experience, seven in development, three in a specific technology, so hiring managers immediately know what you do.

should developers include non-tech jobs on their resume?

on guidance counselor 2.0, alishah novin says to include non-tech jobs like customer service or serving if the skills are relevant to the coding role. he explains that some coding positions are customer facing, so experience as a waiter or in customer service provides valuable transferable skills regardless of what others might think.
