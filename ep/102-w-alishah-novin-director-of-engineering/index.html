ep 102 · jan 17, 2022

w/alishah novin, director of engineering

guest: alishah novin, director of engineering at salero commerce

  • skip take home coding tests and instead ask candidates to walk through a project they actually built
  • in interviews ask what you were trying to build, what went wrong, and what you would change next time
  • resumes still matter so put your programming languages and tools at the top followed by quantifiable experience
  • avoid vague skill ratings like javascript four out of five since reviewers have no idea what your scale means
  • include non tech jobs like customer service or serving on your resume if the skills are relevant to the role
