questions this episode answers

what does david messinger say about asking questions when networking?

on guidance counselor 2.0, david messinger says people should embrace asking what they call dumb questions, because it shows you are intentionally trying to learn and expand your knowledge. he contrasts this with people who ask questions just to show off what they already know. messinger believes most people are more willing to help than you expect when you approach them with genuine curiosity.

how does david messinger recommend showing skills without professional experience?

on guidance counselor 2.0, david messinger emphasizes the importance of showcasing personal projects even when you lack professional experience in a specific technology. he recalls taylor's story of a candidate who had no professional azure experience but was working with a company on a side project using azure functions. messinger says the new profile will have a dedicated projects section so people can display work they have done outside of their regular job.

what is david messinger's advice on finding and working with mentors?

on guidance counselor 2.0, david messinger recommends finding mentors and putting recurring meetings on the calendar, even if it is just once every six weeks. he acknowledges that meetings might get canceled and you might only meet once every eighteen weeks, but having that scheduled commitment keeps the connection alive. messinger says staying in contact this way helps mentors know you are looking for opportunities and can lead to helpful referrals.
