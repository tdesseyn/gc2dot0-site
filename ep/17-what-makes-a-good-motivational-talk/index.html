what makes a good motivational talk

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • bring real energy to a new year pep talk or your team will feel flat and uninspired
  • share personal stories about your own struggles so employees relate to you as a leader
  • balance honesty about hard times with genuine positivity instead of dwelling on the past year
  • keep new year talks clear and concise since long rambling meetings lose people's attention
  • practice radical candor by being direct with specific numbers instead of vague statements

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say makes a good new year motivational talk for teams?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says three things make a good new year motivational talk: energy, messaging, and being clear and concise. he emphasizes that leaders need to bring genuine energy because teams feed off their enthusiasm, and without it the team will not feel energized. he also stresses that messaging should include personal stories about struggles so employees can relate, balanced with positivity about the new year rather than dwelling on the past. finally, he argues that these talks should be efficient and direct rather than rambling for hours, because employees appreciate concise information.

what does taylor desseyn say about leaders showing weakness to their teams?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn says leaders should tell their teams when they are struggling with something rather than pretending everything is perfect. he explains that when employees know a leader is struggling with the same things they are, it makes it easier for them to work on improvement together. he criticizes leaders who get on podiums acting like everything is great when it is not, arguing that employees need to hear about both the bad and the good.

what does taylor desseyn say about radical candor with employees?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 episode, taylor desseyn says he is working on being more direct with his employees through radical candor, which means being very direct rather than beating around the bush. he admits he struggles with this because he tends to avoid conflict and keep the peace, but he has learned his team does not like vague communication. he advises leaders giving new year talks to be direct, give specific numbers, and be quantitative rather than vague.
