what makes a good motivational talk

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • bring real energy to a new year pep talk or your team will feel flat and uninspired
  • share personal stories about your own struggles so employees relate to you as a leader
  • balance honesty about hard times with genuine positivity instead of dwelling on the past year
  • keep new year talks clear and concise since long rambling meetings lose people's attention
  • practice radical candor by being direct with specific numbers instead of vague statements
