questions this episode answers

what does michael chan say about showing your work even when you're employed?

on guidance counselor 2.0, michael chan says you have to keep showing what you're about even when you're happy at your current job. he explains that given five years there will be some major shift at your company that makes you want to leave, and if you haven't been building visibility it takes years to catch up. chan learned this after hating the freelance cycle of constantly selling himself, only to realize that staying visible is just part of work regardless of employment type.

how does michael chan recommend building an online presence without becoming a content machine?

on guidance counselor 2.0, michael chan says you should pick one lane that fits what you already like doing instead of trying to match everyone else's output. he suggests if you scroll tiktok all day then post there, or if you read a lot then write some posts. chan points to his own react patterns site, just a simple markdown file that still gets two thousand views per month seven years later, as proof you don't need polished content to be valuable.

what networking tactic does michael chan recommend for getting noticed by speakers and experts?

on guidance counselor 2.0, michael chan and taylor desseyn agree that summarizing someone's talk or post and tagging them is a powerful networking move. chan calls it a cheat code, explaining that speakers will retweet or share your summary all day because you've done work they didn't have to do. he compares it to writing a book report of something already created, noting that people love when you show respect by distilling their content in a concise way.
