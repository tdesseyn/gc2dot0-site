finding work in the direction you want to go w/ michael chan

guest: michael chan

  • keep showing your work even when you're happy at your job so you're ready if things change
  • pick a lane for putting your work online instead of trying to match everyone else's content output
  • build a tight network of people who will actually pick up the phone instead of chasing huge numbers
  • summarize or recap someone's talk or post online and tag them, it often gets noticed and shared
  • treat every conference like you paid for it yourself and prioritize meeting people over hanging with coworkers
