ep 504 · nov 28, 2025

feeling demotivated in your career and how to fix it w/ emma bostian

guest: emma bostian, engineering manager at spotify

  • before chasing the next promotion ask yourself why you actually want it and what the next level job really involves
  • consider lateral moves like switching companies at the same level, trying product or engineering management, or contracting if the ladder up feels stuck
  • figure out if you are running toward something new or running away from a bad situation before you make a career move
  • in interviews admit what you do not know and show a growth mindset instead of pretending to have every answer
  • use ai to understand code history and improve your skills rather than having it generate code you do not review before shipping

questions this episode answers

what does emma bostian recommend saying in interviews when you don't know the answer?

on guidance counselor 2.0, emma bostian says to admit when you do not know something in an interview instead of pretending you have all the answers. she explains that leaders who actively admit they do not know but show a growth mindset and willingness to learn are actually perceived as more competent than peers who act like they know everything. she has hired people who did not have all the answers because she values curiosity and humility over false confidence.

how does emma bostian say engineers should use ai tools at work?

on guidance counselor 2.0, emma bostian says to use ai to enhance your skills and productivity rather than having it generate code you do not understand or review before shipping. she gives the example of using ai tools to analyze code history when joining a new company to learn why certain code was written and what the context is. she warns that having ai write code that you do not review and then submitting it in a pull request is dangerous, especially when dealing with financial systems or data privacy.
