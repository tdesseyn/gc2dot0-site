ep 463 · jun 9, 2025

the grueling job search w/ bradley stokes, vp of it

guest: bradley stokes, vp of it, triple point

  • avoid easy apply on linkedin and instead apply directly on the company career site to reduce the competition pool
  • after making a connection, suggest coffee or lunch instead of just networking small talk at events
  • build a two column document mapping job requirements against your own experience and send it proactively
  • stay active and transparent about your job search on linkedin so your network can support and advocate for you
  • always send a genuine thank you or close out note even after rejection since the industry community is small
