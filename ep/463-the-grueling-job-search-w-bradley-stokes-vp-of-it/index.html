the grueling job search w/ bradley stokes, vp of it

guest: bradley stokes, vp of it, triple point

  • avoid easy apply on linkedin and instead apply directly on the company career site to reduce the competition pool
  • after making a connection, suggest coffee or lunch instead of just networking small talk at events
  • build a two column document mapping job requirements against your own experience and send it proactively
  • stay active and transparent about your job search on linkedin so your network can support and advocate for you
  • always send a genuine thank you or close out note even after rejection since the industry community is small

questions this episode answers

what does bradley stokes say about applying to jobs on linkedin versus company websites?

on guidance counselor 2.0, bradley stokes says he stopped applying through linkedin easy apply and instead went directly to company career sites. he believed this reduced the candidate pool significantly because far fewer people apply directly on company sites compared to the vast numbers applying through linkedin. this approach helped him stand out and eventually led to his job offer.

how does bradley stokes recommend networking during a job search?

on guidance counselor 2.0, bradley stokes says networking at events is not enough during a job search. after making a connection at an event, he would follow up by inviting people to coffee or lunch for one-on-one conversations. he also reached out directly to ceos, cfos, and board members at companies with very specific messages about his relevant experience, which led to his current role when a board member responded.

what document does bradley stokes create to match job requirements?

on guidance counselor 2.0, bradley stokes says he creates a two-column document that maps job requirements on one side against his specific experience on the other side. he sends this proactively to employers before they even ask, going line by line through the job description. he says this approach makes it impossible for hiring managers to ignore his qualifications and was a game changer in getting multiple offers.
