questions this episode answers

what does rizel scarlett say devrel is and why companies need it?

on guidance counselor 2.0, rizel scarlett, developer advocate at github, says devrel is an umbrella of roles that sit between a company and developers, showing how to use products while listening to community feedback and bringing it back to the product team. developers don't trust marketers who claim a product is the best, so devrel shows the product in action through demos, live coding and twitch streams instead of just promoting it. without devrel, a company can build a great product but if nobody knows how to use it or why they should, it's wasted effort.

how does rizel scarlett say you can break into devrel roles?

on guidance counselor 2.0, rizel scarlett, developer advocate at github, says to look for junior developer advocate roles that are specifically about training someone with potential even if they don't have a huge portfolio of blog posts and talks. she recommends checking devrelcareers.com, joining devrel-focused slack or discord communities like the black devrel discord, and subscribing to newsletters like mary thangbal's that post devrel job openings. building in public by documenting what you learn helps hiring managers see you understand the process, even if it's just writing down how you made an html button element.

what does rizel scarlett say about building your brand as a developer?

on guidance counselor 2.0, rizel scarlett, developer advocate at github, says brand is your online reputation and how people know you. she recommends learning in public by tweeting what you're learning as you go, even if it's something you think you should already know, because when people follow your learning journey they get invested and excited about your growth. you don't need to be a robot or share every controversial thought, just be yourself and share your genuine learning moments. taylor and rizel agree that you create community around the things you talk about, so consistently posting about one technology can make you known for that area even if you're still learning it.
