questions this episode answers

what does hank taylor say about negotiating a job offer?

on guidance counselor 2.0, hank taylor says most people treat negotiation like a battle, but once both sides decide they want to work together, they are on the same side of the table solving a problem. he recommends approaching the hiring manager as a human and having a real conversation about what you need, whether that is trading equity for cash or planning a conversation in six months. instead of demanding five or ten thousand more, explain what you are hoping for and ask what levers the company has to help you get there.

how does hank taylor decide who to hire for his team?

on guidance counselor 2.0, hank taylor says he looks for people with the ability to learn new skills quickly rather than specialists with years of experience in one specific role. he would rather hire someone who has stories about learning multiple things on the job with minimal input and high autonomy than a specialist who has done the same job for five years. he also wrote down every person he had to let go in the past and analyzed why, which taught him to avoid roles that were fundamentally incorrect and to hire dynamic people who can wear multiple hats.
