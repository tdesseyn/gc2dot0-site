interview with mary haynes

guest: mary haynes, recruiter at vaco

  • mary haynes moved from the music industry into tech recruiting with zero tech background and learned the terminology on the job
  • when networking at events, aim to make one or two real connections instead of sticking with people you already know
  • use the holiday season to prep your resume and research companies rather than waiting until january to start your search
  • recruiters can help you avoid getting lost in automated application systems that companies use to screen resumes
  • posting about your work or passions on linkedin can lead to unexpected opportunities since you never know who is paying attention
