interview with mary haynes

guest: mary haynes, recruiter at vaco

  • mary haynes moved from the music industry into tech recruiting with zero tech background and learned the terminology on the job
  • when networking at events, aim to make one or two real connections instead of sticking with people you already know
  • use the holiday season to prep your resume and research companies rather than waiting until january to start your search
  • recruiters can help you avoid getting lost in automated application systems that companies use to screen resumes
  • posting about your work or passions on linkedin can lead to unexpected opportunities since you never know who is paying attention

questions this episode answers

what does mary haynes say about networking at events when you're introverted?

on guidance counselor 2.0, mary haynes says you should aim to make one or two new connections at every event instead of sticking with people you already know. she recommends not clumping with your existing friends in a corner because that does nothing for your career. mary points out that everyone feels uncomfortable talking to strangers at networking events, so you're not alone in that discomfort.

what does mary haynes recommend doing during the holiday season if you want a new job?

on guidance counselor 2.0, mary haynes recommends using the holiday season to prepare rather than actively job hunting. she says you should get your resume ready, research companies you're interested in, and connect with a recruiter to understand the market. mary advises staying put through the holidays because most companies go on cruise control until january, so right now is prep time for a new year job search.

what does mary haynes say about why you should use a recruiter instead of applying online?

on guidance counselor 2.0, mary haynes says that when you submit your resume directly to corporate companies, it goes into a black hole through an automated system that kicks back responses. she explains that recruiters can paint a picture of you to clients instead of having your resume disappear into a machine. mary also points out that recruiters are in tune with what's happening across town at different companies and can get you involved with many different positions.
