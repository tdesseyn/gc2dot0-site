questions this episode answers

what does taylor say about using pressure test this as a prompt when rewriting your linkedin?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says he uses the phrase pressure test this consistently in his ai prompts. he asks ai to pressure test his headline, about section, and experience bullets as if it were a hiring manager for his target role, which uncovers specific gaps he missed. he found that even though he had already used ai to write his linkedin sections, adding the pressure test prompt revealed eight additional improvement points he needed to fix.

how does taylor recommend quantifying community manager experience on linkedin?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says when ai reviewed his linkedin, it flagged that he had no community size numbers anywhere across eleven bullet points. the ai told him he needed to include metrics like member count, daily active users, monthly active users, repeat attendance, member retention, and growth rates. without engagement or retention numbers, a skeptical hiring manager might read the profile as a marketing person who just calls it community rather than a true community builder.

what does taylor say about asking ai for keywords recruiters search for on linkedin?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn asks ai to list the top fifteen to twenty keywords and phrases recruiters and linkedin algorithms search for in his target role and where to place each one. the prompt returns specific titles like head of community and vp community, strategy phrases like community led growth and developer marketing, and craft terms like discord and event marketing. taylor says this prompt should actually come earlier in the process, before writing your resume, because these keywords are massive and need to be included throughout your profile.
