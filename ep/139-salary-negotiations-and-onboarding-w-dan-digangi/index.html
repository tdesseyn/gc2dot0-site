ep 139 · may 13, 2022

salary negotiations and onboarding w/ dan digangi

guest: dan digangi, engineering manager at docusign

  • ground your salary ask in specific reasons like skills or market gaps, not just a number you want
  • avoid saying a competing offer like a big name company is why you deserve more pay
  • it is fine to counter an offer within reason, just do not throw out wildly unrealistic numbers
  • look at the whole compensation picture including benefits, 401k match, and pto, not just base salary
  • schedule one on ones with teammates early in a new role to build connections and speed up onboarding
