ep 139 · may 13, 2022

salary negotiations and onboarding w/ dan digangi

guest: dan digangi, engineering manager at docusign

  • ground your salary ask in specific reasons like skills or market gaps, not just a number you want
  • avoid saying a competing offer like a big name company is why you deserve more pay
  • it is fine to counter an offer within reason, just do not throw out wildly unrealistic numbers
  • look at the whole compensation picture including benefits, 401k match, and pto, not just base salary
  • schedule one on ones with teammates early in a new role to build connections and speed up onboarding

questions this episode answers

is it okay to counter a job offer according to dan digangi?

on guidance counselor 2.0, dan digangi says it is fine to counter an offer within reason and he will not pull an offer just because someone asks for more money. he warns against throwing out wildly unrealistic numbers, like asking for four hundred thousand dollars for a one hundred thousand dollar role, because that shows misalignment. as long as the counter is reasonable, asking for twenty or thirty thousand more is okay and he is willing to have the conversation.

what does dan digangi recommend new hires do during onboarding?

on guidance counselor 2.0, dan digangi recommends scheduling one on one meetings with everyone on your team early in the onboarding process to build relationships and learn faster. he also tells new hires to take notes on what is working and what is not working in the onboarding process and feed that information back so the next person benefits. he emphasizes that onboarding is not one hundred percent the company's job; new hires need to take initiative to get themselves engaged and connected.
