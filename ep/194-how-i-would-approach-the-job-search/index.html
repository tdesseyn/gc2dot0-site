ep 194 · dec 16, 2022

how i would approach the job search

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • connect with two to three local recruiters even in a remote job market to get a real pulse on hiring trends
  • ask recruiters direct questions like what are the hot jobs and what companies should i research instead of just applying on job boards
  • take interviews outside your target role as a junior to figure out what you actually enjoy and are good at
  • show up consistently to meetups and conferences even if you stay quiet at first, presence matters more than talking
  • connect with every guest and commenter you interact with online, networking is really just making friends

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about using recruiters in a job search?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says he would search linkedin for recruiters in his city and dm them to meet for coffee or lunch, since recruiters are incentivized to buy candidates meals. he would ask them what the hot jobs are, what companies to research, and how to start the job search instead of applying on job boards. he recommends connecting with two to three local recruiters even in a remote market to get a real pulse on hiring trends.

what does taylor desseyn say about showing up to networking events?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says his mentor scott gordon at vaco told him to show up to meetups and not say anything. when taylor lived in raleigh, he attended meetups without speaking for three months, just taking notes, and an organizer was surprised to learn he was a recruiter. he believes just showing up consistently means more than anything else, even if you stay quiet at first.
