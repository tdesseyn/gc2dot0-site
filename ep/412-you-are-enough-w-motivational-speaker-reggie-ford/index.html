you are enough w/ motivational speaker reggie ford

guest: reggie ford, motivational speaker

  • notice when you chase awards or titles just to prove your worth to others instead of for real growth
  • get honest feedback from people you trust, like a partner, close friend, or therapist, to build real self awareness
  • practice sitting in silence for a few minutes without your phone, music, or tv to reconnect with your own thoughts
  • if you get rejected after a good interview, remember rejection is often about timing or budget, not your worth
  • give yourself permission to feel emotions instead of numbing out with social media scrolling after a tough day
