questions this episode answers

how does reggie ford say you can develop awareness that you're chasing external validation?

on guidance counselor 2.0, reggie ford says you need honest feedback from people you trust, like a therapist, partner, or close friend. a therapist can help you spot thought patterns you've normalized that aren't helpful. once you get that external feedback without treating it as judgment, you can start to recognize when you're chasing awards or titles just to prove your worth instead of for genuine growth.

what does reggie ford recommend for building self-awareness through meditation?

on guidance counselor 2.0, reggie ford and taylor desseyn agree that sitting in silence without your phone, music, or tv for fifteen minutes is a powerful starting point. ford explains that meditation isn't about clearing your mind but becoming aware of where your mind goes. he suggests sensory deprivation tanks or just checking in every few minutes to notice where your thoughts are and why.

how should you think about job rejection after a good interview, according to reggie ford?

on guidance counselor 2.0, reggie ford says when someone doesn't give you the job, that's their loss, not a reflection of your worth. ford and taylor desseyn both note that ninety percent of rejections aren't about you but about budget changes, timing, or other factors. ford adds that rejection is just a not right now, not a permanent no to you as a person.
