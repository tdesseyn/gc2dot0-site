ep 319 · jan 15, 2024

why do people keep doing this w/ kelley griggs, founder of britepeer

guest: kelley griggs, founder of britepeer and startup nash

  • start a community around a shared interest even if your first goal is personal, like finding a job
  • give a community a clear purpose and set expectations early so members know why they joined
  • when networking on linkedin, accept connections based on shared interest or local ties rather than just tech vs non-tech
  • pick meetup locations that feel different from an office and make sure parking is easy or people will not show up
  • if you feel stuck in your career, try writing or creating content about an industry you care about to build connections

questions this episode answers

what does kelley griggs say about starting a community to find a job?

on guidance counselor 2.0, kelley griggs, founder of britepeer and startup nash, says you can start a community around a shared interest even if your first goal is personal, like finding a job. she started a slack community about nashville startups while job hunting with a newborn at home, and someone posted a job opening in the slack that she applied for and got. the key is playing to your strengths when you cannot network the traditional way.

how does kelley griggs decide which linkedin connection requests to accept?

on guidance counselor 2.0, kelley griggs says she accepts connection requests based on shared interest or local ties rather than just tech versus non-tech. she accepts people from nashville even if they are not in tech because they know people and can help. for those outside her region, she vets by looking at whether they are interesting and talking about things that will teach her something.

what does kelley griggs say about choosing locations for in-person meetups?

on guidance counselor 2.0, kelley griggs says you should pick meetup locations that feel different from an office environment or people will not show up. she chooses places where parking is easy because if parking is not easy, people will not come. the location needs to be somewhere that does not feel exhausting or like just another work obligation.
