ep 56 · jul 19, 2021

w/amanda hughes from vaco technology

guest: amanda hughes, vaco technology

  • expect a tough first one to two years in recruiting since you hear no far more often than yes
  • burnout happens when you treat the job as task volume instead of building real depth with clients
  • focus on relationship based recruiting over transactional work to build trust and reduce candidate attrition
  • hiring managers should move fast and make quick decisions since the market is highly candidate driven right now
  • pay attention to interview process and speed since candidates are now turning down offers over slow or impersonal interviews
