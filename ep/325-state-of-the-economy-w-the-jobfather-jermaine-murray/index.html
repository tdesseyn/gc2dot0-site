ep 325 · jan 29, 2024

state of the economy w/ the jobfather, jermaine murray

guest: jermaine murray, career coach and technical recruiter known as the jobfather

  • treat your goals as abstract but firm, stay flexible on the path you take to reach them
  • a resume that is too long can hurt you, cut it down so a busy hiring manager will actually read it
  • if you cannot land your target role right away, take an adjacent job like qa or business analyst that builds skills toward it
  • look beyond typical tech companies and consider startups and older established companies that still rely heavily on tech
  • stay flexible on relocation and contracting, and keep networking and building community to create opportunities
