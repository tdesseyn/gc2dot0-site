questions this episode answers

what does jermaine murray say about resume length for job seekers?

on guidance counselor 2.0, jermaine murray says a resume that is too long will hurt your chances because hiring managers are busy and will not read it. he learned this lesson when a recruiter rejected his six-page resume for only six months of experience, asking why anyone would want to read something that long. after that experience, he researched how to create effective resumes and began getting callbacks nine times out of ten.

what does jermaine murray recommend if you cannot land your target tech role right away?

on guidance counselor 2.0, jermaine murray recommends taking an adjacent role like qa or business analyst that builds skills toward your ultimate goal. he says to be abstract with your goals but firm about where you are going, while staying flexible about how you get there. if you want to be a software architect but cannot get a developer job immediately, a business analyst role puts you in contact with stakeholders, which is a key skill for architecture work.

where does jermaine murray suggest job seekers look for tech opportunities right now?

on guidance counselor 2.0, jermaine murray suggests looking at old, established companies like consumer packaged goods brands, airlines, grocery stores and their head offices. he explains that if a company has been around for a hundred years, they are probably technologically advanced or at least good at adapting technology relative to their business. taylor desseyn adds that startups are also hiring and recommends looking at inclusive startup lists and growing companies covered by tech news sites.
