ep 239 · apr 3, 2023

investing in your career w/amy dutton who is the director of design at zeal

guest: amy dutton, director of design at zeal

  • build things and do real work because real bugs and problems are what push your skills forward
  • try open source by looking for issues labeled best first issue in well maintained repos on github
  • content you make is for others to discover you, not for you, so do not judge it by your own taste
  • learning how to learn matters more than any single framework since it lets you pick up new tools faster
  • network by genuinely building relationships rather than treating it as a sleazy transactional activity
