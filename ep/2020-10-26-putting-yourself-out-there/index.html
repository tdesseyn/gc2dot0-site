questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about posting your job search publicly on linkedin?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says job seekers should document their job search process publicly on linkedin instead of separating it from their personal life. he shares an email from a candidate who posted about frustrations and disappointments in his job hunt, and that post reached a friend at a company who forwarded his resume to the vp of software development, leading to a job offer. taylor explains that when someone likes your post on linkedin, their entire network sees it, even if you only have two followers.

what does taylor desseyn recommend posting about on linkedin to get noticed?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends posting about challenges you face at work and how you overcame them. he says if you struggle with something at work yesterday, post that challenge and your solution on linkedin because people resonate with real problems. he also suggests tagging people in your posts because then their followers will see it too, expanding your reach beyond your own follower count.

how long should linkedin videos be according to taylor desseyn?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says linkedin videos should be 90 seconds or less for optimal engagement, with a maximum of two minutes. he also emphasizes staying consistent with posting and commenting for 90 days rather than quitting after a week, noting that people on his team have already fallen off the bandwagon in just one week.
