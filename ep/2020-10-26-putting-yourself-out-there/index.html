putting yourself out there

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • document your job search process publicly instead of keeping it separate from your personal life
  • post about a challenge you faced at work and how you overcame it to build engagement
  • tag people in your posts so their followers see it too, expanding your reach
  • keep videos short, aim for 90 seconds or less and no more than two minutes
  • stay consistent posting and commenting for 90 days instead of quitting after a week
