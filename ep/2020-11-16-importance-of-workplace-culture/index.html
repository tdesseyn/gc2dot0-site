importance of workplace culture

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • workplace culture used to mean free beer and ping pong but that disappears once everyone works from home so culture has to come from somewhere else
  • regular reviews are a sign of good culture so if you have not had one in years that is a red flag worth questioning
  • treat one on ones as real conversations and not just strategy talks since sometimes people just want to connect
  • explain the why behind new initiatives at work because skipping that breeds distrust instead of buy in
  • when interviewing ask to speak with other team members about culture and request a second interview if you still are not sure

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about regular reviews and company culture?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says if your company is not giving you some sort of barometer of where you are year over year, that is not good culture. he has talked to people who have not had a review in three years, and he believes reviews are intentional conversations that leaders need to have with their teams. if you are not having them, you are doing it wrong.

how does taylor desseyn recommend assessing company culture during an interview?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends investing time to message people on linkedin and ask to speak with them about their time working at the company. if you do not get a sense of culture during the interview, he says to ask for another interview to have a conversation with team members specifically about culture. he believes every manager will respect you greatly if you take the time to have another interview to vet their company.
