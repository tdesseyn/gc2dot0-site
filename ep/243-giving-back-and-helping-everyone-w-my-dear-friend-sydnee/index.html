questions this episode answers

what does sydnee sampson say about building community in your career?

on guidance counselor 2.0, sydnee sampson says building community is essential because family often doesn't understand what you do in tech or the struggles you face. she recommends surrounding yourself with like-minded people who speak your language and can support you when you're down, especially during layoffs or career challenges. sydnee credits her community from career karma, the young and ambitious squad she formed in 2019, with helping her and others navigate job searches and career growth together.

what tech areas does sydnee sampson recommend focusing on right now?

on guidance counselor 2.0, sydnee sampson says to prioritize cloud certifications, particularly aws and google cloud certificates, because her employer accenture wants most software engineers cloud certified by the end of quarter three. she also strongly recommends data science and python skills because ai is rapidly growing. sydnee emphasizes these two areas—cloud and data—as the most important for career growth right now.

how does sydnee sampson say to figure out if a career is right for you?

on guidance counselor 2.0, sydnee sampson says to close your eyes and think about your life ten years from now—who you want to be, how you want to show up, and how you want your story told. she warns not to chase a paycheck in engineering just because it pays well; you need to think about what you can see yourself doing without getting paid much money first. sydnee emphasizes that if you don't actually like coding or engineering, you'll be wasting your time and taking a job away from someone who truly wants it.
