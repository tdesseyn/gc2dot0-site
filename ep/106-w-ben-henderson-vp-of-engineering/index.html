ep 106 · jan 26, 2022

w/ben henderson, vp of engineering

guest: ben henderson, vp of engineering at celera health

  • understand the real difference between management and leadership: management hits deadlines and kpis, leadership develops people and builds followers
  • fix communication gaps by running regular company-wide updates so everyone knows why priorities are changing
  • avoid judging engineers by lines of code or individual output, instead track whether the team is meeting its commitments and dig into why when it misses
  • to hire fast in a tight market, consider contract-to-hire or part-time contractors to fill urgent gaps while you search for full-time talent
  • job seekers should rely on warm network connections instead of cold outreach or mass job board applications since postings can pull in dozens of applicants overnight

questions this episode answers

how does ben henderson interview engineers without a five hour code test?

on guidance counselor 2.0, ben henderson says he uses a two-part coding interview. in one part, he asks candidates to bring code they've written and are proud of, then walk through it and explain their decisions. in another part, he gives them a piece of his team's code and asks them to explain what it does or find an issue. he's looking for problem solvers who can dig through a codebase, follow imports, and show they're self-motivated learners.

what does ben henderson say job seekers should do instead of applying on job boards?

on guidance counselor 2.0, ben henderson says job seekers should rely on their network and warm connections instead of submitting cold applications through job boards. he posted one engineering role and had seventy-five applicants overnight, making it too noisy to stand out. the best way to get to the top of the list and land an interview is to have someone vouch for you or reach out through a warm connection.
