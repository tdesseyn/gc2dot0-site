ep 106 · jan 26, 2022

w/ben henderson, vp of engineering

guest: ben henderson, vp of engineering at celera health

  • understand the real difference between management and leadership: management hits deadlines and kpis, leadership develops people and builds followers
  • fix communication gaps by running regular company-wide updates so everyone knows why priorities are changing
  • avoid judging engineers by lines of code or individual output, instead track whether the team is meeting its commitments and dig into why when it misses
  • to hire fast in a tight market, consider contract-to-hire or part-time contractors to fill urgent gaps while you search for full-time talent
  • job seekers should rely on warm network connections instead of cold outreach or mass job board applications since postings can pull in dozens of applicants overnight
