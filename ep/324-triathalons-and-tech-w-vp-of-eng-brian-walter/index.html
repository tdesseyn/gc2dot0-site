questions this episode answers

what does brian walter say about preventing burnout on engineering teams?

on guidance counselor 2.0, brian walter, vp of engineering, uses the equation stress plus rest equals growth to manage high-performing teams. he tells stressed teammates to take real time off, even an afternoon or an hour for a nap, so they return ready to perform at a high level. he acts more as a coach than a manager, picking up on cues when someone needs to step away and regroup.

what job search strategy does brian walter recommend over mass applying?

on guidance counselor 2.0, brian walter, vp of engineering, says networking is the best thing you can do in a job search. he warns that applying to hundreds of jobs on linkedin or job boards is the number one pitfall and a definition of insanity. the most effective approach is reaching out through someone else and asking for an introduction, rather than cold calling or immediately asking for a job.
