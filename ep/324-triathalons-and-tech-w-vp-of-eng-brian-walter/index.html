triathalons and tech w/ vp of eng, brian walter

guest: brian walter, vp of engineering

  • use the equation stress plus rest equals growth to avoid burnout on high performing teams
  • when a teammate is stressed, encourage them to take real time off so they return ready to perform
  • job titles vary wildly between companies so tailor your resume title to match the role you are applying for
  • networking beats mass applying, cold outreach works better when you ask for an introduction instead of a job
  • in interviews prioritize team fit and communication over pure technical skill since technical ability can be taught
