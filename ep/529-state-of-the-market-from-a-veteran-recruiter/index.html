questions this episode answers

what does tony payne say about tailoring your resume for each application?

on guidance counselor 2.0, tony payne says you must tailor every single application because generic resumes no longer work when companies receive up to a thousand applicants per job. he explains that if you are not in the first 250 applicants reviewed, you often have no chance at the role because talent teams cannot physically review more than that. tailoring helps you match the specific requirements so screening tools, whether human or ai, can see you fit the must-have criteria.

why does tony payne recommend asking for feedback after every rejection?

on guidance counselor 2.0, tony payne recommends always asking politely for feedback after every rejection, even after just an application, because it pushes talent teams to build the habit of providing it. he explains that ai screening systems can generate very detailed and accurate feedback easily, but companies are not in the habit of sharing it. if you ask in a way that gives the recruiter permission to say no and explain why, you are more likely to get useful insight that helps you improve.

what does tony payne say about taking contract roles versus waiting for permanent jobs?

on guidance counselor 2.0, tony payne says you should consider contract roles because there is no such thing as a permanent role anymore. he explains that contract work typically pays well, lets you demonstrate specific skills you have done before, and often leads to references or even permanent offers if you perform well. he notes that contract roles have notice periods just like any job, so if something better comes up you can leave, and you should judge each opportunity by what you will gain from it.
