how to answer the 'tell me about yourself' - solo show

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • keep your tell me about yourself answer to about forty five seconds instead of rambling into your life story
  • end your answer by asking if there is anything else they would like you to expound on so you land the plane instead of trailing off
  • build multiple about me statements tailored to networking, a specific job, or general introductions
  • research the company and interviewer so you can tie in relevant details even if you don't know the exact role
  • practice on video by recording yourself answering the question to catch filler words and nervous habits
