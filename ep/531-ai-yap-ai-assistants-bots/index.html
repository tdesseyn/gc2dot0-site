ai & yap - ai assistants & bots

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • use ai assistants like instinct or muse to automatically search linkedin and x for people posting about layoffs so you can reach out and help them
  • set up watches with your ai assistant to search job boards daily and surface roles that match what you are looking for
  • try assistantbenchmark.com to compare different ai assistants before picking one to commit to
  • start small with any ai bot setup and add a monitoring or spend tracking bot early so costs do not spiral
  • have your ai assistant track your paychecks to confirm taxes and 401k are actually being withheld correctly
