ai & yap - ai assistants & bots

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • use ai assistants like instinct or muse to automatically search linkedin and x for people posting about layoffs so you can reach out and help them
  • set up watches with your ai assistant to search job boards daily and surface roles that match what you are looking for
  • try assistantbenchmark.com to compare different ai assistants before picking one to commit to
  • start small with any ai bot setup and add a monitoring or spend tracking bot early so costs do not spiral
  • have your ai assistant track your paychecks to confirm taxes and 401k are actually being withheld correctly

questions this episode answers

how does taylor desseyn use ai assistants to find people who were laid off?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says he has instinct running automated searches on x and linkedin for people posting that they are laid off, then surfaces those posts to him. he approves the outreach message he wrote and the assistant posts it for him. he also has instinct monitoring layoffs.fyi for company layoff announcements, then searching for affected people on social media so he can invite them to his community.

which ai assistant comparison site does taylor desseyn recommend for choosing a bot?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends assistantbenchmark.com as the starting point for anyone interested in picking an ai assistant. he says the site tests and rates multiple ai assistants and the creator is doing the work of reviewing all of them. taylor calls it substantial and says if you want to get into using an assistant, that website is where he would check first.
