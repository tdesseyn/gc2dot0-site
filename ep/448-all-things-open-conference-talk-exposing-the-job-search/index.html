questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about resume length and one-page resumes?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says the one-page resume rule is absurd and candidates should let their resume breathe. he recommends two to three pages instead of cramming everything into 10-point font on a single page. he also advises removing outdated skills like cold fusion if you don't want recruiters calling about those technologies.

when should you send a thank you note after an interview according to taylor desseyn?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says to send the thank you message right after the interview instead of overthinking the timing. he had someone ask whether to send it in the morning, at night, or at midnight so it arrives at six a.m., and his advice was to relax and just send it immediately after with something authentic and conversational.

what does taylor desseyn mean by depth not width in the job search?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says applying to six hundred jobs will not work in the current market. he explains that depth means being meticulous with each application, crafting your resume for each job, connecting with hiring managers on linkedin, and sending follow-up messages after interviews. he warns that activity does not equal productivity and most people mistake mass applications for effective job searching.
