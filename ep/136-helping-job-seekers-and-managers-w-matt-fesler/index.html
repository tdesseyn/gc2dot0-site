ep 136 · apr 29, 2022

helping job seekers and managers w/ matt fesler

guest: matt fesler, engineering manager at navi health

  • when reaching out to a hiring manager, don't hesitate, even if the job feels out of reach you'll gain advice or a connection
  • ask a hiring manager for a short call on what they like to see in junior developer interviews to sharpen your approach
  • keep public code repos accessible and compilable since managers or senior engineers may look through them before an interview
  • be fully honest on your resume and be ready to speak in depth about anything you list, no matter your seniority
  • after an interview, give tactful feedback through your recruiter or in the q&a portion to help improve the process for others

questions this episode answers

what does matt fesler say about reaching out to hiring managers on linkedin?

on guidance counselor 2.0, matt fesler says job seekers should not hesitate to reach out to hiring managers even if the job feels out of reach. he explains that even if you don't get the job, you'll walk away with advice or connections because people naturally enjoy giving advice. fesler recommends asking hiring managers for tips on interview preparation or what they look for in candidates.

what does matt fesler recommend about honesty on resumes?

on guidance counselor 2.0, matt fesler says to be fully honest on your resume and be prepared to speak in depth about everything you list, regardless of your seniority level. he warns that padding your resume with extra content is risky because you must be crystal clear and able to discuss it thoroughly in interviews. fesler notes this applies universally, from junior developers all the way up to vice president candidates.
