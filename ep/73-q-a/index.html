ep 73 · aug 27, 2021

q&a

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • never message someone at a company just to ask about a job they posted, it feels disingenuous
  • when reaching out, use admiration, ask for a specific amount of time, and bring one specific question
  • build your brand before you are job searching, not during, so it does not feel forced or desperate
  • how a company treats candidates through the interview process reflects directly on its culture and brand
  • one genuine conversation at a conference or coffee shop can completely change your career trajectory years later
