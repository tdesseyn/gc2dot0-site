questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about messaging someone at a company to ask about a job posting?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says you should not message someone at a company asking about a job they have posted because it feels disingenuous and people start feeling used. instead, he recommends a three-part approach: start with admiration or flattery, ask for a specific amount of time like four, six, or eight minutes, and bring one specific question. have the conversation without asking about the job, then stay in touch on linkedin as a long game strategy.

when should you build a personal brand if you already have a job, according to taylor desseyn?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says you should start building your brand when you are not looking for a job, not during the job search. he compares it to going to the gym to get in shape rather than waiting to be in shape before going. when people try to create a brand during a job search it feels forced and awkward because they need a job, but starting when you are happy in your current role makes it authentic and actually becomes the best way to find future opportunities.

what does taylor desseyn say about how companies treat candidates during interviews?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says the way a company treats candidates through the interview process is a direct reflection of the company's culture and brand. he acknowledges that things can fall through the cracks occasionally, but if a candidate follows up two or three times and the company does nothing, that is the company's responsibility and suggests poor internal communication. he mentions that some companies in nashville have created bad reputations because they go dark and treat candidates poorly.
