productivity mindset w/ colin young

guest: colin young, exos

  • focus on autonomy, mastery, and purpose when trying to build a new habit that actually sticks
  • start small with stillness practice, even one minute, instead of jumping straight to long meditation sessions
  • treat burnout as a signal to add recovery and restraint, not just an excuse to work more hours
  • create friction against bad habits, like moving your phone to another room or keeping snacks out of reach
  • be authentic in your career instead of copying how other leaders act, and take time to reflect on what you have accomplished

questions this episode answers

what does colin young say about building habits that stick?

on guidance counselor 2.0, colin young from exos says quality behavior change comes through three concepts: autonomy, mastery, and purpose. autonomy means it has to be your journey, not someone telling you what to do. mastery means you need to feel competent at it and see yourself doing it six months from now. purpose means you need a clear goal to anchor yourself when change gets hard.

what does colin young say about preventing burnout at work?

on guidance counselor 2.0, colin young from exos says burnout prevention requires recovery and restraint, not just working more hours. recovery includes sleep, stillness practices, and physical recovery, because sleep is a natural performance enhancer. restraint means understanding that everything has a cost and being willing to turn off the laptop or step away. he recommends creating friction against bad habits, like putting your phone in another room to make it harder to reach for it automatically.
