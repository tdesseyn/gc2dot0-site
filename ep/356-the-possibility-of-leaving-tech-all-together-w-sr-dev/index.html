the possibility of leaving tech all together w/ sr dev advocate chris demars

guest: chris demars, senior developer advocate, digitalocean

  • be direct and specific in interviews about what you do and how you will benefit the company

questions this episode answers

what does chris demars say about tracking job applications during a search?

on guidance counselor 2.0, chris demars says he used notion to track every job application he submitted. he logged the company name, the specific role title, the application stage (applied, first interview, second interview, take home, declined, accepted), and the date he applied. he applied to over 100 jobs in a two and a half month period and tracking was essential because he was applying to 10 to 15 roles every single day.

how does chris demars recommend standing out in developer advocate interviews?

on guidance counselor 2.0, chris demars says you should be blunt and direct in interviews, not beat around the bush. he says to immediately tell the hiring manager exactly what you do, how you do it, and how the company will benefit from hiring you. he also recommends building a clear personal brand so people know what to expect from you at conferences and in job conversations, which helps establish trust in the community over time.

what does chris demars say about timing in the hiring process?

on guidance counselor 2.0, chris demars says timing matters as much as your qualifications when job searching. he explains that company budgets, fiscal year calendars, holidays like thanksgiving and christmas, and major company events all affect when hiring actually happens versus when jobs are posted. he was laid off in november and saw many job postings, but companies did not start interviewing until january because of year-end budget cycles and time off.
