ep 356 · apr 19, 2024

the possibility of leaving tech all together w/ sr dev advocate chris demars

guest: chris demars, senior developer advocate, digitalocean

  • expect job searching to feel like a second job, applying 10-15 roles a day when unemployed
  • track every application in a notion or spreadsheet with role type, stage, and date applied
  • be direct and specific in interviews about what you do and how you will benefit the company
  • build a clear personal brand so people know what to expect from you at conferences and in interviews
  • timing matters as much as skill, since hiring speed depends on company budgets, fiscal calendars and holidays
