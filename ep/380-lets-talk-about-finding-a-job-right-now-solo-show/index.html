questions this episode answers

how many conversations a day should a job seeker have according to taylor desseyn?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says job seekers without a job should aim for two meaningful conversations per day. in 30 days that means 60 conversations, not just a handful per month. he shared an example of someone who only had 14 conversations in four weeks and emphasized that was six times less than needed.

what should junior developers ask when networking according to taylor desseyn?

on guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn says junior developers should ask people how they got into tech and request one piece of tangible advice. if the conversation is going well, ask to be introduced to another person instead of cold messaging strangers. he emphasizes that warm leads and referrals are far more effective than reaching out to complete strangers.

how can developers build portfolio projects according to taylor desseyn?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn recommends walking into local businesses like veterinarians or restaurants and offering to build or improve their websites for free. he shares that one person got three part time freelance projects just by talking to uber drivers. these real projects give developers actual portfolio work to show employers.
