let's talk about finding a job right now (solo show)

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • aim for two meaningful conversations a day during a job search, not just a handful per month
  • when networking, ask people how they got into tech and request one piece of tangible advice
  • if a call is going well, ask to be introduced to another person instead of cold messaging strangers
  • treat job searching like marathon training by mixing networking, applications, social engagement, and outreach every day
  • offer to build something free for a local business to gain portfolio work and real experience
