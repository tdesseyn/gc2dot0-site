ep 451 · may 7, 2025

the blocking & tackling of finding a job in 90 days w/ lawrence lockhart

guest: lawrence lockhart, developer advocate at befree

  • structure job search days around four buckets: body, spirit, brain and family, then block them on a calendar
  • use a visual system like google calendar or a whiteboard with reminders since relying on memory alone does not work
  • target applications instead of mass applying, researching each company and role before sending anything
  • track every application, rejection and interview in a spreadsheet so you can learn from the data over time
  • use ai tools like gemini to research companies and tailor a condensed resume from a longer master version

questions this episode answers

what does lawrence lockhart recommend for structuring your day during a job search?

on guidance counselor 2.0, lawrence lockhart says to organize each day around four buckets: body, spirit, brain, and family, then time-block them on a visual calendar. he used google calendar with reminders because he knows he is very forgetful and needs something visual to stay on track. lockhart treated the job search like a real workday, working five days a week from morning until around 4 p.m., including workout time, prayer, research, applications, and dedicated family time in the evening.

how does lawrence lockhart approach job applications instead of mass applying?

on guidance counselor 2.0, lawrence lockhart says he applied to only 47 companies in 89 days because every application was highly targeted. he researched each company to ensure they were best in class and a good fit for his skills, often using gemini ai to do deep research on their financials and growth. lockhart would copy information from the company website into gemini, ask questions, and only apply if he felt confident they were strong and he was competitive. he also used ai to tailor a condensed one-page resume from his three-page master resume for each specific job description.
