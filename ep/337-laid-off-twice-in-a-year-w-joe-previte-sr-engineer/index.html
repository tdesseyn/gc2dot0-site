ep 337 · feb 26, 2024

laid off twice in a year w/ joe previte, sr. engineer

guest: joe previte, senior full stack engineer at mercury

  • after a layoff take time to let your head stop spinning before jumping back into the job search
  • searching publicly after a layoff can open doors because people start coming to you with opportunities
  • build a flexible daily routine that mixes interview calls with prep time and personal time so you do not burn out
  • think about non-negotiable parameters like benefits, stability, and family leave before picking your next company size
  • be careful about posting too much detail on failures or exact interview stages since companies can use that against you

questions this episode answers

what does joe previte say to do right after getting laid off?

on guidance counselor 2.0, joe previte says to take time before jumping back into the job search so your head can stop spinning. he recommends going for a walk and talking to your partner right away. the benefit of a layoff over a toxic job is you can search publicly, which opens more doors because people come to you with opportunities.

how did joe previte structure his day during his second job search?

on guidance counselor 2.0, joe previte explains that his first search he scheduled calls eight to five like a full workday, but it wore him out. the second time he did three hours of calls in the morning, then a two-hour block of interview prep in the afternoon, then used any leftover time flexibly for family or rest. he says this balanced approach worked better than grinding all day.

what does joe previte warn about posting your interview progress publicly?

on guidance counselor 2.0, joe previte says he posted about failing interviews publicly, but a head of engineering told him their team saw that as a red flag. he also warns against posting exactly what interview stage you are at with each company, because other companies will watch your updates and might slow down if they see you got rejected elsewhere or use it to negotiate against you.
