ep 222 · feb 20, 2023

from hourly wage at target to beating out 2k applicants w/tim stoneberg

guest: tim stoneberg, steelseries

  • when you spot a job you are genuinely passionate about, skip the easy apply and find someone on the team to reach out to directly
  • being one real person who reaches out beats being one of thousands of applicants in the pile
  • narrow your job search to companies and industries you actually care about so your passion comes through naturally
  • let small personal details like your interests or hobbies show up in your resume, linkedin, or even your email signature to spark real connection
  • if your current job search approach keeps failing, change your strategy instead of just sending more applications the same way
