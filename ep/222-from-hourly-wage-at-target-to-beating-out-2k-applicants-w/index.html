ep 222 · feb 20, 2023

from hourly wage at target to beating out 2k applicants w/tim stoneberg

guest: tim stoneberg, steelseries

  • when you spot a job you are genuinely passionate about, skip the easy apply and find someone on the team to reach out to directly
  • being one real person who reaches out beats being one of thousands of applicants in the pile
  • narrow your job search to companies and industries you actually care about so your passion comes through naturally
  • let small personal details like your interests or hobbies show up in your resume, linkedin, or even your email signature to spark real connection
  • if your current job search approach keeps failing, change your strategy instead of just sending more applications the same way

questions this episode answers

what does tim stoneberg say about reaching out to someone directly versus applying online?

on guidance counselor 2.0, tim stoneberg says he chose not to submit his resume through the standard application portal when he found a job at steelseries. instead, he looked up employees on linkedin, found someone in hr, and sent a direct message explaining his genuine interest in the role. this approach made him the one person who wasn't buried in a pile of 2,000 applicants, and he says it put him ahead of everyone else.

how does tim stoneberg recommend narrowing down your job search?

on guidance counselor 2.0, tim stoneberg recommends focusing your job search on companies and industries you genuinely care about rather than applying everywhere. he says that when he applied broadly in the summer of 2020, he got interviews but would have been unhappy at those jobs because he didn't care about the products. narrowing his focus to a gaming company he was passionate about made it easier to convey genuine enthusiasm during interviews.

what does tim stoneberg say about showing personality in the job search?

on guidance counselor 2.0, tim stoneberg says letting small personal details show up can create genuine connections during the job search. his email signature on his phone said sent from firelink shrine, a reference to a video game, and when he emailed a steelseries engineer from his phone, that engineer recognized the reference. tim says it immediately made him more than just a name on a resume and helped him connect as a real person with interests beyond the job.
