what is out there for free w/ julian dasilva, software mgr, stem advocate

guest: julian dasilva, senior software engineering manager, agtech, stem advocate

  • tie your first coding project to something you already care about so the lessons stick better than a generic tutorial clone
  • cold applying still works but spend most of your job search time networking instead of only sending applications
  • reach out to strangers on linkedin since it is one of the few places that kind of outreach is considered normal
  • in interviews set your ego aside and focus on being coachable since peer interviewers weigh heavily on hiring decisions
  • bring notes into an interview and tell the interviewer ahead of time so it doesn't look like you're distracted
