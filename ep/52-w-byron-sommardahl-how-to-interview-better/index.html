ep 52 · jul 9, 2021

w/byron sommardahl & how to interview better

guest: byron sommardahl, cto at acorn avenue and hero unit

  • algorithmic tests mostly show what someone recently studied, not real job skill for most developer roles
  • pair programming through a coding kata with test driven development reveals how someone actually thinks and solves problems
  • being able to teach or explain your code during an interview signals strong communication skills
  • developers need emotional intelligence since poor communication can lead to solving the wrong problem for a client
  • if being yourself hurts you in interviews, work on your emotional intelligence instead of just your technical skills
