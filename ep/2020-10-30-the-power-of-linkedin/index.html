the power of linkedin

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • post on linkedin consistently since organic reach can expose your content to a much wider audience than your follower count suggests
  • use hashtags strategically by thinking of them like google searches for the audience you want to reach
  • challenge yourself to post daily, but even twice a week is far better than not posting at all
  • share authentic, specific stories about your work instead of generic self help or get rich quick content
  • stay patient since it may take months of consistent posting before a post takes off and builds your following

questions this episode answers

what does taylor desseyn say about organic reach on linkedin?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says linkedin's organic reach is powerful because when someone likes your post, all of their followers see it. he explains that even if you have zero followers and haven't posted in years, a single post can get hundreds of likes and thousands of views. this reach happens because the content spreads through your connections' networks automatically.

how does taylor desseyn recommend using hashtags on linkedin?

in this guidance counselor 2.0 solo episode, taylor desseyn says you should think about hashtags like google searches and reverse engineer the people you want to reach. if you're targeting restaurants in nashville, for example, you would use hashtags like restaurants, nashville, and friday night. he emphasizes that hashtags help people find you and recommends using multiple hashtags despite criticism he receives for it.
