how to hire and navigate your business during covid with alex belew

guest: alex belew, chef and owner of dalts and jane in murfreesboro

  • look for people with integrity, self-awareness, and a genuine desire to learn over raw talent or experience
  • give candidates a trial shift so both sides can see if the fit and culture actually work
  • handle performance or culture problems with a calm, private conversation instead of public conflict
  • be honest and transparent with your community and staff about business struggles instead of hiding them
  • stay creative and keep pivoting your offerings when your usual business model stops working
