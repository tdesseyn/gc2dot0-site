the importance of taking care of devs w/ vitor oliveira, talent @ gun.io

guest: vitor oliveira, talent success manager at gun.io

  • when giving feedback or bad news as a freelancer, show up honest and transparent instead of avoiding the conversation
  • managing up means proactively updating your manager with your progress instead of waiting to be checked on
  • in coaching sessions, let the other person talk most of the time so you can find the real root cause of a problem
  • practice soft skills like active listening and clear communication in everyday life, not just at work
  • build genuine rapport with clients since strong relationships are often what turn freelance work into full time offers

questions this episode answers

what does vitor oliveira say about delivering bad news or feedback as a freelancer?

on guidance counselor 2.0, vitor oliveira, talent success manager at gun.io, says that when you need to deliver bad news or negative feedback as a freelancer, you cannot under-communicate. you have to show up and be honest and transparent with clients, maintaining a positive attitude. these situations happen frequently in software engineering careers, such as when dealing with incidents or when you don't know how to solve a specific problem.

how does vitor oliveira define managing up?

on guidance counselor 2.0, vitor oliveira explains that managing up means instead of waiting for your manager to check in with you, you proactively manage your manager by updating them with your progress. you build things but you build them publicly for your manager to see. if you are able to do this, you will stand out and probably be a star in your engagement, because not everybody does it.

what does vitor oliveira say about coaching sessions and listening?

on guidance counselor 2.0, vitor oliveira says that when running a coaching session, he allows the developer to talk about 70 percent of the time while he pays attention, takes notes, and asks questions. this approach allows for a deep dive into the situation so you can identify the root cause of the problem. once you understand what is causing the issue, then mentorship can begin to help the person improve.
