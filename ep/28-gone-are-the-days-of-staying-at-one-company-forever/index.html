gone are the days of staying at one company forever

with taylor desseyn (solo)

  • its okay to job hop, gone are the days of staying at one company your whole career
  • if you have a short job stint, still put it on your resume and be ready to explain why it ended
  • in interviews, tell a clear story about why you left a job instead of just saying it wasnt good
  • the average job duration people see is around a year and a half to two years
  • staying at one company for many years is fine, but you need to keep innovating and show your title changes and growth on your resume and linkedin
