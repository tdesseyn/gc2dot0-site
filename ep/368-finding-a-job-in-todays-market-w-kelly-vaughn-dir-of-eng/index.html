ep 368 · may 17, 2024

finding a job in today's market w/ kelly vaughn, dir of eng @ spot ai

guest: kelly vaughn, director of engineering at spot ai

  • mention that you already applied in your outreach message, but skip asking for 20 minutes of a hiring manager's time to sell yourself
  • expect it to take a few messages over a few weeks before a hiring manager connects your name with your resume
  • getting the company name wrong in a cover letter or message is a fast way to get passed over
  • keep a senior resume to about two or three pages and focus on showing impact and results, not just duties
  • always update your resume every quarter or during performance review season so you are ready if you lose your job unexpectedly

questions this episode answers

what does kelly vaughn say about messaging a hiring manager when you apply for a job?

on guidance counselor 2.0, kelly vaughn, director of engineering at spot ai, says you should mention that you already applied in your outreach message. she appreciates knowing someone applied but warns against asking for 20 minutes of her time to sell yourself, because she does not have time for those meetings with every interested candidate. it typically takes a few messages over a few weeks before she connects your name with your resume.

how does kelly vaughn say you can get passed over before an interview?

on guidance counselor 2.0, kelly vaughn, director of engineering at spot ai, says getting the company name wrong in a cover letter or message is a fast way to get rejected. she says it has happened more than once, and the work at most companies requires deep attention to detail. if you mess up, at least own the mistake immediately.
