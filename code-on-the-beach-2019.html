about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: conference q&a session about job search strategy, recruiting, resume tips, linkedin profiles, and salary negotiation in tech.

ask recruiters about sign-on bonuses and reload offers; if they don't know, they should find out. format resumes with company summary, specific bullet points, and technical skills; two to three pages max depending on experience level. work with recruiters who have stayed at the same company and local market for over two years; they know market rates better. vet recruiters first, then let them work for you; stop blasting resumes everywhere or recruiters lose ability to represent you. keep linkedin, github, dice, indeed profiles updated with keywords and professional photos; avoid personal content you wouldn't show your parents. work with no more than two or three recruiters at once and vet them using pointed questions about the role before trusting their job orders. update your linkedin, dice, and indeed profiles with specific keyword-rich tech skills and remove old unprofessional content recruiters might find.
