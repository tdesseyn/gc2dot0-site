about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: developer austin shares how posting content on linkedin led to his first job through a recruiter who saw his interview story video.

post content consistently on linkedin showing projects, learning, and your journey to get recruiter attention. video content gets more views than graphics or text, but takes longer to create so start simple. end posts with questions to spark conversation and engage your audience in response. don't fear judgement when posting; focus on your goal and stay consistent until momentum builds. share personal stories about challenges and feelings, not just technical work, because people relate to your journey.
