about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with eddie hinkle: how to approach technical interviews as conversations and recognize when it's time to leave your job.

technical interviews test communication skills as much as coding ability, so talk through your thinking. track frustrating work days on a calendar and start looking when more than half the month is marked. understand your job is fluid due to layoffs and manager changes, so document reviews and decisions. make intentional career choices with specific goals rather than moving aimlessly between positions. document unusual situations and keep copies of your reviews since managers and companies can change without warning. know what you want from a role, like skill building or a big name company, before deciding whether to take a step down to get there.
