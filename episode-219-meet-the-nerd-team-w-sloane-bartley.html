about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with sloane bartley: sloane bartley discusses her transition from higher education to technical recruiting, emphasizing relationship-building, consistency, and mental health challenges of remote work.

recruiters help people through major life transitions; apply counseling skills to understand what candidates truly value in roles. consistency matters more than talent in recruiting; results show in two to four weeks, so you must sustain effort every single week. learn tech by reading job descriptions like comprehension tests and asking developers about their own skills rather than memorizing jargon. remote work isolation affects extroverts especially; build virtual community through team check-ins and intentional connection outside work tasks. ask questions relentlessly and focus on what brings joy and aligns with your values rather than chasing one perfect career path. be upfront with candidates when you have nothing for them instead of ghosting them. ask more questions early in a new role instead of pretending to already know everything.
