about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with sloane bartley: meet sloane bartley, a recruiter who transitioned from higher ed to tech recruiting and shares what she's learned about supporting candidates through career transitions.

consistency matters more than quick wins. recruiting results take 2-4 weeks, so you have to show up the same way every single week. detail-oriented organization and memory for candidate information is your competitive edge. use tools like calendly, trello, and automated workflows to manage many candidates at once. don't pretend to know everything about tech. ask developers to explain their skills, read job descriptions like reading comprehension exercises, and keep learning. remote work is isolating even for extroverts. build community intentionally through team check-ins and give yourself permission to relax and celebrate wins with people. be upfront with candidates when you have nothing for them instead of ghosting them. ask more questions early in a new role instead of pretending to already know everything.
