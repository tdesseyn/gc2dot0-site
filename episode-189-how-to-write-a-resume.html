how to write a resume

3 takeaways

  • > put only your name and a work specific email at the top of your resume, skip phone number and address.

  • > list each job with company, start and end date by month and year, position, location, and a quick summary of what the company does.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to structure a resume that gets recruiter attention, with specific formatting and content strategies.

structure professional experience by company, dates, position, then break down projects with overview and three to four bullet points each. list all technical skills you've ever worked with to increase keyword matches in recruiter searches. keep resume plain, simple and single column with no colors or pictures unless you work in design. put only your name and a work specific email at the top of your resume, skip phone number and address. write a summary section with four or five bullet points on years of experience and top skills so recruiters can decide fast. list each job with company, start and end date by month and year, position, location, and a quick summary of what the company does.
