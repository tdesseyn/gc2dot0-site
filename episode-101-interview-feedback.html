interview feedback

3 takeaways

  • > expect no interview feedback most of the time since over half of interviews end with little to no response from hiring teams.
  • > wait about 24 to 48 hours before following up with a recruiter and 48 to 72 hours before following up with a hiring manager.
  • > always work through your recruiter instead of dming the hiring manager directly or you risk getting rejected on the spot.
