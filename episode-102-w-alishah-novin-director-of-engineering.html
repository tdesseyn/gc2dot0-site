about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with alishah novin: how to evaluate engineers fairly through portfolios and conversations instead of stressful coding tests and whiteboarding.

skip take home assessments and whiteboarding. ask candidates about projects they built, problems they solved, and what they'd change. review portfolios and github with written explanations of what the person tried to build and challenges they faced. pair programming or conversational code review shows how someone thinks and collaborates better than solo performance. resumes need clear language, years of experience plus project count, relevant accomplishments from any field, clickable links. look for desire to learn shown through blogs, github activity, and honest discussion of struggles and current work. avoid vague skill ratings like javascript four out of five since reviewers have no idea what your scale means. include non tech jobs like customer service or serving on your resume if the skills are relevant to the role.
