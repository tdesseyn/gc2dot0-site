about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with alex friedman: learn how to build a fulfilling life beyond work achievements and set healthy boundaries as a high achiever and founder.

practice gratitude daily by appreciating your baseline—health, shelter, relationships—before chasing next milestones. find mentors and leaders who set clear boundaries around family time and fitness, showing success doesn't require always working. fail intentionally and often to desensitize yourself to rejection so everything else feels easier. set clear work boundaries like fixed start and stop times so family and health dont get sacrificed for output. practice gratitude on purpose and remember failing early and often makes rejection easier to handle later.
