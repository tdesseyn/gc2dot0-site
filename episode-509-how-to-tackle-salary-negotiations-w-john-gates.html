about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with john gates: learn how to negotiate salary effectively by being collaborative instead of aggressive, building value early, and exploring multiple negotiation fronts beyond just base pay.

eighty percent of people don't negotiate at all, leaving five to six figures on the table without knowing it. ask the recruiter for the budget range early to preserve negotiating power for later interview stages. provide a range overlapping theirs with your high number above theirs to create flexibility for signing bonuses and other benefits. build value throughout the process by positioning yourself as high-demand talent solving expensive problems for the company. negotiate beyond salary using signing bonuses, title, level, remote flexibility, pto, and milestone bonuses when salary is constrained. position yourself as talking to other companies to create competition, since recruiters move faster and offer more when they fear losing you. even when an offer looks good take time to review it collaboratively before accepting, since there is often more left on the table than you realize.
