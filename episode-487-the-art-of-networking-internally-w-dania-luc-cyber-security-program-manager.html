about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with dania luc: how to network effectively within your organization to get work done and advance your career.

managers should vocally advocate for employees by sharing specific impact and outcomes, not just that work got done. quantify your achievements with numbers and concrete results on resumes and in interviews to stand out. research companies deeply including financials and strategy to show you understand where they're headed. only apply to roles that fit your five-year career plan rather than taking every opportunity. build internal relationships by being social, asking specific questions, and helping others without always asking for something in return. build real relationships at internships and conferences since those connections can lead to referrals and support years later. when networking for help, ask specific questions like requesting feedback on a resume or about particular job openings instead of a vague ask.
