about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to approach a job search by building relationships with recruiters instead of just applying to jobs.

update your resume first using a clear template, then set up linkedin and job board profiles within 24-48 hours. search for technical recruiters on linkedin, filter by location, and dm ones with at least two years experience at their company. write intentional dms with flattery about their work, a specific time commitment like six minutes, and a clear ask. send at least 50 customized dms daily to build relationships rather than blasting applications to job postings. find recruiters who have been at the same company for at least two years before dming them.
