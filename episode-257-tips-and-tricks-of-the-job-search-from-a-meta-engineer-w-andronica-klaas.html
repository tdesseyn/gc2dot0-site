about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: job search strategies for a tough market: stop mass applying, do volunteer work, network intentionally, and be willing to do free projects to get your foot in the door.

mass applying alone doesn't work anymore; you need to research companies, ask thoughtful questions, and show genuine interest. junior devs need more than a capstone project now; add volunteer work or side projects and find a mentor. offer free work strategically to land clients or jobs; pick one or two projects to build credibility without overcommitting. coffee chats work better when you add incentive; consider sending lunch or coffee to busy people you want to meet. contract and part-time roles can become full-time jobs; stay open to different work arrangements to get your foot in the door. if you want time with a busy manager be willing to sweeten the ask with something like a coffee or lunch delivery. showing up consistently and commenting on networking content can directly lead to real job offers.
