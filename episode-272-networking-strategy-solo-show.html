networking strategy (solo show)

3 takeaways

  • > treat networking like a meetup where showing up and saying hey is the first step, then engage further in comments.
  • > find a common denominator with someone, like a shared interest, to start a real connection.
  • > keep conference conversations short, five minutes covering what someone does and whether they like it is plenty.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to network effectively at conferences and online without overthinking it or trying too hard.

networking is just five minute conversations finding common ground not lengthy pitches or scripts. follow up after meeting people is where real opportunities happen not the initial connection. create content about speakers and tag them at conferences to get noticed and build relationships. reach out directly to people at companies via dm instead of applying online to stand out. conferences require work and you should be exhausted by end from meeting as many people as possible. recruiters focus on the body of your resume, not links to portfolios or github, so make that content count.
