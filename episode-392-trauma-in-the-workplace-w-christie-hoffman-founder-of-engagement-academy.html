about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with christie hoffman: how workplace trauma and childhood patterns shape your leadership style, and practical ways to build engagement through values and self-awareness.

company values must come from employees, not leadership dictates, to actually guide decision-making. burnout signals your body needs help; add personal goals, movement, and therapy to cope with work stress. recognition works when specific and tied to impact, not generic praise for doing your job. childhood regression therapy helps you notice when you're repeating old family patterns in stressful moments. basic needs like clear expectations and project management matter more than recognition programs. pay attention to how you react when stressed or angry and ask if it mirrors a parent or caretaker, that awareness can change bad leadership habits. small daily habits like walking, joining a gym even without a plan, and watching nutrition can meaningfully reduce anxiety and burnout.
