about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with josh comeau: how to build a successful course business through blogging, reputation building, and strategic content creation over years.

courses take years of full-time work to build properly, not months; start by building an audience first. include multiple content types beyond video like interactive articles, exercises, mini-games, and workshops for better learning outcomes. share what you learn publicly even as a beginner; mistakes and corrections are part of the process and build credibility over time. speaking at conferences and being visible in your field attracts better job opportunities than job listings alone. teach at a local coding bootcamp to learn where beginners actually get stuck. treat your time and effort like a limited resource and spend it on work that serves your real goals.
