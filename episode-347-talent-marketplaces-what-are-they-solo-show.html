talent marketplaces - what are they? - solo show

mar 27, 2024 · 17 min · hosted by taylor desseyn

3 takeaways

  • > a talent marketplace gives you access to a pool of already vetted talent you can pick from instead of sourcing from scratch like a staffing agency does.
  • > talent marketplaces mainly serve senior level roles while staffing agencies are more likely to place junior developers too.
  • > ask any staffing recruiter if they are showing you every open role or only the ones that are fastest and easiest for them to fill.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: talent marketplaces connect you with vetted senior-level roles pre-screened by technical teams, different from staffing agencies that search for talent on demand.

most talent marketplaces focus on senior roles and niche skill sets, not entry or mid-level positions. you can see all available jobs and submit to everything, unlike staffing agencies that may hide opportunities. format your resume properly, keep your linkedin title seo-focused, and maintain both a resume and linkedin profile. join multiple talent marketplaces and sign up for their talent pools to access more opportunities. when picking an agency focus on finding a good individual recruiter you vibe with rather than judging the company as a whole. fix your resume formatting first since font choices bold text and font size issues cause more rejections than the actual content.
