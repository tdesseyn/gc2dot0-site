about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with rob joseph: how to build a freelance development career through portfolio, networking with developers, and financial planning.

build a portfolio of real work you can show to prove you can deliver, starting with personal projects. get your first gig any way you can, then use it as a reference for better paying work. develop relationships with other developers who can refer you work instead of cold pitching yourself. create content like blogs or podcasts to build credibility when people google your name. referrals from other developers often become your best source of new gigs once you have done good work for someone. put your work where people can find it, like an app store listing or simple webpage, so your name backs up what you say you can do.
