about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with Ashten Fizer: navigate a tough tech job market by upskilling strategically, communicating effectively with leadership, and building genuine community connections.

upskill in high-demand areas like machine learning and ai, but choose skills that genuinely excite you. master upward communication: be succinct, use bullet points, stay solution-oriented, and communicate early and often. your career is built from all your experiences; highlight how different skills combine to solve problems. network in person at meetups and events; introduce yourself and ask questions to build real relationships. remember your value comes from being a jack of all trades; companies need people who can do multiple things. when communicating upward be succinct, use bullet points, lead with data and always bring a solution not just a problem. in interviews ask leaders when they last took time off to gauge if the company culture actually supports work life balance.
