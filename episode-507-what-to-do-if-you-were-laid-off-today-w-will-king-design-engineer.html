about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with Will King: how to prepare for a job search now, build targeted projects for companies you want to work for, and use ai responsibly to research and speed up your process.

start preparing for job search now, not after layoff. identify three companies you want and build something that solves their real problems. for smaller startups, build a project that demonstrates you understand their technology and have thought through their product challenges. for open source companies, contribute real fixes to their projects over time. maintainers can spot surface-level ai-generated contributions immediately. use ai for research and domain knowledge, not to generate resumes or projects. if you can't explain your work in detail, it signals you didn't do it. contributing real fixes to open source tools you actually use heavily can be a stronger signal than a cold application. say yes to ambitious side projects while still employed since those unexpected efforts tend to open the most career doors.
