about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to write a resume when you unexpectedly need a job, focusing on length and quantifiable achievements.

remove soft skills like communication and teamwork, replace with specific numbers and results. include quantitative data like revenue generated or performance metrics to stand out to employers. recruiters need concrete details tied to job descriptions or they pass on resumes. use specific numbers like sales figures or revenue generated to show impact instead of vague job duties. when you're unexpectedly job hunting, start by rebuilding your resume since most people haven't touched it since college. skip the one page resume rule if you have real experience, longer resumes can work for people with more years in the field. make sure your resume has enough detail tied to the job description or employers will pass on it.

What do you do when you first find yourself on the job market unexpectedly? We'll start breaking the process down bit by bit so you can approach this daunting process with ease.
