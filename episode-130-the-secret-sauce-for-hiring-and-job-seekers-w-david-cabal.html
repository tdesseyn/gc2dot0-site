about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with david cabal: how to hire well and interview successfully by focusing on soft skills, culture fit, fast hiring processes, and intentional preparation.

pay people well and communicate often during hiring to avoid losing candidates to recruiters. prioritize humble candidates who take criticism well over technically brilliant people with poor attitudes. move hiring quickly by setting clear timelines, using tools like calendly, and giving feedback within 24-48 hours. build team culture through shared experiences like game nights to create trust that makes feedback easier. stay laser focused on shipping features by cutting non-critical work, deferring nice-to-haves, and iterating after launch. stay laser focused on the delivery date and push non critical bugs and extra features to the backlog instead of chasing perfection. as a candidate come with prepared questions for the interviewer since most people skip this and it instantly sets you apart.
