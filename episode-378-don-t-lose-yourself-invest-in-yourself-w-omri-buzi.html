about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with omri buzi: how to invest in yourself whether you stay at a company or go independent, and why networking conversations matter more than any single job.

stop doing only what your employer expects and start investing in your own growth even while employed. getting started on your own is just having conversations with no agenda, not a permanent decision. use affinity groups like school networks, hobbies, conferences, and professional communities to lower the barrier to networking. you are your own business first, your job is secondary, so prioritize your own development over company loyalty. going independent is not closing doors to full-time work later, it just gives you new perspective and skills to bring back. find affinity groups like alumni networks, scouting, or hobby communities since people respond more to shared connections. if you worry about seeming too salesy when networking, that worry itself means you are unlikely to ever cross that line.
