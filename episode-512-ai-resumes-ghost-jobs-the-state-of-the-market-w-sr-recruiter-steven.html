about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with steven evans: how ghost jobs work, navigating the job search, and recruiting insights from a decade-plus industry veteran.

recruiters see hundreds of resumes and need searchable skills, years of experience, and proof of what you built, not just tech buzzwords. research the company and recruiter before interviewing, find commonalities, and use ai to prep; this makes you memorable and breaks down walls. solve problems and tell the story of what you built and why, using situation-task-action-result; code is commoditized, problem-solving skills matter. network with recruiters directly, get referrals, join communities, and remember jobs exist off the market; most importantly, keep applying and keep going. research the recruiter and company before an interview so you can build a real connection instead of a purely transactional chat. on your resume, state tangible skills and years of experience up top and avoid vague lines like used react or used aws without context.
