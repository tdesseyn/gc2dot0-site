about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to search for and connect with recruiters on linkedin and build relationships that help your job search.

customize every message you send to recruiters on linkedin instead of using generic templates. ask recruiters you already like for referrals to other good recruiters in their network. check in with recruiters regularly via text or email every couple days to maintain engagement. always customize your connection note every time you message a recruiter on linkedin. use the linkedin search bar with terms like recruiter or technical recruiter plus your skill set, then filter by city. consider skipping recruiters with under two years experience since it often takes about two years for a recruiter to find their footing in a market. check a recruiter's linkedin profile first and reach out to ones who show personality rather than a boring profile.
