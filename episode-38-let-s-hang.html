let's hang

3 takeaways

  • > instead of applying through a company website, find people you know on the company's linkedin page and dm them directly.
  • > when you dm someone at a target company, ask for a quick virtual coffee to learn about the role rather than asking for a job.
  • > build a small mock-up or proof of concept using the company's own product or api before reaching out.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: build something with a company's product to start a conversation with hiring managers instead of just applying online.

dm people at target companies with thoughtful messages that show genuine interest. dm your work to relevant people like developer relations or senior engineers. get immediate attention by showing you've already built something valuable. use that mock-up as an excuse to message a developer advocate or senior engineer at the company for feedback. creating something real for a hiring manager gets more attention than offering to work for free or sending a generic greeting. instead of applying through a company website, find people you know on the company's linkedin page and dm them directly. when you dm someone at a target company, ask for a quick virtual coffee to learn about the role rather than asking for a job. build a small mock-up or proof of concept using the company's own product or api before reaching out.
