working with recruiters (solo show)

3 takeaways

  • > reach out to agency recruiters first since they work across many clients and often know the market better than corporate recruiters.
  • > remember agency recruiters only get paid when they place someone, which shapes how responsive they are.
  • > text or email recruiters instead of calling since most are on calls with other candidates.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to work with recruiters during your job search, including agency vs corporate recruiters and specific tactics to stay on their radar.

contact recruiters by text or email, not phone calls, and keep checking in until they respond. ask recruiters for feedback and linkedin tips, leaning on them even if you're not an immediate fit. use the bcc trick to email multiple recruiters about job postings you find to get introductions or job leads. send a personalized note when connecting with recruiters on linkedin, it increases your visibility and chances of response. if a recruiter goes quiet, send a direct message asking for honest feedback so you can move on in your search.
