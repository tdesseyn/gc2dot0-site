about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: specific strategies to interview better including research, quantifiable achievements, conversation skills, and post-interview reflection.

research the company and hiring managers on linkedin to find conversation starters and common ground. use pen and paper instead of typing to reduce distraction during video interviews. quantify your accomplishments with specific numbers and be honest if you don't know something. keep conversations going by asking for clarification and connecting new topics to your experience. after each interview keep a journal of what went well and what to improve to build your own study guide. bring printed resume and pen and paper to interviews so you are not distracted by typing on screen. research the company and the people interviewing you so you can turn the interview into a conversation. prepare three to four clear bullet points on your background tied directly to the company's specific needs.
