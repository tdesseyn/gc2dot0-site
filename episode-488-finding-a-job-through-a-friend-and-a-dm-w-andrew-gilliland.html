about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with andrew gilliland: how to find your first tech job by building community connections and targeting smaller companies willing to take chances on junior developers.

start meetups in your area even if attendance is low at first, people remember who puts in the work. smaller agencies and local companies are more likely to hire juniors than big tech firms. contact hiring managers directly with your application rather than relying on linkedin alone. be willing to learn unfamiliar tech stacks when opportunities come up, embrace discomfort. prioritize working with people you like over salary or title when choosing between job offers. when juggling two interview processes, be upfront with the company you prefer instead of using offers just to negotiate. use ai tools to learn unfamiliar tech by asking questions back and forth, but always validate what they tell you.
