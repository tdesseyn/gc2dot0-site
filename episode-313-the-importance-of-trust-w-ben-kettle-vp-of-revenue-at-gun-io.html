about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with ben kettle: how gun.io builds trust with clients through technical vetting, dedicated support, and matching senior developers with companies that need specialized skills.

gun.io has engineers vet all developers before they join, not recruiters doing surface-level screening. company success managers check in regularly with clients to remove ambiguity and address issues before they become problems. clients get real interviews with pre-vetted developers so they can assess cultural and work style fit themselves. senior engineers often avoid traditional job boards, so accessing gun.io's community gives you talent you won't find elsewhere. be yourself in interviews and conversations instead of being overly formal; the best working relationships happen between peers. when interviewing, aim for a peer to peer conversation instead of a formal interrogation, the best interviews feel like two experts talking shop. early career professionals should get good at articulating business problems and reasoning through tradeoffs, not just citing technical skills.
