about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with Pauline Narvas: how building community from the ground up creates loyal customers and advocates for your company and career.

community roles don't need to sit under marketing, consider them their own department or in customer success. to get a community job, you have to do community work first. start writing, speaking, and connecting people before applying. authenticity and showing up consistently over time builds a personal brand that opens career doors. community touches every part of the customer journey from awareness through advocacy and growth. connect with people you admire over coffee chats and build your portfolio on a website or linkedin over years. put yourself out there in dms, comments, and events the same way you would advocate for a community, because no one else will do it for you. stop hiding your authentic self at work, since showing up as you are opens doors instead of closing them.
