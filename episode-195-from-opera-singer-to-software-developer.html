about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with anna mcdougall: how an opera singer became a software developer by learning to code, building community on social media, and using strategic job applications.

learning technical skills is step one, but building social proof through blogs, youtube, or twitter matters equally for getting noticed. use social media to distribute your work and connect with people, then guide them to your portfolio as the deeper resource. research companies and contact someone before applying, adapt your materials to each job, and show what makes you different. attend events and post about them to create social proof; posting about conferences counts as much as attending them. one or two polished portfolio projects beats ten small ones, and choosing projects you care about makes them stronger. record a short video cover letter answering the questions employers usually ask, like your tech stack and work authorization status. pick one or two polished, complete portfolio projects instead of many small ones so you can show you can handle real world complexity.
