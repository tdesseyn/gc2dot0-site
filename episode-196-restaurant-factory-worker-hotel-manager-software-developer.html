about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with ray morrison: boot camp grad and career changer ray morrison shares his path from restaurant worker to software engineer, emphasizing networking and consistent effort.

pick a boot camp based on your learning style, not just reputation. ray chose general assembly for full-time structure and accountability. networking beats cold applications. ray's first job came from a cousin referral after genuine conversation, not asking for favors. treat job search like a full-time job. ray studied eight to nine hours daily after boot camp while balancing family responsibilities. keep applying during wins. ray slowed down after a strong interview and missed a week of pipeline opportunities before getting the offer. ask about top performers in interviews to learn if company values long hours or actual contribution and mentorship. instead of asking about company culture, ask who the top performers are and what makes them stand out. treat the questions you already answer for people as ready made content instead of straining to invent new posts.
