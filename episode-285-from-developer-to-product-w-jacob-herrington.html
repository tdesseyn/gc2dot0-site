about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with jacob herrington: developer jacob herrington shares why he switched to product management and what changed moving from startups to walmart.

product managers get paid well and can use technical background to bridge engineering and business perspectives. being heard matters: product roles have strategic influence that individual contributors sometimes lack as engineers. sales skills are critical for product transitions because you must convince people your work has value. pace and risk change dramatically at scale; decisions at walmart involve physical and financial consequences startups never face. follow the money and learn to identify business value, then connect your work directly to revenue or impact. moving from a startup to a large company means adjusting your sense of scale since small decisions can carry much bigger consequences.
