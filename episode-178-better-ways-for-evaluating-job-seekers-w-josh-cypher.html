about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with josh cypher: how to redesign interview processes to better evaluate candidates through conversation and collaboration instead of rigid coding tests.

code challenges without context or conversation can eliminate good candidates unfairly. hiring managers should have intentional initial screens to understand what you actually need. pair programming exercises and real project work reveal how people think and collaborate. ask companies hard questions about salary, pace, and culture during interviews as much as they evaluate you. lean into your full background and unique skills when interviewing, not just technical credentials. when asking about compensation, get a salary range and ask what drives the top and bottom of that band. lean into your non-software background and personal experiences since they can set you apart in interviews.

In today's episode, I'm joined by Josh Cypher, DevOps Engineer at Sonos. We dive into his recent interviewing journey. Josh has some amazing thoughts on how the interview process can change for the better!
