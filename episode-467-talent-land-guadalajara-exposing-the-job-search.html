about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with taylor desseyn: how to stand out in tech job searches through resume optimization, networking, interviews, and consistent follow-up.

use bullet points on resumes, include specific keywords for your skills, and quantify your impact with numbers. network actively on linkedin by posting, sliding into dms professionally, and engaging with others' content. research companies before interviews, find problems they're solving, and speak directly to how you help. write down job description details before interviews to reference when you blank out from nerves. follow up consistently with contacts using tools like trello to track outreach and stay top of mind. before an interview find commonalities with the interviewer and research what problem the company is trying to solve. always follow up after an interview since checking back in can turn a soft no into a yes.

honored to speak at talent land in guadalajara this april.shared a few hard truths (and some strategies) about job searching in tech right now. check it out.
