about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with lawrence lockhart: how to stand out in a tough job market through quality work, strategic positioning, and building your network to get real opportunities.

apply to way more jobs than you think, expand beyond one role type, and consider adjacent roles like support engineer or qa. build projects that show growth and solve real problems you care about, not just tutorial clones. get referrals and networking to your applications in front of hiring managers faster than applying cold. consider non-traditional career paths like sys admin or business analyst as stepping stones to your target role. show up in person at meetups and conferences even if you're introverted, building relationships one or two people at a time. make at least one project personal and meaningful since passion for your own work signals you'll care about the employer's work. get out of your head and network in small chunks, even as an introvert, since people often make the difference in landing a job.
