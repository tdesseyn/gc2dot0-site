about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with rich rader: how to transition from individual contributor to effective leader by building influence, supporting your team, and creating a culture of growth.

understand what others want before telling them what to do, like fishing with what fish want not what you prefer. set a clear vision and explain the why so your team understands where you're going and works toward success. protect your team from burnout even if it costs you professionally because trust and loyalty compound over time. protecting your team sometimes costs you personally, so decide early whether you will defend your people even if it affects how you look to bosses. pair juniors and seniors as mentors for each other so growth keeps moving up and down the ladder, not just top down.

I caught up with dear friend Rich Rader on his journey to becoming a leader in software. Hope you enjoy!
