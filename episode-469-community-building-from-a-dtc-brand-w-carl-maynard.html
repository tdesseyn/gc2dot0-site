about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with carl maynard: how dtc brands build community through consistent presence, authentic relationships, and storytelling over transactional sales tactics.

show up consistently in the same spaces with the same people instead of chasing new audiences constantly. focus storytelling on people and their experiences, not product features or benefits. remove yourself from the credit and recognition so community members feel supported not sold to. measure community success by genuine relationships and feeling rather than data points or roi. community cant be measured by a clean roi so dont force a data point onto every event. let the brand or product sit in the background of the story instead of making it the main focus.

I am hanging out with my dear friend Carl who is head of creative and community at the DTC brand freaks of nature. talking about community building from the lense of DTC.
