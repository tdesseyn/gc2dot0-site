about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: understand what problems are actually worth leaving a job over before you jump to the next opportunity.

know your full compensation package including base, bonus, benefits costs, 401k match, hsa, and pto before comparing offers. try having a direct conversation with your manager about concerns before deciding to leave. avoid jumping ship just because it feels easier than confrontation or hard conversations with leadership. nine month stints on your resume signal instability, so be thoughtful before making your next move. try a direct conversation with leadership before quitting, since many issues can be addressed in a meeting. when raising concerns with a manager, frame it as how you feel and bring suggestions instead of attacking.

This episode is just me, myself & I giving you some thoughts on what you should do before you jump to a new company.
