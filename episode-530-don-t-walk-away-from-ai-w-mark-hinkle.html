about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with mark hinkle: learn why avoiding ai is a mistake and how to apply it practically to your work and career without replacing your role.

28% of knowledge worker time goes to email and 50% to copying data between systems—ai can reclaim that time. don't ask what ai can do, ask what you should do with the time it frees up for your actual work. build an ai stack using different models for different tasks since no single model excels at everything. own your data by connecting ai to your email, calendar and files so agents know your context. in-person relationships remain critical because remote-only work disconnects you from your community. ask yourself not just what ai can do but what you should actually hand off to it. keep showing up to in person events since trust and empathy still get built face to face, not through a screen.
