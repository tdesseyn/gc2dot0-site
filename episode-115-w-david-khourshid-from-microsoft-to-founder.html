about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with David Khourshid: how open source contributions led to a microsoft job and then to founding a company, plus what matters in hiring beyond salary.

open source work gets you noticed by big tech companies and builds real community around what you build. working at big tech companies is less necessary now than before, especially if you find a role matching your interests elsewhere. interview conversations should focus on candidate goals and company mission fit, not just qualifications and benefits lists. salary needs to be competitive but won't retain people if the work itself doesn't interest them. early startup hires can skip technical interviews if you already know their work from open source.
