about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to search for and evaluate recruiters on linkedin using specific criteria like experience and personality.

evaluate recruiter profiles for personality and genuine engagement before reaching out to them. when messaging recruiters, use specific odd numbers for time requests like seven or nine minutes instead of five. follow up with recruiters regularly on a calendar schedule to stay top of mind during your job search. build relationships with recruiters before you need a job, then set reminders to check back in every few weeks. search linkedin for technical recruiter plus your city to find relevant recruiters fast. check a recruiter's connection count and years of experience before reaching out, aim for at least two years in the industry. look for personality in a recruiter's profile, not just their title or company.
