about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: recruiter taylor dessen discusses how to evaluate and work with recruiters, what makes strong resumes, and navigating the tech job search.

look for recruiters with 2+ years experience at same company who meet candidates face-to-face, not just by phone. make resumes quantitative with specific metrics and results rather than generic soft skills like 'works well with others'. keep linkedin profiles simple with 1-2 front end tech, main mid-tier language, and back-end tools; mark yourself open to opportunities. work with 1-2 trusted recruiters to avoid duplicate submissions that cancel each other out. use linkedin and twitter to find internal recruiters at companies and ask directly about openings instead of relying on job descriptions. keep your linkedin and resume simple by listing only your main front end, mid tier and back end technologies rather than every tool you have touched. stick to working with about two recruiters at a time and avoid mass submitting your resume to job postings since recruiters cannot represent you once you have applied directly.
