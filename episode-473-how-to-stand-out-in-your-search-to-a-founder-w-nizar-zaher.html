about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with nizar zaher: how to stand out in job applications by doing research, networking, building public projects, and taking extra effort instead of relying on ai-generated materials.

research the company deeply and find ways to contact founders directly via dm or in-person rather than using generic applications. build and share projects publicly on social media to demonstrate you can work independently and take initiative. video is a powerful medium to show your work and communicate your skills during applications and interviews. most companies are always hiring for exceptional people even without posted job openings if you bring real value. companies now hire based on what you build in public and your online presence more than traditional resumes. post what you're building on x consistently so companies can see your work before you ever apply. practice explaining your work out loud since communicating clearly is as valuable as the coding itself.
