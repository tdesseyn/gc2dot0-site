about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with emmett naughton: how emmett got laid off twice in a year, stayed resilient through five months of job searching, and landed a technical support role by networking authentically.

share what you find interesting on social media and tag creators; people notice and opportunities follow over time. book interviews and networking calls back-to-back during your quietest hours of the day to stay focused and top of mind. when the job market is rough, read the room before negotiating; sometimes getting back into tech matters more than salary. be the same person online as you are in person; authenticity and consistency open doors that image management closes. cheer other people on and be intentional with your time; your network and attitude matter as much as your skills. a support or non-coding tech role can still keep you in the industry and valuable to a company.

I am joined by my friend Emmett Naughton who has absolutely BATTLED when it has come to the job search. He has a really cool story on being resilient.
