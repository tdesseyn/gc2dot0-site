about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with kyle allen: how to speed up hiring, what candidates really value beyond salary, and leadership growth areas.

companies hiring for talent fit vs role requirements make faster decisions and attract scrappy teams. candidates undervalue flexibility and autonomy; a $15k raise after taxes is roughly $4 extra per hour. hiring is only a priority if leadership makes it one; lack of response in four days signals low commitment. leaders must listen more, show up to small events, slow down decisions, and own bad hires quickly. do technical assessments live as a paired exercise during the interview instead of giving take home tests.
