about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with alex berk: soft skills and genuine connection matter more than you think when landing tech jobs and building your career.

walk up and talk to people after events. one handshake led to a job offer before graduation. research people before interviews. find real connections about school, region, kids, or interests to disarm them. practice speaking constantly. church talks, meetups, streaming while coding, or volunteering all build communication skills. dm people on twitter and linkedin. hundreds of people do it, ten percent respond, and it leads to real opportunities. be genuinely interested in what others build. most senior people want to connect with interesting engineers, not cold applications. keep building your own small projects between jobs, like an app or tool, so you always have something recent to show and talk about. cold dming people on linkedin or twitter about roles or questions can lead directly to interviews, even without a warm intro.
