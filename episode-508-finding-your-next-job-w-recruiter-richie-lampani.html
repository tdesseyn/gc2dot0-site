about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with richie lampani: recruiter richie lampani shares job market trends and job search tactics in a challenging hiring environment focused on specialized backend roles.

build real relationships with recruiters who know their space and check in monthly with genuine updates. apply to jobs still, but prioritize networking and engagement over volume of applications. specialize deeply in one area like backend, devops, or java rather than becoming a generalist. avoid fully ai-generated resumes that use obvious templates and instead personalize ai assistance. think beyond saas tech roles into manufacturing, healthcare, and other industries hiring engineers. add a short italic description under unfamiliar company names on your resume so recruiters understand the context of your experience. after a rejection thank the recruiter and ask if they know anyone else hiring, since recruiters often pass good candidates to each other.
