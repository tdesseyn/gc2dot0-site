about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with rishab kumar: cloud engineer rishab kumar shares his path from gas station to staff engineer at twilio, covering breaking into tech, landing big tech roles, and navigating ai's impact on software development.

be direct in emails and cold outreach by researching the company and showing how your skills solve their specific problems. unpaid internships can be worthwhile if they lead to real opportunities, but go far beyond minimum expectations to build trust. ai tools like github copilot increase productivity and help identify patterns but won't replace engineers in the near term. stay consistent with your big picture career goal even when facing culture shock or challenges early in your career. practice soft skills by recording yourself talking through technical topics and reviewing the tapes over time. when reaching out for a job, skip the fluff and directly explain how your skills solve the company's specific problem.
