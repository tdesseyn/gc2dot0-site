about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: Gone are the days of ping pong tables and beer on tap. Listen to this episode to hear my thoughts on what is important now!.

culture now centers on vulnerability, trust, and personal connection rather than office amenities. regular one-on-ones and reviews matter more than before; they should be conversations, not just checkboxes. leaders must explain the why behind decisions and initiatives to build buy-in and prevent resentment. admitting burnout and being your authentic self as a leader gives permission for team members to do the same. when evaluating a company, ask current employees about culture and request multiple interviews to assess fit. explain the why behind new initiatives at work because skipping that breeds distrust instead of buy in. when interviewing ask to speak with other team members about culture and request a second interview if you still are not sure.
