about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with adewale abati: staff dev advocate shares how creating value and expressing yourself openly builds a lasting career in tech.

share your work and struggles openly. it resonates with people and compounds over time. get involved in communities, help solve problems, and let opportunities come to you naturally. document what you learn and build. it sharpens your thinking and gives you material to discuss. think about the why behind your projects. understand who benefits and what problem you solve. getting involved in open source or community slacks solving problems can open doors you never expected. in interviews, frame your projects around the value and impact they created, not just the tech stack used.

Joined by Staff Dev Advocate Adewale (Ace) Abati from Block today to talk about his career growing up in Nigeria to being a Staff Engineer and the importance of providing/adding value to get where you want to go!
