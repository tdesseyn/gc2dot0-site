hiring off eq

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with brad miller and andrew may: how startups evaluate personality and emotional intelligence alongside technical skills when hiring developers at all levels.

assess personality fit before technical skills by having casual conversations to see if candidates handle startup pace. use coding tests including unsolvable problems to watch how candidates handle failure and ask for help. for junior devs, prioritize how they respond to not knowing something over perfect solutions. for senior devs, focus on leadership qualities shown outside work like community involvement or coaching. build teams in open spaces where collaboration happens naturally rather than isolated cubicles. ask about hobbies and life outside work, leading a kids sports team or community group shows leadership that is hard to fake. if you are an introvert who codes alone, join a team or attend meetups regularly to build the people skills hiring managers look for.

In this episode I catch up with Brad Miller (@_bradmiller) and Andrew May (@mantamay) about what they look for when they hire for Codetank Labs and how EQ plays a big part of it!
