about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with Drew Robbins: bootcamp graduate shares how to navigate the job search after graduation through networking, linkedin activity, and persistence.

bootcamp structure differs from college and self-taught learning. choose one that matches your learning style and has strong career support. after bootcamp graduation, treat your job search like a full-time job. create a daily routine with specific goals for networking, applying, and skill-building. use linkedin strategically by sending personalized connection requests, responding to all recruiter outreach, and posting visual content consistently. rejection and ghosting are normal in job searching. track applications in a spreadsheet so you can reapply to companies with future openings. get organized and do uncomfortable things daily. consistency in networking, commenting, and engagement led to recruiter outreach and job opportunities. always reply to recruiters even to decline, since responding back is a small effort that builds goodwill and future opportunities. track every job you apply to in a spreadsheet so you can revisit rejections and reapply when new openings come up.
