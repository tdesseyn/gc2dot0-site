about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: learn what hiring and leadership looks like across industries, from restaurant management to tech, with practical advice on interviews, character, and employee retention.

stand out in interviews by showing your character and integrity, not just experience or credentials. ownership and accountability matter more than perfection; employees who own mistakes and ask for help outperform those who repeat errors. check in on people's lives and mental health as a leader; connection and caring drive retention better than pay alone. young people need to develop face-to-face communication skills; ability to talk to people matters across any industry. look for ownership, responsibility, and people who ask questions and take initiative without waiting to be told what to do. as a leader, check in on people's lives outside of work, not just their tasks, so they feel a real connection. when someone makes a mistake, want ownership and a plan to improve rather than excuses or blame.
