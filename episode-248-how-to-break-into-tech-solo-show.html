about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: practical strategies for entering tech without college or bootcamp, emphasizing networking and structured daily routines.

bootcamps offer valuable community; if self-paced, spend 8am-12pm networking on linkedin and twitter daily. craft dms with three parts: admiration, specific question, request seven minutes of time not five. build projects publicly, ask for help, and document your learning journey on social media. networking matters more than github activity; genuine engagement and asking questions opens doors. people want to be heard; junior developers can still add value by learning from others' experiences. build in public by posting what you are learning and building so people can follow your journey and eventually reach out.
