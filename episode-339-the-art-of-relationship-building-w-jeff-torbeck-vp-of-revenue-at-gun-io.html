about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with jeff torbeck: building relationships and community in tech is more effective than cold calling. focus on being of service, ask genuine questions, and create warm connections instead of pushing sales.

be of service first. help people without expecting immediate return. keep your door open to questions about career building. post on linkedin about real things you think about, including personal life. authenticity matters more than perfection or follower counts. understand buyer timing and intent before reaching out. spam and double calls damage relationships. meet buyers when they want to buy. build a small trusted group of professionals you can vent to and problem-solve with. this matters more than a huge network. talk to people already in the role you want before chasing a title, then ask yourself why you actually want it.
