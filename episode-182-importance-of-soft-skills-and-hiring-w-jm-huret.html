about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with jm huret: soft skills, authentic interviewing, and viewing candidates as whole people matter more than technical gatekeeping in hiring decisions.

show up as a real person in interviews, not just technical skills, especially in remote settings. ask why candidates made decisions about their work, not just what they built or know. interview processes should mimic actual day-to-day work, not artificial algorithm problems. ignore red flags about trust and fit at your peril; poor hiring creates cascading team problems. junior engineers need passion, good questions, and ability to research independently more than perfect knowledge. when you lack experience in something, explain how you approached a similar problem rather than giving a flat no. pay attention early in your career to whether you actually enjoy the work itself, not just the money it can bring.

In today's episode, I'm joined by a very special guest, JM Huret. JM is the Head of Engineering for Vaco's software consulting arm, Vaco Built. We dive into 3 main topics: soft skills, looking at the bigger picture of a person when interviewing, and mistakes on hiring.
