about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to search for jobs on linkedin, connect with hiring people, and follow up to get your resume in front of decision makers.

search job titles on linkedin, review required qualifications, then tailor your professional summary to match those skills. connect with people at companies posting jobs, not just apply blindly through linkedin's job portal. craft direct dms that mention something specific about the company or person, state your relevant skills, and ask for next steps. apply through the company website after connecting with someone, and ask for a referral code to tag their system. follow up in three to four days after sending materials to show initiative and keep things moving in the search process. always click connect instead of follow, since follow does not open a direct way to message someone. craft dms that name a specific connection point, state your fit for the role, and end by asking if they want your resume or who else to contact.
