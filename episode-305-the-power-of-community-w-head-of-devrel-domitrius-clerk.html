about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how community building drives career growth and why companies need to stop treating it as a sales tactic.

move developer communities off slack to discord for better discoverability and message history accessibility. community building requires genuine relationships and consistent presence, not follower counts or acquisition tactics. hiring people who can build community becomes more valuable as companies realize the business impact of real engagement. burnout is real in community and content roles, so build a network of supporters and be honest about your limits. consistency in community work beats perfection; start small, show up regularly, and focus on serving the people actually there. build trust by giving people honest answers even when it means pointing them to a different product or solution. when starting out, prove your value to a community first through things like meetups or workshops before expecting anything back.
