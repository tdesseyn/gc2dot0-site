about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to optimize your linkedin profile to get recruiter attention through banners, titles, featured sections, and keyword strategy.

banner photo is critical since recruiters see it first, include at least your email address. use specific technical titles like dot net developer or react developer so recruiters can find you in searches. feature side projects not your resume to encourage recruiters to message and engage with you directly. write your about section like marketing copy with a hook to draw readers in beyond just bullet points. treat linkedin as a living document to test and iterate what attracts recruiter attention. put specific keywords like exact languages or frameworks in your title since recruiters search by title not by scrolling profiles. add real numbers and comparisons to your accomplishments instead of vague claims like increased user retention.
