about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with james quick: navigate job market uncertainty through networking, content creation, and community involvement to build career resilience.

build your network before you need it by consistently sharing content and engaging authentically in communities. content acts as the ultimate validator for skills and qualities that resumes claim but cannot prove. start networking somewhere, even small: twitter, discord, blogs, or local meetups all create visibility and community. follow up with cold messages two to three times max, with empathy and specific reasons why you're reaching out. ask your employer for learning time and resources instead of always building side projects after hours to protect work-life balance. ask your employer for time and resources to learn new things rather than assuming all growth has to happen on unpaid personal time.
