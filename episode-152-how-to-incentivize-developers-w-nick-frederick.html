about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with nick frederick: how to retain developers through communication, trust, and flexible work arrangements in a remote-first environment.

share business health, pipeline opportunities, and company direction with your entire team regularly. conduct one-on-one meetings with each team member to understand how they want to be communicated with. start with 100% trust in new hires and pay closer attention early to set clear expectations. meet people where they are with flexibility on location while finding middle ground on in-office time if needed. hire for cultural fit and ability to learn over specific technical skills since technology changes quickly. commission plans for developers sound appealing but are hard to execute since you cant fairly attribute revenue to individual code.
