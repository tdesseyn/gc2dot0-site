about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with christina aldan: personal branding, emotional intelligence, and conference networking strategies for tech professionals and job seekers.

emotional intelligence has four parts: self-awareness, awareness of others, self-management, and managing others; pick one facet to improve for three months. introverts can build visibility through blog posts, mentorship, one-on-one shares, or summarizing others' talks instead of creating original content. at conferences, connect with one or two people deeply rather than trying to attend everything; use hallway conversations and group chats. be kind to yourself internally; how you treat yourself shows up in how you treat others and impacts your professional reputation. summarizing a talk or article you learned from counts as content and speakers appreciate it. at conferences, pick one or two people to connect with and offer to volunteer or live tweet to attend cheaply.
