about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with david pine: how to get into big tech through passion, open source, conferences, and networking rather than credentials alone.

start with github to explore open source; find a contributing.md file and issues tab to begin. conferences and meetups are the main networking channel for big tech jobs; hallway tracks matter. speak, blog, and build in public to show passion; leadership recognizes genuine enthusiasm. don't pressure yourself to meet everyone at conferences; one handshake can lead to opportunities later. be patient with career growth; move fast but expect setbacks and learn from them. at conferences spend real time in the hallway track asking questions and talking to people between sessions. big tech roles often come from letting your passion show through speaking, blogging, and community involvement rather than chasing buzzwords.
