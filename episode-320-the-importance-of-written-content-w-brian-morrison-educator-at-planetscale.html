about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with brian morrison: written content offers speed and flexibility for tech educators compared to video, and networking at conferences matters more than attending sessions.

written content is faster to produce than video and lets you work from anywhere, not just at a desk setup. good written content gets to the point quickly without filler, like recipe blogs that make you scroll through backstory. you can start in tech without being a developer first by automating painful tasks and proving value to your company. at conferences, skip the polished pitch and just sit with strangers at lunch, listen more than you talk, build real connections. being consistent and seeing projects through matters more than chasing every new shiny thing in your career. look for tasks you can automate or problems you can solve at any job, that extra effort builds reputation with management over time. at conferences focus on meeting people over attending every session, keep introductions short and let the other person talk more than you do.
