what is 'culture'

feb 1, 2021 · 15 min · hosted by taylor desseyn

3 takeaways

  • > build culture around we not me, make team success the shared win instead of individual stats.
  • > run short daily huddles, five to ten minutes, to get everyone aligned before the work starts.
  • > create a safe space where people can be open and vulnerable without fear of judgment.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: understanding what makes good team culture and how to build it in your organization.

focus on we not me. celebrate team wins and make success about the group effort. be open and vulnerable as a leader. let people be themselves at work and they will perform better. listen first before solving. affirm what people say and make them feel heard before jumping to action. keep culture practices simple. short team check-ins or hangouts work better than long meetings. give people trust and space to work but hold them accountable for results. promote an open door policy so your team feels comfortable bringing issues directly to you.
