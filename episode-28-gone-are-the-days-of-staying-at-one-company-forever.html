gone are the days of staying at one company forever

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: job hopping is normal now, but you need to tell a clear story about why you moved between jobs.

include short job stints on your resume if you gained relevant skills; explain the reason in interviews. average job duration is around one to two years; shorter stints are normal for contract or project work. staying at one company for years isn't bad if you show growth, innovation, and new skills learned. when listing long tenure at one company, document your promotions and role changes on resume and linkedin. employers reject candidates who can't explain job transitions; preparation and storytelling matter most.
