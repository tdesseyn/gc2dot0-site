3 takeaways

  • > at conferences, pick a handful of people you actually want to connect with and leave once you have met them.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: learn how to network authentically by being genuinely interested in others instead of selling yourself.

be curious about other people, not sleazy. just listen and ask genuine questions. when reaching out, be specific. give a time limit and topic instead of asking vaguely. pick five people at conferences or online to connect with deeply rather than working a room. use social media as a networking tool. document your journey and build community over time. networking is friendship, not selling. use context and be real in every message. treat networking as making friends, not making a pitch, and keep outreach messages short and real. if you want to get serious about using linkedin for networking, cut distracting apps and put your focus there instead.
