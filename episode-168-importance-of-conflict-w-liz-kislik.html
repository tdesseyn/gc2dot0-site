about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with liz kislik: how to manage conflict and emotional reactions at work through body awareness and mindfulness techniques.

ground yourself physically when anger rises by noticing your feet, looking around, or feeling textures to calm your brain. refer to yourself in second person to create distance from harsh self-judgment and speak to yourself with grace. build flexibility by breaking routines and habits gradually, taking smallest steps to avoid triggering overwhelm. recognize your brain naturally sticks to negatives for survival, but actively remember accomplishments and skills you already have. find people to model yourself after and create milestones based on qualities you want to emulate. look back at what you have already accomplished before assuming you are failing or an imposter.

In today's episode, I'm joined by Liz Kislik, wearer of MANY professional hats- Contributor to Harvard Business Review, Management consultant, Executive coach, and TEDx speaker. We dive into the importance of conflict when it comes to leadership.
