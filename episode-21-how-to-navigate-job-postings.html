how to navigate job postings

feb 19, 2021 · 11 min · hosted by taylor desseyn

3 takeaways

  • > before submitting your resume to a job posting, try bcc'ing recruiter contacts and asking if they have connections or are working that role.
  • > if you lack contacts at a company, go ahead and submit your resume but also reach out to the hr manager or recruiter to ask about next steps.
  • > remote policies keep shifting, so apply to onsite roles in a city you want to move to and use a remote contract period to slowly research and relocate.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to navigate job postings effectively by connecting with recruiters and hiring managers instead of just submitting resumes blindly.

if you must submit your resume, connect with hr or recruiters at the company first to get on their radar. only submit your resume to positions matching your experience level and salary range to avoid wasting time. use remote job postings as a way to explore new cities and companies before committing to a full move. job descriptions are often stuffed with keywords by hr, so only apply within your actual years of experience and skill level instead of chasing every listed requirement. spending an hour messaging people at companies with open roles beats spending that same hour mass submitting resumes around town.
