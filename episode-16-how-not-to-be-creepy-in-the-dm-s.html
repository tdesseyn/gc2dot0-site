how not to be creepy (in the dm's)

feb 8, 2021 · 12 min · hosted by taylor desseyn

3 takeaways

  • > before dming someone, comment on and engage with their posts for a while so you are not a stranger.
  • > when you do dm, reference something specific the person did or posted instead of just saying hi or whats up.
  • > use the jab jab jab right hook approach, give value through comments and likes before you ask for anything.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to dm people without being weird when networking, focusing on genuine engagement before reaching out.

comment on or admire something specific someone did before sending them a dm. avoid generic openings like hi or how are you, they won't get responses. interact with people's posts and comments first to build familiarity before direct messaging. recruiters should never claim they're different or promise specific numbers in cold messages. when someone changes their job status on linkedin, comment and dm them asking how they found that job to start a conversation. dont open a recruiting or sales dm with claims about being different or promising huge results, just be yourself and stay low key.
