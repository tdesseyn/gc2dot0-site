about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with ashley hunsberger: how to build teams and culture by putting people first, setting boundaries, and leading with motivation instead of fear.

learn what motivates each team member early. involve them in goal-setting alongside company objectives. use one-on-ones for relationship building and coaching, not status updates. get project info from slack or standups. reflect on when you think best. schedule deep work, creative time, and listening during your peak hours. model boundaries yourself. if you take a four-day week or clock off at five, your team feels permission to do the same. defrag your calendar around your natural energy so strategic thinking, creative work, and listening happen at the right times of day.
