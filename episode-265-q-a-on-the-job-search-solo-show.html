about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: taylor answers job search questions live, covering networking, freelancing, contract work, and standing out in a tougher market.

mass applying alone doesn't work anymore—you need to do something different and be uncomfortable. volunteer or free work first gets you sticky with clients and can lead to full time roles. research companies, prepare questions, and show genuine interest—lazy interviewing doesn't cut it now. networking means actually showing up and engaging—comment, say hey, connect with people you see. consider contract or part time gigs as entry points to full time opportunities. be willing to take contract or part time work since it can turn into a full time job within a few months. show up prepared to interviews, research the company and ask real questions since employers are being more selective now.
