about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with jason lengstorf: how to retain engineering talent by prioritizing work-life balance, trust-based leadership, and smarter hiring practices instead of competing on salary alone.

give company-wide days off to let people actually take time off without worrying about coverage or coordination. burnout reduces productivity more than extra hours gain it; humans work best at 30-50 hours weekly with real detachment. engineering managers often lack leadership training and treat people like systems instead of building trust and providing context. batch interview cycles into fewer days and use story-based questions to assess cultural fit, not just technical ability. losing senior talent loses institutional knowledge and mentorship capacity, which costs way more than time off ever would. ask story based interview questions like tell me about a conflict with a coworker to spot red flags early. batch interview stages close together so you do not lose candidates to faster moving companies.
