about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how the tech job market is shifting, what hiring managers and candidates need to know right now.

hiring managers must understand what motivates each employee beyond salary to retain them. candidates are receiving multiple job offers weekly and switching jobs frequently without reputation damage. don't lowball candidates on salary offers or they will keep searching and leave. hire for potential and culture fit rather than requiring every single skill listed. candidates should take unpaid work and real experience over paying for more degrees. junior devs can build real experience through unpaid intern or volunteer work instead of spending money on bootcamp or coursework. hiring managers should stop expecting every skill on the list and instead hire for attitude and train up from there.
