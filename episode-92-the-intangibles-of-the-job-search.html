about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how being kind, grateful and empathetic during your job search makes you stand out to hiring managers.

respond to rejection emails with gratitude and grace to stay top of mind for future openings. hiring managers often pick second or third choice candidates who showed the most kindness during interviews. build depth in relationships with fewer people rather than width with many casual contacts. deploy empathy consistently even when rejected because it shows your character as a person. send a thank you message or linkedin note right after interviewing to stay top of mind. avoid venting or being negative on social media since hiring managers can see your public comments and activity. reply to rejection emails with gratitude since it shows your character to the hiring manager. being kind after a rejection can put you first in line if the top candidate falls through.
