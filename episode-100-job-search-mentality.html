about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how your attitude and kindness shape your job search success and career trajectory.

staying positive during rejection and viewing new connections as opportunities shifts your job search momentum. being authentically yourself attracts the right opportunities and the right companies for you. gratitude and kindness toward hiring managers and recruiters lower interview barriers and improve outcomes. researching companies, asking questions, and showing enthusiasm matters more than having all the answers. flipping your mindset from dreading the job search to being curious and eager changes how interviews go. sending a genuine thank you note after an interview, even a bad one, shows gratitude that pays off later.
