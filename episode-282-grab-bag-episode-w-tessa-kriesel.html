about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with tessa kriesel: developer relations strategies for building authentic relationships, trust and community with developers through events, content and strategic engagement.

developers need four to five touchpoints minimum before engaging; build relationships before asking for conversions. in-person conference attendance creates trust that remote engagement cannot; it's the finishing touch that solidifies relationships. host social events early in conferences (dinners, birds of a feather sessions) to build multiple touchpoints and memorable interactions. avoid sales language and marketing jargon with developers; provide facts, inspiration and value before making any ask. create transparent documentation of your devrel strategy, team roles, roadmap and objectives so leadership understands your function and impact. use birds of a feather sessions at conferences to find and connect with your specific niche audience. build genuine relationships with developers instead of selling to them since they can tell when they are being pitched.
