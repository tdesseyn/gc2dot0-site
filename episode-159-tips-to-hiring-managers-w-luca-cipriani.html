about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with luca cipriani: how hiring managers can recruit passively, what distinguishes mid from senior engineers, and using okrs to align tech and business teams.

managers should actively recruit by engaging candidates on twitter, github, and open source projects before they job search. seniority comes from honest communication and pushback, not just technical skills or years of experience. hire diverse teams with juniors and mids because they help seniors grow through mentoring while learning themselves. involve engineers in design and product planning so they understand user impact and business value from the start. use okrs to set transparent, measurable objectives across the entire company to align tech and business goals. build diverse teams with a mix of junior and senior people since mentoring junior talent helps senior engineers grow too.

Joined by Luca Cipriani, Engineering Manager and OKR Coach out of Turin, Italy, we dive into recruiting tips for managers, expectations of mid-senior engineers, and how to achieve alignment with the business and tech!
