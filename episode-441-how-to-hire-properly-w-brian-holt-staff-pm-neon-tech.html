about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with brian holt: learn what hiring managers actually look for in candidates and how to stand out from application through interview.

show genuine opinions about the company's product and come prepared with specific feedback or improvements. make interviews a conversation instead of a lecture; interviewers evaluate culture fit and whether they want to work with you daily. focus on your thinking process and reasoning rather than having perfect answers; silence and pausing to think is fine. build visibility through open source, conferences, social media posts, or discord communities rather than relying on resume submissions. stay flexible with your career path; being on year one of your five year plan leaves room for unexpected opportunities. let candidates use a real code editor and google things during interviews since that mirrors how engineers actually work.
