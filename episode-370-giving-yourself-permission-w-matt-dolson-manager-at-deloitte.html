about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with matt dolson: how to give yourself permission to lead and grow without waiting for formal titles or approval from others.

apprenticeship and mentorship happen when experienced people intentionally share knowledge through real feedback and preparation. you don't need a formal program to start mentoring; create something like weekly email reflections if you see a need. leadership is a skill anyone can show up with daily, not just a positional title you wait to receive. know yourself first through values exercises and self-awareness before expecting others to find the right role for you. think of your next move as right for now, not your final destination; focus on what sets you up for your longer vision. before chasing a new role, get clear on your own values and who you are so you can tell if a culture fits.
