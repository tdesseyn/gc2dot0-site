about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with jonah dimeo: how to find and work with specialized recruiters who understand your tech career goals and can advocate for you long-term.

interview recruiters the same way they interview you, asking about their specialty and experience in your field. look for recruiters who have been in the industry and your local market for at least two years to ensure market knowledge. check a recruiter's connections on linkedin to see if they specialize in your area, since their network reflects their focus. if changing careers, build a portfolio or volunteer work in your new field to make yourself attractive to recruiters. if you're a junior developer, ask recruiters what skills show up on placed candidates' resumes that you're missing.
