about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with steve alverson: understand rsu's, stock options, vesting schedules and how equity compensation works at public and private companies.

stocks are ownership in a company; rsu's are shares you receive, options let you buy shares at a set price. private company shares are only worth something when there's a liquidity event like investment or sale. ask about company profitability, cash reserves, and timeline to going public when evaluating equity offers. don't put all your eggs in one stock basket; diversify and consider taxes on equity compensation. ask about a company's cash reserves and viability before accepting an offer, especially at smaller or private companies. don't let company stock become a huge chunk of your net worth since smaller company share prices can be very volatile.
