about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with Jerome Hardaway: remote work is now essential for companies to attract talent and build healthy communities, not cultures, by focusing on genuine human connection and data-driven decisions.

remote isn't new but is now required to compete for talent; geography no longer justifies lower pay or forced office attendance. companies claiming collaboration is better in-person lack data to support it; wordpress, gitlab, and nonprofits prove remote teams innovate successfully. build remote community by automating social processes, holding public conversations to prevent cliques, and leading with genuine care for people. fire executives resisting remote work; they're costing you top talent while smaller competitors hire the best people they refuse. add value before asking anything; showcase constant work output, keep github profiles updated, and be human first to build trust remotely. add value before you ask for anything, whether raising money, networking, or trying to get hired. build and show real projects instead of just a portfolio, since demonstrated work speaks louder than claims.
