about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to stand out in your job search by networking, engaging online, and focusing on jobs that fit your current life season.

comment and engage online consistently to build name recognition and attract your tribe. be fearless and reach out to hiring managers and recruiters you admire without fear of rejection. try messaging people on sites where they have fewer followers since you are more likely to get a response. be fearless and message that hiring manager, recruiter, or senior developer you have been hesitant to reach out to. stop chasing a dream job and instead look for the right job for the season of life you are in. build name recognition by consistently commenting and engaging on posts from people and companies you want to work with. being present and consistent at events like meetups or conferences counts as networking even without talking to people.
