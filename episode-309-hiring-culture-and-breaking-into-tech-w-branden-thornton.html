about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with branden thornton: breaking into tech from any background by building a portfolio, networking intentionally, and learning design fundamentals through practice and real projects.

design roles require a strong portfolio showing your process and problem-solving, not just polished final designs. networking at conferences and through genuine conversations matters more than blind applications to big tech companies. you can learn design through youtube, online resources, and hands-on practice alongside experienced designers. any job like retail or food service teaches you people skills that transfer directly into tech if you're intentional. startups value hunger and passion over fancy degrees; your background matters less than your willingness to learn. keep making genuine conversations and collecting contacts wherever you work now, since you never know where those relationships lead.
