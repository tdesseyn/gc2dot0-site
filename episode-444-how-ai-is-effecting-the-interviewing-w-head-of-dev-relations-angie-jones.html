about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with angie jones: how ai is reshaping hiring interviews and why candidates should use it ethically to stand out in a competitive job market.

hiring managers use ai to screen applications, so candidates using ai ethically during interviews is fair game. live coding and pair programming interviews let you demonstrate both ai tool skills and actual problem-solving ability. building in public on ai topics and posting consistently attracts opportunities even without high engagement metrics. get internal referrals before applying since referred resumes float to top of large applicant pools. being a specialist who goes deep in one area while staying generalist in others gives you competitive advantage. posting publicly about tools you use and problems you solve helps opportunities find you, even without likes or comments. ask someone directly to refer you before applying, since a referral can help your resume get noticed among many applicants.
