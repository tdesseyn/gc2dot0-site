about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with matt fesler: how to network, interview, and stand out as a job seeker with a hiring manager focused on helping candidates succeed.

reach out to hiring managers on linkedin without hesitation, ask for advice, set friendly tone to open doors. build public code repositories and be honest on your resume, only include what you can speak to in interviews. ask hiring managers thoughtful questions about their interview process and their expectations for your level. if you don't know an answer, stay in dialogue by connecting to similar work and showing willingness to learn. communicate career goals to hiring managers, give feedback on interview experience, work closely with recruiters. be fully honest on your resume and be ready to speak in depth about anything you list, no matter your seniority. after an interview, give tactful feedback through your recruiter or in the q&a portion to help improve the process for others.
