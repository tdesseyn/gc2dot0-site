about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with shauna kimball: how to find contentment in your career by separating personal feelings from company and industry issues, and prioritizing mission over compensation.

distinguish between company problems, personal problems, and situational problems before making career moves. list your accomplishments and frustrations at work to identify real patterns instead of reacting to single moments. evaluate total compensation including pto, benefits, and flexibility rather than base salary alone. prioritize influence and mission alignment over job titles and immediate pay increases. remove mental and emotional weight from career decisions to let your work speak for itself. weigh how much real influence and leadership buy-in a role offers before chasing a title or a bigger paycheck. in a tough job market, remember rejections and delays are often about the industry, not a reflection of you personally.

I am joined by Shauna Kimball who is Sr. Manager, DEI & University Programs for 2k. She has formerly been with Uber, Nike, and Meta!
