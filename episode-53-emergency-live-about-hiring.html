about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: the job market is extremely competitive right now. hiring requires remote work, higher pay, and smart recruiting strategies to succeed.

offer full remote work and pay above market rates or you won't attract candidates. work with recruiting firms that understand consulting, contracting, and contract-to-hire options. as a candidate, stay responsive with recruiters and be intentional about job applications. hiring managers should consider building junior and mid-level teams instead of only seeking senior talent. job seekers should stay organized, avoid being submitted to the same company twice, and check in consistently with recruiters. if you cant find candidates right now you are probably not offering full remote or not paying enough. expect lead times of two to three or even four weeks to fill open developer roles in this market. consider paying above typical recruiting fee rates if you want to land top candidates quickly.
