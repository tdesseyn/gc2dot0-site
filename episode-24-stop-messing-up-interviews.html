about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: common interview mistakes to avoid including research, preparation, video quality, authenticity and follow-up.

write down key accomplishments related to the job role before the interview. test your video setup, lighting and microphone to ensure good quality on calls. be your authentic self in interviews instead of trying to be someone you're not. skip the thank you letter and instead send a personalized connection note on linkedin after the interview. research the company before any interview so you can actually answer what they do. take phone interviews in a quiet place and have your resume in front of you for talking points. keep pen and paper handy during interviews to jot down questions so you dont forget them.
