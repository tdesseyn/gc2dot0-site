about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with emma bostian: navigate career demotivation by understanding your real motivations, building community support, and taking things one step at a time.

question why you want the next promotion before chasing it; lateral moves or different roles might serve you better. distinguish between industry problems and organization-specific problems before deciding to leave your job. find at least one person who understands your struggle; vulnerability and community are powerful motivators during job search. in interviews, admit what you don't know and show willingness to learn; growth mindset beats having all the answers. learn to use ai tools thoughtfully to enhance your skills, not to ship code you don't understand or bypass your responsibility. use ai to understand code history and improve your skills rather than having it generate code you do not review before shipping.
