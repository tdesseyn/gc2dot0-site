about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with tim cheadle: how to build career ladders that actually work and set clear expectations so people know how they're doing and can grow.

career ladders need to be specific about daily work breakdown, not just abstract company values. involve your team in creating expectations so they have buy-in and voice, not just top-down rules. education and school prestige matter less than problem-solving ability and real-world experience. working from home balance requires physical separation between work and life space when possible. familiarity with the exact tech stack is a plus but companies with room to train will look past it for strong problem solvers.
