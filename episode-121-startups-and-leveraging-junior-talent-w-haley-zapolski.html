about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with haley zapolski: how companies are starting to hire junior developers, what to ask during interviews, and building community in tech.

ask detailed questions during interviews about work-from-home policy, meeting frequency, and management style to avoid surprises. junior developers should interview the company as much as they're being interviewed to find the right fit. companies are beginning to hire bootcamp graduates out of necessity since traditional four-year degrees aren't producing enough talent. most companies are still figuring out training programs for junior developers, so look for structured onboarding before accepting offers. building community doesn't require reinventing anything, just consistency and showing up for people without expecting something in return. before accepting an offer get on another call with questions and see how willing the company is to invest in you. companies struggling to hire senior developers are increasingly building programs to train junior and bootcamp talent instead.
