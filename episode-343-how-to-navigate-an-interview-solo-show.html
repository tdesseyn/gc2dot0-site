about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: prepare for interviews by researching the company and interviewers, then tie your actual experiences to show genuine passion for the role.

research the company and the people interviewing you, then find real connections between their interests and your background. bring a pen, paper, and printed resume to stay focused and take notes instead of typing, which makes you look engaged not distracted. prepare three to four bullet points about your career that directly relate to the job you are interviewing for. ask thoughtful questions and have conversations instead of just answering questions, treat it like a real dialogue not an interrogation. if the interviewer is late or tech fails, email or text them instead of waiting silently, and always have their phone number. keep a pen and paper in front of you to take notes instead of typing, since hiring managers find typing more distracting and suspicious. print your resume, close extra tabs, and silence your phone so you stay focused and avoid blanking during the interview.
