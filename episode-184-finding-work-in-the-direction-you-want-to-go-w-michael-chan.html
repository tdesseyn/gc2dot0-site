about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with michael chan: build visibility for your career by sharing your work publicly and networking authentically, whether through content, community, or one-on-one connections.

networking is making genuine friends and connections, not passing business cards at events. find your own lane for sharing work based on where you naturally spend time, not what feels like high-pressure content creation. attend conferences with intention, take pictures or notes, tag speakers, and give value rather than treating it as a vacation. share imperfect work publicly because your worst work outside your head is better than your best work inside it. summarize or recap someone's talk or post online and tag them, it often gets noticed and shared. treat every conference like you paid for it yourself and prioritize meeting people over hanging with coworkers.
