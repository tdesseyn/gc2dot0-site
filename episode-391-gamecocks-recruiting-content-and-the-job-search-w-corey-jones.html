about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to stand out in tech interviews, network effectively, and adapt your job search strategy in a competitive market.

be energetic and pleasant in interviews; hiring managers sense negativity and it affects their decision. follow up consistently within 24 hours; speed and responsiveness are critical in today's market. develop a clear 90-second pitch about yourself; both job seekers and founders struggle with this. consider taking non-engineering roles to get inside a company and understand the business deeply. use hobbies, shared interests, and bcc email groups to build relationships and crowdsource your job search. add a short hobby line to your resume since shared interests can create real connections with hiring managers. stay pleasant and curious in interviews, even on tough questions, since energy and attitude are read closely right now.
