about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with matt walsh: how hiring managers can improve their interview process and how job seekers can navigate the current market through networking and strategic interviewing.

define your open role with specific key results and cultural competencies, not just a generic job description. build rapport with candidates and ask follow-up questions instead of letting them give packaged three-minute answers. coordinate your interview team so each person focuses on one area rather than asking everyone to make a yes-or-no decision. as a job seeker, prioritize networking to get introductions over mass applying to job postings. check in with the interviewer by summarizing your answer and offering to go deeper on specific points. treat most requirements in job descriptions as a kitchen sink wishlist and apply even if you only meet about half of them. if you want to move teams internally, outperform in your current role and ask both your manager and the target team what results they need to see from you over the next six months.
