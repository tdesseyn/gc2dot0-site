about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with rae lambert: how engaging on social media and hosting events builds visibility, and what startups should look for when hiring.

engineers often skip marketing and go-to-market strategy, limiting product adoption. hosting community events requires only 20 more minutes than attending but dramatically increases your visibility. hire people who are active answering questions in communities relevant to your tech stack. ask candidates to submit a loom video to assess communication and self-presentation skills. build a small, curated community around shared interests or struggles instead of chasing huge follower counts. when applying for jobs, record a short loom video introducing yourself and show side projects you built out of curiosity.
