how to find a recruiter (solo show)

apr 12, 2023 · 15 min · hosted by taylor desseyn

3 takeaways

  • > stop mass applying to job posts and instead message recruiters directly on linkedin to build real connections.
  • > search tech recruiters plus your city on linkedin and use the filters feature to narrow results.
  • > look for recruiters with at least two years in the industry and two years at their current company.

about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to find and approach recruiters on linkedin using specific search filters and evaluation criteria.

search linkedin for recruiters in your location, then filter results by looking for active profiles with inviting headshots. message recruiters even if they cannot help you directly, and ask for feedback on your resume or linkedin profile. request referrals to other recruiters if your contact cannot assist, since timing matters and networks matter. contact recruiters before mass applying to jobs online, as recruiters know the market better than job boards. when a recruiter cant help you directly ask for a quick favor like resume or linkedin feedback. if you find a job posting message a recruiter you know and ask if they can refer you or know someone there.
