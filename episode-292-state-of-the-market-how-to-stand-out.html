about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to stand out in interviews and job search by networking strategically, asking thoughtful questions, and building your professional presence.

connect with second and third degree connections on linkedin by asking your network to introduce you to people in target companies or roles. stay connected with people who reject you in interviews because hiring needs change and you could be perfect fit later. during interviews tie together your past experience with company problems to show you understand their challenges and have relevant solutions. mass connect with recruiters at target companies without sending notes then create content they will see in their feed. maintain sustainable job search habits with short daily effort rather than burnout sprints to stay mentally sharp and effective. during interviews, turn the q&a into a conversation by tying your answers and questions back to your own relevant experience.

I am joined by my dear friend Dan to talk about the state of hiring and how to stand out in the market right now especially during an interview!
