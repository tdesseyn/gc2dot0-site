about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with James Arnold: how in-house recruiters differ from agency recruiters and how to approach them effectively as a job seeker.

in-house recruiters focus on candidate experience and long-term relationships, while agency recruiters prioritize volume and placements. do your homework on the recruiter before reaching out and be specific about what you want from the conversation. network directly with people doing the job you want before connecting with recruiters to increase your chances. bring genuine curiosity and enthusiasm to interviews; it's half psychology and half technical answers. the ats is not rejecting you with hidden magic, a human reviews almost every application. treat interviews as a chance to learn even if they do not work out, every one teaches you something.
