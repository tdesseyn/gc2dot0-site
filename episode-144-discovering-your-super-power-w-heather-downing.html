about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with heather downing: learn what hiring managers look for in contractors, how to communicate effectively upward, and why building community intentionally matters for your career growth.

communicate quickly and clearly, collaborate with others, admit what you don't know, and know your unique superpower. time box your interviews to avoid burnout and talk with other developers about offer fairness before accepting. managing up means telling your manager what you need to perform best, not telling them how to do their job. ask targeted questions when seeking help from senior people and always report back with results to show gratitude. identify your north star goal to stay motivated and connect with the right people who can help you get there. stay in touch with former coworkers since they often become the ones who refer you to your next job. when asking a manager for help, bring what you have already done and what you plan to do next so they can just say yes or no.
