about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with drew dawson: how posting a two-minute video on linkedin got more job interview requests than months of resume submissions.

you don't need fancy equipment, lighting, or editing to make an effective video, just use your phone or computer camera. posting video helps you practice for video interviews and shows your personality in ways a resume cannot. watching yourself speak on video helps you improve communication skills and reduce filler words like um and so. a short intro video that covers background and strengths can double as practice for the common tell me about yourself interview question. posting one short video about your background and interests can replace weeks of applying to jobs one by one. a simple two minute video recorded on a phone with no editing or lighting can get strong response from recruiters and hiring managers. if video feels uncomfortable try live blogging on linkedin or recording audio instead to put yourself out there.
