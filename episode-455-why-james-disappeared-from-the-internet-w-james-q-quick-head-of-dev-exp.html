about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with James Q Quick: james q quick, head of dev experience at makes swift, explains why he stepped back from social media despite ongoing career success, discussing burnout, parenthood, and the reality of developer relations work beyond conference travel.

developer relations includes docs, internal content, and product education—not just conferences and social media visibility. going freelance requires financial runway, calculated risk, and understanding the pressure of continuous income generation. networking is showing up and having genuine conversations, not forced booth interactions; timing and relationships matter more than immediate pitches. attending conferences solo becomes easier with one or two trusted people; reaching out to online contacts before attending reduces anxiety. full-time employment provides stability, parental leave, and mental relief from the constant pressure of creating content for income. volunteering at a conference can get you a free or discounted ticket while still letting you attend talks. bring a friend to a conference so sitting down at a random table feels less intimidating.
