about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with brian walter: vp of engineering brian walter shares how triathlon training principles like goal-setting, rest, and discipline directly apply to leading engineering teams effectively.

stress plus rest equals growth; recovery is as important as pushing hard in both training and work. network strategically during job search rather than mass applying; warm introductions beat cold outreach. hire for team fit and communication first; you can teach technical skills but culture fit matters most. take career steps backward if it aligns with growth and engagement goals; titles matter less than impact. stay relevant as a non-coding engineering leader by understanding tech landscape and knowing who to ask. networking beats mass applying, cold outreach works better when you ask for an introduction instead of a job. in interviews prioritize team fit and communication over pure technical skill since technical ability can be taught.
