about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with donnie skalak: engineering leader shares hiring strategies, fractional work insights, and how to stand out in a tough tech job market through networking and intentionality.

target roles you genuinely care about and personalize your approach instead of mass applying to hundreds of jobs. reach out directly to hiring managers and recruiters on linkedin or via email to stand out from the resume pile. be flexible on salary, location, and level during downturns to get your foot in the door with the right company. job descriptions matter hugely for both hiring and applying, so engineering leaders should write their own instead of using generic templates. if a company gives you a canned rejection or no feedback at all after an onsite interview thats a red flag about their culture.
