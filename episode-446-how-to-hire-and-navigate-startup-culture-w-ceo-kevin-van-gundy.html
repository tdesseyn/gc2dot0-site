about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with kevin van gundy: how to stand out in startup hiring through open source, networking, and showing genuine cultural fit rather than just technical skills.

get into startup networks by contributing to open source projects, especially integration layers and how-to guides. attend conferences and virtual meetups to build genuine relationships beyond cold applications and linkedin. show passion for the work and the company's mission, not just ability to complete assigned tasks. startups require comfort with ambiguity and self-direction; junior folks may struggle without clear structure. as a founder, experiment with ai systems in your business workflows to stay competitive in the coming shift. show genuine interest in a company by using their product or asking how the team works instead of only focusing on technical questions. startups tend to favor people comfortable with ambiguity who can set their own goals and manage up rather than wait for direction.
