about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: hiring managers need better communication upfront, should trust recruiters, and must stay flexible on candidate requirements and hiring processes.

hire leadership and managers before individual contributors to build stable teams. discuss rates and terms clearly at the start, not after offers are made. don't reject candidates based on resume alone if sent by a recruiter you trust. stay open to contract-to-hire and consulting roles, not just direct hire positions. stick to the interview process you promised candidates to build trust and respect. hire your leadership team first before bringing on a wave of developers at a small company or startup. agree on rate expectations with your recruiter upfront so you are not backtracking after an offer goes out. do not reject a candidate solely because of resume formatting or typos, talk to them first to see if they have the skills.
