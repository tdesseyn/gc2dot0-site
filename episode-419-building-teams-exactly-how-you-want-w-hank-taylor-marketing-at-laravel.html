about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with hank taylor: building small teams by hiring people with proven learning ability, strong work ethic, and low ego rather than narrow specialists.

titles matter less than salary, equity, and opportunity. negotiate as partners solving a problem together, not as adversaries. hire for adaptability and learning capacity over specific experience. dynamic people who wear multiple hats scale better in small companies. spend most time developing high performers, not struggling employees. protect your time to scale effectively as a manager. build relationships before you need them. stay in touch regularly with interesting people through calls, texts, and genuine check-ins. look for teammates with the ability to learn new skills quickly rather than only specific past experience. keep relationships alive by actually calling people instead of only messaging, since that effort compounds over time.
