about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with alain robineau: corporate recruiter shares how to stand out in job search, importance of feedback, and staying intentional during tough markets.

research the right person before messaging, mention specifics from their profile to show genuine interest. corporate and agency recruiting involve similar work of finding people, just for different numbers of employers. giving candidates detailed feedback after rejection builds company reputation and helps people improve. during market downturns, target senior roles at established companies or look for scaling startups with growth appetite. take feedback without defensiveness, share different viewpoints in communities, and don't fear making mistakes in your career. stand out by sending a pointed message that connects your background to the specific role instead of a generic pitch. in a tough market be intentional with outreach, tailor your resume to the job description, and get active in slack or discord communities instead of lurking.
