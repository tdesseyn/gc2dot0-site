about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with adam brown: recruiter-turned-developer shares how to navigate early career in tech, emphasizing soft skills and mentorship.

focus on one technology deeply at first, then expand into related tools and frameworks. ask recruiters in your region what skills are in demand rather than chasing every shiny language. soft skills matter as much as coding: frame questions carefully, stay curious, and avoid declaring any tech as definitively best. junior developers ramp up faster when paired with someone a year or two ahead of them for informal mentorship. ask questions freely without fear; most talented people respect engineers who admit gaps and seek understanding. pairing a brand new hire with someone just a year into the role can speed up ramp up for both people. frame questions you feel you should already know the answer to with self aware humor so you come across confident, not clueless.
