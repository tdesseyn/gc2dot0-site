about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with kevin blanco: kevin blanco shares how community involvement, passion, and consistency built his career from costa rica to senior developer relations at appsmith without a formal degree.

show up consistently even after rejections; being the least experienced person in the room means you will learn and grow. skip the formal degree and build real projects instead; employers want to see your code and github, not credentials. invest in soft skills like communication and empathy as much as technical skills to stand out in interviews. identify your end goal, quit comparing yourself to others, take action, build momentum, and surround yourself with the right people. keep applying to speak or contribute even after rejection, since consistency and showing up is what separates people over time.
