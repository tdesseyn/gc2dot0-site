about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with leslie greenwood: leslie greenwood shares her winding career path from retail to startup exits to community building, and why networking before you need a job matters.

leave a job when burnout hits and you've lost your ideal life profile, not just when money runs out. network in communities where your actual customers are, not everywhere; linkedin is where b2b go-to-market leaders live. people buy from people, not companies; build trust through vulnerability and authentic conversation over product promotion. don't wait to network when job hunting starts; begin two years prior so you start at zero or better, not negative five. step through doors when the path is clear and you see the opportunity; your five-year plan will change anyway. people do business with people they trust, not with a company page, so build rapport and show up as yourself. your career path can zigzag through layoffs and side steps and still lead somewhere good, so walk through open doors when you see them.
