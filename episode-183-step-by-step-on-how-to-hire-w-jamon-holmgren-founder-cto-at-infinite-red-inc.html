about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with jamon holmgren: how infinite red structures an intentional hiring process focused on culture fit, communication, and treating candidates with respect throughout.

hire slowly and deliberately, take time to feel the pain of open roles before rushing to fill them. use application forms to help candidates self-select and self-evaluate their skills honestly, not just repeat resume info. involve peers and future teammates in screening, not just leadership, to preserve culture and get authentic perspectives. test communication skills in your actual work environment early, like slack conversations with your ceo before technical screens. treat rejected candidates with respect and specificity in feedback because relationships matter even when they don't get hired. test communication skills in slack since remote work lives there and misreads cause real problems. give candidates honest specific feedback after rejection because even a short no is better than silence.

I am joined by Jamon Holmgren, Founder & CTO at Infinite Red, Inc. We are going to dive into his hiring processes because Jamon really understands being intentional!
