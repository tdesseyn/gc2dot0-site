about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with Brian Dougherty: how startup leaders transition to managing large enterprise teams by building alignment, culture, and intentional career development paths.

offer three career tracks: subject matter expert depth, generalist breadth, or management leadership, each with different growth work. align daily work to each person's career goals so they grow toward their desired path while getting business work done. lead with vulnerability and listening rather than technical expertise, taking blame first and praise last to build trust. be careful when narrowing a strong performer's scope of responsibility since it can feel demoralizing if not handled thoughtfully. help each person grow by figuring out if they want to be a generalist, a subject matter expert, or move into management, then assign work that fits.
