about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to use content creation and online engagement to reach more people in your job search.

post what you learn daily on linkedin or tiktok, even if short, to reach hiring managers and new connections. when someone corrects you online, dm them to start a conversation and ask for introductions to expand your network. tiktok reaches new audiences fast with zero followers, giving you hundreds of views per video compared to resume applications. if posting feels scary or traumatic, start by commenting and liking others posts instead of creating content yourself. hiring managers should post about their leadership style and team philosophy to attract better candidates without job board ads. post video recaps on tiktok of what you learned that day, even with zero followers you can get real views. hiring managers should post about how they lead and what they believe to attract talent instead of relying only on job boards.

In today's episode, it's just me! I talk about ideas on how to get your information in front of as many eyeballs as possible.
