about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with alishah novin: how hiring managers quickly sort resumes into call, maybe, or no piles, and what makes resumes stand out in the first few seconds.

show concrete impact with numbers and context instead of vague duties; saying what you did matters less than proving what it achieved. always include months and dates on every job, even old ones shortened to one line; missing them makes hiring managers suspicious. avoid self-congratulatory language and obvious job duties; tell hiring managers what they wouldn't already know about your work. format and readability matter; a clean resume makes hiring managers relax and want to keep reading instead of immediately rejecting. use numbers and comparisons whenever possible, like how your results stack up against an average or goal, not just raw stats alone. keep your resume format clean and scannable since hiring managers skim fast and a dense or dark page gets set aside.
