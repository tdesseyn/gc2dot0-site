about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: taylor shares recruiting industry insights and career advice for navigating a tough job market in early 2023.

take time in december to reflect on your job situation before making moves in the new year. network with recruiters who know the market well and can give you resume feedback and introductions. use bcc to reach out to multiple recruiters about opportunities since they handle hundreds of conversations. communicate upward with your manager about what you're doing and thinking to build trust. focus on personal branding by documenting your work and sharing online to get noticed by hiring managers. always ask recruiters for quick feedback on your resume or linkedin during any call with them.
