about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: come prepared with thoughtful questions when talking to recruiters to show genuine engagement and make a strong impression.

request clarity on their hiring process so you know what steps and interviews to expect. end every recruiter call by asking for another recruiter introduction to expand your network. don't try to trick people for referrals—just ask directly if they have an internal referral program. show up engaged to every call, chat, or video interview since each one is a unique interaction. always come with questions instead of saying you have none when asked. ask a recruiter how many people they placed last year to gauge how active they are.
