about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with aaron hurt: learn how amazon structures interviews, what hiring managers look for, and how to present yourself effectively in the process.

amazon uses phone screens filtered by experienced interviewers, then a five-hour loop with five one-on-one interviews covering technical skills and leadership principles. they focus on past behavior as a predictor of future success, asking specific stories about how you handled situations. empathy and ability to understand customers and teammates across large-scale projects are key soft skills amazon values. match your resume to the job description with specific technologies mentioned, but be honest about your skill level because interviewers will test claims. amazon managers source candidates themselves on linkedin and spend hours weekly reviewing profiles, so connecting directly can help bypass initial screening. match your resume language to the job description, since missing keywords like a specific os or tool can keep you from getting a phone screen. avoid vague words like expert or massive on your resume or in interviews, be specific and ready to back up any claim you make.
