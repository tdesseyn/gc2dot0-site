about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with jean leggett: how to write resumes that speak to hiring managers' pain points and showcase your accomplishments without apology.

pull language directly from job descriptions to show hiring managers you understand their specific needs. turn soft skills into concrete impact statements with numbers, scope, and results instead of generic phrases. prepare interview stories using the star method (situation, task, action, result) that match your resume bullets. invest time in understanding your career story with compassion and learner's mindset before applying. prep for interviews by reading each resume bullet aloud and making sure you can explain the story behind it. stop apologizing for your achievements on a resume, this is the one place you're supposed to brag.

I am joined by an amazing woman named Jean Leggett who is an amazing coach/speaker/resume writer! We are going to dive into how to build a resume and communicate to hiring managers what you've done!
