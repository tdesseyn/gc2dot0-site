about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with nate taylor: how to improve hiring by reading resumes better, avoiding eliminating qualified candidates, and deciding between waiting for the perfect hire or ramping someone up.

show problem-solving in resumes, not just tech skills and duties listed. make it clear you can code and how many years of relevant experience you have. limit interview process to max four rounds to avoid losing good candidates. top engineers take two to three months minimum to ramp up on new teams. decide based on team maturity whether you can afford time to mentor or need someone productive immediately. long interview processes with too many touch points can cause good candidates to drop out on their own. before hiring, weigh the time to find a perfect fit against the time it takes to ramp up someone less experienced.
