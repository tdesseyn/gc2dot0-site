about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with Will King: how ai affects junior developers and the job market, plus strategies for standing out through relationships and building valuable work samples.

use ai as a mentor to understand thinking processes, not as a code-writing crutch that shortcuts your learning. junior jobs aren't disappearing because ai can't solve business problems or communicate why decisions matter. demonstrate value by building artifacts and projects that show you can learn quickly and think through multiple solutions. start saying hi to people and building relationships sooner, especially with folks doing interesting work in your field. use ai to practice decision making by asking for three variations of a solution plus an argument for the best one. build relationships early by saying hey to people you find interesting and sharing your work publicly instead of waiting until you need something.
