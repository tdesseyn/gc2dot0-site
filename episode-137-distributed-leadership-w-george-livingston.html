about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with george livingston: how to build distributed teams through intentional written communication, structured one-on-ones, and in-person connection.

improve written communication skills since remote work requires clear emails and docs instead of shoulder taps. structure one-on-ones as thirds: chit chat, work updates, then how you can help remove barriers. don't copy in-person activities to virtual; redesign them for distributed teams like game show activities. carve out intentional unstructured time and monthly in-person lunches to build team camaraderie. follow up after interviews with thoughtful written messages to show communication skills job seekers need remotely. job seekers should follow up after interviews since strong written communication stands out in a distributed hiring process.
