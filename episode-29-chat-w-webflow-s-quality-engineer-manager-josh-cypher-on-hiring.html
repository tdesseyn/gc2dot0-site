about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with josh cypher: how remote work changed hiring, compensation, interviews, and what managers look for in candidates during and after the pandemic.

global remote work means competing on salary worldwide, not just tier one markets like sf or nyc. if you plan to return to office eventually, expect attrition risk from candidates who chose remote for flexibility. replace long trial projects with efficient structured interviews that show communication and reasoning. soft skills like articulation, asking good questions, and taking feedback matter as much as technical skills. align career moves with your personal long term goals, not just what pays bills right now. think about succession planning for hires who may only stay a short time during uncertain job markets. in interviews explain your reasoning out loud since communicating how you think matters as much as the right answer.
