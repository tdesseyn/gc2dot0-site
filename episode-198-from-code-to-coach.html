about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with jonny scharer: how a failed bootcamp job search led to technical career coaching and why non-developer tech roles can be the better path.

generic job applications fail. target specific companies, research people there, find common ground, then reach out directly. three-tier application system works: tier one is dream companies with personalized outreach, tier two is okay companies, tier three is easy applies. getting one job offer unlocks others. once you have an offer, tell other companies youre interviewing with and watch more offers come. post on linkedin consistently, build your network passively while also reaching out actively to the right people like recruiters. invest value in people first before asking for anything. write draft recommendations for busy people youre asking for help from. before asking for a referral or recommendation, add value first, think of it like a social bank where you invest before you withdraw. if software development isn't clicking, stay open to roles like qa testing or low code work since internal promotion is easier than restarting a search.

I am joined by Jonny Scharer who recently went through the job search and we are going to talk about how you don't have to be a developer to get into tech!
