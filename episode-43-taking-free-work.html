about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, hosted by taylor desseyn: how to gain job experience by volunteering your time on real projects, even just an hour a day.

volunteer at least one hour a day on real projects like nonprofit websites or small business needs. contact nonprofits, churches, or small businesses directly and offer to help solve their problems. showing your value through free work gets you noticed faster than waiting for paid opportunities. ask people directly if you can help them, get comfortable with the ask. offering a small amount of free value upfront can help people see your worth and lead to paid work. volunteering your time an hour a day can build real skills when you lack job experience. treat volunteer work as professional experience and list it on your resume and linkedin. reach out to nonprofits and churches since they often need web or digital help.
