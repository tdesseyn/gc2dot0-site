about this episode

on this episode of guidance counselor 2.0, taylor desseyn talks with colin young: how to build lasting behavior change through autonomy, mastery, and purpose, plus strategies for avoiding burnout.

behavior change requires three things: autonomy (your own journey), mastery (feeling competent), and purpose (clear goals). recovery and restraint matter more than just grinding harder; rest enables better performance. practice stillness and focus without technology to build mental clarity and presence with work and family. structure your day in 90-minute work blocks followed by 20-minute breaks away from screens. be authentic in your career and take time to reflect instead of copying others' approaches. create friction against bad habits, like moving your phone to another room or keeping snacks out of reach.

In today's episode, I'm joined by my friend, Colin Young. Colin is a Senior Director at EXOS. We dive into what makes people successful in the workplace.
